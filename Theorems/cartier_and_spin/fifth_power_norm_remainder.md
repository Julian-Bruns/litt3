# Frobenius-power norm forces a constant remainder

Version2,3 October2026. Let K be any field of prime characteristic p,
let f be in K but not K^p, and let L/K be
a finite separable extension generated by b. Let F be the monic
minimal polynomial of b, of degree N. If
\[
\operatorname{Nm}_{L/K}(f+b^p)\in K^{\times p},
\]
then N>=p and there is a unique pair consisting of a polynomial H
and nonzero tau in K with
\[
F(W)=(W^p+f)H(W)+\tau.
\]
Conversely such a presentation implies the norm condition. The same
equivalence, uniqueness and lower bound hold for every separable
polynomial F of positive degree N and its actual finite source algebra
K[W]/(F), with b the class of W. F may be nonmonic or reducible.
The equivalence and uniqueness also hold for nonzero constant F, but
the degree lower bound does not: F=1 has zero source algebra and norm1.
This corrects the degree-zero loophole in the 30 September statement;
the primitive-field clause is unchanged apart from removing unused
perfect-constant and one-variable hypotheses.

For the fixed characteristic-five admissible primitive, the
[uniform norm theorem](uniform_admissible_norm.md) therefore yields this
presentation in every primitive source degree. Its low weighted trace
identities and centered energy are given by
[the general remainder theorem](source_quadratic_calculus.md).
This is an algebraic restriction on the actual source polynomial, not
an existence or nonexistence theorem for the source or for a common cover.
