## Horizontal spaces for an arbitrary q≠0 quintic

Write Φ=z⁵+qz⁴+rz³+sz²+tz+u, F=A3+yB1, B1=βz+γ, and ∂=y d/dz. Put C0=Φ″/2. The two sectors of ∂²F=EF are
\[
\Phi A_3''+\tfrac12\Phi'A_3'=C A_3+v\Phi B_1,
\quad (\Phi B_1'+\tfrac12\Phi'B_1)'=CB_1+vA_3,
\]
where E=C+vy. The left side of the odd equation is4βΦ′+B1C0.

For nonpolynomial E, C=C0,v²=q. Hence A3=4βΦ′/v. Substitution into the even equation gives γ=4βr/q. Thus its entire bounded horizontal space is one-dimensional, generated by
\[
F=(4/v)\Phi'+y(z+4r/q).
\]
There is no nonzero solution with β=0.

For polynomial E=2qz²+bz+c, the odd equation forces B1=β(z−b/q); its remaining coefficients are exactly the already proved cubic/c relation for b,c. Its even equation concerns A3 alone. Its leading z⁶ and z⁵ coefficients force the cubic and quadratic coefficients of A3 to vanish. A nonzero linear solution exists IF AND ONLY IF
\[
b^2+3rb+qs=0,
\]
and is then generated by z+(2r−3b)/q. The constant equation is automatic modulo this relation and the dormant cubic. Otherwise A3=0. Therefore each polynomial E always supplies the pure-odd horizontal function, with one additional even direction only under the displayed quadratic condition.

## The five fixed origins give five isolated forms

The F5-Möbius stabilizer of4 acts transitively on∞,0,1,2,3 and preserves this set. Hence the chosen fixed origin can be moved to∞ in the same family, with parameter t′∉F5. Its coefficients satisfy q=−(1+t′),r=−q,s=q,t=−q−1,u=0. The even-direction condition becomes(b+q)²=0, but the dormant cubic at b=−q equalsq²≠0. Thus no polynomial E has an additional even direction.

Set w=z+1. Direct expansion gives Φ=w⁵+qw⁴+4w+4q. The polynomial dormant cubic is now b³=4q²; writing a=b/q gives exactly the three pure-odd forms y(w−a),a³=4/q. The two nonpolynomial horizontal functions are νw³+1/ν+yw withν²=q. Their pole orders are SEVEN.

For the pure-odd functions, Φ(a)=4(a+q)²/q. A zero could be a branch point only if a=−q, which together with a³=4/q would give q⁴=1 and hence q∈F5, impossible. Thus the five branch zeros and the two zeros above a are all simple.

For either mixed function its norm is
\[
(\nu w^3+1/\nu)^2-w^2\Phi
=4w^7+3w^3+qw^2+q^{-1}.
\]
It has no zero at a branch point because Φ′ is nonzero there and its even part is4Φ′/ν. At any nonbranch finite zero the regular second-order differential equation implies that a multiple zero has multiplicity at leastFIVE: its leading coefficient has m(m−1)=0 in characteristic FIVE. A multiplicity≥5 would force the degree-seven norm to be a fifth power of a linear factor times a quadratic, whose w³ coefficient is ZERO. The actual norm has w³ coefficientTHREE, a contradiction. Thus all mixed zeros are simple as well.

## The moving origin leaves precisely the low-A pencil

At P=t, the explicit reciprocal-coordinate normalization followed by translation gives Φ=w⁵+qw⁴+h with qh≠0. Here the polynomial dormant cubic is b³=0, so its only geometric coefficient is E=2qw². The extra-even criterion holds, giving exactly⟨w,yw⟩.

The nonpolynomial coefficients give horizontal functions ±√q w³+yw. Their norm is−w²(w⁵+h). The unique root ρ ofρ⁵=−h is nonzero, is not a branch point because Φ(ρ)=qρ⁴≠0, and gives a zero of multiplicityFIVE on one sheet. These functions cannot give a reduced wild fiber.

The pole-seven members of the remaining pencil are w(y+a). If a²=h, its zero above w=0 on one sheet has multiplicityFIVE because Φ−a²=w⁴(w+q). If a²≠h, both points above w=0 give simple zeros, and all other zeros are simple: away from w=0 the derivativeΦ′=4qw³ is nonzero. For a=0 the other five zeros are the branch points and are still simple in their actual y-uniformizers. Thus precisely a²≠h is the reduced-fiber condition, and the pure-odd member must be kept.

All these horizontal functions satisfy the cubic Cartier gate: the classified oper is dormant, so a second rational horizontal column exists; its Wronskian is a nonzero fifth power. The derivative of the ratio of its first component to F is that fifth power times σ/F². Cartier exactness and its Frobenius rule then give C(σ/F²)=0 and C(F³σ)=0. This is only a low-function construction on the actual Y. It does not build Γ, either endpoint map, or the high large-wild Frobenius remainder.
