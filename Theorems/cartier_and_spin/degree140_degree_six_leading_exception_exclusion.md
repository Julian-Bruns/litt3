# The two degree-six leading traces cannot both degenerate at an actual scale

Version1,30 September2026. Retain the constant degree140 primitive
family, its original ratio open and normalized critical traces from
[degree140_normalized_critical_trace](degree140_normalized_critical_trace.md).
Let H=h*w, q=w^3 and let ell denote the unnormalized scale. Define
\[
Q_3=\operatorname{Tr}(x^3v/\phi),\qquad
Q_y=\operatorname{Tr}(yv/\phi).
\]
Both have degree at most six in ell. The common zero scheme of their
degree-six coefficients on the original ratio open is reduced of length187
over K=F_(5^8). Its residue-field degrees over K are
\[
1,1,5,23,157.
\]
At every geometric point of this entire exceptional scheme, the two
necessary traces Tr(t*v) and Tr(x*t*v) have no common nonzero scale.
Consequently no entirely ramified nonzero critical fibre, and hence no
actual étale candidate in this family, occurs on that scheme.

Every remaining actual candidate is therefore covered by the two charts
where respectively the leading coefficient of Q_3 or Q_y is nonzero.
On each chart there is a monic necessary scale equation of degree six.
This does not assert a single global monic-six combination over the
original ratio ring. It does not decide either remaining chart, the
root-nine family, or the unmarked common-cover problem.
