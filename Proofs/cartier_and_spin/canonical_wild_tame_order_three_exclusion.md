# Proof: the actual tame canonical section has a Weierstrass gap pole

Version1. [Statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/canonical_wild_tame_order_three_exclusion.md). [Independent whole-profile review: PASS](../../Research/audits/CANONICAL_WILD_TAME_ORDER_THREE_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md).

The argument is valid for any actual faithful canonical wild carrier with one wild branch of inertia e and different Δ and one tame branch of inertia m. Let n be its degree and g=gcd(e,m). The accepted exact area ledger gives n=em/g and
\[
m(\Delta-e)=e+g.
\]
Denote the reduced stack divisors by D_w,D_t, and the pullback of the coarse O_{P¹}(1) by U. The canonical different formula is the genuine line identity
\[
K_S=-2U+\Delta D_w+(m-1)D_t.
\]
Using eD_w=U and mD_t=U gives
\[
mK_S=gD_w.
\]
The effective divisor gD_w therefore supplies a nonzero section t_m of mK_S. On the ACTUAL source, normalize its pullback by s^m. Both sections have the same genuine G-action, so their ratio descends to a rational function F_m on Y. Its only possible pole is at P.

If the distinguished point is not over the wild value, the section t_m is a unit there. Thus F_m has exact sole pole order m at P. This includes a distinguished tame cone: t_m's zero divisor is supported only at the wild value.

If the distinguished point is over the wild value, its index-two ramification for φ multiplies the stack divisor pullback at P by TWO. Consequently t_m has zero order2g there, and F_m has pole order m−2g if this is positive. The actual mixed-fiber lemma states n/e=2+u with u≥1 for this position.

For(60,20,3,27), g=1. The sole pole is3 when the distinguished point is ordinary or tame, and1 when it is wild. Both positive orders are gaps at a genus-two Weierstrass point. For(30,30,3,41), g=3 and n/e=1, so mixedness prevents the distinguished point from being wild. Its sole pole is again3. A nonconstant rational function with any of these exact sole pole orders is impossible. This excludes both profiles while retaining the original source and both maps.
