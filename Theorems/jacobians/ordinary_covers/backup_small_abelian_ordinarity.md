# Ordinary exponent-four and exponent-six covers on the good cubic orbit

Version3,3 October2026. The maximal-two theorem removes the old
fifteen-double computation; affine/Frobenius hindsight extends the seed.

Let Y_t:v²=u(u-1)(u-2)(u-3)(u-t) over bar(F5), t outside F5.
Put z=(t+1)^-1, I=(z^5-z)^4. Assume
\[
I^3+2I^2+4I+4=0.
\]
This is the complete sixty-parameter good cubic orbit, including the
backup B=Y_alpha, alpha^3+alpha+1=0. The maximal abelian exponent-four
and exponent-six finite etale covers of every Y_t in this locus have
ordinary Jacobians. Their degrees are respectively256 and1296.

Every connected abelian finite etale cover of Y_t with group exponent
dividing four or six is therefore ordinary, as is every connected
finite etale five-group cover of such a curve. This is Jacobian
ordinarity, not indigenous ordinarity. The stronger actual-span
monodromy exclusion remains in the
[unit-root theorem](../isogeny_sieves/five_by_abelian_unit_root_exclusion.md).
