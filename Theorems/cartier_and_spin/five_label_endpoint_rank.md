# Five endpoint labels span three coefficient directions

Version1, 27 September2026. Let K0=F_(5^14), F=F_(5^56), and let
alpha satisfy (5,2,6,7,1) over the prescribed F25. For a primitive29th
root zeta in K0 put
\[
C_{i,j}=c(\alpha^{25^i})\zeta^{5j},\quad
E_{i,j}=e(\alpha^{25^i})\zeta^{8j},\quad
c=(22,7,9,23),\quad e=(1,3,8,15).
\]
For a multiset L of five labels (i,j) in Z/4 times Z/29, allowing
repetitions, let C_L,E_L be the unaveraged sums. Then
\[
\dim_{K0}\langle1,C_L,E_L\rangle=
\begin{cases}1,&L\text{ is five copies of one label},\\
3,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
This is an exhaustive exact coefficient theorem. It is not a search
over fields of definition of curves or over actual covers.
