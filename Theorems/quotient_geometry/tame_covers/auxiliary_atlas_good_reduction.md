# An auxiliary tame atlas forces good reduction of a prime-degree atlas

Version1,21September2026. Let $R$ be a complete mixed-characteristic
DVR with residue characteristic $p$, and let $\mathscr S/R$ be a
smooth proper effective orbifold curve. Suppose it has a representable
finite etale atlas $\mathscr H\to\mathscr S$ that is a torsor under
a constant group $G$ of order prime to $p$, with $\mathscr H/R$ a
smooth proper connected curve of genus at least two.

Let $C$ be a smooth proper connected generic-fiber curve with
potentially good reduction, and let
\[
C\longrightarrow\mathscr S_K
\]
be a representable finite etale atlas of degree $n$ prime to $p$.
After finite extension of $R$, this actual atlas extends to a
representable finite etale map from the good model of $C$ to
$\mathscr S$. There is no assumption on the order of its own
geometric monodromy group.

In particular, a characteristic-zero complete uniform triangle map
of degree prime to $p$ specializes with its full profile whenever
the same triangle orbifold has such an auxiliary prime-to-$p$
Galois atlas. At $p=5$, this applies to $(3,3,4)$ using
$\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbf F_3)$ of order24, and to $(2,3,7)$ using
$\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbf F_7)$ of order168.

Consequently a characteristic-zero genus-two curve with potentially
good geometric reduction equal to the explicit F125 backup admits
neither triangle profile. This conclusion uses the established
backup tame-atlas exclusion, not good reduction of a presumed
quotient map.
