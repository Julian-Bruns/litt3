## Main route: actual non-Galois intermediate curves

Read PROJECTIVE_FROBENIUS_DEFECT_AND_NONGALOIS_QUOTIENT_TESTS.md.
For an actual Galois étale W -> Y with arbitrary finite group G, the
Frobenius-nilpotent module N = H^1(W,O)_nil is projective over kG.
Writing N = sum m_S P(S), every subgroup H satisfies

    g(W/H) - f(W/H) = sum m_S [k[G/H] : S].

This retains actual intermediate curves and works when 5 divides |G|.
Projectivity is a classical consequence of Köck Corollary 2.3 and Borne
Lemmas 2.1/2.11; no novelty claim. Our proof uses Cartan–Leray and group
cohomology. Its main steps were independently checked by agent
canonical_trace_algebra; no full separate audit has yet been linked.
The useful question is which auxiliary quotients force the source ordinary
without forcing the whole Galois closure ordinary.

QUOTIENT_ORDINARITY_S4_AND_EXTRASPECIAL_TESTS.md supplies two tests:
S4 degree-six quotient ordinary forces the degree-four quotient ordinary;
for extraspecial groups every nonlinear irreducible occurs in every
faithful transitive quotient, so the method cannot hide their defect.
These are conditional on actual covers, not realization assertions.
