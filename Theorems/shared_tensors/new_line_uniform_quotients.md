# No nontrivial uniform Galois-local quotient of the new-line tensor

Version2,23September2026. On the fixed genus-nine curve X let
tau=A^16 theta^13=(df)^13/A^10, with div tau=16R, deg R=13.
Suppose phi:X->C is a separable map to a smooth proper curve,
its geometric Galois closure is ETale over X, and tau is
proportional to phi^*s for a rational weight-thirteen tensor s.
This is the coarse-map condition for a tensor-preserving effective
proper DM quotient with X as an etale atlas.

Then deg phi=1. Equivalently, X has no nontrivial literal
tensor-preserving effective proper DM quotient with an etale atlas.
All wild indices and all monodromy orders are included.

The formerly surviving profile required a tame index13 fiber and
a wild fiber R of index e=75 mod260. Local tensor invariance at R
instead forces the prime-to-five part of e to divide29, so e is
1 mod4, a contradiction. The primitive expressions
t=(f+b^5)^13/A^10 still satisfy their rational tensor identity;
they do not furnish locally Galois covers of that profile.

The theorem assumes an actual etale Galois closure over X. It does
not manufacture a finite common Galois envelope for a coreless
correspondence. Unrestricted new-line field recognition remains open.
