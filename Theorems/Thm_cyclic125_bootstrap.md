2. Every GIVEN compatible T7 extending T2^0 has its GIVEN T4 descend
   compatibly along h to some C4 extending C2^0. This recovers the
   given T3 and T4, including marking, Hodge line, projective graded
   map and original flat twist. C3,C4 may differ from the reference.

The initial third divided normal obstruction for
T3^0+d e123+b e124 is exactly d^5e in k[e]/e², after the intervening
compatible repairs. The convention is rho(S+xi)=rho(S)-Psi(xi).

their free choices do not change this class.

upper Witt tower descends uniquely along the ORIGINAL h and its maps
algebraize over W(k). This does not assert that arbitrary given T7
itself descends to precision7.

The result uses an integral filtration on the combined repairs; it
does not assert the false inclusion (e23 A1)^2⊂e² A1 for separate
function cochains. No early theorem for degrees>125 or unmarked
common-cover exclusion is asserted here.
