# A horizontal rank-three bundle with a degree-one/two/two quotient is stable

Version1, 3 October2026. [Independent five-point static review: PASS](../../Research/audits/OCT03_GRAM_FOUR_DEGREE_ONE_HORIZONTAL_STABILITY_STATIC_REVIEW.md). No computation or literature certificate is needed.

Let Y be a smooth projective genus-two curve over an algebraically closed field of characteristic five. Let E be a rank-three degree-five bundle with a regular algebraic connection. Suppose
\[
0\longrightarrow B\longrightarrow E
\longrightarrow Q_1\oplus Q_2\longrightarrow0,
\qquad\deg B=1,\quad\deg Q_1=\deg Q_2=2.
\]
Then E is stable. The connection need not preserve B, either quotient line, or any subbundle used in the proof.

If P is Weierstrass, B=O_Y(P) and Q1=Q2=ωY, the two extension classes form a basis of H1(O_Y(−P)), of dimension two. Consequently E has the unique ordinary isomorphism class of the universal extension
\[
\boxed{0\longrightarrow O_Y(P)\longrightarrow E
\longrightarrow\omega_Y^{\oplus2}\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{with two independent extension classes}.}
\]
This uniqueness is of the underlying ordinary bundle only; it identifies no marked evaluation, connection, zero-p-curvature structure, relative-Frobenius root or embedded Cartier lattice.

In the actual same-source two-map eight-dimensional perfect Gram-four packet, the [original unit quotient splitting](canonical_ten_gram_four_original_kernel_quotient_splitting.md) therefore makes F_Y*K stable whenever its original saturated relation line B has degree one, without requiring saturation of K in B1,Y. On the accepted saturated constant and surviving linear charts, B=O(P); when their [marked quotient class](canonical_ten_gram_four_degree_one_marked_quotient.md) is trivial, the displayed universal extension applies. Both actual finite étale endpoint maps remain on their SAME original source. No source exclusion or construction follows from this ordinary bundle classification.
