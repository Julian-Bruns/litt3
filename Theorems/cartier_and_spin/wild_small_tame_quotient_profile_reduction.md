# Small tame quotients of wild inertia leave two exact profiles

Version1,3 October2026. Conceptual deduction passed [independent focused review](../../Research/audits/WILD_SMALL_TAME_QUOTIENT_PROFILE_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md).

Retain both actual finite étale endpoint maps and the actual ramified
spin quotient. Let n be its EVEN effective degree, and let the quotient
have exactly one wild and one tame branch value. Write the wild inertia
order as e=h5^s, with 5∤h and s≥1, its different as Δ, and the other
inertia order as m, with m≥2 and 5∤m. Use the established exact ledger
e,m|n and Δ/e−1−1/m=1/n.

If h≤3, the only remaining profiles are
\[
(h,n,e,m,\Delta)=(1,10,5,2,8),\qquad(2,20,10,4,13).
\]
The second profile has first wild lower break one. Tame quotient h=3
is impossible. Neither remaining profile is excluded by this theorem;
inertia with h≥4 remains unrestricted here. Original normalization
degrees are n or3n, according to the projective kernel order.
