# Full signed-cubic monodromy of the root-nine critical cover

Version1,29 September2026. At every allowed geometric ratio of the
actual root-nine family, the norm to k(x) of its critical quadratic
discriminant Delta is nonsquare. This includes positive-content ratios.

Together with the proved nondescent of Delta's square class, the three
cubic conjugates of that class are linearly independent over F2.
Consequently the Galois closure of the critical degree-six map to P1_x
has group
\[
(C_2)^3\rtimes C_3\simeq C_2\times A_4,
\]
where C3 cyclically permutes the three factors. Its degree is24.
There is no A4 or cyclic-six degeneration anywhere on this ratio chart.

This is the **auxiliary critical cover**, generally ramified. It is
not the proposed degree-ten étale source, and the theorem neither
decides the specialized residual-square locus nor the common-cover
problem.
