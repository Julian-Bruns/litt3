# Independent pure-power leading forms give a finite pair algebra

Version 1, 30 September 2026. Let L be a field and m>=1. Suppose two
polynomials in L[x,y] have total degree at most m, and their homogeneous
degree-m parts are independent linear combinations of x^m and y^m.
Then their quotient algebra has L-dimension at most m^2, spanned by
\[
x^iy^j,\qquad0\le i,j<m.
\]
It consequently has at most m^2 geometric solution pairs, without an
existence assertion. The former cubic reconstruction bound is the
specialization m=3, of length at most nine.

If L=H(t) with H finite, any solution x,y in bar(H)(t) is defined
over a constant extension of H of degree at most m^2. This bounds the
solution pair only; further equations and actual maps remain to be checked.
