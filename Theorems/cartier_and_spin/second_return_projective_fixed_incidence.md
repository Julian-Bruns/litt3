in [extension geometry](rank_three_extension_return_geometry.md),
the complete fixed-target matrix is a map of bundles on P18,
\[
\mathcal A=O(-25)^{35}\oplus O(-24)^{16}
 \longrightarrow O^{80}\oplus O(1)^{43}.
\]
Its projective kernel with nonzero determinant, outside the accepted
stability surface, is exactly the stable strict second-return locus.
The number N of its geometric source points satisfies
\[
0\le N\le [t^{18}](1-26t)^{-35}(1-25t)^{-16}
=302049335526089458755236888065935448825400.
\]
Each such point has residue degree at most this integer over F25,
and its isomorphism can be defined over the same residue field.
This is not a positive count or an executed exhaustive search.

stable and have zero fixed-target Hom. Their finite list is excluded,
not a geometric open family. The actual nonsplit quotient domain
still has no certified point. The complete matrix construction
reuses the accepted exact atlas; it is not counted as a new decision.
