Version1,3 October2026. New bounded two-map theorem, pending independent review. Arbitrary-degree tensor recognition remains OPEN.

Use the fixed X, q0, θ and τ=q0⁸θ³ of [the actual exact-root bridge](actual_q0_tensor_exact_root_self_span.md). Suppose two actual finite étale maps h1,h2:S→X from the SAME smooth projective connected source have degree at most TWO and satisfy h1*τ∼h2*τ. Then
\[
h2=γ\circ h1\qquad\text{for some }γ∈\operatorname{Aut}(X)=C3.
\]
Consequently their embedded X-fields are identical. No second endpoint is substituted: if S also has an actual étale Y-map, that original map remains on S. The auxiliary ramified root source used in the proof is not a replacement common-cover source.

Separately, the genus-eleven exact-root model Z with marked differential η has
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(Z,kη)=\operatorname{Aut}(Z,U+V)=C3.
\]
This automorphism statement does not recognize arbitrary higher-degree étale self-spans. The bounded two-map conclusion above uses additional actual X-lifts and their elliptic quotient, rather than applying the automorphism statement to an arbitrary span.
