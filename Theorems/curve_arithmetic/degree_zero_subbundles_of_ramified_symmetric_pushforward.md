# A ramified symmetric primitive pushforward has only its unit degree-zero subbundle

Version1,3 October2026. Independent whole review [PASS](../../Research/audits/DEGREE_ZERO_SYMMETRIC_PUSHFORWARD_AND_FOUR_RADICAL_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). No computation is used.

Let f:T→C be a finite separable map of smooth projective connected curves over an algebraically closed field. Suppose its actual geometric normal closure has monodromy S_n in the degree-n permutation action, n≥TWO. Suppose at least one actual completed inertia group contains an element acting as a transposition on the n sheets. Then every nonzero degree-ZERO subbundle H⊂f_*O_T is exactly the rank-ONE unit subbundle O_C. The claim allows characteristic dividing n or |S_n| and wild ramification elsewhere.

Here a subbundle means an integral locally free saturated subsheaf. An auxiliary normal closure of THIS SINGLE map is used; no simultaneous closure or replacement of two original maps is presumed.
