\[

\theta₁=\kappa t^{16}\theta₂,
\quad\kappa\ne0,

and disjoint actual infinity divisors Hᵢ=hᵢ*O, with div(t)=H₁−H₂. Thus t has fifteen simple zeros and fifteen simple poles and g(C₀)=121. When this source is descended from an original T, retain both original endpoint legs on that SAME T as well.

\[
u=x₂+1,\quad v=t³(x₁+1),\qquad u²-v²=d(t⁶-1),
\]

\[

\[

## Conclusion

The ENTIRE defining section ρ divides neither a nor b. Equivalently neither a/ρ nor b/ρ is a global section of O_S(3D+10F∞).

This does not assert that an individual conductor component cannot divide an extension, that a genuinely mixed constant row cannot extend, or that every degree-fifteen source is excluded.
