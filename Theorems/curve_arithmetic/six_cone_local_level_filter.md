# The relevant six-cone arithmetic place has maximal split level

Version1,21September2026. Let $\Delta$ be a maximal compact arithmetic
Fuchsian lattice of signature $(0;2,2,2,2,2,2)$. Let $F$ be its
invariant trace field, $B/F$ its quaternion algebra, and
$\mathfrak n$ the square-free Eichler level in its normalizer
description. If a place $v\mid5$ has residue degree divisible by
three, then
\[
B_v\simeq M_2(F_v),\qquad v\nmid\mathfrak n.
\]
Ramification of $F_v/\mathbf Q_5$ is allowed. No condition on
other places over five is asserted.

The proof uses the genus-two index-two subgroup, the general bound
$[F:\mathbf Q]\le10$, and odd denominators of $\zeta_F(-1)$.
It does not enumerate arithmetic trace fields or restrict to
derived or congruence surface groups.
