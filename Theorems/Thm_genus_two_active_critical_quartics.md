# Genus-two active connections from fifteen critical quartics

Version2,2026-09-10. Author proof with standalone exact certificates;
not independently audited. Work over an algebraically closed field k
of characteristic5. Let Y: v²=F(u), F monic squarefree of degree5,
eta=du/v, with Weierstrass point O at infinity.

Represent each nonzero class of Pic(Y)[2] by a pair of the six
Weierstrass points. Put R equal to the monic product of the finite
abscissas of the pair, so deg R is1 or2, and put S=F/R. Define

    D=R S²,       J=R' S+2R S',       K=D^[6],
    c=[u^4](S R²),

where D^[6] is the sixth Hasse derivative. J has degree exactly4.
The normalized quartics of ACTIVE regular nilpotent connections
with this Hasse root class are precisely the following DISTINCT tensors:

    c S eta^4                         if c!=0;
    K(h) R(u)(u-h)² eta^4              for J(h)=0, K(h)!=0.

Every listed tensor has four double zeros and reconstructs the actual
regular connection by the scalar nilpotent dictionary. No geometric
root is restricted to the coefficient field. Repeated quartic roots
count only once here; no local multiplicity claim is implicit.
There are at most five such active connections in each nonzero class.

## The already selected high-degree family

For F=u(u-1)(u-2)(u-3)(u-t), if [F5(t):F5]>11 (in particular for
the already selected high-prime-degree t over F25), then:

- every one of the fifteen J is squarefree and coprime to its K,
  and all fifteen c are nonzero;
- there are exactly75 nonsplit active connections, five in each class;
- there are exactly10 split active connections and five dormant ones;
- ALL85 active connections are ordinary in the INDIGENOUS nilpotent
  deformation sense;
- every connected etale double of Y has exactly15 dormant connections,
  all reduced. For every dormant r on Y and every L in Pic(Y)[2],
  its L-twisted dormant tangent kernel is zero.

The last two conclusions use the existing canonical-double counting and
tangent theorem, not an identification of ordinary Jacobians with ordinary
connections. The arithmetic certificate is a single exact specialization
at a²+4a+2=0, together with proved degree bounds at most11 on the relevant
nonzero parameter polynomials. It does not enumerate a huge finite field.

Scope: this removes the endpoint ambiguity in the AP answer for our chosen
Y. It does NOT make an arbitrary common-source connection ordinary, prove
AP, provide a common connection, or exclude an unmarked common cover.

## An explicit one-direction nonordinary boundary

The parameter-degree hypothesis cannot simply be omitted, even when
the Jacobian is ordinary. Let t be a root of the irreducible polynomial

    t^4+4t^3+t^2+4t+3=0

over F5, retain the above F, and put

    P(u)=2u^3+(2t+3)u^2+(3t^2+3)u+2t^3+4t^2+4t+4,
    r=2(F'/F)^2-F''/F+P/F.

This is an actual regular admissible ACTIVE nilpotent connection on
the smooth genus-two curve C:v^2=F(u). Its Jacobian is ordinary: its
Cartier determinant is4. Its FIXED-curve nilpotent tangent space is
exactly the line generated by

    phi=(u^2+(t+3)u+2t^2+4)(du)^2/F.

In particular its indigenous infinitesimal Verschiebung has corank
one. No local scheme length is inferred from this tangent calculation.
No W3 lift or obstruction value, and no common-cover example, is
asserted. This is a minimal-dimension laboratory for the higher Hodge
obstruction, not a replacement choice of endpoints.
