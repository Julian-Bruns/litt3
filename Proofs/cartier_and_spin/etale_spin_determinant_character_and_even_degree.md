## Hurwitz bounds the even carrier degree

Since φ is étale and g(Y)=TWO,
\[

\[
\frac{2a}{e}
=2g(Z/H)-2+\sum_{b}\frac{\delta_b}{|I_b|}.
\tag{3}
\]
Each denominator |I_b| is odd and each numerator δ_b is even. The right side has the form an EVEN integer divided by an ODD integer. Its positive TWO-adic valuation is therefore at leastONE. Equation(3) implies v₂(e)≤v₂(a), or e₂ divides a.
