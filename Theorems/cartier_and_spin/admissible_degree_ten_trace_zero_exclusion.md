# No actual admissible trace-zero degree-ten source

Version1,1 October2026. Use the fixed genus-nine curve and actual
admissibility of [the reconstruction](admissible_line_reconstruction.md).
Let h:S->X be connected finite etale of degree ten, H=h*O, and suppose
phi=f+b^5 has divisor3E-5G-10H, with reduced E<=h*R of degree50,
effective G of degree10, and disjoint E,G. In the established primitive
polynomial, assume N1=0. The N1=N2=0 boundary is already excluded;
in the remaining sector N2!=0 the exhaustive infinity profiles are
[the constant and linear profiles](admissible_degree_ten_two_profiles.md).

Then
\[
H^0(S,\mathcal O_S(10H-2E))=0.
\]
In particular T=E-G-4H cannot be killed by five. Thus no actual
admissible trace-zero degree-ten source exists, for any geometric scale,
any of the four omitted-root supports, or any critical collision.
The proof treats all finite multiplicities of G and does not assume
that E or G avoids H.

The new mechanism is a trace-dual annihilator. A hypothetical function
u with divisor2E-10H gives chi=u/phi. Four global congruences force
Tr(chi W^j)=0 for j=0,1,2,3 in the short frame W=b+L0/y.
Interpolation then gives uD(W)=U(W), deg U<=5. Two small infinity
branches bound the low coefficients of U; a pole-two branch violates
those bounds. Only three fixed determinants of sizes4,2,3 are used.

This excludes actual sources with the stated torsion requirement.
It does not assert emptiness of the relaxed coefficient, square-norm,
or non-torsion etale loci, and does not solve the unmarked common-cover
problem or the N1!=0 sector.
