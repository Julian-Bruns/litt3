# The actual new-line symplectic quotient and its Frobenius connection

Version1,23September2026. On C=X^(1), let lambda=O_C(-2O) be the
canonical second line in the fixed positive Cartier plane. The
symplectic quotient V_lambda=lambda-perp/lambda has the NONSPLIT
extension
\[
0\longrightarrow O_C(9O)\longrightarrow V_\lambda
\longrightarrow O_C(7O)\longrightarrow0.
\]
Use F25 codes a^2=a+3. Set
\[
N=(8,14,19,2,10,19,3,24,18,16),\qquad
N_1=(24,16,12,2,17,12,3,8,11,14),
\]
where N1 is coefficientwise fifth power. In the rational frames
specified in the proof the extension has principal-part class
\[
\epsilon_X=[3N_1(x_1)/y_1]\in H^1(C,O_C(2O)).
\]
Its residue pairing with (1,x1,x1^2,x1^3,x1^4,y1,x1 y1)dx1/y1^2
is the nonzero row (14,21,16,17,23,0,0).

Under relative Frobenius F_X:X->C this class vanishes, and
\[
F_X^*V_\lambda\simeq O_X(45O)\oplus O_X(35O).
\]
Choose Q with Q'=P A^2 and zero coefficients in degrees divisible
by five, where A=(1,21,14,22,13). Put f=Q/y^5. In compatible
rational divisor frames for this splitting, the canonical
connection has diagonal forms zero and upper-right form
\[
-3\,df=2A(x)^2\,dx/y^2.
\]
This is a regular section of omega_X(10O) with zero divisor2R_X.
The statement retains the connection; it is not a horizontal
splitting. No etale vanishing of the contact cup kernels follows.
