# A weak-five/tame-two curve admits no large normalized sign group

Version1,3 October2026. The whole scoped finite-group curve theorem passed [root independent review](../../Research/audits/CANONICAL_TEN_AFFINE_AND_NORMAL_ABELLAN_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). It bounds the target sign cover, not the original common-cover problem.

Let C be a smooth projective connected curve over an algebraically closed field of characteristicFIVE. Let G⊂Aut(C) be finite and faithful, with C/G=P1. Require exactly TWO branch values: one with inertia C5 of lower breakONE, and one with tame inertia C2. Assume G has no nontrivial quotient of order prime toFIVE.

Let Δ⊂Aut(C) be an elementary abelian TWO-group, normalized by G, with Δ∩G=ONE. Then
\[
|\Delta|\le2,
\qquad C\longrightarrow C/\Delta\text{ is étale}.
\]
There is no rank assumption on Δ. The possible étale DOUBLE is not claimed impossible.

Thus the entire unknown sign target has degree at mostTWO and is actually étale. BOTH original maps remain on E=T/H; this theorem does not make its possible extra target involution lift to E or identify Γ2 with Γ/H.
