# A rank-two quadratic complement cannot branch over only the odd-inertia orbit

Version1,3 October2026. Independent whole review [PASS](../../Research/audits/TWO_ORBIT_QUADRATIC_COMPLEMENT_OBSTRUCTION_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). No computation is used.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p≠2. A finite group G acts faithfully on a smooth projective connected curve C, with C/G≅P¹. There are exactly two branch orbits: an orbit D with odd-order stabilizers, and an orbit H with order-two stabilizers. Let F be an even-rank vector bundle with a perfect S-valued symmetric pairing and compatible projective G-action; scalar cocycles may be retained, provided the action on its projective bundle is genuine. Let I⊂F be a saturated invariant subbundle, of codimension TWO, with generically nondegenerate restricted form.

Suppose the determinant divisor of that restricted form is exactly the REDUCED orbit D. At every point of H suppose the action on the orthogonal complement K=I^⊥, which is nondegenerate there, has two opposite eigenvalues and the value-line scalar is the negative of their common scalar square. Then this packet is impossible.

The assertion concerns an actual G-action on C and actual integral bundles. It does not require a presumed simultaneous Galois closure or turn a replacement separable map into an original common cover.
