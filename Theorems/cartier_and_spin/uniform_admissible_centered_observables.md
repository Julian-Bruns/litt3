# Two bounded observables for every admissible primitive degree

30 September2026. Retain the actual primitive etale cover and divisor
hypotheses of [the all-degree energy theorem](uniform_admissible_source_energy.md),
but do NOT assume the quartic remainder coefficient is zero. Put
\[
\phi=b^5+h^*f,\qquad
z=\operatorname{Tr}(b/\phi^2),\quad
c=\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}(b^2/\phi^2),\quad
E_2=\operatorname{Tr}(db^2/\phi).
\]
All traces are along the actual cover h. Define
\[
\mathcal R=z E_2-2df\,(z\,dc-c\,dz).
\]
Both z and R are invariant under b -> b+a, f -> f-a^5 for a in k(X).
For every covering degree one has
\[
A^3 z\in L_X(21O),\qquad
A^5\mathcal R\in H^0(X,\omega_X^2(41O)).
\]
The first space has dimension13. The scalar coefficient of the second
relative to omega0^2, where omega0=dx/(3y^2), lies in L_X(73O), of
dimension65. These bounds are independent of the covering degree and
all multiplicities of the norm support and G.

Writing F_b=(W^5+f)H+tau and H mod(W^5+f)=sum_(j=0)^4 s_j W^j,
the observables satisfy z=s4/tau and c=s3/tau. Thus z=0 is precisely
the lower-remainder branch, where the different invariant Qsharp
gives the stronger fixed32-dimensional condition already recorded.
The present R is identically zero on that branch, so it does not
replace Qsharp there.

Explicit bases of the scalar spaces are
\[
L(21O):\quad x^i\ (0\le i\le7),\quad x^iy\ (0\le i\le3),\quad y^2,
\]
\[
L(73O):\quad x^i\ (0\le i\le24),\quad
x^iy\ (0\le i\le21),\quad x^iy^2\ (0\le i\le17).
\]
The observables are necessary data, not a complete encoding of the
cover. In particular no nonvanishing of R, no bound on covering degree
and no extraction from an arbitrary unmarked common cover is claimed.
