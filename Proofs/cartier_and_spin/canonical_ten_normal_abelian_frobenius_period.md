## The whole paired normalizer has prime-field projective representatives

The accepted [normal abelian reduction](canonical_ten_normal_abelian_coefficient_reduction.md) identifies R with the full even preimage of A₆ in the paired normalizer of A=C₂⁴. Its unique four-dimensional irreducible paired A-module is conjugate to the standard two-factor Pauli module. Work in that basis. All its Pauli matrices have entries in F₅, choosing i=TWO so that i²=−ONE. For every label v∈A choose a lift P_v with P_v²=ONE.

The operator T_v=ONE+iP_v is invertible over F₅: its possible eigenvalues ONE±i are nonzero. For a Pauli label w, conjugation fixes it when b(v,w)=+ONE, and when the labels anticommute it gives
\[
T_vP_wT_v^{-1}=iP_vP_w.
\]
Thus its induced symplectic action is the transvection w↦w+v precisely when the alternating pairing is nonzero. Symplectic transvections generate Sp₄(F₂). The subgroup generated by these prime-field projective operators and A therefore maps onto the full symplectic quotient and contains its entire kernel A. Since the projective paired centralizer is exactly A, this is the whole paired normalizer. In particular R has projective representatives over F₅.

For matrices over F₅ the determinant descends to a genuine homomorphism
\[
\operatorname{PGL}_4(\mathbf F_5)\longrightarrow
\mathbf F_5^*/(\mathbf F_5^*)^4=\mathbf F_5^*.
\]
The actual R has no nontrivial prime-to-FIVE quotient, so its determinant image is trivial. Therefore EVERY R-projective element has determinant-ONE prime-field representatives. The full inverse image of R in SL₄(F₅) has kernel μ₄, all contained in F₅. This gives the actual order-FOUR central lift; it does not replace it by a double cover.

## The scalar transition identity is exact

\[
a_{ij}M_{ij},\qquad M_{ij}\in\operatorname{SL}_4(\mathbf F_5),

where a_ij is an invertible local scalar. The matrices M_ij need only satisfy the projective cocycle condition; their central cocycle lies in μ₄ and is compensated by the scalar cocycle. Absolute Frobenius pulls this transition to a_ij⁵M_ij, because its prime-field entries are fixed. On the other hand detJ_Y has transition a_ij⁴, since detM_ij=ONE. Hence J_Y⊗detJ_Y has EXACTLY the same transitions a_ij⁵M_ij. These local equalities glue to
\[
F_{\rm abs}^{*}J_Y\simeq J_Y\otimes\det J_Y.
\]
The central cocycle causes no extra character or line twist: its fifth power is itself, and the displayed transitions already agree.
