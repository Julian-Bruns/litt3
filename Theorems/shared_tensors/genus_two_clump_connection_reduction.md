# Genus-two clump sizes and characteristic-five connections

Version2,2026-09-15. The size restriction holds over every
algebraically closed field of odd characteristic. The connection
application below retains characteristic five.

Let k be algebraically closed of odd characteristic p,
let X<-Z->Y be an ACTUAL coreless finite etale span,
and assume g(Y)=2. If it has a nonempty clump, its reduced image on Y
has cardinality r satisfying

    r=-1 modulo p,  except that p=5 also permits r=1.

Thus for p=3 or p>=7 one always has r>=p-1. In characteristic5
the sizes are the singleton or4,9,14,... .

This excludes whole unbounded progressions, not only weight7, and
retains arbitrary prime-to5 endpoint torsion and non-Galois degrees.

then every such span with a clump has a shared regular NILPOTENT
projective connection. Its primitive common tensor satisfies
e=-2d modulo5. In the Cartier-zero branch the connection is dormant;
in the nonzero branch it is nilpotent and need not itself be dormant.

singleton clumps are already excluded. Hence the remaining cases are:

1. a positive clump of size4,9,14,... on C_t, necessarily carrying a

2. no clump, where the common regular-connection space is empty or a
   single dormant point. Existence of that point is NOT proved.

The common-cover problem remains unsolved in both alternatives. The
