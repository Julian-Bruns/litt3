# A degree-fourteen scale equation for regular square roots

Version1,29 September2026. For the constant degree140 family, the
normalized incidence of square roots regular away from O and of pole
at most70 is annihilated by an explicit monic polynomial of degree14
in scale, throughout its original ratio chart.

The same assertion holds for the root-nine family after deleting the
six already excluded leading-scale curves. Every remaining actual
curve-square candidate is on this open chart. At a fixed geometric
ratio its normalized regular-root scale scheme therefore has length
at most14, including nilpotents. Keeping both signs of the root gives
the corresponding bound28 on the root incidence.

This is stronger than a necessary norm-square condition. It does not
identify the regular-root incidence with arbitrary nonreduced
norm-square deformations or with unramified quadratic characters.
It does not decide the remaining geometric candidates.
