# Proof: eliminate five directions and retain the cubic

[Statement](../../Theorems/deformations/bad_double_cubic_defect.md).
Author /root,2026-09-10. No independent audit.

## 1. The complete regular quadratic space

The actual etale double and the global basis calculation are those of
[genus_two_active_twists](../genus_two/genus_two_active_twists.md). With
R=u(u-3), S=F/R and gamma²=S, the six regular quadratic coefficients are

                  (1,u,u²,kappa,u*kappa,gamma)/F.

This basis includes regularity at the ramified hyperelliptic fibers and
at infinity. The double involution fixes u,v and negates kappa,gamma.
There is another commuting involution fixing u,gamma and negating
kappa,v. The latter fixes the four-dimensional subspace generated by
(1,u,u²,gamma)/F.

The actual infinitesimal Cartier block on kappa,u*kappa has determinant
(t+3)(t+4)(t+1)^4 before coefficient fifth roots, which is a unit. On the
invariant three-dimensional subspace, direct linearization of N in the
basis (1,u,u²)/F, with output basis its fifth powers, has determinant

                     4t(t+2)(t+3)(t+4)(t+1)^8.

The remaining tangent is gamma/F. These identities hold symbolically
over F5(t), with all denominators covered by t^5-t!=0.

By the scalar nilpotent model, N(R)(du)^10 is a linear combination of
the FIFTH POWERS of these six quadratic forms, with six polynomial
coefficients in the connection variables. The scheme is exactly their
common zero locus, including its nonreduced structure. This distinction
avoids taking fifth roots of deformation parameters.

## 2. Formal elimination is complete

The implicit-function theorem eliminates the five variables transverse
to z, the coefficient of gamma/F. The two kappa variables vanish
identically in this solution: the commuting involution negates them
and fixes z and the invariant variables, and the eliminated solution
is unique. Equivalently, in the kappa-free subalgebra the two corresponding
equations vanish identically, while their two-by-two linear block is a
unit. This proves that the four-variable computation loses no branch.

The double involution changes z to-z and fixes the other three remaining
variables. Thus their unique solutions are even formal power series.
To order z² they equal

               (4t/(t+1), 1/(t+1), 0) z².

Substituting gives exactly

       N(r+z*gamma/F+z²*(u-t)/((t+1)F))
            =3(t+1)z³*(gamma/F)^5 + O(z^4).

Corrections of order>=4 cannot change this coefficient. At order3 no
invariant correction occurs by parity; changing the other transverse
variables at this order would be detected by the invertible blocks.
The single residual equation is therefore z³ times a formal unit.
The completed local algebra is k[[z]]/(z³).

## 3. Exact replay and a transverse check

Run:

    sage scripts/genus_two/bad_double_nilpotent_local.sage --symbolic --order 3
    sage scripts/genus_two/bad_double_nilpotent_local.sage --deform-branch --order 1

The first run differentiates in the quadratic algebra k(u)[gamma]/(gamma²-S).
It replays the actual nilpotence equation, the complete invariant linear
block, its determinant, the omitted two-variable determinant, the unique
second-order correction and the nonzero cubic coefficient. The output
basis is checked exactly, rather than sampling points of C.

The second run works over F5(t,c). The four-branch quartic remains an
actual normalized active datum: if S0=F_c/(u-t) and
c0=[u^4]S0(u-t)², its coefficient is A_c=c0*S0. The scalar derivative
in the gamma direction is computed exactly and has the factorization
in the statement. Its c-derivative at3 is3(t+1)².

The symbolic runs take about0.3seconds each after Sage startup. A separate
run over the exact degree13 field t^13+4t²+3t+3 gave the same identities;
that specialization is a check, not the uniform-family proof.

## 4. Boundary and the useful next question

This calculation settles the characteristic-five local nilpotent length.
It does not evaluate the next inverse-Cartier obstruction after changing
the W3 curve in ker(Psi_C). The latter depends on the previous filtered
object over W2 and on the order-five jet term. A concrete next computation
is the kernel-to-cokernel map for that W4 step, using the canonical lift
of the ORIGINAL Y-cover as its reference. The cubic is a tested geometric
input, not a substitute for that mixed-characteristic construction.
