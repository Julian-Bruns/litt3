# Complete and low-degree cubic-character return exclusions

Version1, 25 September2026. Use the actual family R_xi, L=O_X(-O),
bundle K and complete lower map (f,alpha) from the
[quotient atlas](second_return_actual_quotient_atlas.md). Work over the
entire algebraic closure; alpha is arbitrary in L_X(20O), and neither
R_xi nor alpha is assumed equivariant.

The stable quotient window
\[
\dim\operatorname{Hom}(F_{\rm abs}^{2*}R_\xi,K)=1,
\qquad \operatorname{Hom}(F_{\rm abs}^{2*}R_\xi,L)=0
\]
contains no point whose nonzero f has any of these forms:

| First component | Entire excluded range |
| --- | --- |
| y^2 p(x) | deg(p)<=3: the complete third character space |
| p(x) | deg(p)<=3 |
| y p(x) | deg(p)<=2 |

The first row and the deg(p)<=2 part of the second row are stronger:
there is no nonzero actual map with such nonzero f for any nonzero
source xi, without stability or a Hom-dimension assumption. The other
cases force the source into the already excluded pure-v P5.

For EVERY map with f=y p(x), deg(p)<=7 and p!=0, write z_i=xi_i^25
in the fixed nineteen-coordinate basis. Then z0=z6=z7=0. The other
two source blocks, (z1..5,z8..12) and (z13..18), remain possible.

The unresolved character ranges are p(x) of degree4..10 and y p(x)
of degree3..7; the previously excluded monomials remain excluded.
Mixed-character f, the full quotient window and stable second returns
are unresolved. These results are geometric ideal identities, not
bounded searches of coefficient fields.
