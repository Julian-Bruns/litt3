# The exact endomorphism order at \(31\)

## Status and consequence

**Status: proved.**  **Audit: PASS** -- `/root/audit_exact_31adic_order`,
2026-09-04.  [Audit record](audits/54_EXACT_31_ADIC_ENDOMORPHISM_ORDER_AUDIT.md).

Let

\[
 Y:\ z^2=1-t^{31},\qquad J=\operatorname{Jac}(Y),
\]

and retain the notation of files 40 and 49:

\[
 K=\mathbf Q(\zeta_{31}),\qquad
 E=K^{\langle5\rangle},\qquad
 \mathscr D=\operatorname{End}^0_{\overline{\mathbf F}_5}(J).
\]

This note determines the completion of the geometric endomorphism ring at
the unique prime over \(31\).  The result closes one ambiguity in file 49,
but in the unfavorable direction: the actual completed order is the full
matrix order and is strictly larger than the coefficientwise crossed
order.  Thus coefficientwise integrality cannot be obtained merely by
identifying the ambient endomorphism order.

## Theorem 54.1 (the completed geometric order)

Let \(E_{31}=E\otimes_{\mathbf Q}\mathbf Q_{31}\),
\(K_{31}=K\otimes_{\mathbf Q}\mathbf Q_{31}\), and let
\(\mathcal O_{E,31}\subset E_{31}\) and
\(\mathcal O_{K,31}\subset K_{31}\) be their valuation rings.  Then

\[
 \mathscr D\otimes_EE_{31}
       \simeq\operatorname{End}_{E_{31}}(K_{31})
       \simeq M_3(E_{31}),                                      \tag{54.1}
\]

and, under an identification of the \(31\)-adic Tate module with a
fractional \(\mathcal O_{K,31}\)-ideal,

\[
 \operatorname{End}_{\overline{\mathbf F}_5}(J)
       \otimes\mathbf Z_{31}
   =\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal O_{E,31}}
       (\mathcal O_{K,31})
   \simeq M_3(\mathcal O_{E,31}).                              \tag{54.2}
\]

In particular this is a maximal order in the split algebra (54.1).

### Proof

Write \(T=T_{31}J\) and \(V=T\otimes_{\mathbf Z_{31}}\mathbf Q_{31}\).
The automorphism \(\rho(t,z)=(\zeta_{31}t,z)\) makes \(T\) a module over
\(\mathcal O_{K,31}=\mathbf Z_{31}[\zeta_{31}]\).  It is torsion-free,
and its ranks satisfy

\[
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathbf Z_{31}}T=30
   =\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbf Z_{31}}\mathcal O_{K,31}.
\]

The ring \(\mathcal O_{K,31}\) is a discrete valuation ring.  Hence \(T\)
is a rank-one free \(\mathcal O_{K,31}\)-module (more invariantly, a
fractional ideal), so

\[
                         V\simeq K_{31}.                       \tag{54.3}
\]

All geometric rational endomorphisms are already defined over
\(\mathbf F_{125}\).  Indeed, file 40 proves

\[
                              F^3=\pi\in E=Z(\mathscr D).
\]

The generator of
\(\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbf F}_5/\mathbf F_{125})\)
therefore acts on \(\mathscr D\) by conjugation with the central element
\(F^3\), hence trivially.  This argument does **not** assume that the
integral endomorphism ring is generated by \(\rho\) and \(F\), nor does
it assume the coefficientwise crossed-order assertion of file 49.

Tate's integral isogeny theorem now gives the exact centralizer identity

\[
 \operatorname{End}_{\overline{\mathbf F}_5}(J)\otimes\mathbf Z_{31}
   =\operatorname{End}_{\mathbf F_{125}}(J)\otimes\mathbf Z_{31}
   =\operatorname{End}_{\operatorname{Gal}(\overline{\mathbf F}_5/
                      \mathbf F_{125})}(T)
   =\operatorname{Cent}_{\operatorname{End}_{\mathbf Z_{31}}(T)}(F^3).
                                                                    \tag{54.4}
\]

After tensoring with \(\mathbf Q_{31}\), Tate's rational theorem identifies
this centralizer with \(\mathscr D\otimes_EE_{31}\).  Honda--Tate local
invariants vanish away from the characteristic, so this algebra is split
at \(31\).  It acts on the three-dimensional \(E_{31}\)-space \(V\);
the embedded degree-three field \(K_{31}\) acts with rank one.  Hence

\[
 \mathscr D\otimes_EE_{31}
       =\operatorname{End}_{E_{31}}(V)
       \simeq\operatorname{End}_{E_{31}}(K_{31}),
\]

which proves (54.1).  Intersecting the rational centralizer with
\(\operatorname{End}_{\mathbf Z_{31}}(T)\), and observing that
\(\mathcal O_{E,31}\subset\mathcal O_{K,31}\) preserves \(T\), gives

\[
 \bigl(\operatorname{End}_{E_{31}}(V)\bigr)
       \cap\operatorname{End}_{\mathbf Z_{31}}(T)
       =\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal O_{E,31}}(T).
\]

Finally a fractional ideal of the DVR \(\mathcal O_{K,31}\) is isomorphic
to \(\mathcal O_{K,31}\), and the latter is free of rank three over
\(\mathcal O_{E,31}\).  This proves (54.2). \(\square\)

## Proposition 54.2 (the crossed order is genuinely smaller)

Let \(F\) be Frobenius as in file 49 and put

\[
 \mathcal R_{31}=
 \mathcal O_{K,31}\oplus\mathcal O_{K,31}F
                    \oplus\mathcal O_{K,31}F^2.
\]

Then

\[
 \mathcal R_{31}\subsetneq
 \operatorname{End}_{\mathcal O_{E,31}}(\mathcal O_{K,31}).   \tag{54.5}
\]

More concretely, after writing the split cyclic algebra on \(K_{31}\),
Frobenius has the form

\[
                         F=c\sigma,
 \qquad c\in\mathcal O_{K,31}^{\times},                       \tag{54.6}
\]

where \(\sigma(\zeta_{31})=\zeta_{31}^{5}\).  If
\(\lambda=1-\zeta_{31}\), then

\[
             \partial=\lambda^{-1}(\sigma-1)                 \tag{54.7}
\]

maps \(\mathcal O_{K,31}\) to itself and is
\(\mathcal O_{E,31}\)-linear, but it does not belong to

\[
 \mathcal O_{K,31}\oplus\mathcal O_{K,31}\sigma
                    \oplus\mathcal O_{K,31}\sigma^2.
\]

### Proof

Because \(31\ne5\), Frobenius is an automorphism of \(T\).  In the
rank-one \(\mathcal O_{K,31}\)-model it is a \(\sigma\)-semilinear
automorphism, hence has the form (54.6) with \(c\) a unit.  Multiplying
the three coefficient lattices by units shows that the crossed order
defined using \(F\) is the same kind of lattice as the one defined using
\(\sigma\).

The extension \(K_{31}/E_{31}\) is cyclic, tamely and totally ramified of
degree three.  Therefore
\(\sigma(a)-a\in\lambda\mathcal O_{K,31}\) for
every \(a\in\mathcal O_{K,31}\).  This proves that (54.7) preserves the
valuation ring.  Since \(\sigma\) fixes \(E_{31}\), the operator is
\(\mathcal O_{E,31}\)-linear.

The decomposition

\[
 \operatorname{End}_{E_{31}}(K_{31})
       =K_{31}\oplus K_{31}\sigma\oplus K_{31}\sigma^2
\]

is direct.  In this decomposition the first two coefficients of
\(\partial\) are \(-\lambda^{-1}\) and \(\lambda^{-1}\), neither of which
is in \(\mathcal O_{K,31}\).  Thus \(\partial\) is not in the crossed
order, proving strictness. \(\square\)

## Consequence for the degree-three repair

Proposition 49.7 remains valid and still kills the \(K\)-component of a
bidegree-\((3,3)\) correspondence.  Theorem 54.1 shows, however, that one
cannot kill its two remaining cyclic components by asserting that every
integral endomorphism has coefficients in \(\mathcal O_{K,31}\): that
assertion is false.  The extra operators are exactly the divided
ramification operators typified by (54.7).

Any successful continuation must therefore use a property special to an
effective etale correspondence (or simultaneous global restrictions at
\(5\) and \(31\)), rather than only membership in the ambient endomorphism
ring.  In particular, the local \(31\)-adic order by itself does not repair
the degree-three gap in file 48.
