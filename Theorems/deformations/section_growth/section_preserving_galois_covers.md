# Galois covers preserving sections of bundles with Euler characteristic zero

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p>0, q:T→C a connected
finite étale Galois cover of smooth projective connected curves, and E
a vector bundle with χ(E)=0. Suppose

    r=h^0(C,E)>0,       H^0(C,E) → H^0(T,q^*E) is an isomorphism.

Then G=Gal(T/C) has a characteristic normal subgroup N of order prime
to p with cyclic p-power quotient. Equivalently, G has a normal
p-complement and cyclic Sylow p-subgroups. For every H≤G,

    dim_Fp Hom(H,Fp)=1 if p divides |H|, and0 otherwise.

Pullback H^1(C,E)→H^1(T,q^*E) is zero if p divides |G|, and an
isomorphism otherwise. When p divides |G|, there is a unique degree-p
intermediate cover U→C, and H^1(C,E)→H^1(U,E|U) is already zero.

No self-duality, spin structure, odd-characteristic or genus hypothesis
is required. These are necessary conditions for preserving a positive
section space.

For a spin line A in odd characteristic, χ(A)=0. On the unique
degree-p intermediate, every inherited section of K(C,A) therefore
acquires a global regular spin primitive, by the
[primitive exact sequence](../../cartier_and_spin/spin_primitive_matching_defect.md).
For a prime-to-p cover, a primitive exists upstairs only if it exists
downstairs. This applies to the Galois kernels discarded in the
[spin-series reduction](../../cartier_and_spin/spin_probe_common_cover_reduction.md),
without restricting the full endpoint Galois closures.
