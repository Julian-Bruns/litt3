# A residue-degree filter for maximal arithmetic six-cone orbifolds

Version1,21September2026. Let $\Delta$ be a maximal compact arithmetic
Fuchsian lattice of signature $(0;2,2,2,2,2,2)$, with invariant trace
field $F$. Let $C/\overline{\mathbf Q}$ be its unique torsion-free
index-two source, a genus-two curve.

There is a finite elementary abelian two-extension $M/F$ over which
the orbifold and the geometric isomorphism class of $C$ are defined.
An actual model of $C$ over $M$ is not required for this assertion.

Fix a place above an odd prime $p$, and suppose $C$ has potentially
good reduction $C_0$. If $v$ is the induced place of $F$, then
the geometric moduli point of $C_0$ is rational over
\[
\mathbf F_{p^{\,2f(v/p)}}.
\]
Its exact residue degree therefore divides $2f(v/p)$.

For the explicit backup genus-two curve in characteristic five,
whose exact moduli residue degree is three, this forces
\[
3\mid f(v/5).
\]
In particular, every maximal six-cone lattice over $\mathbf Q$ or
a quadratic trace field is excluded, as is any higher trace field
having no residue degree divisible by three at five. No identification
of the trace field with the Jacobian endomorphism field is made.

This applies even when the index-two surface subgroup is noncongruence.
It is a filter on the remaining arithmetic family, not an exhaustive
classification or a solution of either common-cover problem.
