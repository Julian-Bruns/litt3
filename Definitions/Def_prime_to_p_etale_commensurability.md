# Prime-to-p etale commensurability

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p, a connected finite
etale cover has prime-to-p monodromy if its geometric Galois closure group
has order prime to p. Prime-to-p DEGREE alone is not this condition.

Smooth projective connected hyperbolic curves C1,C2 are prime-to-p etale
commensurable when there is an actual smooth projective connected D and
finite etale maps D->C1,D->C2, both with prime-to-p monodromy.

This is an equivalence relation. Reflexivity and symmetry are immediate.
For transitivity take a connected component of the fiber product over
the common endpoint. Base change, intermediate covers and towers preserve
prime-to-p monodromy: Galois closures of towers embed in successive wreath
products of the original closure groups. This also explains why a finite
chain of such covers has a single common-source presentation as above.

This definition is a RESTRICTED class for positive examples. It does not
replace the unrestricted common-cover problem, and no given arbitrary
common cover is presumed to have prime-to-p monodromy.
