# A forced fourth endpoint jet and the degree40 exclusion

Version1, 26 September2026. Retain the actual same-source V4
hypotheses of [trace descent](klein_four_trace_branch_finiteness.md).
Use the coefficient convention beta^2=beta+3, ascending rows,
P=(11,22,18,5,19,20,15,16,9,22,1), A=(1,21,14,22,13).

Let alpha be a root of A and B its canonical29th root in F_(5^8),
as in the trace theorem. At a zero-endpoint label B xi, xi in mu29,
put Z=epsilon^-1 t^3 v. Its fourth coefficient is forced:
\[
[t^4]Z=\xi^{17}F_0(\alpha)+\epsilon^{-1}\xi^4F_1(\alpha),
\quad F_0=(20,12,13,8),\quad F_1=(21,21,20,2).
\]
This holds for every geometric value of the free second coefficient.
It asserts necessity, not extension of every truncated germ.

For an endpoint multiset Q of four labels (alpha_i,xi_i), define
\[
U(Q)=\sum_i\xi_i^{17}F_0(\alpha_i),\qquad
V(Q)=\sum_i\xi_i^4F_1(\alpha_i).
\]
If Q,H are the zero and infinity multisets, eta=[22], and
M_j=sum m_c c^j, then both actual trace identities hold:
\[
\epsilon U(Q)+V(Q)=\eta(\epsilon M_3-M_{-1}),\qquad
U(H)+\epsilon V(H)=\eta(M_{-3}-\epsilon M_1).
\]
All common-pole weights, including triples and their regular
companions, are retained. No action on the cubic source is assumed.

In particular the entire fixed degree40 configuration of
[the small-scalar classification](klein_four_small_scalar_group.md)
is impossible, with every inertia, endpoint ordering and additional
branch choice allowed. Both original common-cover questions remain open.
