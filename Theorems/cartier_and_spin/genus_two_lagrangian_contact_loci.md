# Two Lagrangian contact loci as exact zero-norm primitive tests

Version1,23September2026. Let Y be a smooth genus-two curve in
characteristic five, F:Y->C=Y^(1), B=F_*O_Y/O_C and omega=omega_C.
The primitive Wronskian identification is
\[
(\bigwedge^2B\otimes\omega^{-1})_0
\simeq F_*\omega_Y^{-2}.
\]
Its quadratic form is denoted Q. In an etale coordinate t, for
r=sum_(j=0)^4 r_j t^j in a regular (dt)^-2 frame, with r_j on C,
\[
Q(r)=r_2^2+2r_0r_4+2r_1r_3.
\]

Saturated degree-one Lagrangian planes Pi in B are equivalent to
degree-minus-one lines M with a map j:M->F_*omega_Y^-2 satisfying
Q(j)=0 and adjunction divisor one point z of Y. Necessarily
M=det(Pi)omega^-1 and F^*M=omega_Y^-2(-z). Evaluation of Pi is
surjective and its second fundamental divisor is exactly z.
Its canonical second line has degree zero and adjunction divisor2z.

Saturated degree-zero Lagrangian planes S in B with evaluation
base2P and simple residual Wronskian zeros Q1,Q2, all three points
distinct, are equivalent to degree-minus-two lines M and maps
j:M->F_*omega_Y^-2 with Q(j)=0 and adjunction divisor
\[
4P+Q1+Q2.
\]
Here M=det(S)omega^-1 and F^*M=omega_Y^-2(-4P-Q1-Q2).
Such an S is automatically strongly semistable.

These are geometric equivalences over the algebraic closure;
they are not finite-field point searches. In each case M ranges
over ALL appropriate Picard classes, including every fifth-root
choice. On the explicit backup Y, the first locus now has
[twelve geometric planes](backup_lagrangian_degree_one_census.md),
and the second contains an [explicit curve component](backup_isotropic_double_base_locus.md).
The second norm equation is forced to vanish at P1; its effective
target has dimension two, so a finite-locus heuristic is incorrect.
Neither endpoint nonemptiness constructs either original common cover.
