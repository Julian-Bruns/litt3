# Ordinary dihedral towers with linear spin-section growth

Let k=bar(F_5), t^5-t!=0, and

    Y: y^2=G(x)(x-t),  G=x(x-1)(x-2)(x-3).

For any i in {0,1,2,3}, let W_i=(i,0) and L=O_Y(W_i). Then L is a
spin line. There is a nested tower of connected finite etale Galois covers
f_n:T_n->Y, n>=0, with

    deg f_n=2*5^n,  Gal(f_n)=C_(5^n) semidirect C_2,
    g(T_n)=2*5^n+1,  T_n ordinary,
    h0(T_n,f_n^*L)=5^n.

The involution acts on C_(5^n) by inversion; at n=0 the group is C_2.
For n>=1 the pulled-back spin is globally generated. In particular
ordinarity, even of EVERY curve in an etale tower, does not bound the
spin-section dimension or force it to be sublinear in genus.

These towers exist on every allowed member of the SAME genus-two family
used in bounded_atlas_partner_finiteness. They supply only the one leg
f_n. No second endpoint, coreless span, shared section h, or alternating
normal-closure realization is asserted. They do not refute
alternating_spin_growth or the equivariant seventh-root question.
