# The remaining admissible supports in degrees ten and eleven

Version 1, 24 September 2026. For an actual admissible cover of
degree n=10 or11, the unrestricted norm gives h_*E=5B. Necessarily
\[
B=(n-9)O+x^*(r_1+r_2+r_3),
\]
where r_1,r_2,r_3 are three distinct roots of A. These are exactly
four remaining projected support choices. In particular B~nO and
h_*G~nO. This does not assert that any choice is realized.

The classification of effective B with coefficients at most two,
support size at least seven, and 2B~2nO leaves sixteen possibilities:
the four above and twelve repeated-fiber choices. The latter are
excluded by the local argument of admissible_double_fiber_boundary.

After the exact finite-pole, selected-sheet collision and infinity
conditions are imposed, the coefficient spaces in the primitive
polynomial have homogeneous dimensions18 at n=10 and14 at n=11.
Fixing the required nonzero constant kappa leaves affine dimensions
17 and13. These are necessary coefficient spaces only. Irreducibility,
everywhere etaleness and the nontrivial order-five class remain to be
established or contradicted; no higher-degree existence decision follows.
