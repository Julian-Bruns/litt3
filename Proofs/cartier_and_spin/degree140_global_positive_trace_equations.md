Use the [residue identities](degree140_infinity_residue_traces.md).
Only expansions of bounded length at O4,O7 and O occur. All input
coefficients are Laurent polynomials in h,w. The small critical root
is solved with a constant unit derivative. The other inversions have
leading coefficients equal to constants times powers of h,w and Psi.
Specifically, the relevant leading terms, listing parameter monomials,

## Cubic covariance

Let zeta have order three. Every monomial h^a w^b x^i y^j in each
G2,G3,G4,G5 has weight2a+b+j=1 modulo3. Consequently the substitutions
(h,w,y) -> (zeta^2 h,zeta w,zeta y), with Z unchanged, multiply each
G_i by zeta and identify the two critical curves. They transform
phi,Lambda,eta by zeta; omega0 transforms by zeta. Thus v transforms
by zeta and ell*Tr(x^j*phi*v) is invariant after ell -> zeta ell.
Its coefficient of ell^n multiplied by w^n is therefore a rational
function of H=h w and q=w^3. This proves the asserted descent of the
coefficient expressions, without choosing a preferred cube root w.

## Rigorous degree propagation

For this paragraph treat Psi as an independent Laurent variable.
For each xi coefficient retain a rectangular enclosure of its possible
(h,w,Psi) exponents. Addition takes coordinatewise minima and maxima;
multiplication adds the respective endpoints. Inverting a series whose
leading coefficient is a parameter monomial uses its ordinary triangular
coefficient recurrence, and negates the leading monomial's exponents.
Fifth powers multiply all exponents by five; xi differentiation discards
terms with xi-exponent divisible by five. Cancellations may shrink these
enclosures but cannot enlarge them. The exact leading identities in the
table permit the stated leading cancellations before an inversion.

The new
[bound propagation source](../../scripts/arithmetic/degree140_trace_degree_bounds_20260929.cpp)
implements these rules on the residue circuit, including explicit
precision tracking. For a coefficient enclosure
[hmin,hmax] x [wmin,wmax] x [pmin,pmax] at ell^n, choose
\[
a=\max(0,-h_{min}),\quad c=\max(0,-p_{min}),\quad
b=\max\left(0,\left\lceil\frac{h_{max}-w_{min}-n}{3}\right\rceil\right).
\]
After h=H/w and multiplication by w^n H^a q^b Psi^c, the expression
is polynomial in H,w,Psi. Cubic covariance makes it polynomial in
H,q after Psi is restored. Its bidegree is bounded by
\[
\deg_H\le a+h_{max}+c+p_{max},
\qquad
\deg_q\le
\left\lfloor\frac{3b+w_{max}-h_{min}+n}{3}\right\rfloor
+7(c+p_{max}).
\]
These are upper bounds, not fits to numerical samples. The retained
coefficientwise bounds give maxima139 and877. The top zero coefficient
of P0 is omitted using its independently proved degree15 bound.

## Exact interpolation and retained equations

The new
[grid construction](../../scripts/arithmetic/degree140_global_trace_grid_20260929.cpp)
chooses140 distinct nonzero H values and878 distinct nonzero cubes q.
Every chosen point satisfies all required denominator nonvanishings.
It evaluates the infinity-residue circuit, multiplies by the specified
coefficientwise denominators, and performs exact interpolation in H
and q over K. Polynomial uniqueness on the rectangular grid proves that
the result is the global numerator in each case. The computation contains
122,920 actual trace profiles. It finished on the local machine without
replaying any incoming computation.

The resulting fifty polynomials have, in total,376,857 nonzero terms
before cancelling unnecessary denominator powers. Their respective
largest individual bidegrees are(102,494),(109,525),(114,556), strictly
inside the proved interpolation box. Original grid values, global
coefficients, degree enclosures, and construction summaries are retained
in the
[external trace directory](../../../litt3-computation-data/seventeen_hour_continuation_20260929/traces/).
The native coded-field tables are accepted inputs from the source
construction. The separate
[symbolic equation builder](../../scripts/arithmetic/degree140_global_trace_polynomials_20260929.sage)
uses the absolute field generated by alpha. Indeed beta=-u(alpha)/v(alpha),
where u=z^4+2z^3+z^2+2z and v=z^3+z^2+1; its modulus is
u^2+uv-3v^2. Thus conversion of the recorded field codes is explicit.

Common-zero elimination is a further task. Neither exact interpolation
nor an empty bounded search would by itself prove the geometric locus
empty; no such conclusion is included here.
