# Weak cyclic two-branch curves forbid the rank-(p−1) reflection representation

Version1,3 October2026. Independent whole general-scope review PASS, including the changed prime, dimension and possibly nonfaithful representation: [audit supplement](../../../Research/audits/CANONICAL_TEN_POSITIVE_FULL_TRACE_EXCLUSION_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md).

Let p be a prime with p≡ONE moduloFOUR, let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p, and let a finite group G act faithfully on a smooth connected projective curve Z. Suppose Z/G=P¹, with exactly TWO branch values: cyclic inertia Cp of lower break ONE at the first, and cyclic inertia C2 at the second.

Then G has no irreducible projective representation
\[
\rho:G\longrightarrow\operatorname{PGL}_{p-1}(k)
\]
in which the tame inertia involution has eigenspace dimensions ONE and p−TWO. The projective representation need NOT be faithful. There is no hypothesis on the abelianization or prime-to-p quotients of G.

The obstruction is intrinsic to these actual curve and inertia data. An explicit local Kummer lift produces two inverse-conjugate generic Cp branch classes. The corresponding irreducible matrix tuple is rigid, is defined over F_(p²), and has regular unipotent p-generators. Their simultaneous companion determinant differs by an element of order FOUR, which cannot be a (p−ONE)th power because the (p−ONE)th-power subgroup of F_(p²)× has order p+ONE≡TWO moduloFOUR.

For p=FIVE, this directly forbids the original positive complete rank-FOUR degree-ONE trace in any actual faithful canonical-TEN carrier with its accepted ONE-plus-THREE tame action. It may be applied to the ORIGINAL target group G itself; taking a faithful coefficient quotient, invoking finite reflection classification, or discarding an original endpoint field is unnecessary. The original common-cover problem and other trace branches remain outside this conclusion.
