# Positivity, all-twist vanishing and étale sections of the explicit degree-one bundle

Version3,3October2026. Let X be the fixed genus-nine cubic curve,
O its point at infinity, and K=H_X(-24O), with the actual extension
class specified in the proof. Then
\[
0\longrightarrow O_X(8O)\longrightarrow F_{\rm abs}^{2*}K
\longrightarrow O_X(17O)\longrightarrow0
\]
is an exact sequence of line subbundle and line quotient. Consequently,
for every finite h:C->X and every line quotient Q of h^*K,
\[
25\deg Q\ge8\deg h.
\]
In particular K is ample. A nonpositive line quotient at any
Frobenius height, even after finite pullback, is impossible.

For EVERY geometric degree-zero line bundle L,
\[
H^0(X,K\otimes L)=0.
\]
This follows directly from the later all-geometric vanishing for
K(3O), which also gives exact maximum line degree minus four.
The separate degree-zero twist census is no longer needed.

K has a nonzero section on some finite étale cover if and only if
it has a nowhere-zero section on some such cover. No connected
finite ABELIAN étale cover has a nonzero section. For a Galois cover,
the descended image E of the full section evaluation has rank two,
and either:

1. deg E=0, E is étale-trivializable, and K/E=k(P); or
2. E=K and the pulled-back K is globally generated.

After adjoining the cubic conjugate covers, the first case reduces
to22 specified equivariant elementary modifications at O and the
ten cubic branch points, two eigenquotients per point. Every such
modification is stable of degree zero. Its étale trivializability
requires actual Frobenius periodicity, not merely eventual periodicity.
These are distinct images on a particular cover. The subsequent
[finite-coefficient theorem](finite_coefficient_generation.md),
using the six nonperiodicity exclusions and arithmetic conjugation,
proves that existence in the first case also implies global generation
on another etale cover. Thus they are no longer separate existential
questions, although excluding all22 modifications alone still leaves
the possibility of a higher-rank irreducible finite coefficient
surjecting directly onto K.

Neither existence nor nonexistence of an étale section is proved.
This is a one-endpoint necessary condition for the degree-one
radical-orbit branch, not a common-cover resolution.
