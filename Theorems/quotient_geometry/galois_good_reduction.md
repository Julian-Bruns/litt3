# Good reduction preserves a Galois etale map of hyperbolic curves

Version1,21September2026. Let $R$ be a complete mixed-characteristic
DVR. Let $D,C$ be smooth proper geometrically connected generic-fiber
curves of genus at least two, both with potentially good reduction.
Every finite etale Galois map $D\to C$ extends, after finite extension,
to a finite etale map of their smooth proper models. Its degree may
be divisible by the residue characteristic.

There is a useful auxiliary-atlas consequence. Let $\mathscr S$ be
a proper effective hyperbolic orbifold over the generic field.
Suppose it has a finite etale Galois curve atlas $H\to\mathscr S$
whose source has potentially good reduction. Let
$C\to\mathscr S$ be a finite etale Galois curve atlas of degree
prime to the residue characteristic, and suppose $C$ also has
potentially good reduction. Every connected component of the ACTUAL
fiber product $C\times_{\mathscr S}H$ has a good model, and both
of its maps to $C,H$ extend finite etale.

No bound or prime-to-characteristic condition on the deck group
of $H\to\mathscr S$ is imposed. The Galois hypothesis on the
original maps is essential to the argument; the statement does not
create a simultaneous Galois source for arbitrary correspondences.
