# A local replacement for repeated-fiber exclusions in degrees ten and eleven

Version 1, 24 September 2026. Let h:S->X be an actual admissible
cover of degree n=10 or11, with primitive and divisors as in
reconstruction. Write h_*E=5B, as forced by the unrestricted norm
theorem. Suppose
\[
B=(n-9)O+2x^*(r)+x^*(s),\quad A(r)=A(s)=0,\quad r\ne s.
\]
Then no such cover exists, with arbitrary monodromy and arbitrary
multiplicities in G.

At degree ten the contradiction is a nonzero first logarithmic
derivative at r. At degree eleven a four-dimensional trace system
is invertible. These replace the large finite-pole/collision matrix
certificates for all twelve repeated-fiber choices in each degree.
The four distinct-triple-fiber supports are not excluded.
