# Parameter decks preserve the two actual theta lines

Version1, 3 October 2026. [Independent whole audit PASS](../../Research/audits/OCT03_NONSPLIT_TWENTY_TWO_AND_PARAMETER_DECK_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md).

Use the fixed genus-nine X, its point O, theta=dx/y², and the established unrestricted [proportional-theta recognition](new_line_comparison_normal_form.md). Let C0 have two actual finite étale maps h_i:C0→X whose embedded fields jointly generate k(C0). Suppose their reduced infinity divisors D1,D2 are disjoint and a nonconstant z satisfies div(z)=2D1−2D2. Let B′=C0(t), t²=z, be a connected double cover. It is automatically étale; its deck sigma fixes C0 and sends t to−t.

Then Aut(B′/k(t)) embeds into Aut(X)²=C3². In particular its order is odd and B′ has no nontrivial involution fixing t.

If additionally k(C0)=k(x1,x2,z), as in the exact index-one tensor packet, then Aut(B′/k(t)) is trivial. Consequently the single monodromy of B′/k(t) cannot have a block system of pairs: its actual own-pair intermediate field would give a quadratic t-preserving deck.

Both original X legs remain actual finite étale maps on the same B′. This is a parameter-symmetry theorem, not an exclusion of unrestricted common covers.
