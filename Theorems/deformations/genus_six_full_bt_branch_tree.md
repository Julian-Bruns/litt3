# The complete genus-six full-extension tree

Version3,23 September2026. Work over $k=\overline{\mathbf F}_5$ on
the fixed genus-six curve $T$ of the
[full-existence descent counterexample](explicit_full_bt_existence_descent_failure.md).
Fix its actual everywhere-versal BT1 $H_T$, marking, normalized
finite determinant, and specified first periodic datum.

Let $\mathcal F_N$ be the set of normalized marked BT$_N$ extensions
of $H_T$ admitting a compatible full $5$-divisible extension. Then
\[
|\mathcal F_2|=375,\qquad
|\mathcal F_N|=375\cdot25^{N-2}\quad(N\ge2).
\]
Every BT2 admitting BT3 already belongs to $\mathcal F_2$.
Each fiber of $\mathcal F_{N+1}\to\mathcal F_N$ has exactly
$25$ elements for $N\ge2$. These are geometric isomorphism-class
counts, not reducedness assertions for the original parameter schemes.

Consequently $T$ carries continuum many pairwise non-isogenous full
height-two, dimension-one groups with this SAME marked BT1 and
normalized determinant. Only countably many can descend to any
finite subfield. No uniform finite field is claimed for this tree.

There are at least $169$ pairwise non-isogenous full groups on $T$
which DO have one common finite field of definition. Their Hodge
towers are all defined over $\mathbf F_{5^{456}}$; one additional
fixed extension handles the common initial tame marking and line.
They have $169$ different BT2 classes and give $845$ different
full-extendible BT2 classes on the genus-eleven closure over one
finite field. No arithmetic existence or nonexistence conclusion
is made for the other $206$ geometric initial classes.
Already the degree-three factor of the finite-field calculation gives
three such groups on $T$ with Hodge towers over $\mathbf F_{5^{12}}$,
and fifteen on the closure over one further fixed finite extension.

Choosing one full extension of each of the $375$ BT2 classes and
pulling to the fixed genus-eleven dihedral closure gives $1875$
pairwise non-isogenous full groups in disjoint cyclic-five orbits;
their BT2 classes are already different.
