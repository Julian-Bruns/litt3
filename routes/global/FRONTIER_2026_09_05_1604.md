## New general mechanisms

[Polarization-volume bound](../../Theorems/Thm_polarization_bad_fiber_bound.md)
is an author proof, not independently audited. For a finite bad-fiber
scheme B in an r-dimensional quotient, and a complementary isogeny of
degree e, it proves

    e length(B) <= c1(L_P)^r.

A fiber with generic determinant corank delta contributes at least
binomial(r+delta-1,r) to length(B). Applied to the audited ordinary
cyclic-triple test, the weighted sum is at most 10. Symmetry forces
delta <= 2; delta=2 is confined to specific small torsion loci. Reduced
isolated delta=1 fibers remain possible. This is a general intersection
argument, not a claim that those exceptions are eliminated.

[Small-p interpolation](ORDINARY_PRINCIPAL_P_TORSION_INTERPOLATION_BELOW_P.md)
is an author proof with source-checked ingredients. On an ordinary ppav,
restriction of sections of any line bundle of class m times the principal
polarization to the reduced p-torsion subgroup is injective for 1<=m<p.
Consequently the Raynaud section is determined up to scalar by its
mandatory torsion zeros in its fixed determinant line bundle.

[Dirac trace obstruction](../../DIRAC_TRACE_POLARIZATION_OBSTRUCTION_2026_09_05.md)
proves that a Dirac section cannot exist if a suitable residual line
bundle is generated at the identity. In particular, classes m<=p-2 are
excluded. The bound is sharp on products of ordinary elliptic curves.
The residual class Theta+q*D does NOT automatically satisfy the needed
generation condition; both its Picard twist and the target theta's
vanishing at zero must be retained.
