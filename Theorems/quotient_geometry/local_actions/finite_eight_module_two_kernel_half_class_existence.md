# A compatible finite two-kernel group and order-eight half-class exist

Version 1, 3 October 2026. The construction and its elementary abelian
refinement passed the [fresh independent conceptual audit](../../../Research/notes/oct03_ten_hour/eight_module_finite_two_kernel_half_class_construction_audit.md).
This theorem is strictly about finite groups and projective modules.

Let \(k=\overline{\mathbf F}_5\), \(H_0=SL_8(\mathbf F_5)\), and
\(Q=PSL_8(\mathbf F_5)\). Let \(\alpha\in H^2(Q,k^\times)\) be its natural
multiplier, of exact order four. Use the explicit \(A,B\) of
[abstract finite-tuple existence](actual_eight_dimensional_finite_tuple_existence.md),
so \(A^5=B^5=1\), \(\langle A,B\rangle=H_0\), and \((AB)^2=3I\).

There exist a FINITE group \(G\), an exact sequence
\(1\to N\to G\to Q\to1\), and a class \(\beta\in H^2(G,k^\times)\)
with these simultaneous properties:

1. \(N\) is a nontrivial elementary abelian two-group, is noncentral in
   \(G\), and \(G\to Q\) is nonsplit.
2. \(G\) has generators \(a,b\) mapping to the classes of \(A,B\), with
   exact orders five, \(c=(ab)^{-1}\) of exact order two, and \(b\)
   conjugate to \(a^{-1}\) INSIDE this SAME \(G\). It has no nontrivial
   quotient of order prime to five.
3. The natural projective eight-dimensional \(G\)-module, pulled from
   \(Q\), has normalized matrix image \(H_0\), exact multiplier order
   four, wild type \(J_5\oplus J_3\), and balanced tame eigenvalues
   \(\pm\lambda\), each four times, with \(\lambda^4=-1\).
   It retains the absolute irreducibility, primitivity, tensor and form
   exclusions of the explicit matrix tuple.
4. The inflated class retains exact order four, while
   \(\operatorname{ord}\beta=8\) and
   \(2\beta=10\beta=-\operatorname{inf}\alpha\).
5. The EXACT labeled pullback \(H=G\times_Q H_0\) is generated by its
   unique order-five lifts of \(a,b\). A finite group \(E_8\), also
   generated by those lifts, has exact sequences
   \(1\to\mu_8\to E_8\to G\to1\) and
   \(1\to\mu_2\to E_8\to H\to1\). The latter is nonsplit; a faithful
   scalar character of the former transgresses to \(\beta\).

The construction uses a specified central eightfold cover of the
hyperbolic triangle group of signature \((5,5,2)\), followed by the
finite core of a character of its surface kernel. This is an exact finite
quotient recipe. No minimal group, small matrix model or exact kernel
dimension is claimed; no numerical computation is needed.

Thus the even/noncentral/nonsplit kernel and order-eight half-class
conditions are compatible for finite groups, including with a two-kernel.
They do not by themselves exclude the eight-dimensional actual-source
sector. This theorem has no source-geometry dependency.
It constructs no weak cyclic characteristic-five curve, invariant original
canonical line, actual spin image, generating trace inclusion, or either
finite étale endpoint map. BOTH maps from the SAME smooth projective
source remain required. The unmarked common-cover problem remains UNSOLVED.
