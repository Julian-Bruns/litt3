# Constancy of the secondary trace for minimal defect growth

Version1,2026-09-11. Focused geometric audit PASS by
/root/audit_cyclic5_returned_w4. Audited prose, not Lean verification.

Let h:T→C be an actual connected finite etale cyclic degree-five cover
of smooth projective curves of genus at least two over bar(F5). Pull
back a regular active admissible nilpotent projective connection and
its specified weight-one periodic tuple, including its actual flat
square-trivial line, grading, and original canonical marked W2 data.
Write V_S=H1(S,T_S), O_S=coker(Psi_S), R=k[e]/e^5, e=sigma-1.

Assume the actual Frobenius-linearized Hodge map on the free R-modules
has Smith factors (1,...,1,e²). Equivalently here, d(C)=1 and d(T)=2.

The compatible marked W3 lifts of the pulled-back T2 form a nonempty
affine plane. For every such S3, define epsilon_T(S3) in O_T by the
next normal Hodge obstruction, using rho(S+xi)=rho(S)-Psi_T(xi).
Then

    epsilon_T(S3) mod e O_T

is INDEPENDENT of S3 over the full algebraic closure. Equivalently,
ordinary trace induces O_T/eO_T≅O_C and

    Tr_h(epsilon_T(S3)) in O_C

is constant. No division by five is made. No deck action on a repaired
S3, no lifted involution, and no simple-zero Fitting hypothesis is
assumed. The higher construction retains the actual preceding tuple.

The result determines constancy, not the constant's value or its
nonvanishing. In particular it supplies no universal scalar multiplier
between this secondary trace and epsilon_C(C2).
