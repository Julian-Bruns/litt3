# Two-branch low-different atlases of the fixed curve

Let \(X\) be the [fixed genus-nine curve](../Definitions/Def_fixed_pair.md)
over \(\overline{\mathbf F}_5\). Let \(X\to\mathcal S\) be a
representable finite étale atlas of a smooth proper connected effective
orbifold with coarse \(\mathbf P^1\) and exactly two branch points,
one wild of inertia order \(e=qt\), \(q=|I_1|\), and different
\(\delta\), and one tame of order \(d\). Assume \(1<\delta/e<2\).
Its degree \(n\) satisfies
\[
n\le2240,\qquad\text{or }n=112000,\qquad\text{or }n=336000.
\]
In the latter cases, respectively,
\[
q=125,\quad \delta-e=143,\quad (t,d)=(8,7)\text{ or }(24,21),
\]
and the positive lower breaks are 1 and 6, with
\(|I_1/I_2|=25\) and \(|I_2|=5\).
No second endpoint, solvability, commutativity, upper-jump integrality,
or denominator bound is imposed. The alternatives are necessary
conditions, not assertions that these atlases exist.
