# All dormant projective opers on X are 96 explicit quadrics

For the [fixed X](../Definitions/Def_fixed_pair.md), let F be its degree-ten
polynomial and D=d/dx. Every globally regular projective connection has
a unique potential u''=r u with

    r=R+B/F+C y/F+A y^2/F^2,
    R=2F''/F+2(F'/F)^2+2x^8/F,
    deg B<=7, deg C<=4, deg A<=10.

It is dormant EXACTLY when D^2r=3r^2, scheme-theoretically, including
over nonreduced parameter algebras. This gives 96 quadratic coefficient
equations in 24 variables over F_25. The
[full equations](../Research/computations/fixed_x_dormant_equations.txt)
are generated by the [active Sage script](../scripts/fixed_x_dormant_opers.sage).

theorem from the complete oper identification and the literature-based
degree. The subsequent [complete exact enumeration](Thm_fixed_x_oper_enumeration.md)
also certifies its algebra and exports28990 distinct points, preserving
all29375 multiplicities; its evidence is recorded separately.

Its b_7 coordinate generates its algebra, with distinct irreducible

defined over F_25 and the others over the indicated extensions.
