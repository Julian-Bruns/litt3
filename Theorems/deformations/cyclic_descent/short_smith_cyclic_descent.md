# Cyclic descent with Smith lengths one and two

Version1,21 September2026. Focused independent audit PASS.

Let $h:T\to C$ be an actual connected cyclic finite etale cover of
degree $5^a$ between smooth proper hyperbolic curves over
$k=\overline{\mathbf F}_5$. Supply a regular active admissible oper,
its complete first periodic datum and original marked $C_2$; retain
their pullbacks through the original map. Write $e=\gamma-1$ for a
generator of the deck group. Suppose the actual Frobenius-linearized
Hodge map on the free regular tangent and normal modules has Smith
factors consisting of units and $e$ or $e^2$. There is no restriction
on the number of nonunit factors.

For $n\ge3$, given a compatible marked $C_n$ and its original cover
$T_n$, a GIVEN compatible $T_{n+a+1}$ extending this tuple forces its
GIVEN $T_{n+1}$, including the entire periodic tuple, to descend to a
compatible $C_{n+1}$. At $n=2$ the same conclusion holds if one
compatible $C_3^0$ extending the prescribed $C_2$ exists; it need not
pull back to the given upper third lift.

Consequently a supplied full compatible upper tower descends through
the original map whenever the lower tuple has a compatible third
reference. By the actual all-height dictionary, the supplied full
normalized upper group descends if the lower actual BT1 has one BT2
extension and the stated Smith condition holds.

The initial third-reference hypothesis is essential to this argument.
No assertion is made for Smith lengths three or greater. This is a
theorem about an actual one-leg comparison, not an existence theorem
for a common object on an arbitrary two-map span.
