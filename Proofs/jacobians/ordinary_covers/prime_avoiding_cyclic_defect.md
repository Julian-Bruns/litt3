# Supporting proof: Raynaud cyclic refinements

This proves the Raynaud specialization in the
[simultaneous growth theorem](../../../Theorems/jacobians/ordinary_covers/prime_avoiding_section_growth.md).
Use the [relative Frobenius convention](../../../Definitions/theta_cartier.md).
All covers below are actual covers of curves, not isogenies substituted
for them.

## Apply the simultaneous coefficient theorem

Use the [general section-growth theorem](prime_avoiding_section_growth.md)
with the single bundle B_C on C^(1) and the stated subvariety T.
It constructs actual connected cyclic character covers of C^(1),
with arbitrarily many distinct contributing tame characters, avoiding
all prescribed primes. Pull these covers back by relative Frobenius
C to C^(1). A universal homeomorphism preserves their connectedness,
and the Frobenius square recovers the original character cover on
the twist. Étale base change gives B_(C_j)=q_j^(1)*B_C, hence
\[
a(C_j)=\sum_{L\in\Lambda_j}h^0(C^{(1)},B_C\otimes L)
\ge a(C)+j.
\]
The a-number is bounded by the eventual nilpotent Frobenius dimension
Delta(C_j), proving both inequalities. The coefficient theorem already
supplies pairwise-coprime new orders and nested actual cyclic covers.

## Why the Raynaud geometric hypothesis holds

If C is nonordinary, 0 belongs to Theta_C. Select an irreducible
component T through0, of dimension g(C)-1>=1. The connected closed
subgroup generated by T is a positive-dimensional abelian
subvariety A0. Since0 belongs to T, containment in an abelian
translate is equivalent to containment in that abelian subgroup.
Minimality of A0 gives the stated generating property. Notice that
A0 need not be the whole Jacobian.

If J(C) is simple, any positive-dimensional component of Theta_C
generates the whole Jacobian up to translation, so take A0=J(C^(1)).

In characteristics two, three and five, the
[abelian-component theorem](../theta_divisors/raynaud_abelian_components.md)
excludes every translated abelian divisor from Theta_C, in every
genus. The [low-genus theorem](../theta_divisors/low_genus_raynaud_cosets.md)
also gives this in genera two and three in other odd
characteristics, and in genus four for p at least seven.
Any component T of Theta_C has dimension g(C)-1. If it lay in
a translate of a proper abelian subvariety, that subvariety would
have dimension exactly g(C)-1, and the irreducible closed subset
T would equal its translate. This is excluded. Thus T generates
the whole Jacobian up to translation, and A0 can be the whole
Jacobian. This proves all these cases with the same argument.

## Pulling the tower through the actual second leg

Apply the second-leg part of the coefficient theorem to the actual
twisted map g^(1), avoiding all degree primes of g. It proves that
Z_j=Z times_C C_j is connected. Étale Frobenius base change gives
B_Z=g^(1)*B_C, so its section-growth inequality is exactly
\[
a(Z_j)\ge a(Z)+j.
\]
The two original endpoint maps remain finite étale after composing
with the retained projection to Z. No Galois hypothesis on g or
simultaneous Galois closure is needed.

## Provenance and scope

The [bounded independent audit](../../../Research/audits/PRIME_AVOIDING_CYCLIC_DEFECT_AUDIT_2026_09_15.md) passes all parts of Version1.
Version2 added the low-genus cases. Version3 applies the independently
audited abelian-component theorem to every genus in characteristics
two, three and five. The component-dimension argument above is the
only additional deduction; the tower and two-map construction are
unchanged. No new arithmetic calculation is required.

The [earlier author note](../../../../litt3-computation-data/unmarked_ideas_20260915/prime_avoiding_cyclic_towers_original.md) constructed these towers when a theta
component generates the whole Jacobian, including ordinary genus
two. The argument weakens that condition to a suitable abelian
subvariety, adds all nonordinary curves and thus every genus-two
curve, and retains an arbitrary actual second leg. The
arithmetic-progression argument also removes the former
prime-density detour. The literature inputs are identified above;
no novelty is claimed for those theorems.

The covers add new primes indefinitely. Their defect may be
sublinear in degree. Neither the original two-leg source nor any
refined source is asserted to be jointly minimal. In particular
this construction does not contradict finite-prime-support
stabilization or the existence of tails approaching normalized
ordinary p-rank.

Version4 isolates the already reviewed arithmetic-progression and
character-cover argument in the general coefficient theorem. The
Raynaud geometric hypotheses and all original conclusions are retained.
