# Genus bounds for the first wild ramification layer

Version2,2026-09-14.

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic \(p\ge3\), let
\(C\to\mathcal S\) be a representable finite étale atlas of degree
\(n\), where \(C\) is smooth projective connected,
\(h=2g(C)-2>0\), and \(\mathcal S\) is a smooth proper connected
effective orbifold with coarse curve \(\mathbf P^1\) and exactly two
branch points. One is wild of inertia order \(qt\), different
\(\delta\), and wild subgroup \(P=I_1\) of order \(q\); the other
is tame of order \(d\). Assume \(1<\delta/(qt)<2\).

Put \(c=\delta-qt\), \(g_0=\gcd(t,d)\), \(t=g_0t_0\),
\(d=g_0m\), and \(D=cm-qt_0\). The atlas identities give
\(D\mid h\), \(D>0\), \(\gcd(m,t_0)=1\), and \(t_0\mid(m+D)\).
Let \(b\) be the first positive lower break, \(N=P_{b+1}\), and
\(|P/N|=p^r\). Then \(b,r\) are effectively bounded in terms of
\(p,h\), with \(b\le2(h+1)\).

If \(N\) has an abelian subgroup of index at most \(J\), the atlas
degree is bounded in terms of \(p,h,J\). No assumption on upper-jump
denominators, commutativity of \(P\), or size of \(N\) is needed for
the first-layer bound.

For the auxiliary local HKG curve \(H\), put
\(S=2g(H)=c-q+2\). If \(0<S<(p-1)q/p\), then \(b=1\);
writing \(v=|P_2|\), one has
\(S\ge(p-1)v p^{\lceil r/2\rceil}/p\).
When also \(p=5,h=16\), necessarily \(P/P_2\simeq C_5^2\).
The theorem does not bound the unrestricted deeper subgroup \(N\).

If \(|I_2|=p<|I_1|\), the whole atlas degree is bounded in terms
of \(p,h\), since then \(b=1\) and \(N=I_2\) is abelian.
For \(p=5,h=16\), this case has \(|I_1|=125\), lower jumps1,6
and exactly the two necessary signatures
\((e,d,n)=(1000,7,112000),(3000,21,336000)\).
