# Opposite elliptic forms lie in a source interpolation kernel

Version1,3 October2026. New scoped actual-source reduction, pending independent review. No uniform kernel dimension or tensor recognition is assumed.

Retain two actual finite étale maps h1,h2:S→X from the SAME source with proportional τ=q0⁸θ³, and their specified common cubic root S′=S(w1), with wi³=q0(xi). Let I1=h1*∞ and D0 the common reduced q0-zero divisor on S. Write q0=x²+bx+c and put
\[
Q_E=2x^3+3bx^2+cx,\qquad Q_E'=q_0.
\]
The two pulled elliptic forms λi=dxi/wi² have primitives fi=QE(xi)/wi⁵. The functions
\[
A_i=w_1^5 f_i\in k(S),\qquad i=1,2,
\]
belong to the concrete source interpolation kernel
\[
\mathcal K_{h_1}=
\{A\in H^0(S,\mathcal O_S(10I_1)): \operatorname{ord}_z(dA)\ge1
\text{ for every }z\in D_0\}.
\]
Conversely every A in this kernel gives a regular exact differential d(A/w1⁵) on S′. These are exactly the forms of the w-deck character ζ having a primitive with poles at most5D′, where D′ is the reduced root ramification divisor. The ambiguity is A=J⁵ with J∈H0(S,O(2I1)).

Their intrinsic Petri images descend to the ORIGINAL source S and are
\[
\mu(\eta_1,d(A/w_1^5))
=-\pi^*C_S(Aq_0(x_1)\theta_1),
\qquad \eta_1=w_1^8\theta_1.
\]
If the nonzero image space C_S(q0(x1)θ1·K_h1) is one-dimensional, the accepted Petri recognition theorem identifies the original X-fields. On fixed X itself the kernel is spanned by1,QE; its nonzero Petri image is one-dimensional. This fixed-X dimension is NOT asserted after an arbitrary étale pullback.

Both original maps, and any original étale Y-leg, remain on S. The interpolation kernel and its image are an exact, bounded-pole formulation of the missing opposite-line selection, not a proof that the image stays one-dimensional on S.
