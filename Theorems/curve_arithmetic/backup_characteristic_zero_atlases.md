# Characteristic-zero atlas reduction for the explicit backup

Version5,3 October2026. Put $k=\overline{\mathbf F}_5$ and
\[
Y:\quad v^2=u(u-1)(u-2)(u-3)(u-\alpha),\qquad
\alpha^3+\alpha+1=0.
\]
Let $C/\overline{\mathbf Q}$ be a smooth genus-two curve with
potentially good geometric reduction isomorphic to $Y$ at a place
over five. All statements retain this place; the covering maps below
are not assumed to have good reduction.

## Specialization filters

The geometric automorphism group of $C$ is $C_2$ and
\[
\operatorname{End}^0 J(C)\hookrightarrow
K=\mathbf Q(\sqrt{21},\sqrt{-25+\sqrt{21}}).
\]
Thus $J(C)$ is absolutely simple and its endomorphism algebra is
isomorphic to $\mathbf Q$, $\mathbf Q(\sqrt{21})$, or $K$.
It has no elliptic quotient or endomorphism with square $[5]$.
For a Weierstrass base point $O$, the prime-to-five Abel-torsion
restriction $W_1(C)[24]$ consists only of Weierstrass classes.
Every cyclic etale cover of $C$ of degree dividing six has ordinary
potentially good reduction at the chosen place.

If a complete uniform map $C\to\mathbf P^1$ has geometric monodromy
of order prime to five, it has degree two and is hyperelliptic.

## Complete uniform atlases

A complete uniform map means that every point in each branch fiber
has the same ramification index. There is no restriction on those
indices modulo five in the following conclusions.

Every complete uniform map $C\to\mathbf P^1$ is its degree-two
hyperelliptic quotient. In particular, $C$ admits no uniform triangle
atlas, including atlases with inertia divisible by five.

## The remaining arithmetic family

Suppose also that $C$ is uniformized by a compact arithmetic
Fuchsian surface group. Then its hyperelliptic orbifold group is a
MAXIMAL arithmetic lattice of signature $(0;2,2,2,2,2,2)$.
The curve $C$ is the unique torsion-free index-two cover
of that orbifold, with every cone generator mapping nontrivially
to $C_2$. This family is finite up to isomorphism and includes
noncongruence surface subgroups. Its subsequent exclusion at five
is recorded separately in [the complete arithmetic theorem](backup_arithmetic_reduction_exclusion.md).
This atlas reduction alone does not settle either original
common-cover problem.
