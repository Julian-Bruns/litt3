# Reciprocal finite-part rigidity in characteristic five

Version1, 27 September2026. Let k be algebraically closed of
characteristic five. Let R,S in k(t) be bounded at infinity, vanish
to order at least three at zero, and have only simple poles elsewhere.
At every pole c of either function, suppose its Laurent expansion is
\[
\frac{A}{t-c}+\frac{2A}{c}+O(t-c),\qquad A\ne0.
\]
If, for lambda nonzero and a,b in k,
\[
R(t)-\lambda t^7S(t^{-1})=a t^3+b t^4,
\]
then R=S=0. The pole locations may be arbitrary nonzero geometric
points; their number is unrestricted. No finite-field restriction,
cyclotomic hypothesis, curve correspondence, or computation is required.
