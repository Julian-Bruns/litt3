# Critical coprimality in the smallest nonzero degree-ten profile

Version1,1 October2026. Retain an ACTUAL nontrivial admissible
degree-ten source from
[reconstruction](admissible_line_reconstruction.md), with primitive
irreducible polynomial, connected everywhere étale normalization and
the prescribed reduced divisor. Use the short frame $W$, raw polynomial
$F$, cubic $D=S'$ and degree-at-most-five trace-dual numerator $U=uD$
from [the uniform annihilator theorem](admissible_annihilator_trace_vanishing.md).
Suppose the infinity profile is $(d,m)=(10,3)$.

There is NO rational function $c\in k(X)$ such that
\[
D(c)=U(c)=0.
\]
Consequently
\[
\gcd_{k(X)[T]}(D,U)=1.
\]
Neither assertion assumes global squarefreeness of $D$; repeated finite
critical fibers and finite zeros of its leading coefficient are retained.
The proof combines finite critical-root affinity, two fixed translation
gap rows, numerator infinity bounds and the at-least-four distinct large
residues in [the nine-profile theorem](admissible_degree_ten_nonzero_profiles.md).
The coprimality conclusion additionally uses the established polynomial
and reduced-linear annihilator exclusions.

This excludes partial cancellation of the critical denominator on this
profile. It does NOT exclude an actual source whose numerator and full
cubic critical denominator are coprime, or decide the remaining
degree-ten or common-cover problem.
