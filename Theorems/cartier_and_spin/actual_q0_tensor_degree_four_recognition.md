Any original actual étale map from S to Y stays on S. Auxiliary joint-field quotients are not replacement common-cover sources. The proof excludes every jointly minimal degree-FOUR alternative: rational and genus-two quotients, both elliptic Q positions, both elliptic numerator forms and all derivative classes. This is not a solution of the unmarked common-cover problem or all-degree tensor recognition.
