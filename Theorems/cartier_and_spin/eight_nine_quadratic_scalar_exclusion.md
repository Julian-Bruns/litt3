# Eight- and nine-label trace solutions cannot have an F25 scalar

Version1, 27 September2026. In the standard four common-moment trace
system with independently chosen endpoint multisets of cardinality
eight, or both of cardinality nine, there is no nonzero scalar in F25.
All phases, root multiplicities and characteristic-five blocks are
retained. This excludes that scalar sector for actual balanced pole24
and pole27 comparisons.

The proof extends the lower-degree prime-field argument using a complete
four-phase quotient lemma. The balanced remainders are closed by the
two-block theorem for eight labels and the established one-block theorem
for nine labels. Other scalar sectors remain open.
