# A coreless actual span has a nonproper profinite amalgam

Version1,26 September2026. Let X<-T->Y be an actual finite etale span
of smooth projective connected curves over an algebraically closed
field, with both endpoint genera at least two. Choose a geometric
base point on T, and identify H=pi1(T) with its open images in
G_X=pi1(X) and G_Y=pi1(Y). Form the profinite pushout
\[
G=G_X\amalg_HG_Y.
\]
If both natural vertex maps G_X->G and G_Y->G are injective, the
given span has a core. More precisely, one connected finite etale
refinement W->T is simultaneously Galois over X and Y.

Consequently a coreless actual span has a nonproper profinite
pushout: at least one vertex map is not injective. This does not
assert that the pushout is trivial, that it has no finite quotients,
or that every compatible finite coefficient is trivial. No converse
is needed or claimed. In particular, the simultaneous Galois refinement
cannot be assumed in the coreless problem.

The group-theoretic ingredient is the common-open-normal-subgroup
lemma, also stated as Lemma3.4 in
[Cusinato, On commensurations of pro-C groups](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.01234v2).
The geometric implication below retains the actual two embeddings.
