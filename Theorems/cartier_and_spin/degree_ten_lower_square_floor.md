# The lower square boundary has only degrees140 and142 remaining

Version1, 25 September2026. In all eleven fixed-v spaces of the
[degree-ten boundary theorem](degree_ten_square_boundaries.md), retain
the exact open conditions a*kappa!=0 and impose U=V=0. Then d!=0
automatically. Every geometric point satisfies
\[
R_0\ne0,\qquad 138\le\deg R_0\le142.
\]
There is no square R_0 of degree138 at any such point, over the full
algebraic closure. Consequently any nonzero square on this lower
boundary has degree140 or142. Neither remaining degree is decided.
The separate degree144 square locus also remains open.

Each lower-boundary coefficient space is explicitly isomorphic to
G_m^3 times A^2. The three invertible coordinates can be kappa,a,d.
This description retains all coefficient boundaries compatible with
the original open set; it is not a generic chart with missing points.

The exact degree138 strata are nonempty coefficient loci: for v=1
there are36 geometric G_m components, and for each of the ten linear
v there are147. Their entire residual-square loci are empty. The
certificate treats their free scaling parameter geometrically, rather
than testing a bounded list of values.

A separate pole comparison excludes the particular factorization
F/v=G*gamma(G), with G monic of degree5 and gamma the cubic endpoint
automorphism, throughout the surviving profiles. A square norm does
not imply that factorization, so this does not close degrees140,142
or144 or construct an everywhere-etale cover.
