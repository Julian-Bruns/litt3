# Evaluate the remainder at the purely inseparable root

Original argument30 September2026; Version2 scope correction3 October2026.
[Statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/fifth_power_norm_remainder.md).

Let c^p=-f. Since f is not a p-th power and K has characteristic p,
the minimal polynomial of c is W^p+f, of degree p. In an algebraic
closure write the roots of the monic separable polynomial F as b_i.
Then
\[
\operatorname{Nm}(f+b^p)
=\prod_i(b_i-c)^p
=(-1)^{Np}F(c)^p.
\]
The assumed p-th root of this nonzero norm lies in K. Injectivity of
the p-th power map in a field therefore implies F(c) in K^times.
Write F=(W^p+f)H+R with deg R<p. Since 1,c,...,c^(p-1) are
linearly independent over K, the relation R(c)=F(c) in K forces
R to be the nonzero constant tau. Evaluating any two presentations
at c gives equality of their constants; cancellation of the nonzero
factor W^p+f then gives equality of their quotient polynomials.
The converse follows immediately from the same norm formula.

The bound N>=p uses positive source degree. If H=0, then F=tau
is constant. Otherwise the displayed product has degree p+deg H,
which cannot cancel against a constant, so N=p+deg H>=p.
For a primitive field the minimal polynomial always has positive degree.

For an arbitrary nonzero separable polynomial F, multiply by the
inverse of its leading coefficient to obtain a monic G. The actual
quotient algebras K[W]/(F) and K[W]/(G) are canonically isomorphic,
preserving the class of W and its determinant norm. Apply the monic
argument, then multiply its presentation by the original leading
coefficient. This proves the same equivalence and unique original
presentation for nonmonic and disconnected source algebras. The
degree lower bound still requires positive degree.

The omitted hypothesis is necessary: F=1 is monic and separable,
its actual quotient is the zero algebra, and its determinant norm
is the empty product1. Thus the nonzero p-th-power norm condition
holds while N=0<p. The concrete choice K=F_5(z), f=z satisfies
f not in K^5, as the normalized derivation takes z to1 and kills
every fifth power. Both this counterexample and the corrected
uniform theorem are kernel checked in Lean; the
[focused audit](../../Research/audits/NORM_FROBENIUS_DEGREE_ZERO_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md)
records the exact modules and evidence.

No division by N or prime-to-p monodromy hypothesis occurs. If an
original cover is not generated by b, the implication cannot be
obtained merely by extracting a p-th root from a norm raised to an
index divisible by p. One must first apply the primitive norm theorem
on the actual intermediate source, with its hypotheses checked there.
The present assertion deliberately states the primitive situation.
