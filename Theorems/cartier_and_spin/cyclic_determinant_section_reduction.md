# Cyclic symmetry reduces twisted lines to determinant-twisted sections

Version1,26September2026. Let C be a smooth proper connected curve over
an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from three.
Let gamma have order three, with quotient C/<gamma>=P1, and let O be
a fixed point. Let E be a gamma-linearized rank-two bundle with
det E=O(delta O), delta>=0.

If H0(C,E(delta O)) is isotypic for gamma (including the zero space),
then every saturated line M in E of nonnegative degree has
gamma-invariant isomorphism class. Consequently, if all invariant
degree-zero twists of E have no sections, every geometric degree-zero
twist has no sections. This requires only a section-space calculation
and invariant twists, not a discriminant-square classification.

There is also an exact norm criterion when E has no positive-degree
line subbundle. A degree-zero subline with three distinct conjugate
embedded lines gives a nonzero section s of E tensor det E such that
\[
N(s):=s\,\gamma(s)\,\gamma^2(s)
=(s\wedge\gamma(s))G,
\qquad G\in H^0(C,\operatorname{Sym}^3E).
\]
The wedge is nonzero. Conversely, any nonzero s with nonzero wedge
and this identity has zero divisor of degree delta and therefore
gives a degree-zero subline of E. The identity is global, so it retains
all common zeros, repeated points and the point O. No finite etale
trivialization, return or common-cover realization is asserted.
