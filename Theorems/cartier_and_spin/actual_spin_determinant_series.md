# Actual spin determinant series: index four and rank at least eight

Version1,2 October2026. Computation-free strong reusable consequence; independent focused review PASS. This does not decide the unmarked common-cover problem.

Retain an actual same-source finite étale span from the fixed genus-nine X to a genus-two Y. On the actual Galois sixteen-spin refinement, write q:T→Y of degree8d, L¹⁶=ωT, degL=d, and W⊂H⁰(T,L) for the basepoint-free infinity-section orbit span. Write r=dimW, so8|r. Let P be the RAW first-jet image and D its descended common critical divisor, degD≤2.

\[
A=\det P=\omega_T\otimes L^2(-q^*D).
\]
Its dimension s satisfies
\[
4\mid s,\qquad s\ge r-1,\qquad s\ge8.
\]
These assertions apply to every actual common cover, without a hypothesis on its original Cartier trace degree or contact.

In exact noncyclic degree-one contact eight, D=2Q and F₀=ωY(-2Q), so C generates A=L²⊗q*F₀ of degree2d. Its actual descended projective-coefficient presentation has rank s≥8, degree s/4≥2 and slope1/4. It is not asserted to equal the rank-four degree-one original Cartier trace.
