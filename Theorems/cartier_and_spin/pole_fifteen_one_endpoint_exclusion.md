Version1, 27 September2026. For every actual pole-fifteen comparison
in [cubic descent](pole_fifteen_cubic_descent.md), neither unramified
five-point endpoint fiber can have five copies of one leading label
and phase. This strengthens the exclusion of concentration at both
endpoints. All geometric scalars and covering degrees are retained.

Moreover the actual scalar epsilon necessarily belongs to F_(5^56).
This does not bound a model field of the curve or its two maps.

A further reusable local fact is proved: at a concentrated endpoint,
under the actual quintic differential and pole hypotheses, neither
Tr(u) nor Tr(v) could vanish identically. The other endpoint need not
be concentrated for this fact.

The mixed-label quintic case remains unresolved; conditional recognition
is still known through covering degree14, not15. Both original
unmarked common-cover questions remain UNSOLVED.
