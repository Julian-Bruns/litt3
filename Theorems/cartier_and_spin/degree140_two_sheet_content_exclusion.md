# Fixed content of a constant degree140 square is squarefree

Version1, 27 September2026. On the entire remaining normalized constant
degree140 chart, no geometric square residual has positive fixed
coefficient content at two distinct curve points above the same root
of t. This includes all three endpoint roots, arbitrary geometric ratio
and scale, three-sheet incidence and the nonreduced degree-drop strata.

Combining this with the common-critical and individual-content exclusions,
the monic fixed x-content c_W of any possible square satisfies
\[
c_W\mid t,\qquad \deg c_W\le3.
\]
In particular the full x-discriminant is not identically zero as a
polynomial in scale at those ratios. Its value at a square scale is
still zero; the assertion does not itself exclude square specialization.

Squarefree-content and content-free square loci remain open. A square
coefficient point would itself still be only a necessary cover condition.
