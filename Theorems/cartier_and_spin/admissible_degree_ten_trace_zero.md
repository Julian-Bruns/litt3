# The nonzero-y exclusion and polynomial pole strata at degree ten

Version2, 25 September2026. The nonzero-y exclusion and the initial necessary
profile list are retained; the later two-profile theorem closes seven profiles.
In the degree-ten actual admissible-primitive
problem of [the derivative boundary](admissible_degree_ten_derivative_boundary.md),
restrict to a_1=0 and a_2!=0 in F_b=(W^5+f)H_5(W)+kappa t_B^3/v.
Then v has NO nonzero y-component. The entire geometric norm system
\[
P+g^3=H^2J^3,\qquad \deg g\le3,\quad H,J\text{ monic quadratics}
\]
is empty, including repeated-root and common-root boundaries. Combined
with the older square-norm obstruction this excludes that entire sector.

For the surviving polynomial v, let d=deg v and D_2=v a_2. Then
D_2 belongs to L_X(14O), with possible pole orders0,3,6,9,10,12,13.
The only possible infinity multiplicity partitions of G are:

| d | positive multiplicities above O |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | (4,4,2), (4,4,1,1), (3,3,3,1), (3,3,2,2), (3,3,2,1,1), (2,2,2,2,2) |
| 1 | (3,3,1), (2,2,2,1) |
| 2 | (1,1,1,1) |

The polynomial shapes are constant; a linear factor at a root of P;
two distinct such factors; or a scalar square of a linear factor.
For d=2, D_2 is in L_X(12O). If v=(x-r)(x-s) with distinct P-roots,
\[
a_2=d_2(x)+\lambda y/((x-r)(x-s)),\qquad\deg d_2\le2.
\]
If v=(x-r)^2 and P(r)=0, the G partition at (r,0) is (3,3) or (2,2,2).
In the latter case D_2 has no y-component and its polynomial component
is divisible by x-r. If P(r)!=0, the partition over each of the three
points at x=r is (1,1), and D_2 is nonzero at each point.

All assertions apply to the four possible supports and arbitrary
geometric coefficients. These are the initial necessary conditions. The
[two-profile reduction](admissible_degree_ten_two_profiles.md) now excludes
every quadratic v and seven of the nine listed infinity profiles. Only
constant v with (4,4,2) and linear v with (3,3,1) remain in this sector.
Their actual covers, and the separate cubic-derivative sector, are not decided.
