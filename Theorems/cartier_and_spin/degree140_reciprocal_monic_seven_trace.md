# A uniform monic degree-seven critical scale equation

Version1,30 September2026. Retain the actual normalized constant-degree140
critical curve and all primitivity hypotheses of
[the normalized trace theorem](degree140_normalized_critical_trace.md).
In its notation define
\[
J(\ell)=\operatorname{Tr}_{k(C)/k(\Lambda)}(x\,t(x)\,v/\phi).
\]
For every admitted geometric ratio this is a polynomial of EXACT degree
seven. Its leading coefficient is
\[
[\ell^7]J=\langle154370\rangle\,w h^{-13}.
\]
It vanishes at every nonzero entirely ramified fibre of Lambda, including
the admitted multiple-discriminant and wild endpoint cases. Thus
\[
\frac{h^{13}}{\langle154370\rangle w}J(\ell)
\]
is a uniform monic degree-seven necessary scale polynomial. In the ratio
coordinates H=h w, q=w^3, ell=w mu, the leading coefficient of J(w mu)
is <154370> q^7/H^13.

This is a pointwise polynomial-trace theorem, not a claim that all lower
coefficients have only H,q,Psi denominators. The latter question matters
for global parameter elimination. It does not exclude the simultaneous
trace/square locus or solve the unmarked common-cover problem.
