# The degree-eleven critical quintic is separable

Version1,1 October2026. Retain an ACTUAL primitive nontrivial
admissible degree-eleven source in
[the support reduction](admissible_degree_ten_eleven_reduction.md).
Let $K=k(X)$ and write its raw primitive polynomial in either
translated frame as
\[
F(T)=(T^5+q)H(T)+\kappa t^3,\qquad
H=vT^6+H_5T^5+H_4T^4+H_3T^3+H_2T^2+H_1T+H_0,
\]
where $\kappa\ne0$. Its critical polynomial is $D=H'$.
Then $H_4\ne0$, and hence
\[
D'=2H_4T^2+H_3T+2H_2
\]
has exact degree two. This holds for all four projected support
choices and retains every finite critical coefficient drop.

Together with
[critical irreducibility](admissible_degree_eleven_critical_irreducibility.md),
it proves that $D$ is irreducible and separable over $K$, and therefore
squarefree over $K$. Repeated specialized critical fibers are still
allowed. This is not an actual-source existence decision or an
unmarked common-cover decision.
