Put g=g(B), and count b2 fibers of type (THREE,THREE) and b1 of type (THREE,ONE,ONE,ONE). Hurwitz gives TWO b2+b1=g+FIVE, with b2≥ONE from infinity and b1>ZERO. Therefore b=b1+b2≤g+FOUR≤NINE. It follows that Z1=W/C1=W/C2=Z. Each Gi acts faithfully on Z, with fixed field k(xi). Let H0 be the group generated by these TWO actual A6 subgroups of Aut(Z). We now exclude the necessary wild action. The [small-area cubic-tensor lemma](../quotient_geometry/local_actions/cubic_tensor_small_area_tame_action.md) applies to T_Z. For clarity its exact local argument is retained here. At a wild point of Z write its inertia order as e=wa, where w is a positive power of FIVE and a is prime to FIVE. Its lower groups Ij have I0 of order wa, I1 of order w. Put
\[
D=\sum_{j\ge1}(|I_j|-1),\qquad \delta=wa-1+D.
\]
Every nontrivial lower wild group has FIVE-power order, so FOUR divides D. A tame complement of order a exists and can be linearized in a source parameter t. For a quotient parameter u its invariance under t→ζt implies ζ^(δ+ONE)=ONE from the leading term of u′. Thus a divides δ+ONE, hence a divides D. These assertions use the actual Galois completed local extension Z/V.

Let m be the order of T_Z at this point. The complete tensor ledger gives m in {minusTWO,ZERO,EIGHT}. The same tame leading-term calculation gives a dividing m+THREE. Thus a=ONE except for the possible pairs (m,a)=(ZERO,THREE) and(EIGHT,ELEVEN). If n is the integer order of the descended cubic at its image, the exact tensor pullback formula is
\[
m+3=wa(n+3)+3D.

\[

for a positive integer r. Modulo TWELVE the left side is ELEVEN. A power of FIVE is ONE orFIVE modulo TWELVE, so r is ELEVEN orSEVEN modulo TWELVE, respectively. In particular r≥SEVEN and k≥(SEVEN w+ONE)/THREE. Therefore
