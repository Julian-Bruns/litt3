# Hermitian local rigidity: primary-source locations

2026-09-06. The former candidate calculations in this note are now
superseded by the audited proofs of
[local rigidity](../Solutions/Sol_hermitian_local_normality.md) and
[global identification](../Solutions/Sol_completed_local_orbifold_rigidity.md).
This note retains source locations only.

- Bleher–Chinburg–Poonen–Symonds, [HKG curves](https://math.mit.edu/~poonen/papers/AutK.pdf):
  Theorem 4.9 and Proposition 4.8, printed p.9, give existence of an HKG
  realization and restriction to the normal Sylow subgroup. Remark 4.5
  distinguishes quotient coordinates. Theorem 4.9 does not assert uniqueness.
- Lehr–Matignon, [p-cyclic covers](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0307031):
  Section 2, printed p.3, gives the Artin–Schreier presentation and genus.
  Proposition 3.3, proof Case 1, printed p.4, supplies the triangular
  coordinate calculation from the pole-space bases. Numbering is manuscript-specific.
- Montanucci–Zini, [Hermitian quotients](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.03398):
  Section 2(I), printed p.2, gives the PGU rational-point stabilizer;
  the paragraph after (IV), p.3, gives its intersection with PSU.
  The canonical global proof verifies the full signatures from these
  inputs; no unstated full-signature theorem is being quoted.

The relevant proofs were read. Fixed-base local equivalence uses the
separate coordinate-absorption and scalar argument in the canonical
proof, not HKG existence alone.
