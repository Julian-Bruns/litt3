# The paired BT and higher-Hodge torsors agree at every last digit

Version1,21 September2026. Extend the
[BT2 comparison](bt_hodge_obstruction_comparison.md) to every height.
Retain an actual everywhere-versal height-two, dimension-one
BT1 $H/C$, its normalized finite determinant, its canonical marked
$W_2$ curve, and its compatible first periodic filtered datum.
Here $W_m=W(k)/5^m$ and $k=\overline{\mathbf F}_5$.

For every $N\ge1$, normalized marked BT$_N$ extensions of $H$
correspond to compatible marked higher-Hodge solutions through
$W_{N+1}$ retaining that first datum. This correspondence respects
truncation and actual etale pullback. At each last digit the local
torsor map is
\[
F_*T_C/\mu(T_C)\xrightarrow{\ 2\mathscr D_H^{-1}
\overline{\mathfrak b}_r\ }\mathcal B_H.
\tag{1}
\]
Consequently, for a PAIRED preceding BT$_N$ and Hodge solution,
the actual obstruction to BT$_{N+1}$ agrees, under this map,
with its obstruction to a compatible $W_{N+2}$ Hodge solution.
The previous filtered object, divided Frobenius, grading and flat
periodicity twist remain fixed in this assertion. The mod-five
projective oper alone does not specify higher obstructions.

In particular, a compatible full Hodge tower gives an actual full
group, and an actual full group with this BT1 marking gives a
compatible full Hodge tower. If the maximum compatible marked Witt
length is finite and equal to $m\ge2$, then the maximum actual BT
level with this first marking is $m-1$.

This is a dictionary, not a vanishing theorem. It neither constructs
a common first oper from a bare span nor forces any next obstruction
to vanish. No finite isomorphism is inferred from a full object
unless that full object is actually supplied.
