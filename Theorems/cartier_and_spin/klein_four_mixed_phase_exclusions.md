# Mixed-phase exclusions for Klein-four endpoints

Version1, 26 September2026. Retain the actual same-source hypotheses
of [trace descent](klein_four_trace_branch_finiteness.md). A canonical
label has type i in Z/4 and phase xi in mu29. Root-type rotation
acts on this finite label set; no such action on the actual curve
is assumed.

In every degree:

1. If one endpoint has a common phase, the other must have at least
   three distinct phases. Arbitrary type multiplicities are allowed.
2. The two endpoint multisets cannot both be invariant under
   (i,xi)->(i+2,xi). Equivalently, they cannot each consist of two
   opposite-type pairs, with the two phases at either end arbitrary
   and possibly equal.

These follow from the old moments and the
[forced fourth traces](klein_four_fourth_endpoint_trace.md), before
choosing pole masses, inertia or the parameter curve. Together with
[balanced exclusion](klein_four_balanced_endpoint_exclusion.md),
remaining actual configurations have two unbalanced endpoints and
epsilon outside F_(5^14). No complete degree17..87 or original
common-cover problem is decided by these sector statements.
