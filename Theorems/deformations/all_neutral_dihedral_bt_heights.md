# All fourteen neutral dihedral families have exact finite BT height

Version2,24 September2026. This integrates the retained complete
fourteen-case integral calculation with the audited general
secondary-trace and height-translation results. No new independent
audit of the whole family engine is claimed.

Use the actual F625 genus-two BT1 $H/C$ of
[the existence-descent counterexample](etale_p_witt_obstruction.md).
For each branch pair OTHER THAN $\{\tau,\infty\}$, use the actual
dihedral tower $W_a,T_a$ of
[neutral dihedral towers](cyclic_descent/neutral_dihedral_towers.md),
with original first markings, for $a\ge1$. Then
\[
\boxed{
\max\{N:H|_{T_a}\text{ admits BT}_N\}
=
\max\{N:H|_{W_a}\text{ admits BT}_N\}
=a+1.
}
\]
The curves have respective genera $5^a+1$ and $2\cdot5^a+1$.
Thus all fourteen non-Galois degree-five repairs have BT2 and no BT3,
and none of these quotient or closure towers has a full prolongation
of the specified pulled-back BT1.
For EVERY one of the fourteen resolvents, the corresponding maximal
compatible marked Witt length is $a+2$ on both curves, with the
original first marking and full preceding tuple. The
[finite-level translation proof](../../Proofs/deformations/cyclic_descent/neutral_dihedral_height_translation.md)
retains the original quotient marking in both directions.

All choices of earlier repair digits and normalized determinant are
included. The exceptional resolvent $\{\tau,\infty\}$ is NOT covered;
its quotient has defect four and its cyclic closure defect eight.
It instead admits a [full prolongation](explicit_full_bt_existence_descent_failure.md).
Other monodromy groups, other connections and the original common-cover
problem remain outside this statement.
