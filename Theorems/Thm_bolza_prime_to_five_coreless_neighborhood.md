# A whole prime-to-five commensurability class with coreless common covers

Let k=bar(F5) and C:v^2=t^6+3. Suppose C1 and C2 are each prime-to-five
etale commensurable with C, in the explicit
[common-source sense](../Definitions/Def_prime_to_p_etale_commensurability.md).
Then there exists a smooth projective connected curve Z and finite etale maps

    C1 <- Z -> C2

whose endpoint function fields have intersection k inside k(Z).
Each leg's geometric Galois closure also has order prime to5.

Neither endpoint need map to C. The witness covers may be different,
non-Galois, and arbitrarily large. In particular C itself and the Hermitian
curve H:X^6+Y^6+Z^6=0
each admit a coreless etale self-correspondence, since H->C is a Galois
etale cover of degree9. More generally this applies to prime-to-five
Galois-closure covers of H, by composition.

This gives actual positive examples and a limitation on proposed
exclusions. Superspeciality of the endpoint, prime-to-five monodromy
on both legs, or Hermitian commensurability does not forbid corelessness.
It is not a counterexample to the common-cover conjecture, does not
concern the fixed genus-nine/genus25 pair, and does not claim a prescribed
source equation or a numerical degree bound for Z.
