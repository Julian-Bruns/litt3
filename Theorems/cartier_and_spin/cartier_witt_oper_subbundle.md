# A proper common Witt subbundle detects a dormant oper

Version1, 23 September 2026. Let \(k=\overline{\mathbf F}_5\)
and let \(X\xleftarrow fZ\xrightarrow gY\) be an actual finite
bi-étale span of smooth projective hyperbolic curves, with \(g(Y)=2\)
and no clump. On the first Frobenius twists put
\[
W=F_*\omega^{-1}=\operatorname{Der}_{\mathcal O}(F_*\mathcal O).
\]
Any nonzero proper common saturated subbundle \(K\subset W\) is
unique, has rank three and degree zero, and is common-simple; its
quotient \(Q=W/K\) is common-simple of rank two. The vector-field
bracket and quotient action give
\[
\Lambda^2K\simeq K\simeq\operatorname{End}^0(Q).
\]
The bundle \(Q\) is the canonical-determinant Bol bundle of a
common regular dormant projective oper. Conversely every such
oper yields this \(K\) as the kernel of its complementary Bol map.
Thus a proper common subbundle of \(W\) is equivalent to a common
dormant oper, without assuming a vanishing centered Atiyah class
or a common connection on \(K\).

The [full monodromy theorem](first_cartier_full_monodromy.md)
determines the entire first algebraic group and the no-oper tower.
This criterion does not produce an oper or its second common
Cartier descent.
