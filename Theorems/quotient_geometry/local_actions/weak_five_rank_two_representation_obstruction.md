# Weak cyclic-five and tame-two curves have no irreducible projective rank-two representation

Version1,3 October2026. New scoped general group obstruction; focused review pending. No finite coefficient extraction from an arbitrary trace is asserted.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristicFIVE, and let a finite group G act faithfully on a smooth connected projective curve Z. Suppose Z/G=P¹ with precisely TWO branch values, cyclic weak C5 inertia of lower break ONE at one and cyclic C2 inertia at the other. Then G has no irreducible projective representation into PGL₂(k). The representation need not be faithful, and no assumption on the abelianization of G is needed.

The prescribed smooth cyclic lift gives a generating tuple of orders(FIVE,FIVE,TWO) with the first two classes inverse conjugate. In dimensionTWO Scott's inequality forces regular J2 lifts and rigidity over F5. Its entire projective image lies in PSL₂(F5), but a simultaneous companion basis makes the conjugator between its two unipotents have nonsquare determinant, contradicting inverse conjugacy.

For an actual canonical degree-TEN target with these branch data, this excludes any irreducible rank-TWO projective representation that genuinely factors through its ORIGINAL target group. It does not prove that a rank-TWO bundle or finite coefficient extracted after a further source refinement factors through that group; such a bridge remains an additional obligation. It preserves the original same-source endpoint problem rather than replacing its maps.
