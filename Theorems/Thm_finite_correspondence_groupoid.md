# Finite composition-closed images give an effective orbifold

Let C be a smooth projective connected curve over an algebraically
closed field of arbitrary characteristic. Let F be a FINITE set of
distinct integral reduced curves in C x C whose normalizations R_Gamma
are finite etale over both factors. Suppose F contains the diagonal,
is transpose-closed, and contains the reduced joint image of every
connected component of R_Gamma x_C R_Lambda.

Then R=disjoint_union R_Gamma has a canonical finite etale groupoid
structure. Its quotient S=[C/R] is a smooth proper connected effective
DM curve, C -> S is representable finite etale, C x_S C=R, and
deg(C/S)=sum_Gamma deg(R_Gamma/C).

If another effective atlas C -> T has self-relation a subunion of R,
there is a unique compatible factorization C -> T -> S, up to unique
2-isomorphism, with T -> S representable finite etale. The construction
also applies to a finite disjoint union of curves; connectedness of
the quotient means that the relation connects the components.

If S has a hyperbolic curve atlas, its geometric automorphism group is
finite, including in wild characteristic. Effectivity refers to faithful
formal germs, NOT faithful tangent representations. This theorem does
not establish finiteness of a relation generated by finitely many seeds.
