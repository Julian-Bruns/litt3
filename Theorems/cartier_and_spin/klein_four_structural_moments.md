# Structural reductions for the Klein-four moment problem

Version1, 26 September2026. Retain the actual V4 hypotheses and the
two moment identities of
[trace descent](klein_four_trace_branch_finiteness.md). Put
K=F_(5^14), K+=F_(5^7), and bar(x)=x^(5^7). An endpoint is balanced
if its four B-labels use the four roots of A once each, all with one
common mu29 phase.

1. If epsilon belongs to K, both endpoints must be balanced, in every
degree and every permitted pole profile. Neither moment equation may
be used to assume this subfield condition without proof.
2. For any specified pair of endpoint multisets, the moment determinant
is a scalar quadric on the solution space of a seven-by-four linear
system over K+. The variables are the two coordinates each of
x=M2 and y=M6 in K/K+. All zero-vector and zero-scale boundaries
are retained when recovering epsilon.
3. Given x,y and total mass modulo5, all29 residue weights are uniquely
recovered by inverse Fourier transform. If their integer representatives
have sum r and exactly o of them are1, an allowed lift to weights
{0,1,2,3,4,6} of total mass m exists exactly when
(m-r)/5 is an integer in[0,o]. This does not impose inertia or
the nonlinear endpoint identities.
4. If both endpoints have four identical labels, the actual character
minors give 2g+j1<=3e+4j-18. This excludes that entire sector in
degrees14..17, with arbitrary epsilon and arbitrary extra branch points.
5. In the complementary-double-label sector defined in the proof,
every moment solution has residue mass at least37. Hence that sector
is excluded in all degrees14..48. A degree49 moment-only datum
exists with epsilon outside K; it is not an actual curve.

These are structural and sector results, not a decision of the
remaining degree17..87 actual comparisons.
