# A native proper positive Cartier image forbids wild genus-two étale atlases when p is one modulo four and exceeds five

Version2,3 October2026. Root and independent whole-scope review **PASS** for the strengthening below; see the [Version2 audit](../../Research/audits/PROPER_CARTIER_ETALE_ATLAS_WILD_GENERALIZATION_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). The Version1 exact scope and proof are preserved in the provenance subsections.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p≡ONE moduloFOUR, p>FIVE. Let q:C→Y be an ACTUAL finite étale R-Galois cover with g(Y)=TWO, and let ρ:C→D be an ACTUAL finite étale R-equivariant map of degree e. Suppose R acts faithfully on D. Require an integral R-stable Cartier image K_D⊂B_D of rank at most p−TWO and degree g(D)−ONE, with its native action inherited from B_D and everywhere surjective adjunction F_D*K_D→ω_D.

Then D→D/R has NO wild inertia. No ordinarity, even-atlas, rational-quotient, coefficient-representation, inertia-depth or group-order hypothesis is assumed. The native determinant supplies the stabilizer lcm, and the rationality of an attempted wild coarse quotient follows from genus-TWO Hurwitz.

More generally the degree requirement on K_D can be replaced by the explicit actual stabilizer-lcm equality ℓ=e; the same proof needs neither evenness nor a preassigned rational quotient. Neither original opposite endpoint map is inferred to descend to D.
