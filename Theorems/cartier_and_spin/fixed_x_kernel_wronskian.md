# The fixed Cartier-kernel Wronskian and its transport obstruction

Version1,2026-09-20.

Let $V=\ker C_X$ for the fixed genus-nine curve in characteristic
five. Its generic Hasse order sequence is $(0,1,2)$, and its
Hasse--Wronskian has canonical weight six. Its zero divisor consists
of the ten finite cubic branch points with multiplicity six,
twenty-one further distinct points with multiplicity one, and
the point at infinity with multiplicity fifteen.

Thus the set $T_V$ where the multiplicity is nonzero modulo five
has exactly31 points. For **any** actual finite bi-étale span from
$X$ to a genus-two curve, $f^{-1}(T_V)$ cannot be saturated under
$g$. In particular, the Wronskian-multiplicity congruence cannot be
constant on every $g$-fiber of a hypothetical span.

This is an exact endpoint calculation and a necessary failure of
the proposed transport property, not a nonexistence theorem for
the span.
