# Small-rank Frobenius-dual sources cannot map to K

Version1,26 September2026. Let R be a degree-zero vector bundle on X
of rank three or four and admit a surjection R->K. Then
\[
F_{\rm abs}^*R\not\simeq R^\vee\otimes T
\quad\text{for every degree-zero line bundle }T.
\]
No stability or finite-monodromy hypothesis is needed for this assertion.

Consequently every irreducible finite etale coefficient R of rank
two, three or four having such a character-valued Frobenius-dual
pairing satisfies Hom(R,K)=0. The rank-two case only requires that
a nonzero map be generically an isomorphism.

In particular, Hom(R,K)=0 for EVERY irreducible rank-three finite
etale coefficient whose finite monodromy order is prime to five.
The accepted classification reduces its only remaining possibility
to the Hessian pairing, which is now excluded.

This also excludes projective unitary pairing types in these ranks,
including wild finite monodromy when the stated pairing holds. It is
not a classification of all wild groups or all rank-four sources.
Higher ranks and either original unmarked common-cover problem remain open.
