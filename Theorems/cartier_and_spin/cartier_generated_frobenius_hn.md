# The generated Cartier kernel is K(24O), with exact maximum line degree twenty

Version2,3October2026. Let X be the fixed genus-nine curve and
F:X->X^(1) relative Frobenius. In its actual Cartier bundle B_X let
U be the saturated subbundle generated by the three global exact forms.
Retain the canonical evaluation map
\[
\alpha:F^*U\longrightarrow\omega_X,\qquad H=\ker\alpha.
\]
Then alpha is surjective, H has rank two and degree 49, and H is
GEOMETRICALLY STABLE. Consequently the actual HN filtration is
\[
0\subset H\subset F^*U,\qquad
(\operatorname{rk},\mu)=(2,49/2),(1,16).
\]
More precisely, \(H\simeq K(24O)\), and
\[
\max_{M\subset H}\deg M=\max_{M\subset F^*U}\deg M=20,\qquad
0\longrightarrow O_X(20O)\longrightarrow H
\longrightarrow O_X(29O)\longrightarrow0.
\]
Both maxima are over geometric line subbundles on X.

If D is the reduced degree-31 radical-evaluation divisor, then
\[
0\longrightarrow\omega_X^3(-D)\longrightarrow H
\longrightarrow\omega_X^2\longrightarrow0.
\]
These canonical connection grades have degrees 17,32 and are not the
HN filtration. The actual compatible split quotient U/ell also gives
\[
0\longrightarrow H\longrightarrow
O_X(30O)\oplus O_X(50O)\longrightarrow\omega_X|_D\longrightarrow0,
\]
and a second presentation
\[
0\longrightarrow O_X(19O)\longrightarrow H
\longrightarrow O_X(30O)\longrightarrow0.
\]
The last extension's actual cubic-character class identifies H(-24O)
with K. The later exact maximum line degree minus four for K proves
stability and the sharper line bound without the old principal-parts
case list or moment recurrence.

This does not force a common line from a degree-one radical-orbit plane:
its Y-descended Frobenius line has degree 24d on a degree-d cover of X,
strictly below the slope 49d/2 of the pulled-back H. Neither original
common-cover problem is resolved.
