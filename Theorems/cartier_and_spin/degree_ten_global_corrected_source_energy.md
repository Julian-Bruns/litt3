# A uniform finite-dimensional energy condition for actual degree ten

Version2,30 September2026. Retain the fixed curve X and both remaining
trace-zero quadratic-derivative degree-ten profiles. In the regular
finite source coordinate write
\[
F=L\phi^2+\phi(\gamma W^3+bW^2+cW+e)+\tau,
\quad\phi=W^5+q,
\]
where L is a nonzero constant times v, v=1 or x-r with P(r)=0,
q=(Q-B0^5)/y^5, and tau is a nonzero constant times t^3. Here B0 is
the original regular finite primitive interpolant, and t is the
fixed cubic support polynomial. Let B be the degree-ten source
field. If its smooth proper model is everywhere etale over X, then
\[
t\left[
\operatorname{Tr}_{B/k(X)}\frac{dw\,dw}{\phi(w)}
-\frac{dq\,d\gamma}{\tau}
-\frac{\gamma\,dq\,d\tau}{\tau^2}
\right]\in H^0(X,\omega_X^2(4O)).
\]
Equivalently, with omega0=dx/(3y^2), the scalar coefficient of this
quadratic differential relative to omega0^2 belongs to L_X(36O).
This is the explicitly known twenty-eight-dimensional space with basis
\[
x^i\ (0\le i\le12),\qquad
x^iy\ (0\le i\le8),\qquad
x^iy^2\ (0\le i\le5).
\]

The condition applies to every constant or linear-v family and every
residual degree, with arbitrary geometric coefficients. It requires
no separate exclusion of fixed critical content or of critical
discriminant collisions. It is a necessary finite-dimensional condition,
not a proof that its parameter locus is empty or that it yields an
actual common cover.
