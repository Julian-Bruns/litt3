Version4, 3 October2026. The quotient and domain theorem is unchanged.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic five. On projective
three-space with coordinates x_i indexed by (Z/2)^2, let G=(Z/2)^4
act by coordinate translations and character signs. Set
\[
S=\sum_i x_i^4,\quad P=x_0x_1x_2x_3,\quad
A=x_0^2x_1^2+x_2^2x_3^2,\quad
B=x_0^2x_2^2+x_1^2x_3^2,\quad
C=x_0^2x_3^2+x_1^2x_2^2.
\]
These five quartics define the finite geometric quotient
\(\pi:\mathbf P^3\to Q\), of degree sixteen, where Q is the
normal threefold in projective four-space with equation
\[
P^2S^2-SABC+A^2B^2+A^2C^2+B^2C^2
-4P^2(A^2+B^2+C^2)+16P^4=0.
\tag{1}
\]
In particular \(\pi^*\mathcal O_Q(1)=\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^3}(4)\).

and their coefficient Frobenius twists. Its actual relative
Frobenius map V descends to a rational map W between two copies
of this same fixed Q. Five quintics modulo (1) represent W and satisfy
\[
\pi\circ V=W\circ\pi.
\tag{2}
\]

true indeterminacy of V. The intrinsic finite scheme of unstable
bundles, divided by G, has length five. This scheme must not be
confused with the possibly thicker common-zero scheme of the five

finitely and birationally to D, hence is its normalization. Thus
D is an irreducible surface of degree sixteen in projective
