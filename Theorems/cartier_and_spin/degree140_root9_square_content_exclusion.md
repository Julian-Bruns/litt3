# Every root-nine degree140 square-norm point is content-free

Version1,29 September2026. In the original geometric root-nine degree140
chart, a nonzero scale whose regular residual has polynomial-square cubic
norm has no positive fixed affine content. This includes every geometric
ratio, all three labelled endpoints, all three sheets, and the original
unit and degree conditions. It is stronger than the corresponding result
for an actual everywhere-etale degree-ten cover.

The proof excludes all twelve rational-scale graphs forced by parity at
a content point. The three actual-scale graphs were already excluded;
the other nine now have complete exact projection and residue-fibre
exclusions. Across the twelve graphs, all371 retained irreducible base
fibres are checked with their entire H fibres. Scalar contents and
leading-coefficient degenerations are retained.

The content-free square locus remains undecided. This theorem does not
exclude an arbitrary common cover, an arbitrary admissible line, or the
other residual degrees.
