# An open-family criterion for norm vanishing in every covering degree

Version 2, 24 September 2026. Let k be algebraically closed of
characteristic five. Let P and A be squarefree polynomials of degrees
ten and four, coprime to each other, with P monic. Assume PA^2=Q'
for a polynomial Q of degree nineteen. On X:y^3=P(x), put
theta=dx/y^2, f=Q/y^5 and R=O+x^*div_0(A).

Use the two noninvariant character bases U_i=x^i theta/A,0<=i<=9,
and V_i=x^i y theta/A,0<=i<=6. Write C^2(v)=B v^[1/25] on the
V-space, and put T=B^[25]. Let l=(0,...,0,1), and form seven rows
l_0=l, l_(i+1)=l_i^[25]T. Assume:

1. These seven rows are independent.
2. The operator U -> 4PA'U'-3PA''U-P'A'U modulo A is invertible
   on the four-dimensional space of polynomials of degree at most three.

For every smooth proper connected S, finite separable h:S->X unramified
over R, and q=f+b^5
with div(q)=3E-5G-10h^*O, E<=h^*R reduced, and G effective,
the norm Nm_h(q) is a fifth power. In particular h_*E is divisible
by five. There is no degree, missed-support, torsion or monodromy
restriction on this assertion.

The two conditions are open algebraic conditions on (P,A) within
the locus PA^2 exact. They hold at the fixed endpoint, so define a
nonempty open subfamily. No assertion is made for endpoints where
one of the two conditions fails, or for arbitrary common-cover spans
that have not supplied the required primitive.
