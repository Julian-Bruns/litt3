# Conditional field recognition through covering degree twenty-three

Version1, 27 September2026. Let h1,h2:T->X be finite etale maps from
the same smooth proper connected curve to the fixed genus-nine X,
of common degree at most23. If their embedded second Cartier lines
agree, equivalently the specified13-tensors are proportional, then
their embedded X-function fields agree.

This does not assert that an unmarked common cover supplies such a
shared line or tensor. No unmarked common-cover decision is made.
