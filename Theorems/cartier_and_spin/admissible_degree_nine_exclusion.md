# No admissible line on an actual etale cover of degree at most nine

Version 1, 24 September 2026. On the fixed curve and embedded positive
plane, there is no admissible nontrivial order-five line, in the sense
of [reconstruction](admissible_line_reconstruction.md), on ANY connected
finite etale cover of degree at most nine. No condition is imposed on
the order of its geometric monodromy group.

The returned degree-nine proof excludes every actual degree-nine
primitive missing a finite marked point. Its two-parameter reduction
is eliminated over the entire algebraic closure by polynomial-square
and resultant certificates, including the leading-coefficient boundary.
[The unrestricted norm theorem](uniform_admissible_norm.md) now shows
that a cover of degree at most eleven necessarily misses a finite
marked point. Together these close the previous all-finite-support
gap through degree nine.

This is a one-leg admissible-line exclusion. It does not prove that
an arbitrary common-cover span yields such a line, and does not
exclude admissible lines on all higher covers.
