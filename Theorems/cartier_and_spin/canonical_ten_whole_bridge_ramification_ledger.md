# A whole degree-ten bridge has only uniform local ramification and single folds

Version1, 3 October2026. [Independent whole review PASS](../../Research/audits/OCT03_WHOLE_BRIDGE_RAMIFICATION_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md); the standalone field hypothesis is explicit. No numerical computation is used.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic FIVE. Let B and A be smooth projective connected k-curves with separating maps to a smooth projective k-curve R of degrees TEN and m. Suppose the normalization T of their WHOLE fiber product is connected, T→B is étale, and φ:T→A has only indices ONE or TWO. Require, at every A-point, at most ONE point in its φ-fiber with index TWO; all other points are unramified.

Then every geometric point v∈R has exactly one of the following local types.

1. All A/R completions above v are unramified. The B/R fiber has profile (TWO,ONE⁸), and its unique quadratic local extension is tame.
2. Every completed field above v in A and B is the SAME finite Galois extension of k((r_v)), of degree e dividing TEN. The φ-fibers above all A-points over v are entirely unramified. When e=ONE this is the wholly unramified type.

In particular A/R has uniform Galois completed local fields everywhere, and its single normal closure is étale over A. The fold branch values in R and the A/R branch values are disjoint.

In the retained canonical-ten source packet, the one-fold-per-fiber hypothesis follows from the actual fixed-endpoint normal form: the reduced different q*P₀ maps bijectively onto the ordinary G-orbit in Γ over f(P₀), a third value distinct from the weak and tame quotient values. Thus the lemma applies whenever B×_RΓ is the WHOLE actual connected degree-ten base change.

For the split joint-degree-TEN bridge B=C₀ of genus EIGHTY ONE, R=P¹, [T:B]=m, N=EIGHTY m and g(Γ)=FOUR m+ONE. There are exactly EIGHTY distinct single-fold branch values of B/R. The uniform part of its different has degree ONE HUNDRED, and Γ/R has total different TEN m.

For the nonsplit quadratic-resolvent bridge B=B′ of genus ONE HUNDRED SIXTY ONE, g(R)=g≤NINE, [T:B]=m, N=ONE HUNDRED SIXTY m and g(Γ)=EIGHT m+ONE. There are exactly ONE HUNDRED SIXTY distinct single-fold branch values of B/R. The uniform different has degree TWENTY(9−g), and Γ/R has total different TWO m(9−g).

Consequently at g=NINE, Γ/R is étale. At g=EIGHT, every uniform ramification point is tame, and there are exactly FOUR uniform branch values, each with inertia C₂ and B/R profile TWO⁵.

These are exact constraints on actual bridges. The two endpoint maps remain on T. None of these local types or branch counts constructs a source or excludes the entire remaining bridge.
