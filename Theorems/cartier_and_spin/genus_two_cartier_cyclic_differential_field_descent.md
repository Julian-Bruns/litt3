# One Cartier-cyclic differential descends the actual genus-two map

Version1, 3 October2026. NEW reusable lemma, frozen for focused independent review. No computation is required.

Let k be algebraically closed of odd characteristic p, and let Y be a smooth hyperelliptic genus-two curve with a separable coordinate x, function field k(Y)=k(x,w), and nonzero differential η=dx/w. Suppose its absolute Cartier operator satisfies
\[
\boxed{C_Y\eta=a\eta+b x\eta,\qquad a,b\in k,\quad b\ne0.}
\]
Thus η and Cη generate the canonical pencil. Let T,Z be smooth connected projective curves, and q:T→Y,r:T→Z actual finite separable maps. Suppose a nonzero meromorphic differential γ on Z has
\[
q^*\eta=r^*\gamma.
\]
Then the ACTUAL map q factors uniquely as q=πr for a finite separable π:Z→Y. If q is étale, both r and π are étale. No simultaneous Galois closure or group quotient is assumed.

For the sparse characteristic-five model
\[
Y:w^2=Ax^5+Bx^4-1,\qquad A,B\ne0,
\]
the premise holds for η=dx/w, with a=(3B)^{1/5},b=(2AB)^{1/5}. In particular it applies to the fixed BACKUP model without an additional ordinarity or field-size hypothesis.

This lemma requires descent of the ONE actual pulled differential to the actual intermediate field k(Z). A divisor class, an independently chosen form on another curve or one-leg Jacobian data does not establish that premise.
