Let a finite group G act faithfully on a smooth proper connected curve C over an algebraically closed fieldk. Let
\[
\ell=\operatorname{lcm}_{c\in C}|G_c|,
\qquad D=|G|/\ell.
\]
For a G-invariant Picard class A of positive degree m, the obstruction α(A)∈H²(G,k*) to genuine linearization has finite ordero satisfying
\[
\frac{D}{\gcd(D,m)}\mid o.
\]
This is valid for wild actions and uses actual invariant divisors, not a tame-root-stack description.

Apply it to the actual sixteen-spin source q:T→Y, free Galois of degree8d, whose common spin line L has degree d and G-invariant class. Then EIGHT divides the order of α(L). If L=φ*M for a G-equivariant spin carrier, its obstruction equals α(M), so EIGHT also divides the order of the target spin obstruction.

Because L16≅ωT and the canonical line has a genuine G-linearization, this order also divides SIXTEEN. Thus the actual spin obstruction has exact order EIGHT or SIXTEEN. Every nonzero G-invariant actual section space W⊂H0(T,L) of dimensionr inherits the same projective multiplier, and its determinant gives
\[
\operatorname{ord}\alpha(L)\mid r.
\]
In particular8 dividesr; if the obstruction has order16 then16 dividesr. This is a fixed-rank constraint, not a uniform bound onr.
