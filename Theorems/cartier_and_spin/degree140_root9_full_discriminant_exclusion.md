# The entire root-nine cubic discriminant is excluded

Version1, 28 September2026. Use the fixed r=[9], fixed alpha linear
degree140 source and the original ratio open in the
[cubic atlas](degree140_linear_cubic_cusp_exclusion.md). Write
\[
f(U)=(a_0(q)-sd(q))U^3+b(q)U^2+c(q)U+e(q),\qquad f(u)=0,
\]
and let I be its complete normalized square ideal, retaining the
free nonzero scale and every geometric coefficient extension.

The entire selected-simple companion locus
\[
J=(c^2-4be)u^2+3ceu+2e^2=0
\]
has square ideal equal to the unit ideal, including nilpotents.
Together with the [selected-root ramification exclusion](degree140_root9_ramification_exclusion.md),
this proves
\[
(I,\operatorname{Disc}_U f)=(1).
\]
Equivalently, the homogeneous cubic has three distinct projective
roots at every remaining geometric square point, and its discriminant
is a unit in the whole square coordinate ring.

This is a complete decision of the discriminant stratum only. It does
not decide the discriminant-nonzero square locus, any other P-root
family, or the unmarked common-cover problem. It also does not make
the square subscheme etale over its ratio base. The separate factors
b,e,c^2-4be,c^2-3be were licensed only on J=0, not on its complement.
