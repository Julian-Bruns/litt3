### The proof ingredients worth reusing

For ANY ordinary genus-two Y and double U/Y, A=a*JY is an abelian
surface in JU and the elliptic Prym P satisfies P intersect A=P[2].
The Raynaud divisor restricts to an effective degree-eight divisor on P.
Its mandatory Verschiebung contribution is four distinct nonzero
five-torsion points when P is ordinary, or multiplicity four at zero
when P is supersingular. The zero A-fiber is good by the C2 character
decomposition and Raynaud's theorem on Y.

Two distinct bad A-fibers would exhaust degree eight on P and leave
mandatory contribution unaccounted for. There is therefore AT MOST ONE
bad fiber among all JU/A points. Symmetry makes its quotient class
nonzero two-torsion, represented on P by exact-order-four points.
This is a finite, uniform test for ALL translates.

An actual F5 pair with ordinary Y and Frobenius acting as a three-cycle
on P[2] cannot have that unique bad fiber. The certified equations are

    f=t(t-1), g=(t-2)(t^3+t+1),
    Y: y^2=fg, U: u^2=f, v^2=g.

[Full example and certificate](EXPLICIT_ORDINARY_GENUS_TWO_DOUBLE_WITH_FROBENIUS_CYCLIC_PRYM_TWO_TORSION.md).
This verifies ONE double of that Y, not all fifteen doubles of that
same explicit curve. Irreducibility of the finite degree-15
double-character moduli cover, plus closedness of the finite bad locus,
then yields the stated nonempty open V where ALL fifteen doubles work.
There is no countable intersection or very-general-point argument.
