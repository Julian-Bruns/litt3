\]
In the [centered moving-origin coordinates](../quotient_geometry/wild140_moving_coarse_map_family.md), suppose
\[
y_0^2=w^5+qw^4+s,\qquad
F=A_3(w)+y_0(w-b),\qquad \deg A_3=3.
\]
The normalization of the nonzero linear odd part by a scalar is harmless. Then this carrier is impossible, for every b. In particular no alignment of the zero of the odd part with the center is assumed.

The proof works for EVERY smooth model y₀²=w⁵+qw⁴+s with q,s≠0, independently of the parameter degree or endpoint. The cubic horizontal equations force the zero of the odd part to be the center and F=w(y₀+aw²),a²=q; this function has a zero of order five. This excludes the whole moving-origin cubic slice, and no other Weierstrass origin or low-degree even part. The common-cover problem remains unsolved.
