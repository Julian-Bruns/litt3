# Audit: canonical Poisson rings and the etale condition

Date: 2026-09-05.
Auditor: /root/canonical_poisson_etale_finiteness_check.
Verdict: PASS, including the added base-point criterion, low-weight
compatibility checks, and characteristic-five center statement.
Scope: mathematical proof check of
[the finished note](../CANONICAL_POISSON_RINGS_RETAIN_EXACTLY_THE_ETALE_CONDITION.md)
under its stated assumptions. No proof-file edits were made.

The degree-one homogeneous localization really is the space of rational
differentials: high canonical powers recover the function field, and a
nonzero global one-form supplies an invertible degree-one element.
The extended bracket identity forces both separability and the exact
differential multiplier. Thus Theorem 1 excludes inseparable maps and
spurious degreewise scalings. The displayed symplectic sign convention
is consistent with the local formula.

Theorem 2 is correct. Etale pullback gives a finite section module by
projection formula and ampleness. At any zero of the differential,
evaluation annihilates the ideal generated by all positive-degree
images; global generation in arbitrarily high degrees makes the
quotient infinite-dimensional. This contradicts module finiteness.
The argument uses zeros of the differential and therefore includes wild
ramification. Global generation of the third canonical power proves
the added base-point criterion without any further hypothesis.
The common-cover corollary follows exactly as stated.

Checking the bracket on homogeneous algebra-generator pairs suffices:
the difference of the two bracket expressions is a biderivation
relative to the proposed algebra homomorphism. Generators of degrees
at most three give identities of weights at most seven. The existing
qualification that the ring relations must already be satisfied is
essential and correctly included. The generation bound agrees with
Chapter 2 of [Voight--Zureick-Brown](https://jvoight.github.io/articles/stacky-canonical-rings-final-fixederrata.pdf),
including its hyperelliptic cases.

Proposition 4 is correct. Homogeneous centrality extends to the
homogeneous localization. Testing against rational functions forces
the weight to be divisible by five; testing against the rational
one-form forces the coefficient into the fifth-power subfield.
Valuations of a rational pluriform multiply by five under fifth
powers, so regularity of the power gives regularity of its root.
Additivity of fifth powers handles nonhomogeneous central elements.
Perfectness of the stated base field gives finite generation over the
fifth-power subring, and the function field of the canonical ring has
fifth-power index 25. Generic rank, rather than freeness at the vertex,
is the correct conclusion.

Nonbreaking remarks:

- The base-point criterion could use weight one: the canonical bundle
  of a smooth projective curve of genus at least two is globally
  generated. Weight three remains a valid convenient choice.
- The bracket attribution matches Proposition 1.1 of
  [Enriquez--Odesskii](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0112148).
  The note correctly distinguishes that characteristic-zero source
  from its own characteristic-five verification.
- These are exact reconstruction and finiteness criteria, not a
  restriction excluding two embeddings into a common canonical ring.
  The note states this limitation explicitly and does not claim a
  Litt3 obstruction or historical novelty.
