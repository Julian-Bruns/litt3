# Every remaining actual degree140 scale satisfies a monic quadratic

Version1,30 September2026. Retain the actual-source hypotheses, the
primitive chart and the notation of
[the divided endpoint traces](degree140_divided_endpoint_traces.md).
Exclude the unique leading-cubic line by
[the complete line exclusion](degree140_uniform_monic_cubic_actual_trace.md).

For the seven multipliers
\[
t,\ tx,\ tx^2,\ tx^3,\ ty,\ tx^4,\ txy,
\]
the divided traces have respective degree bounds3,4,5,5,6,6,6.
Four constant row operations with invertible leading pivots leave three
quadratic necessary equations. Their leading coefficients have no common
geometric zero on the allowed parameter chart.

Consequently the remaining parameter chart is covered by three open sets
on each of which every actual scale satisfies a monic quadratic. The
affine common trace scheme, allowing the zero scale for this statement,
is finite over the parameter chart with fibre length at most two.
Its nonzero-scale open subset is therefore quasi-finite. Where the three quadratic coefficient rows have
rank two, the common scale, if it exists, is unique and reduced.

This is a bound on the necessary scale locus. It neither excludes the
joint locus nor constructs either map of a common cover.
