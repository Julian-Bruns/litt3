# All effective orbifold quotients of the fixed genus-nine curve

Let \(X\) be the [fixed curve](../Definitions/Def_fixed_pair.md) over
\(\overline{\mathbf F}_5\). Every representable finite étale atlas
\(X\to\mathcal S\) of a smooth proper connected effective
Deligne–Mumford orbifold curve satisfies \(\deg(X/\mathcal S)\le336000\).
Wild stabilizers and non-Galois atlases are allowed.

Consequently, for every smooth projective connected hyperbolic curve
\(Y/k\), every jointly minimal cored finite bi-étale span
\(X\leftarrow Z\to Y\) has \(\deg(Z/Y)\le336000\). For each fixed
\(h\ge2\), only finitely many isomorphism classes of genus-\(h\) curves
\(Y\) admit a cored common cover with \(X\).

For every coreless finite bi-étale span involving \(X\) and any such
\(Y\), the pulled-back regular one-form spaces have zero intersection,
including the Cartier-zero case. No ordinarity, simplicity, or Hom-zero
hypothesis on \(Y\) is imposed.

An endpoint assertion used here is also retained: every nonzero regular
Cartier-zero form on \(X\) whose zeros have one common positive
multiplicity has only simple zeros.

These conclusions neither exclude coreless spans nor decide existence
of bounded cored spans. [Proof](../Solutions/Sol_fixed_x_orbifold_bound.md).
