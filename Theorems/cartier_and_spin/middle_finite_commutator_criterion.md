# Exact finite commutator criterion for the middle source

Version1,26 September2026. Let k be algebraically closed, of arbitrary
characteristic, and let U,V be n-by-n matrices over k, with n>=1.
Put C=UV-VU. The following conditions are equivalent:

1. U and V have a common eigenvector.
2. The intersection of the kernels of [U^i,V^j], for
   1<=i,j<=n-1, is nonzero; for n=1 the intersection is k.
3. The stack of matrices C U^a V^b, for a,b>=0 and a+b<=n-1,
   has rank less than n.

More generally, over any commutative coefficient ring, the row module
generated by Cw for all words w in U,V of length at most d equals the
row module generated by C U^a V^b with a+b<=d.

Apply this to the exact block form in
[the middle-source theorem](middle_common_eigenvector_boundary.md):
\[
H(b)=\begin{pmatrix}b_0I_{15}+U(z)\\b_1I_{15}+V(z)\end{pmatrix},
\qquad z=(b_2,\ldots,b_9).
\]
For any geometric z!=0, there exist b0,b1 and s!=0 with H(b)s=0
if and only if the1800-by15 stack
\[
S_{14}(z)=\operatorname{stack}_{a+b\le14}[U(z),V(z)]U(z)^aV(z)^b
\]
has rank less than15. It has120 blocks and entries of degree at most16.
The alternative power-commutator stack has2940 rows and degree at most28.
For z=0, H(b) has no nonzero kernel unless b0=b1=0 as well.

This is an exact geometric criterion, eliminating the two scalar
source variables from the existence test. It is not a proof that the
remaining determinantal locus is empty. It gives no bound on polynomial
left-inverse degrees, no generic-specialization shortcut, and no strict
return or common-cover conclusion.
