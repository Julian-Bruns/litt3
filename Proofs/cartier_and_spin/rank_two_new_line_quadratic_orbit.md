# Proof: rank-two new lines and their quadratic kernel orbit

This integrates the second response in the returned message (the
reply to request3). All maps are the original maps on the first
Y-Galois closure. Repetitions are removed only when counting
distinct lambda lines. Equal lambda lines give equal positive
planes and equal R_i.

## Branch support and pairwise incidences

Write R_i^circ=R_i-q^*C. It is reduced of degree(13-8c)d.
At one of its points choose a horizontal frame of F^*q^*S with
evaluations f dz,g dz, f a unit and ord g=2. Its raw Wronskian
has order one. Every used point is therefore a simple zero of W_S.

If c=1, saturation and base evaluation order two give the fiber
primitive plane <[z^3],[z^4]> at C. Horizontal frame evaluations
have leading orders2,3, hence raw Wronskian order4. The residual
W_S avoids C. It has degree two and at least one used, simple point,
so both its points are distinct.

Distinct lambda_i,lambda_j have determinant divisor Gamma_ij in
q^*det S of degree4d. Where both R_i^circ and R_j^circ vanish,
the two fiber directions coincide in the kernel of a surjective
evaluation. Hence their reduced intersection, transported to the
first twist, is at most Gamma_ij. Its degree is at most4d.
Over C the entire evaluation plane vanishes, so this implication
does NOT apply.

The complete deck orbit gives constant incidence numbers k_y on
each q-fiber. Counting incidences and pairs proves the two formulas
in the statement. For c=0 and n used points, Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
169M^2/(64n)-13M/8\le M(M-1)/2.
\]
If n<=4, then M<=288/41<8. If n=5, then M<=40.
At least five points are simple in a degree-six divisor, which
forces the remaining point to be simple as well. At M=40,n=5
equality forces k_y=13 and equality for every pairwise collision
divisor; this is a numerical equality case, not a clump.

## Squared-line trace

The direct image q_*(lambda_0^2) is strongly semistable of slope
-1/2: after the actual étale Galois pullback it splits into
equal-degree conjugate lines. Three distinct squares in Sym^2
of a two-space are independent in characteristic five. Thus its
rank-three trace image has degree at least ceil(-3/2)=-1.
The target Sym^2 S has degree zero, proving colength at most one.
Original generation supplies at least two directions everywhere;
three distinct directions generate the symmetric square fiber.

## The intrinsic multiplication map

Let R_V=F_V^*F_(V*)O_V, with augmentation ideal I. Unit and
augmentation canonically identify F_V^*B_V with I. In an étale
coordinate it is O[epsilon]/epsilon^5 with I=(epsilon).
Evaluation is I->I/I^2 and its kernel is I^2.
This identification does not assert that multiplication is
horizontal for the quotient's Cartier connection.

Multiplication restricted to F^*S gives m_S. If its frame elements
have coefficients (a1,a2,a3,...) and (b1,b2,b3,...) in epsilon,
the matrix into (epsilon^2,epsilon^3,epsilon^4) is
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
a1^2&a1b1&b1^2\\
2a1a2&a1b2+a2b1&2b1b2\\
a2^2+2a1a3&a1b3+a2b2+a3b1&b2^2+2b1b3
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its determinant is (a1b2-a2b1)^3, checked symbolically by the
[independent source](../../scripts/arithmetic/check_flag_reply_algebra.py).
For evaluations f dz,g dz this is ((fg'-gf')/2)^3.
The raw Wronskian divisor is4C+W_S, proving the determinant formula.
Its degree18 equals deg I^2, while the source has degree zero.

Generically a canonical lambda represented by [z] has positive
plane <[z],[z^2]>. To verify uniqueness, a Lagrangian containing
[z] has other primitive s z^2+t z^3, and its canonical line has
primitive4s^2z+2stz^2+2t^2z^3. Equality with [z] forces t=0.
The evaluation kernel is therefore generated by (delta z)^2.

At an R_i point, a local primitive of lambda can be chosen z=t^3.
Modulo epsilon^5 its squared increment is
\[
(3t^2\epsilon+3t\epsilon^2+\epsilon^3)^2
=3t^3(3t\epsilon^2+\epsilon^3).
\]
The last parenthesis is primitive in the saturated plane's kernel.
This proves the exact saturation divisor3R_i; away from R_i
the square is primitive. The degrees give19d-(-20d)=39d.
Three distinct lines and the generic isomorphism m_S then prove
the full rank-three kernel span. The individual saturations explain
why the colength18 of m_S does not contradict the earlier trace
colength-at-most10.

## Pairing information and scope

If S is isotropic, pairing gives a nonzero map
P_i/lambda_i -> (q^*S/lambda_i)^vee tensor omega, of degrees9d
and14d. It cannot be zero, since then P_i would lie in the
degree-zero saturated plane q^*S. Its zero divisor has degree5d.
Composing ell_i->P_i/lambda_i adds exactly the known degree12d
divisor h_i^*Delta_X.

If S is nonisotropic, its pairing vanishes at C in the c=1 case
because the fiber <[z^3],[z^4]> is isotropic. The old simultaneous
modification q^*S-perp -> N_(lambda_i) has length16d and cyclic
torsion over q^*D_S. Intersect its degree9d HN line with that
subbundle. Semistability forces a loss of at least9d; the total
modification bounds the loss by16d.

None of these comparisons makes an individual contact divisor
descend through h_i. The remaining cases are geometrically open.
