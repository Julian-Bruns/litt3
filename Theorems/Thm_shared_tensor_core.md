# Shared simple-root tensors force a core

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p>=5. Any actual finite
etale span X <- Z -> Y of smooth projective connected hyperbolic curves
whose nonzero regular weight-d tensors have equal pullbacks and divisors
dD_X,dD_Y with D_X,D_Y reduced HAS A CORE, for every integer d>=1.
This is a consequence of the canonical-quotient theorem.

Separately, in characteristic p>=3 a cored bi-etale span with a shared
nonzero Cartier-fixed regular one-form has a positive-genus coarse core
if every zero on its source has order strictly less than p-2.

Consequently, in p>=5 endpoints with Hom(JX,JY)=0 cannot share a regular
one-form having simple zeros and nonzero Cartier eigenvalue in an actual
bi-etale span. A rational core alone would not suffice for this conclusion.
