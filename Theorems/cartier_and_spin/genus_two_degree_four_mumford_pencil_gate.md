# The inverse line and complete pencil of a degree-four Mumford divisor

Version1, 3 October2026. [Fresh independent static audit PASS](../../Research/audits/OCT03_GRAM_FOUR_DZERO_DIVISOR_CHART_AND_MUMFORD_GATE_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md).
This is a general divisor theorem. Its application to the full Cartier
parameter family is PARTIAL: an actual exact line and the following
graph hypotheses must first be established.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic different from two,
Y:W²=H(X) with H squarefree of degree five, and P its point at infinity.
Let U be monic of degree four, degV≤3 and U∣H−V². Suppose the graph
C=(U,W−V) is an effective affine degree-four divisor with no
hyperelliptic conjugate pair and no repeated branch point. Repeated
unramified points with their graph multiplicities are allowed.
Put Q=O_Y(3P−C) EXACTLY and d=[X³]V. Then:

1. Q⁻¹ is effective if and only if d=0.
2. The COMPLETE two-dimensional space H0(O(C−P)) has basis
   \[
   h=(W+V)/U,\qquad g=Xh-d.
   \]
3. If d≠0, this pencil is base-point-free and g/h has degree three.
   If d=0, it has exactly one fixed point ιE, where E is the residual
   degree-one divisor in div(W−V)=C+E−5P; its moving map is X,
   of degree two.

For an application after relative Frobenius, use the ACTUAL Y₁ and
EXACT descended Q. A norm polynomial does not choose the sheet, and
agreement after fifth pullback does not fix the descended fifth-torsion
class. Inverse-sheet pivots, conjugate-pair loci and branch multiplicities
outside the stated graph remain separate cases. This theorem supplies
no actual finite source or endpoint map.
