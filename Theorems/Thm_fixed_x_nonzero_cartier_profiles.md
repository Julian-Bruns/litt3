# Two zero profiles for the nonzero-Cartier coreless branch

Let X be the fixed genus-nine curve. Suppose an ACTUAL finite etale
span X <-f- Z -g-> Y of smooth projective connected hyperbolic curves
is coreless, and its canonical-ring intersection is k[s] with primitive
weight d. Assume its eligible generalized Cartier image is NONZERO.

Then d is 2 or 4, and exactly one of the following profiles holds,
where D_X,D_Y are reduced effective divisors:

| profile | div(s_X) | deg D_X | div(s_Y) | deg D_Y |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| half-weight multiplicity | (d/2)D_X | 32 | (d/2)D_Y | 4g(Y)-4 |
| double-weight multiplicity | 2dD_X | 8 | 2dD_Y | g(Y)-1 |

In particular (d,e) is one of (2,1),(2,4),(4,2),(4,8), where e is
the common positive zero multiplicity. There is no degree, Galois,
ordinarity or Hom-zero assumption on either leg or on Y.

For the active genus-25 Y the two reduced support sizes are 96 and 24.
For a genus-two partner they are 4 and 1. These are necessary profiles,
not realizability assertions or a no-common-cover theorem. This theorem
does not produce a shared tensor or treat the Cartier-zero branch.
