# Nonzero quadratic trace of every remaining Klein-four scalar

Version1, 26 September2026. Retain the actual V4 comparison hypotheses
and canonical labels of
[trace descent](quartic_trace_obstruction.md). Put
K=F_(5^14), F=F_(5^56), L=F_(5^28), and let sigma generate Gal(F/K)
by cycling the root types.

Every actual comparison satisfies
\[
\epsilon^2\notin L.
\]
Equivalently, epsilon has degree four over K and
epsilon+sigma^2(epsilon) is nonzero. Thus epsilon^2 itself generates
F over K. Neither endpoint multiset is invariant under the half-turn
(i,j) -> (i+2,j).

The additional exclusion of epsilon+sigma^2(epsilon)=0 follows
already from the four finite trace equations, with arbitrary x,y in K;
no pole masses, covering degree or remaining curve coefficients are
enumerated. A matching identity reduces the complete endpoint test
to503 pairs, and none satisfies even the old moment equations.

The general quartic scalar with nonzero quadratic trace, other
monodromy cases, and original unmarked common covers remain open.
