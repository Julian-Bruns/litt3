# Cofinal saturation of inherited Raynaud theta directions

Let C be a smooth projective connected curve of genus at least two over
an algebraically closed field of characteristic p>0. Write
B_C=F_*O_C/O_(C^(1)) and let Theta_C be its Raynaud divisor.
There is a connected finite étale Galois cover q:W→C with solvable
group of order prime to p, of degree bounded in terms of p and g(C),
such that

    H^0(W^(1), B_W ⊗ q^(1)*L) ≠ 0
    for every L∈J(C^(1))(k).

Connected finite étale covers with this property form an upward-closed
cofinal collection among all connected finite étale covers of C. The
total cover in this cofinal assertion need not have prime-to-p degree.

For any actual finite bi-étale span X←f—C—g→Y and any prescribed
connected finite étale refinement V→C, there is a connected finite
étale refinement W→V such that, for the composite q:W→C,

    H^0(W^(1), B_W ⊗ (fq)^(1)*L ⊗ (gq)^(1)*M) ≠ 0
    for every (L,M)∈J(X^(1))(k)×J(Y^(1))(k).

This persists under every further connected finite étale refinement.
Both maps remain actual finite étale maps from the same source.
Thus generic vanishing on the fixed endpoint parameter space cannot
hold on a cofinal subsystem, even if the original span is jointly
minimal. The refined presentations need not be jointly minimal; the
assertion does not refute vanishing on jointly minimal spans or on
enlarged parameter spaces using new Jacobian directions.
