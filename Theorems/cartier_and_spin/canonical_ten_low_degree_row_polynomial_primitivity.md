\[

Retain the accepted natural coefficient image R=PSL₄(F_q), q=25^a, and at least ONE actual original component b_i primitive over k(Γ_R), as proved by the [original-component lemma](canonical_ten_original_row_component_primitivity.md). For EVERY integer m with ONE≤m<q and EVERY nonzero constant homogeneous polynomial F of degree m in the FOUR row components, the evaluated section F(b) is nonzero and is primitive of degreeTEN over k(Γ_R) in ANY rational Q^(3m) frame. In particular the normalized projective row image has NO constant homogeneous equation of degree less than q.

This supplies no independence over k(Γ_R), no lower bound on the generic rank of the row as a k(Γ_R)-linear span, and no assertion about polynomials whose coefficients vary over Γ_R. The degree bound m<q is essential to the finite-field interpolation argument; degrees at least q are unaddressed.
