# Proof of original-source half-class and full-linear kernel constraints

This proves the [statement](../../../Theorems/quotient_geometry/local_actions/canonical_ten_source_half_class_and_full_linear_kernel_constraints.md).
Its inputs are actual source geometry and accepted section/subrow results.
Abstract finite-tuple existence is not an input. The two new arguments were
reviewed separately in the
[source audit](../../../Research/notes/oct03_ten_hour/eight_module_source_half_class_audit.md)
and [quotient audit](../../../Research/notes/oct03_ten_hour/eight_module_kernel_etale_quotient_audit.md).

## The original line has the compulsory half-class

Since \(G\) permutes the original infinity divisors, the common line
\(L=\mathcal O_T(H_i)\) is \(G\)-invariant. Transports of this line above
each \(g\in G\) exist, and their composition defects are scalars, because
\(T\) is connected and projective. They define its obstruction
\(\beta\in H^2(G,k^\times)\). Tensor products add obstruction classes.
The specified canonical comparison and the actual paired module give
\[16\beta=0,\qquad
\alpha_W=-2\beta\quad\hbox{or}\quad\alpha_W=-10\beta.
\]
If \(n=\operatorname{ord}\beta\mid16\), either right side has order
\(n/\gcd(n,2)\), since multiplication by five is invertible on a cyclic
group of order dividing sixteen. Its exact order \(d=4\) or \(8\)
therefore gives \(n=2d\). This uses the original invariant line on \(T\),
not a presumed invariant square root on another curve.

## A finite transport group that retains the original line

Let \(E_L\) consist of transports of \(L\) above the ORIGINAL \(G\)-action
whose sixteenth tensor power, under the fixed \(L^{16}=\omega_T\), is
the canonical transport. An arbitrary transport can be scalar-rescaled
to satisfy this condition. There are exactly sixteen choices, as \(k\)
is algebraically closed and its characteristic is prime to sixteen.
Composition preserves the normalization. Hence
\[1\longrightarrow\mu_{16}\longrightarrow E_L\longrightarrow G
\longrightarrow1
\]
is a finite central extension acting on the original line.

Every element of order five in \(G\) has a unique order-five lift. Indeed
an arbitrary lift \(x\) has \(x^5\in\mu_{16}\); multiplying by the unique
central \(z\) with \(z^5=(x^5)^{-1}\) gives that lift. Let \(H_L\) be
generated by the lifts of the prescribed \(a,b\). It surjects onto \(G\).
Any character \(H_L\to k^\times\) kills both generators, because \(k\)
has no nonidentity fifth root of unity, and is thus trivial.

Put \(K_L=H_L\cap\mu_{16}\). The central-extension five-term sequence
injects \(\operatorname{Hom}(K_L,k^\times)\) into \(H^2(G,k^\times)\).
The scalar inclusion of \(K_L\) has transgression \(\beta\), up to inverse
cocycle convention. Its character order is \(|K_L|\); therefore
\[K_L=\mu_{2d}.
\]
No perfection assumption on \(G\) or \(H_L\) is needed here.

## The quotient is a double of the labeled group, not the matrix image

The actual section module carries the genuine \(H_L\)-action obtained
from the original \(G\)-action on \(q^*K\) and the constructed transports
on the inverse \(L^2\) factor. It restricts to the specified invariant
subquotient \(W\). Its central character is \(\zeta\mapsto\zeta^{-2}\),
or \(\zeta\mapsto\zeta^{-10}\) under coefficient Frobenius. Its
determinant is a character of \(H_L\), hence trivial. The matrix
representation \(R_W\) therefore lands in \(SL_8(k)\).

Write the full labeled pullback explicitly as
\[E=\{(g,M)\in G\times SL_8(k):[M]=\rho(g)\},
\]
where \(\rho\) is the original projective representation. The map
\(x\mapsto(g(x),R_W(x))\) has image exactly the accepted group \(H\): its
two generators are the unique order-five lifts of the SAME original
\(a,b\) in this pullback. The determinant-one pullback has kernel
\(\mu_8\), so fifth powers on that kernel also give uniqueness.

An element in the map's kernel lies over the identity of \(G\), hence in
\(K_L=\mu_{2d}\). Its scalar character has kernel \(\mu_2\) in either
presentation, since \(\gcd(2d,2)=\gcd(2d,10)=2\). Thus
\[1\longrightarrow\mu_2\longrightarrow H_L\longrightarrow H
\longrightarrow1.
\]
This extension cannot split. A split central double would be
\(\mu_2\times H\); every element of order dividing five has trivial
\(\mu_2\)-coordinate, so the two prescribed order-five lifts could not
generate the whole product. The possible nontrivial kernel of
\(H\to H_0\) is precisely why this argument cannot replace \(H\) by its
matrix image without further input.

## Odd and central matrix kernels are excluded by central closure

Suppose the ACTUAL matrix image \(H_0\) is centrally closed and put
\(N_0=\ker(H\to H_0)\). If \(|N_0|\) were odd, its preimage \(D_0\) in
\(H_L\) would be a central extension of \(N_0\) by \(\mu_2\).
Coprime averaging splits it. Its complement \(\widetilde N_0\) is
unique, because two splittings differ by a homomorphism \(N_0\to\mu_2\),
which is trivial. In the product \(D_0=\mu_2\times\widetilde N_0\), that
complement is exactly the set of odd-order elements. It is characteristic
in \(D_0\), and hence normal in \(H_L\).

The quotient \(H_L/\widetilde N_0\) is a central extension of \(H_0\) by
\(\mu_2\). Central closure splits it. It remains generated by the two
images of the order-five generators, which cannot generate the central
\(\mu_2\)-factor. This contradiction proves \(2\mid|N_0|\).

If \(N_0\) were central in \(H\), central closure would split
\(H\to H_0\), with \(H=N_0\times H_0\). The projections of the two
order-five generators would generate the abelian group \(N_0\), making
it an elementary abelian five-group, possibly trivial. Its odd order
contradicts the preceding conclusion. Thus \(N_0\) is also noncentral.

The primary input for a full linear matrix image is Steinberg,
*Lectures on Chevalley Groups*, §7, Corollary 2 after Theorem 14:
\(SL_n(F)\) is centrally closed for a finite field \(F\) and \(n\ge5\).
The corollary is on printed page 95, zero-based PDF index 101/viewer page
102 of the [original Yale notes](https://www.math.utah.edu/~ptrapa/math-library/steinberg/steinberg-yale-notes.pdf#page=102).
The audit records the
[downloaded primary PDF](../../../../litt3-computation-data/source-half-class-audit-20261003/steinberg-yale-notes.pdf)
and [visually checked page](../../../../litt3-computation-data/source-half-class-audit-20261003/steinberg-page-index101.png).
This applies in particular to \(SL_8(\mathbf F_{5^m})\), not automatically
to one of its proper subgroups.

## The exact full-SL8 specialization excludes split projective kernels

Now assume all additional generating-subrow hypotheses in the statement,
with \(H_0=SL_8(\mathbf F_5)\), \(Q=PSL_8(\mathbf F_5)\), and \(d=4\).
The labeled scalar kernel \(\ker(H\to G)=\mu_4\) maps isomorphically onto
the full scalar center of \(H_0\). Hence
\[N_0\simeq N=\ker(G\to Q).
\]
Indeed every lift of an element of \(N\) has scalar matrix coordinate;
multiplication by the unique opposite scalar in the labeled kernel puts
it in \(N_0\). Conversely the map \(N_0\to G\) has trivial kernel.
This proves evenness of \(N\).

If \(N\) were central in \(G\), let \(H_N\) be its inverse image in \(H\).
For \(x\in H_N\), \(y\in H\), the commutator has original label one
and matrix coordinate one, because the matrix of \(x\) is scalar.
An element of the labeled pullback is determined by this pair, so the
commutator is identity. Then \(H_N\), and in particular \(N_0\), is
central in \(H\), already excluded. Thus \(N\) is noncentral.

Elementary commutator identities make \(SL_8(\mathbf F_5)\) perfect;
central closure makes its central quotient map to \(Q\) universal.
Equivalently the five-term sequence gives
\[H^2(Q,k^\times)\simeq\operatorname{Hom}(\mu_4,k^\times),
\]
an exponent-four group. The module class on \(G\) is the inflation of the
natural exact order-four class \(\alpha_Q\). If a section \(Q\to G\)
existed, restricting \(2\beta=-\alpha_W\) or \(10\beta=-\alpha_W\)
along it would divide \(\alpha_Q\) by two on \(Q\). Twice any class in
an exponent-four group has order at most two; multiplication by ten
has the same effect there. This is impossible. Thus \(G\to Q\) is
nonsplit. This argument uses the ORIGINAL \(G\), not a root on a quotient
curve.

## The actual free-kernel carrier quotient

Both nontrivial inertia groups on \(\Gamma\) inject into \(Q\), since
their image orders are five and two. Thus \(N\) meets every stabilizer
trivially, so \(\pi:\Gamma\to D=\Gamma/N\) is an actual connected finite
étale \(N\)-torsor. Its weak wild break and tame signature over
\(D/Q=\mathbf P^1\) are unchanged. Hurwitz gives
\(g(D)-1=|Q|/20\). This also follows from the accepted general
[free-kernel quotient result](../../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/canonical_degree_ten_no_a5_quotient.md).

The original action on \(T\) is free, so \(p:T\to C=T/N\) is also an
étale torsor. The equivariant map
\[T\longrightarrow C\times_D\Gamma
\]
is an isomorphism of \(N\)-torsors over \(C\). Therefore the quotient
square is cartesian and \(\phi_C:C\to D\) has separating degree ten.
Its monodromy is a subgroup of \(S_{10}\) containing the full monodromy
of its connected base change \(\phi\); it is thus \(S_{10}\), and the map
is primitive. Étale base change preserves and reflects local different
and ramification index, giving the stated simple folds and different
\(q_C^*P_0\). The quotient \(q_C:C\to Y\) is finite étale and \(Q\)-Galois.
No simultaneous Galois closure has been introduced.

## What the accepted subrow identity transfers

Apply the accepted
[étale subrow theorem](../../../Proofs/cartier_and_spin/rank_three_generating_subrow_etale_and_primitive_field.md)
and [identity theorem](../../../Proofs/cartier_and_spin/rank_three_generating_subrow_identity_reduction.md)
to the specified GENERATING \(V_8\) and its actual adjoint
\(W_8=V_8^{[5]}\). Coefficient Frobenius preserves the projective kernel.
Their normalized adjoint row is \(D_W=C\), whereas the carrier quotient
is \(D=\Gamma/N\). The exact source/Frobenius conventions in those
proofs supply paired scalar descent, the native integral Cartier image,
and the carrier line \(B\) with
\[p^*A_C=L^6,\qquad\pi^*B=M^6,\qquad A_C=\phi_C^*B.
\]
Its degree is \(3|Q|/40\). This use of an actual generating inclusion
cannot be made with only a composition factor.
The field equalities are
\[k(\Gamma)k(C)=k(T),\qquad
k(\Gamma)\cap k(C)=k(D).
\]
They are compatible with a nontrivial étale \(N\)-torsor and do not
identify \(D\) with \(C\), or force \(N=1\).

## The specific square-line descent and its exact Frobenius power

The accepted
[scalar theorem](canonical_ten_eight_source_scalar_and_wild_constraints.md)
gives the compatible genuine \(M^8=\omega_\Gamma\) when \(d=4\).
The paired square \(P=M^2\) has an \(H\)-action with inverse central
character and compatible \(P^4=\omega_\Gamma\). In the coefficient-
Frobenius convention start instead with \(R=M^{10}\) and the genuine
canonical \(S=M^8=\omega_\Gamma\). The scalar theorem supplies the
compatible \(R^4=\omega_\Gamma^5=S^5\). Setting \(P=R\otimes S^{-1}\)
gives, with these actions,
\[P^4=\omega_\Gamma,\qquad P^5=R.
\]
Indeed \(P^5=R^5S^{-5}=R\) by \(R^4=S^5\). This is the exact paired
Frobenius exponent, not just an equality of line degrees.

The matrix coordinates on \(H_N\) are scalar and define
\[\chi:H_N\longrightarrow\mu_4.
\]
Multiply the \(H_N\)-transport on \(P\) by \(\chi\). The inverse central
character cancels, so the corrected transport factors through a genuine
\(N\)-linearization. It is preserved under conjugation by \(H\), because
scalar matrix coordinates are preserved. Étale descent gives \(P_D\)
with \(\pi^*P_D=M^2\). The projective \(Q\)-action is more precisely the
genuine descended \(H_0\)-action: \(\ker(H\to H_0)\) acts trivially on
the descended line, while the scalar center of \(H_0\) acts by the
inverse scalar character. Its obstruction is therefore the inverse
natural class of exact order four.

Since \(\chi^4=1\), fourth powers of the corrected transports are exactly
the canonical transports of \(P^4=\omega_\Gamma\). Étale canonical
descent gives the exact compatible identity
\[P_D^4=\omega_D.
\]
There is no unrecorded line or character twist killed by \(\pi\).

## Full adjunction identifies the actual row line as the cube

Put \(U=\phi^*P\). The original compatible carrier identity gives
\(U^8=\omega_T\) with its genuine action. A discrepancy in transports
under this global isomorphism would be a character of \(H\); all such
characters vanish, as \(H\) is generated by order-five elements.
The adjoint of the ACTUAL paired inclusion is
\(\phi^*R\otimes W_8\to\omega_T\). Its full evaluation-line transport
therefore gives
\[A_T=\omega_T\otimes U^{-5}=U^3=L^6.
\]
This comparison holds for all of \(H_N\), with their base actions; it is
not merely agreement on the central \(\mu_4\).

The basepoint-free actual row supplies its \(N\)-linearization by
cancelling the scalar matrix action on \(W_8\). The correction factor is
\(\chi^{-1}\); when written from the pre-Frobenius presentation it is
\(\chi^{-5}\), the same factor since \(\chi^4=1\). Cubing the corrected
\(P\)-action gives factor \(\chi^3=\chi^{-1}\). Combined with the full
equivariant identity \(A_T=U^3\), the native row action on \(L^6\) is
exactly the pullback of this corrected cubed action on \(M^6\).

The accepted
[higher-row line-comparison proof](../../../Proofs/cartier_and_spin/canonical_ten_higher_trace_row_geometry.md)
defines the \(N\)-action on \(M^6\) by equality after pullback with that
actual source row action. Equality after the surjective \(\phi\) forces
equality of the transports on \(\Gamma\). Thus their descents are the
SAME line, not only lines of the same degree or central character:
\[B=P_D^3,\qquad B^4=\omega_D^3.
\]
This is where the full \(\chi\)-transport comparison is necessary.

## No missing invariant root or endpoint descent is inferred

The order-four obstruction of \(P_D\) is not twice a class on \(Q\), by
the exponent-four computation above. Hence no \(Q\)-invariant square
root \(M_D\) with the required paired action can be supplied. This does
not exclude an underlying nonequivariant square root. The original
invariant \(L\), and its additional half-class, may retain data lost in
the quotient.

If the projective ratios of the specified relation row ON \(\Gamma\)
generate its full function field, every element of \(N\), which fixes
those ratios, is identity. Then \(N=1\), contrary to the source half-class
restriction. This additional generation is not implied by \(D_W=C\),
the joint-field identity or primitivity of \(C\to D\).

The original actual \(q,h_i\) remain literally on the SAME \(T\). Their
descent, the descent of their infinity sections, and descent of original
\(L\) are not proved. The composite \(T\to\Gamma\to D\) preserves the
canonical-square/different identities but has degree \(10|N|\). An
actual even noncentral nonsplit kernel source has neither been excluded
nor constructed. The unmarked common-cover problem remains UNSOLVED.
