# Global traces in the remaining quartic comparison

Version1, 25 September2026. Use the actual simultaneous quotient of
[pole-twelve cubic descent](pole_twelve_cubic_descent.md). Write its
parameter as t, its two degree-n functions as u,v, and its scalar as
epsilon. Put u_infinity=D+3t_infinity, v_infinity=D+3t_zero. For c in
mu_29 define the integer d_c as the degree of D over c, with its
multiplicities, and S_j=sum d_c c^j in characteristic five.

Every such actual quotient has 0<=d_c<=6. For the endpoint sums
C_infinity,M_infinity,C_0,M_0 specified in the proof, and a=[8],
\[
\epsilon(M_0+aS_{-2})=C_\infty+aS_{-6},\qquad
M_\infty+aS_2=\epsilon(C_0+aS_6).
\]
The entire branch in which the residues d_c modulo five are constant
except possibly at one value of c is impossible. Equivalently, there
are no delta in F5 and c_0 in mu_29 for which S_j=delta c_0^j for
j=-6,-2,2,6. This is a geometric exclusion, with repeated endpoint
labels, all quartic monodromy groups, and unrestricted initial scalars.

Consequently n=12,13,185,186 are excluded within the pole-twelve branch;
its remaining degrees are 14..184. At n=183 or184 the quartic monodromy
must be D4 or S4. A cyclic quartic quotient cannot have all its totally
ramified points in D. Every possible scalar epsilon belongs to F_(5^56);
this does NOT bound a model field for the curve or its two functions.

Together with the previously closed comparison poles below twelve,
this extends conditional line/tensor recognition to covering degree
twelve. It does not by itself settle covering degree thirteen, where
comparison pole thirteen is a separate possibility.

No actual quotient in the remaining degrees is constructed or excluded
by these necessary trace equations alone. The unmarked common-cover
problems remain open.
