# A small-area cubic-tensor action in characteristic five is tame

Version1,3 October2026. Independent whole review: [PASS](../../../Research/audits/Q0_COINCIDENT_CLOSURE_AND_CUBIC_WILD_AREA_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). No computation is used.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic FIVE, let C be a smooth projective connected curve, and let H be a finite group acting faithfully on C. Suppose H fixes exactly a nonzero meromorphic cubic differential T, and every local order of T belongs to {minusTWO,ZERO,EIGHT}. For every wild branch point of the actual Galois quotient C→C/H, its local different exponent δ and inertia order e satisfy
\[
\delta/e\ge16/5.
\]
Consequently the entire action is tame whenever
\[
2(g(C)-1)/|H|<6/5.
\]
The conclusion concerns actual Galois completed local extensions of this group action. No assertion follows merely from a non-Galois local ramification index, a tensor fixed only up to scalar, or an assumed simultaneous completion.
