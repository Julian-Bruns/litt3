# Cartier eigenforms on the fixed curve and vanishing of shared one-forms

Version2,2026-09-14.

Let \(X\) be the [fixed genus-nine curve](../../Definitions/fixed_pair.md)
over \(k=\overline{\mathbf F}_5\), and let \(C\) denote ordinary Cartier.
Every \(0\ne\omega\in H^0(X,\omega_X)\) with
\(C(\omega)=\lambda\omega\), \(\lambda\in k^\times\), has sixteen
simple zeros, none at \(O\).

For ANY ordinary smooth projective connected curve \(Y\) of genus at
least two with \(\operatorname{Hom}(J_X,J_Y)=0\), every actual finite
bi-étale span of smooth projective connected curves
\(X\xleftarrow f Z\xrightarrow g Y\) satisfies
\[
f^*H^0(X,\omega_X)\cap g^*H^0(Y,\omega_Y)=0.
\]
No degree, joint minimality, or corelessness assumption is required.
This does not assert nonexistence of the span.
