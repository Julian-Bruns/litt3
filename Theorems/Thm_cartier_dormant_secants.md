# Quadratic Cartier tensors as pairs and tangent directions of dormant opers

Let C be a smooth projective connected curve over an algebraically closed
field of characteristic five. A projective connection is written locally
as u''=r u, with change-of-coordinate rule
r_t=(x')^2 r_x−{x,t}/2. It is dormant exactly when r''=3r^2.
For a nonzero regular quadratic differential s=a(dt)^2, put

    r_0=a''/a,              r_−=r_0−a,              r_+=r_0+a.

These expressions define rational projective connections, independently
of t. Their exact classification is:

* C_1(s^3)=0 iff r_0 is dormant. Then a''=r_0 a is its infinitesimal
  dormant-deformation equation.
* C_1(s^3)=s iff r_− and r_+ are dormant. Conversely every ordered pair
  of distinct rational dormant connections gives this normalized tensor
  by a=(r_+−r_−)/2.
* In either case the indicated connections are regular everywhere iff
  every positive zero multiplicity of s is 0 or 1 modulo five.

Thus unordered pairs of distinct REGULAR dormant connections correspond
bijectively to nonzero-Cartier projective quadratic eigenforms whose zero
orders are 0 or 1 modulo five. Nonzero tangent directions at regular
dormant connections correspond bijectively to Cartier-zero quadratics
with that zero-order condition. These constructions commute with actual
finite etale pullback. They do not turn dormancy into a core criterion.

For any actual coreless bi-etale span, at most TWO distinct regular dormant
projective connections descend through both endpoints to its common source.
For a span involving the fixed X, their scheme-theoretic intersection is
also reduced. Moreover a primitive shared quadratic tensor with Cartier
image zero, if present, must have uniform zero multiplicity FOUR and
reduced X-support of size EIGHT; the simple-zero case is excluded.

The general correspondence makes the completed fixed-X oper census usable
for the simple-zero quadratic branch: its projective eigenform pool is a
subset of the 420195555 unordered pairs of the28990 distinct opers. No
pairwise enumeration, compatible two-endpoint pair, or emptiness is claimed.
The no-invariant and other-weight Cartier-zero branches remain open.
