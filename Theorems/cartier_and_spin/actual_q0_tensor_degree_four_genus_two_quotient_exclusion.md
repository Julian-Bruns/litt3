# The whole genus-two degree-four tensor quotient is excluded

Version1,3 October2026. Independently accepted in the [whole genus-two audit](../../Research/audits/Q0_FOUR_GENUS_TWO_WHOLE_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). No computation is used beyond the separately documented tiny branch Sidon assertion.

An actual jointly minimal q0-tensor self-span of degree FOUR cannot have a genus-TWO joint x-curve B in its cubic-index-THREE alternative. This excludes BOTH Weierstrass and non-Weierstrass positions of the common simple infinity pole Q.

The result preserves the two actual finite étale maps to the fixed X from the SAME smooth projective source. An original Y-leg stays on that source and is not replaced by a B-map. This is not all-degree tensor recognition and not a solution of the original common-cover problem. The genus-ONE degree-four quotient remains open; the genus-ZERO case has a separate exclusion.

Inputs are the [Weierstrass-Q exclusion](actual_q0_tensor_degree_four_weierstrass_simple_pole_exclusion.md) and the [non-Weierstrass common square-class reduction](actual_q0_tensor_degree_four_nonweierstrass_square_class_reduction.md). The new step excludes every remaining common cubic-root subset.
