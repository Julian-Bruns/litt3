# Finite leading support in the homogeneous half b-zero boundary

ID: `admissible_degree_ten_m9_half_bzero_finite_leading_support`.
Version1, 2 October2026. Fixed quadratic certificate; whole-implication
[audit PASS](../../Research/audits/M9_HALF_BZERO_EXCLUSION_AUDIT_2026_10_02.md).

Retain the actual primitive degree-ten m9 source, both original actual
étale maps, $\rho=0$, $m_5\in L_9$, $B=0$, and
$\delta_2\le12$. Use the canonical full-source scalar normalization
with monic selected cubic $t$ and raw remainder $t^3$; it multiplies
the defining polynomial by a constant and preserves the original
source functions and maps. Write the actual critical leading coefficient
$d=pq_3+rq$, $p\ne0$, and the short coefficient
$\delta_2=A+yb+3y^2\operatorname{rem}(Zd,P)/P$.

Suppose $b=0$. The accepted nonzero quadratic trace gives
$n_0=\lambda q_3$, $\lambda\ne0$. If $V$ is the coefficient of $y$
in $\eta_2/\lambda$, then, for EACH of the four possible selected
omissions, the actual tuple $(p,r,V)$ belongs to a fixed set of
exactly two necessary linear-compatibility candidates. In particular
there are at most TWO possible actual leading-coefficient pairs
$(p,r)$ per omission.

This conclusion includes repeated leading roots and leading roots on
$P$ or $t$. It does not invert $d$ at any endpoint. The fixed quadratic
candidate algebra is reduced; $p$ is a unit in it. The three-by-two
coefficient matrix has rank two for EVERY value of $V$, so no
rank-drop family is omitted. The actual leading scale $p$ is retained.

Other source coefficients, first-pair moments and the remaining source
constant parameter are not excluded or proved finite. Two candidates
are not two actual sources, and the union over omissions is not a
common-cover solution.
