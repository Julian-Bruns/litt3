# The root-nine critical cover and its primitive square-scale budget

Version1, 29 September2026. Use the actual fixed-r=[9] degree140
source and its original ratio open in the
[six-curve statement](degree140_root9_six_scale_curves.md). This concerns
all allowed ratios, not only the six curves already excluded. Put
\[
\Delta=\frac{4G_3^2+3G_2G_4}{y^6}\in k(X).
\]
This function is regular on affine X, has exact pole32 at O, and is
nonsquare at every allowed geometric ratio. An explicit identity using
only three high coefficient equations proves nonsquareness. Thus the
critical double cover has a connected smooth normalization C with
\[
17\le g(C)\le33.
\]

Let v=x-[9], let \(\bar f_\lambda\) and \(\bar D\) be the actual
polynomials after Z=yW-B0 and division by y10 and y4, respectively, and
write
\[
e(\lambda)=\frac{\operatorname{Res}_{(10,2)}(\bar f_\lambda,\bar D)}{t^5v}
=d_0+d_1\lambda+d_2\lambda^2.
\]
All three coefficients are regular on affine X. The original residual
is their cubic norm, up to a nonzero ratio scalar. A ratio where this
quadratic has zero discriminant carries no square scale.

Call a ratio primitive if d0,d1,d2 have no common affine zero on X.
At EVERY primitive allowed ratio, the full geometric square-scale locus
has at most12 distinct points and its normalized scheme has length at
most21, including nilpotents. Scale is arbitrary and nonzero. These
bounds improve the previously available bounds on this primitive part;
they are not emptiness assertions.

More precisely, at such a ratio with nonzero scale discriminant the
critical scale map C->P1 has degree140 and has two poles of order4
above O. It has exactly ten odd poles: one above each of the nine
points over t=0, and one above ([9],0). Its finite different has degree
at most2g(C)+211. The plane normalization has at least30 units of
delta-invariant on the scale-infinity fibre. These facts imply the
distinct-point bound. The original discriminant in x has degree at
most1541 in scale and is divisible by lambda24; its order along the
square scheme is at least70, giving the length bound.

Primitivity is an explicit ADDITIONAL hypothesis in the scale-budget
part. It has not been proved for the whole linear family, and the
constant-family positive-content exclusion is not imported into it.
No common cover is excluded or constructed by this theorem.
