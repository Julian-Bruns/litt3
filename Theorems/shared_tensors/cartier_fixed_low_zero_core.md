# Low-zero Cartier-fixed forms force a positive-genus core

Let \(k\) be algebraically closed of characteristic \(p\ge3\). A cored
finite bi-étale span \(X\leftarrow Z\to Y\) of smooth projective
connected hyperbolic curves with a shared nonzero Cartier-fixed
regular one-form has a positive-genus coarse core if every zero on
the source has order strictly less than \(p-2\).

Consequently, for \(p\ge5\), endpoints with
\(\operatorname{Hom}(J(X),J(Y))=0\) cannot share a regular one-form
having simple zeros and nonzero Cartier eigenvalue in an actual
bi-étale span. The later
[canonical marked quotient theorem](../quotient_geometry/canonical_marked_quotient.md)
first supplies the core from the reduced zero marking; the present
lemma shows that its genus is positive. A rational core alone does
not yield the Jacobian contradiction.
