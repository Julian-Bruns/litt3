# Symmetry quotients of triangle orbifolds

Version1,2026-09-13. Bounded independent argument audit PASS.

Let k be algebraically closed, and let a,b>=2 with6ab invertible in k.
Write P1(r,s,t) for the effective root stack at three distinct points
with the indicated inertia orders. There are representable finite
etale maps

    P1(a,a,b) -> P1(2,a,2b),    degree2;
    P1(a,a,a) -> P1(3,3,a),     degree3;
    P1(a,a,a) -> P1(2,3,2a),    degree6.

They are the quotients by a transposition, a3-cycle, and the full S3
of equally labeled points. Thus any actual curve atlas of a source
orbifold composes to an actual atlas of the target, with degree
multiplied by2,3 or6. No Galois hypothesis on that curve atlas is needed.
