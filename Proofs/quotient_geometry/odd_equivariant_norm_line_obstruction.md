# Proof: the actual finite norm multiplies the scalar cocycle

Version1,3 October2026. [Root whole-scope review PASS](../../Research/audits/RANK_THREE_SCALAR_TARGET_AND_TAME_IDENTITY_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). See the [exact statement](../../Theorems/quotient_geometry/odd_equivariant_norm_line_obstruction.md). No computation is used.

Choose line lifts for the invariant class M. Their composition defects are a scalar TWO-cocycle c(g,h), representing α. Properness and connectedness of D ensure these discrepancies are in k×. The finite map f between smooth curves is flat, so f_*M and f_*O_D are locally free of rank a. Use the genuine determinant norm
\[
\operatorname{Nm}_f(M)=\det(f_*M)\otimes\det(f_*O_D)^{-1}.
\]
Equivariance of f pushes each chosen line lift to f_*M. A scalar discrepancy c acts on this rank-a bundle by c times the identity, and on its determinant by c^a. The structure sheaf O_D has its natural genuine group action, as does the denominator determinant. Hence the induced lifts on Nm_f(M) have obstruction EXACTLY aα. This identity does not require a faithful action on P¹ or a separable map f.

Every line on P¹ is O(d). Its A-action is induced by the given homomorphism A→PGL₂(k). Choose arbitrary GL₂ lifts of those projective transformations. The determinant of their composition defects is the square of the scalar cocycle, so this cocycle is killed by TWO in H²(A,k×). The standard action on O(ONE), and every tensor power O(d), has obstruction killed by TWO. Additional choices of lifts differ only by a scalar cochain and do not change this conclusion. Kernel elements of A→PGL₂ may be assigned the identity lift, so no injectivity hypothesis is hidden here.

The norm line is O(d) for some d. Its obstruction aα is therefore killed by TWO, proving TWO aα=ZERO. If a is odd, multiplication by a is an automorphism on every finite TWO-primary subgroup, so the TWO-primary order of α is at mostTWO. In particular it cannot be FOUR orEIGHT. The argument also applies after pulling the cocycle back to an original source group.
