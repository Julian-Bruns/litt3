# The remaining degree140 square loci are finite

Version1, 26 September2026. Retain every source equation, pole condition,
normalization and geometric parameter of
[the nonzero-pivot reduction](degree140_nonzero_pivot_reduction.md).
There are eleven families, with v=1 or v=x-r for one of the ten roots
of P. The previously proved exceptional-pivot and coefficient-boundary
exclusions remain in force.

For each family, the complete remaining degree140 square locus has
finitely many geometric points. This excludes positive-dimensional
fixed-q fibers as well as components dominating the q-line. It does
not assert that the finite scheme is reduced, empty, or enumerated.

For each linear-v family there are at most60 geometric square scales
over any allowed ratio pair (H,q). The double leading-scale degeneracy
has exactly147 geometric ratio pairs, presented by three squarefree
degree49 algebras. Every scale over all these pairs is excluded.
These147 pairs are not an exhaustive support list for the square locus;
other H-values over their q-coordinates have not been excluded.

For the constant family a complete residual polynomial and an equivalent
70-equation square circuit have been reconstructed and verified. Their
common zero set has not been decided. No square witness or actual etale
cover follows from these results. Degrees142/144 remain separate.
