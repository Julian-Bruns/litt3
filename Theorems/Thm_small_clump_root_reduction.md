# A finite root-weight test for small clumps in an unbounded degree family

Let X be the fixed genus-nine curve, and let X <-f- Z -g-> Y be an
ACTUAL finite etale span from the SAME smooth projective connected
source, with g(Y)>=2 and Hom(J(X),J(Y))=0. Assume the span is coreless,
deg(f)=2^a 3^b 5^c for nonnegative integers a,b,c, and that a nonempty
clump has image D_X on X of size r<=3. Let s be its primitive shared
canonical generator.

Then s_X is a positive power, up to scalar, of a regular tensor t_X
of one of the following weights b_X:

| r | possible b_X |
|---|---|
| 1 or2 | 3,9,27 |
| 3 | 9,27,81 |

Its divisor is (16b_X/r)D_X. If1<=u<=4 satisfies u b_X=1 modulo5,
then the necessary ROOT condition is

    C_(X,(u b_X-1)/5)(t_X^u)=0.

It is independent of the exponent expressing s_X as a power of t_X.
The possibilities for D_X, hence for t_X up to scalar, form a FINITE
set defined over F_(25^684). This is an algebraic finite reduction,
not a feasible enumeration bound or a claim that all its tests fail.

Neither leg is assumed Galois; their degrees may be unbounded and
divisible by5. No clump is asserted to exist or to have image size<=3.
The restriction on prime divisors of deg(f) is essential to this
particular weight table. No common cover is excluded by the table alone.
