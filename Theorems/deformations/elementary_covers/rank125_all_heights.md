# A persistent late slope in the marked rank125 lifting problem

Version2,2026-09-15. The conclusion concerns one marked
periodic-Hodge laboratory, not a common cover.

Retain the original rank125 cover, scalar coefficient transport,
canonical ordinary reference, two tame involutions, actual flat
two-torsion twist and graded identifications of the
[actual sixth theorem](rank125_fixed_line_sixth.md). Fix the third
curve Hdagger=Hstar+[8]nu39 and the fourth curve at the unique
sixth-admissible invariant value tau=[105]. Work over any perfect
field F containing F125, with all actual compatible prefixes retained.

At every n>=7, suppose a Gamma-invariant compatible W_(n-1) tuple
has been reached above this fourth curve. Keep its truncation
through W_(n-3) fixed. The invariant (n-2)nd source choices that
still admit a compatible W_(n-1) tuple form an affine F-line,
with difference direction the original nu39. On this line the
remaining, (n-1)st-origin-independent nth obstruction satisfies

    psi_n(x)=b_n+[25]*x.

The constant b_n depends on the actual prefix and on the origin
chosen on the line; it is not asserted to equal a previous constant.
It belongs to F. Every (n-1)st source choice and terminal nth
repair is allowed. Exactly one x=-b_n/[25] admits W_n, over F.
The assertion at n=6 is the actual scalar[101]+[25]*tau.

Consequently there are compatible marked tuples of every finite
Witt length over F125 above Hdagger, and a compatible formal marked
tower obtained by stabilizing their lower truncations. This formal
Gamma-invariant tower is unique above Hdagger, up to the specified
marked projective tuple isomorphisms. One need
not compute b7,b8,... separately to prove existence. An arbitrary
given W_m tuple is not asserted to extend without adjusting its
permitted later digits.

The stabilization is a three-flat-digit secondary comparison.
The old two-flat-digit response eliminates all but nu39 at every
reached level. The only exceptional square at the reference sixth
stage has AS degree2 and is killed by E100. This proves persistence
of the independently computed sixth slope; equality with the fifth
slope alone would not suffice.
Existence and uniqueness then follow from the general
[delayed extension criterion](../marked_obstruction_torsors.md).

In fact its given third curve admits NO lift of the original
rank125 finite etale map h:T->C to any C3 extending the specified
reference C2. The scalar Hdagger has a nonzero degree-five part,
whereas a descended third displacement must lie in the invariant
norm line F*nu42. Thus no amount of one-endpoint compatible height
alone can force that given third curve to descend along h.
Finite-abelian late descent requires an already descended lower
stage and does not contradict this example. There is no claim about
even or other third branches, a simultaneous two-leg lift, or either
unmarked common-cover candidate.
