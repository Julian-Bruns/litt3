# No marked-branch content in a root-nine square

29 September2026. Use the fixed root-nine degree140 source, its original
open chart, and its actual residual from
[the root-nine construction](degree140_root9_six_scale_curves.md).
Write its regular critical quadratic on affine X as
\(e(\mu)=e_0+\mu e_1+\mu^2e_2\), up to the fixed nonzero scalar
normalization, and let \(Q_9=([9],0)\).

If its cubic norm is a geometric polynomial square, then
\[
\min_i\operatorname{ord}_{Q_9}(e_i)=0.
\]
Thus no square specialization has positive fixed content at the marked
cubic branch. Every geometric parameter and scale on the original chart
is covered; this is not a finite-field parameter search.

The proof excludes a full content line by local parity. On the remaining
content curve it first forces
\[
\mu=[203829]\frac{(H-[356769])^3}{(H-[42135])^2},\qquad H=u/q.
\]
All65 exceptional base points are excluded for arbitrary scale. The
remaining one-variable problem has two exact tail norms whose gcd is
the375th power of one irreducible quintic. On that entire exceptional
fibre the common tail equations force H=0, outside the original chart.

Together with the
[common-critical exclusion](degree140_root9_common_critical_exclusion.md),
fixed content at a square can occur only over the three roots of t.
Those endpoint cases, the content-free square locus and the original
unmarked common-cover problem are not decided by this theorem.
