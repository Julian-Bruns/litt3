# An ordinary atlas with non-weakly-ramified mixed inertia

**Status:** exact author construction and scalar certificate, 2026-09-05.
**Author:** `/root/canonical_trace_algebra`. No independent audit claimed.

This disproves the assertion that an effective rational-coarse orbifold
admitting an ordinary finite étale atlas must have G2=1 at every point.
The atlas is genuinely non-Galois. It illustrates the boundary of
[the different-ratio bound](ORDINARY_ATLAS_LOCAL_DIFFERENT_BOUND.md), not a
counterexample to Litt or a construction for the fixed pair 76.

## 1. Explicit curves and both étale maps

Work over k=Fbar5; all equations below are defined over F5. Set

    f1=x(x-1),  f2=(x-2)(x-3),  f3=x-4,
    f1*f2*f3=x^5-x.

Let W be the smooth projective normalization with function field
k(x)(u1,u2,u3), where ui^2=fi. Its Galois group over k(x) is C2^3:
independence of the three square classes follows from their disjoint finite
branch supports. The six branch points are F5 together with infinity.
At each finite branch point inertia changes exactly one sign; at infinity
it changes only u3, since only f3 has odd degree.

Let H be the even-sign subgroup and sigma the simultaneous change of all
three signs. Put

    Y0=W/H: y^2=x^5-x,       y=u1*u2*u3,
    X=W/<sigma>.

Neither H nor <sigma> meets any nontrivial inertia subgroup. Hence

    W -> Y0 is finite étale Galois of degree 4,
    W -> X  is finite étale Galois of degree 2.

Riemann--Hurwitz gives g(Y0)=2, g(W)=5, g(X)=3. Explicitly, X is the
smooth projective normalization of

    a^2=x(x-1)(x-2)(x-3),
    b^2=x(x-1)(x-4),

with a=u1*u2 and b=u1*u3. Its third quadratic subfield is generated by
c=ab/f1, satisfying c^2=f2*f3.

The character decomposition gives the isogeny

    J(X) ~ J(z^2=f1*f2) x J(z^2=f1*f3) x J(z^2=f2*f3).

These are three genus-one factors. Their branch polynomials and the
coefficient of x^4 in their squares are

| Polynomial | Coefficient |
| --- | --- |
| x^4+4x^3+x^2+4x | 3 |
| x^3+4x | 3 |
| x^3+x^2+x+1 | 3 |

For a genus-one hyperelliptic equation in characteristic five, this is
the Cartier coefficient before taking its fifth root. It is nonzero in
all three cases. Thus every factor, and consequently X, is ordinary.
For comparison, the 2x2 Cartier matrix of y^2=x^5-x is zero: its square
has support in degrees 2,6,10, none of 3,4,8,9. Therefore Y0 has p-rank
zero and, in dimension two, its Jacobian is supersingular. The same
character decomposition of J(W) gives f(W)=3<5. The intermediate étale
cover W of the ordinary curve X is already nonordinary.

## 2. The effective proper orbifold and its exact ramification

On Y0 let

    s_alpha(x,y)=(x+alpha,y), alpha in F5,
    iota(x,y)=(x,-y).

They generate G=C5 x C2, acting faithfully. The smooth proper effective
Deligne--Mumford curve S=[Y0/G] has rational coarse coordinate

    v=y^2=x^5-x.

Its only stacky points are v=0 and v=infinity. The stabilizer at a point
with y=0 is <iota>=C2; there are five such points, forming one G-orbit.
The unique point at infinity is fixed by all of G. All other stabilizers
are trivial.

Here is a direct lower-numbering calculation at infinity. The valuations
of x and y on Y0 are -2 and -5, respectively, so u=y/x^3 is a uniformizer.
For alpha nonzero,

    s_alpha(u)/u=(1+alpha/x)^(-3),
    ord(s_alpha(u)-u)=1+ord(1/x)=3.

The leading coefficient -3*alpha is nonzero. Meanwhile iota(u)=-u,
so its displacement has order one. The ramification groups are therefore

    G0=C5 x C2,  G1=G2=C5,  G3=1.

The wild point has

    e=10,  d=(10-1)+(5-1)+(5-1)=17,

and is not weakly ramified. Its ratio d/e=17/10 nevertheless satisfies
the necessary strict inequality d<2e. The tame point has e=2,d=1.

## 3. An actual representable finite étale atlas X -> S

The morphism W -> Y0 is equivariant for the injection

    <sigma> -> G,       sigma |-> iota,

because changing all three ui signs changes y to -y while fixing x.
It therefore induces

    X=[W/<sigma>] -> [Y0/G]=S.

To check every assertion on this map, pull it back along the étale atlas
Y0 -> S. The resulting space is the induced torsor

    G x^{<sigma>} W.

As a scheme it is a disjoint union of five copies of W. On each copy its
map to Y0 is W -> Y0 followed by a translation s_alpha, so it is finite
étale of degree four on that copy. It follows by descent that X -> S is
representable, finite étale, surjective, and of degree 5*4=20. This is a
global quotient-stack construction, not an assertion of existence from
local ramification data.

Equivalently, the normalization of X x_(P1_v) Y0 has these five
components. Each is isomorphic to W, and the natural G-action makes
their union an étale G-torsor over X. The coarse map of the atlas is

    X -> P1_x -> P1_v,       v=x^5-x.

As a numerical check, its degree is 20 and

    2g(X)-2=20*(-2+17/10+1/2)=4.

## 4. Why this atlas is non-Galois and does not contradict Nakajima

The extension k(X)/k(x) is biquadratic. Its three nontrivial quadratic
characters have branch sets equal to the unions of pairs of blocks in

    {0,1}, {2,3}, {4,infinity}.

A lift of x -> x+1 to X would have to permute these three branch sets,
equivalently preserve this block partition. It does not: the block
containing infinity changes from {4,infinity} to {0,infinity}. Thus this
translation has no lift. Since k(x)/k(v) is Galois C5, normality of
k(X)/k(v) would force all its automorphisms to lift. The coarse cover,
and hence the atlas X -> S, is not Galois.

The classical weak-ramification statement assumes a finite automorphism
group acting on the ordinary source itself. This is also the explicit
scope recalled in the introduction of Köck, *Galois structure of Zariski
cohomology for weakly ramified covers of curves*.
[Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0207124).
Here G acts on the nonordinary curve Y0, not on X. There is no conflict
with that theorem and no valid step replacing X by an ordinary Galois
closure.

The example settles the proposed unrestricted strengthening negatively.
It does **not** settle existence of an ordinary atlas for the different
profile C5 semidirect C4 with lower break 3, e=20,d=31, and one tame
order-two point. No inference about that particular profile is made.

Run the [scalar certificate](ORDINARY_ATLAS_WITH_NONWEAK_MIXED_INERTIA_CERTIFICATE.py)
with `sage -python`. It verifies the polynomial product, squarefreeness,
all three nonzero Cartier coefficients, the zero Cartier matrix of Y0,
the numerical different/genus identities, and failure of translation
invariance of the block partition. No extension-field matrix backend,
point-count search, or local-to-global existence assumption is used.
