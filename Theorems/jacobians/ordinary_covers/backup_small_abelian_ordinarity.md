# Unbounded small-abelian-by-five monodromy is excluded for the backup

Version 1, 2026-09-11. Author integration of complete finite tests and
existing cover lemmas; not a new independent whole-statement audit.

Let B be the genus-two backup with alpha^3+alpha+1=0. Its maximal
abelian exponent-four and exponent-six finite etale covers both have
ordinary Jacobians. Their degrees are respectively 256 and 1296.

Consequently, in any actual common finite etale span X<-Z->B, the
Galois closure of the B-leg cannot have a group fitting into

    1 -> P -> G -> A -> 1,

where P is a 5-group and A is abelian of exponent dividing 4 OR 6.
There is no bound on |P| or on the original source degree. Neither
original leg is assumed Galois, and no coredness or connection match
is assumed.

The disjunction does not assert exponent 12, arbitrary abelian
quotients, or arbitrary solvable groups. This is Jacobian ordinarity,
not indigenous ordinarity.
