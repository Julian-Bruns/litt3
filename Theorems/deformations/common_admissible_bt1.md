# A common admissible oper has actual compatible BT1 realizations

Version2,3 October2026. Let $k=\overline{\mathbf F}_5$ and let
$X\xleftarrow f Z\xrightarrow gY$ be two actual finite etale maps
of smooth proper connected hyperbolic curves. Suppose admissible
active projective opers $r_X,r_Y$ have equal pullbacks on $Z$.

There are actual everywhere-versal height-two, dimension-one BT1
groups $H_X/X$, $H_Y/Y$, inducing the given projective opers, and a
flat line $D$ of order dividing four on $Z$ such that
\[
f^*H_X\simeq g^*H_Y\otimes\Lambda_D.
\tag{1}
\]
Here $\Lambda_D$ is the rank-one etale $\mathbf F_5$-local system,
with the contravariant Dieudonne convention. Its connected character
cover $h:Z'\to Z$, of degree1,2 or4, makes the two actual groups
isomorphic. Neither endpoint is replaced. No indigenous ordinariness
or bound on the original covering degrees is assumed.

Consequently, for any such span, existence of a common admissible
active projective oper is equivalent to existence of compatible
actual everywhere-versal BT1 data after a connected cyclic source
refinement of degree at most four. A compatible BT1 comparison on
any finite etale source refinement induces equality of the projective
opers on the original source.

If $r_Y$ is indigenous-ordinary, $H_Y$ may additionally be chosen as
the first truncation of its determinant-normalized full group.
For the selected genus-two endpoints this applies to all85 active
opers. Otherwise only the BT1 realization is asserted. Neither
endpoint's higher extension nor compatibility of full groups on the
source follows from (1).

This supplies no common oper from a bare span and no object in its
dormant or no-common-connection branches. Both original common-cover
problems remain open.
