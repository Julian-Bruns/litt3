# Prime support of a central étale kernel over a rational quotient

Version1,3 October2026. [Root whole-proof review PASS](../../Research/audits/CENTRAL_ETALE_KERNEL_AND_HUMBERT_CLOSURE_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). The quotient and covering curves below are actual curves; neither a simultaneous Galois closure nor a tame quotient-stack model is assumed.

Let H be a smooth projective connected curve over an algebraically closed field of arbitrary characteristic. Let a finite group V act faithfully on H with H/V≅P¹. Let Γ′→H be a connected finite étale Galois cover with group J, and suppose the V action lifts to an ACTUAL faithful extension action
\[
1\longrightarrow J\longrightarrow E\longrightarrow V\longrightarrow1
\]
on Γ′, with J CENTRAL in E. Then
\[
\{\ell:\ell\text{ prime},\ell\mid |J|\}
\subseteq\{\ell:\ell\mid |V|\}.
\]
In particular if V is a TWO-group, J is a TWO-group. The assertion allows wild V-inertia. For primesℓ not dividing |V| it also allowsℓ equal to the characteristic.

More precisely, a hypothetical surjection J→Cℓ withℓ∤|V| would induce a connected ACTUAL étale Cℓ cover of P¹, which is impossible. It is the given étale Γ′→H leg that supplies this descent, not an unrelated source map to H.
