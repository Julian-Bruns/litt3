# Proof: the exact power lattice and the degree-one exclusion

This integrates the returned character-rigidity reply. The two
omitted cases in its degree-one counting argument are included below.

The inverse image H_A of A in F_*O_S has exact sequence
0->O_C->H_A->A->0. The multiplication counit on F^*H_A
retracts its unit section, so F^*H_A=O_S plus F^*A=O_S^2.
Multiplication defines Sym^3H_A/O_C*1^3 -> B_S. Locally its
image is generated by [g],[g^2],[g^3], where [g] generates A.
Since dg is nonzero, 1,g,g^2,g^3 are independent over the
Frobenius-constant field. Thus this is an injective map of sheaves.
Its image J_pow has a filtration with quotients A,A^2,A^3,
hence degree zero and determinant A^6=A.

All three generators lie in A^perp: the Cartier images of
g d(g^j), j=1,2,3, vanish because they are exact differentials.
At a point of D remove the Frobenius-constant part of g. Its
initial order is three since dg has order two. If T=t^5 is a
parameter on C, g^2/T and g^3/T are regular. Their classes
remain perpendicular to [g]. Consequently J_pow is contained
in A+A^perp(-D), with no saturation taken.
The latter has degree deg(A^perp)-2 deg D=16n-16n=0. Equality
of ranks and degrees makes the inclusion an equality everywhere.

The counit splits F^*H_A as above. Given the normalized frame
of F^*A, its unique lift t_A to the counit kernel satisfies
nabla t_A=nu t_A-zeta*1. In the symmetric cube modulo1^3,
the frames 1^2 t_A,1 t_A^2,t_A^3 give the displayed matrix.
The evaluation row is (zeta,0,0); the trace is6nu=nu in
characteristic five. This verifies the actual unsaturated
lattice, Frobenius triviality and determinant connection together.

For n=1 a candidate would yield a nonzero regular differential
zeta=(U(x)+V(x)y)theta, deg U<=5 and deg V<=2, with
div zeta=2D and D a degree-eight reduced subdivisor of
R_X=O+x^*div_0 A_0. The nine basis differentials have orders
16,13,10,7,4,1,6,3,0 at O. These are distinct and none is two,
so O cannot belong to D. This removes infinity from the count.

The four finite x-fibers in R_X are unramified and each has
three distinct y-values. If V(r) is nonzero, at most one point
of that fiber is a zero. Two zeros force U(r)=V(r)=0, hence
all three are zeros. As all must be double, differentiating
also gives U'(r)=V'(r)=0.
If V is nonzero, its degree at most two allows at most one
such double root, so the total is at most3+1+1+1=6.
If V=0, each used fiber is complete and double; deg U<=5
allows at most two such fibers, again at most six points.
Both contradict deg D=8. This proves the degree-one exclusion.

If zeta on a cover were h^*zeta_X, etaleness would preserve
its zero multiplicities. Then div zeta_X would be twice a
reduced degree-eight subdivisor of R_X, the case just excluded.
This argument concerns the adjunction form zeta, not the
connection form nu whose descent remains unproved.
