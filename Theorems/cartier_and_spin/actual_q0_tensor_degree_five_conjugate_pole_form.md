# Degree-five conjugate simple poles give one quartic-parameter form

Version1,3 October2026. New necessary form, pending independent review. This does not exclude the stratum.

In the actual jointly minimal degree-FIVE tensor alternative, suppose its low-genus coarse B has TWO common simple infinity poles which are conjugate under the degree-two z-map. If B has genus TWO, or B has genus ONE and its two odd numerator polynomials are proportional, necessary local-condition symmetries give
\[
Y^2=\Psi(z)=z[A(z)^2+(z^2+z+1)(z-a)^2],\qquad\deg A\le2,
\]
\[
x_1=A/[z(z-a)]+Y/[z^2(z-a)],\qquad x_2=A/(z-a)+Y/(z-a).
\]
Here a≠ZERO,ONE, A(a)≠0, p=A2²+ONE≠0 and Ψ′(0)=A0²+a²≠0. In genus TWO, Ψ is the smooth degree-five hyperelliptic polynomial. In the proportional genus-ONE case, Ψ=(z−e)²Φ3, e≠ZERO,a; it is a singular presentation of the actual elliptic field, not a smooth genus-two model.

Set J=z−a and
\[
U=A'zJ-A(2z-a),\quad V=\Psi'zJ/2-\Psi(3z-2a),
\quad C=A'J-A,\quad W=\Psi'J/2-\Psi.
\]
Then BOTH degree-TEN polynomials
\[
N_1=(V^2-z^2\Psi U^2)/z^2,\qquad N_2=W^2-\Psi C^2
\]
must be scalar multiples of quintic squares. Their leading coefficients are4p² and p², respectively. Exact square substitution gives TEN necessary equations in only a,A0,A1,A2.

No smoothness claim is made for Ψ in the elliptic case. Both actual original maps and any original Y-leg remain on the SAME actual source.
