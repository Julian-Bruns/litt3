# A centered cubic trace for every remaining trace-zero degree-ten profile

30 September2026. Retain an ACTUAL primitive degree-ten etale
admissible source in one of the eleven trace-zero profiles. Write
\[
F_b=(W^5+f)H+\tau,\qquad
H\bmod(W^5+f)=s_3W^3+s_2W^2+s_1W+s_0,
\]
with the nonzero scale normalized as usual. Put
\[
c=s_3/\tau,\quad d=s_2/\tau,\quad
E_j=\operatorname{Tr}_{S/X}((db)^j/(b^5+f)).
\]
Define the rational cubic differential
\[
\mathcal I_3=c^2E_3-\frac32(c\,dd-d\,dc)E_2.
\]
The first d in dd is the exterior derivative; c,d are rational
functions. This expression is invariant under every base translation
b -> b+a, f -> f-a^5. If c!=0 it equals
c^2 Tr((db-d(d/(2c)))^3/(b^5+f)). Its cleared definition remains
valid at zeros of c.

Let t be the monic polynomial for the three selected roots of A,
and let v be constant or x-r for a root r of P, as specified by
the profile. Then
\[
v t^9\mathcal I_3\in
\begin{cases}
H^0(X,\omega_X^3(54O)),&v\text{ constant},\\
H^0(X,\omega_X^3(57O)),&v=x-r.
\end{cases}
\]
Relative to omega0^3 these are the spaces L(102O) and L(105O),
of dimensions94 and97. In a scale family
F=lambda v(W^5+q)^2+(W^5+q)S+tau with quadratic D=S_W,
the numerator Delta^2*I3 has degree at most six in lambda.

This is an additional necessary condition, not an etaleness test
or an exclusion. The quadratic energy can be regular at a cubic
ramification point where the cubic moment has a double pole;
the proof includes an exact local example.
