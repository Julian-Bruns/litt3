# Minimality makes the endpoint multiplicity representation faithful

Author: `/root`, 2026-09-06. Status: author proof; not independently
audited. No new common-cover degree exclusion is claimed. This adds
the actual map and its point stabilizer to the packet bound of file 96.

Let X <-f- Z -g-> Y be finite etale maps of smooth projective connected
curves over an algebraically closed field, with both endpoint genera at
least two. Assume k(Z)=k(X)k(Y) in k(Z). Let q:W->Y be the connected
Galois closure of g, let G=Gal(W/Y), and let H=Gal(W/Z). Thus H is
core-free and d=[G:H]=deg(g). Put A=J(X), and assume A is simple with
End^0(A)=K a number field. Work up to isogeny.

## Theorem

In V=Hom^0(A,J(W)), viewed as a right K-vector space, put
v=(f r)^*, where r:W->Z. The left G-action is induced by inverse
pullback on J(W). Then the cyclic K[G]-module U generated by v has:

1. stabilizer Stab_G(v)=H;
2. faithful G-action;
3. a surjection K[G/H] -> U sending the identity coset to v.

If Hom(A,J(Y))=0, this surjection kills the constant vector. In
particular U has no trivial constituent and dim_K(U)<=d-1.

Every complex irreducible constituent chi of U, after choosing the
corresponding embedding of K in C, satisfies BOTH

    dim chi^H > 0,
    g(X) <= (g(Y)-1)[K intersect Q(chi):Q] chi(1)/e_K(chi).

Here e_K is the Schur index specified in file 96. Consequently the
intersection of the kernels of all nontrivial H-spherical characters
satisfying this inequality must be trivial. If that intersection is
nontrivial, the specified monodromy/stabilizer pair (G,H) is impossible.
This is a necessary condition, not an existence criterion.

## Proof

First recall a geometric fact valid in every characteristic: two
surjective maps a,b:W->X with a^*=b^*:J(X)->J(W) are equal. Dualizing
identifies their Albanese homomorphisms. Thus i a-i b is constant in
J(X), for an Abel--Jacobi embedding i. Surjectivity says that this
constant translates i(X) onto itself. The translation stabilizer of
an Abel--Jacobi curve of genus at least two is trivial. A proof using
the free quotient and norm identity, valid in p-divisible order, is
given in Section 3 of
NONGALOIS_JACOBIAN_ORTHOGONALITY_AND_ETALE_MAP_STABILIZATION.md.

Apply this fact to f r and f r h^{-1}. If h fixes v, it fixes the
specified subfield k(X) in k(W). It also fixes k(Y), since h belongs
to G. Minimality makes it fix their compositum k(Z), so h belongs
to H. The converse is immediate. This proves assertion 1 and the
permutation-module surjection in assertion 3.

The kernel of the G-action on U is normal and is contained in the
stabilizer H of v. It is therefore contained in Core_G(H)=1. This
proves faithfulness. The same argument makes the action on the full
A-isotypic multiplicity space V faithful.

The sum of the images of the coset basis is

    sum_(G/H) rho(h)v = q^* g_* f^*.

This is the usual trace/base-change identity for the sheets of Z/Y
on its Galois closure, with each sheet counted once. Under Hom(A,JY)=0
it vanishes. Since the coefficient field K has characteristic zero,
the permutation module splits as its constant line and augmentation
module. The latter has no trivial constituent (the action is
transitive). The assertions about U follow.

Semisimplicity and Frobenius reciprocity show that every complex
constituent of U has an H-fixed vector. Apply the proof of Theorem
96.2 to W->Y, target X, and the rational packet containing that
constituent. The nonzero A-isotypic component containing it satisfies
exactly the displayed inequality; the proof of 96.2 applies to each
such constituent, not only to one chosen in advance. Finally the
kernel of U is the intersection of the kernels of its complex
constituents. Intersecting over the larger set of all allowed
H-spherical characters can only make that kernel smaller, proving
the necessary kernel test. QED.

## Limitation

Neither faithfulness nor the dimension bound bounds d. For example,
in the natural action of A_d or S_d (d>=5), the augmentation module
is already an irreducible faithful module of dimension d-1. The
displayed inequality then becomes a lower bound on d, not an upper
bound. This theorem does not solve the alternating-monodromy case,
and must not be advertised as an all-degree obstruction by itself.
