# A first-jet factor generates the complete endpoint content ideal

Version1,30 September2026. Work in characteristic five with uniformizer t
at any cubic endpoint of the constant degree140 family. In the integral
translated primitive coordinate V write
\[
S(V)=aV^3+bV^2+cV+e,\quad \phi(V)=V^5+t^3u,\quad
\tau=t^3(-ue+t^2\kappa),\quad c=t\chi.
\]
Here a,b,e,u,kappa,chi are regular local functions; e and u are units,
and a,b do not both vanish at the endpoint. These are precisely the two
accepted source congruences, retaining arbitrary higher jets.

Let D(ell) be the fixed-degree(10,2) resultant of
ell*phi^2+phi*S+tau and the critical quadratic 3aV^2+2bV+c.
Put
\[
R=\kappa_0b_0^5+u_0\chi_0^2b_0^4+2e_0\chi_0^5.
\]
Subscript zero denotes reduction at t=0. Then D is divisible by t^5 and
\[
\left.(D(\ell)/t^5)\right|_{t=0}
=4R\bigl(b_0^5\ell+a_0^3(a_0^2e_0+2b_0^3)\bigr).
\]
Thus the ideal of all endpoint coefficients of the scale quadratic is
exactly(R), on the stated open. This is an equality of ideals, not just
equality of reduced zero sets. Multiplying the resultant by its original
y-unit normalization leaves that assertion unchanged.

On the type-A branch b_0!=0, the actual critical-value pole has expansion
\[
\Lambda=-\frac{R}{b_0^5u_0^2}t^{-1}+O(1).
\]
In particular, the inverse of this moving pole coefficient introduces
no zero other than the already excluded endpoint-content condition R=0.
For type B, b_0=0, primitivity forces chi_0!=0 and
R=2e_0*chi_0^5!=0.

Over the finite étale algebra of all nine endpoint sheets, the endpoint
content-free open is equivalently the nonvanishing of Norm(R). This
identification does not by itself construct every lower trace coefficient
or prove that every apparent type-B denominator has cancelled.
