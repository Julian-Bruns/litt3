# Translation-invariant source energies

Version1,30 September2026. Let K be a differential field of
characteristic p>=5, with derivation delta. Let
\[
\phi(W)=W^p+q,\qquad
F(W)=\kappa\phi(W)^2+\phi(W)S(W)+\tau,
\]
where kappa,tau are nonzero, deg(S)<=p-2, and F is separable.
Let B=K[W]/(F), let w be its distinguished element, and extend delta
to the finite etale K-algebra B. Define
\[
E_m=\operatorname{Tr}_{B/K}\left((\delta w)^m/\phi(w)\right).
\]
Then E_0=E_1=0. Under any base translation w'=w+a, with a in K,
and the matching q'=q-a^p, one has
\[
E'_m=\sum_{j=0}^m\binom mj(\delta a)^{m-j}E_j.
\]
Consequently E_2 and 3E_2 E_4-2E_3^2 are unchanged. In
characteristic five the latter may be normalized as
\[
I=E_2 E_4+E_3^2.
\]

Suppose locally K is the fraction field of k[[r]], delta preserves
k[[r]], the actual source roots w are integral and split, and phi(w)
is a unit at each source root. Then E_2 and I are integral. The same
integrality holds in every translated coordinate, even when a has
a pole. It is the summed invariant, not each translated summand,
that is asserted integral.

This applies to the source form used in the degree140 families. It
does not prove a global pole bound for I, decide another parameter
locus, or construct or exclude an unrestricted common cover.
