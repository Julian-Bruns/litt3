# Exact Hodge degree for maximal abelian covers of genus-two families

Version4,3 October2026. Let F_q have odd characteristic p, let
a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3 be distinct elements of F_q, and put
\[
C_t:y^2=\prod_{i=0}^3(u-a_i)(u-t),\qquad
U=\mathbf P^1_t\setminus\{a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3,\infty\}.
\]
For m≥2 prime to p, the pullback of [m]:J(C_t)→J(C_t) along the
Abel map based at infinity is the geometrically connected maximal
abelian exponent-m etale cover C_(m),t→C_t, of degree m^4 and genus m^4+1.

After any finite base change B→P1, of degree b, admitting its stable
extension D→C, with λ_D=det f_*ω_(D/B), the exact Hodge degree is
\[
\deg\lambda_D=b\,\frac{m^2(m^2+5)}6.
\]
If one smooth C_(m),t is ordinary, there is a nonzero E∈F_q[T] with
\[
\deg E\le (p-1)\frac{m^2(m^2+5)}6+4
\]
such that E(t)≠0 implies ordinarity of C_(m),t and of every connected
abelian etale cover of C_t with exponent dividing m. In particular a
parameter of field degree greater than this bound suffices. No ordinary
seed is asserted for arbitrary m, including exponent eight.

## The characteristic-five family

For q=5 and (a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3)=(0,1,2,3), the later
[good-cubic theorem](backup_small_abelian_ordinarity.md) supplies ordinary
seeds for BOTH m=4 and m=6. The respective bounds are228 and988;
the maximal covers have degrees256 and1296, and genera257 and1297.
The selected [bounded-atlas partner](../../quotient_geometry/bounded_atlas_partner_finiteness.md)
meets both field-degree bounds.

For m=4 or6 and a t of F5-degree greater than its corresponding bound,
any nonordinary X over bar(F5) admits no actual
finite etale span X←Z→C_t whose C_t-leg Galois closure has group
1→P→G→A→1, with P a five-group and A abelian of exponent dividing
the corresponding m. The original legs need not be Galois, and |P|
is unrestricted.
