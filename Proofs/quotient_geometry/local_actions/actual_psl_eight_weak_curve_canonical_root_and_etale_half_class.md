## Actual field cohomology gives obstruction surjectivity

Put \(K=k(D)\), \(F=K^Q=k(\mathbf P^1)\), and
\(\operatorname{Prin}(D)=K^\times/k^\times\).
The faithful action makes \(K/F\) an actual finite Galois extension,
including in characteristic five. Hilbert 90 gives
\(H^1(Q,K^\times)=0\).
Moreover \(H^2(Q,K^\times)\) injects into \(\operatorname{Br}(F)\):
apply absolute-Galois inflation to
\[
1\to\operatorname{Gal}(F^{\mathrm{sep}}/K)
\to\operatorname{Gal}(F^{\mathrm{sep}}/F)\to Q\to1
\]
and use Hilbert 90 for its kernel. The target Brauer group is zero
by Tsen. Thus \(H^2(Q,K^\times)=0\).
These primary facts are recorded in
[Stacks, Hilbert 90](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03QV),
[Brauer/cohomology](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03R7)
and [Tsen](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03RF).

The sequence \(1\to k^\times\to K^\times\to\operatorname{Prin}(D)\to0\)
therefore identifies
\[
H^1(Q,\operatorname{Prin}(D))=H^2(Q,k^\times).
\]
The divisor group is a direct sum of permutation modules
\(\mathbf Z[Q/Q_x]\). Its \(H^1\) vanishes by Shapiro and
\(\operatorname{Hom}(Q_x,\mathbf Z)=0\); finite-group cochains commute
with this direct sum. The boundary from
\(0\to\operatorname{Prin}(D)\to\operatorname{Div}(D)\to
\operatorname{Pic}(D)\to0\) is consequently surjective onto
\(H^2(Q,k^\times)\).
Its composite is the scalar transport obstruction, with one fixed
uniform inverse convention.

The accepted
[two-point wild Picard theorem](../../../Theorems/quotient_geometry/local_actions/two_point_wild_orbifold_picard_group.md)
and [proof](two_point_wild_orbifold_picard_group.md) apply to this
actual faithful two-branch action. Genuine linearized lines have
presentation
\[
\operatorname{Pic}_Q(D)=
\langle U,D_w,D_t\mid5D_w=U,\ 2D_t=U\rangle.
\]
Writing \(U=10v,D_w=2v,D_t=5v\), the actual weak different eight gives
\(\omega_D=-2U+8D_w+D_t=v\). It generates this group.
The forgetful map is injective by its positive degree, and its image
is precisely the zero-obstruction classes. Using
\(H^2(Q,k^\times)=C_4\), we have
\[
0\to\mathbf Z[\omega_D]\to\operatorname{Pic}(D)^Q\to C_4\to0.
\]
Central closure and its primary Steinberg citation are supplied by
the accepted
[source half-class proof](canonical_ten_source_half_class_and_full_linear_kernel_constraints.md);
an ambient matrix group is not substituted for a smaller image.

## The actual tame fiber makes the invariant Picard extension primitive

Choose invariant \(E\) with generator obstruction. Then
\(E^4=\omega_D^r\) for some integer \(r\).
Its transports pulled to \(H_0=SL_8(\mathbf F_5)\) split, by central
closure, giving a genuine \(H_0\)-linearization.
The resulting character of \(\mu_4\) is faithful: transgression
from \(\operatorname{Hom}(\mu_4,k^\times)\) is injective because
\(H_0\) has no characters, and the obstruction has order four.

At an ACTUAL tame fixed point let \(u\) be the fiber action of
\(\widetilde c\). Its square is the faithful character evaluated on
the primitive scalar \(\widetilde c^2\), so \(u^4=-1\).
Thus \(c\) acts by \(-1\) on \(E^4\).
It acts by \(-1\) on the canonical fiber as well: a nontrivial tame
involution has cotangent multiplier \(-1\).
The underlying comparison \(E^4=\omega_D^r\) is equivariant, since
both sides are genuinely \(Q\)-linearized and their discrepancy would
be a character of the perfect \(Q\). Therefore \(r\) is odd.

The exact sequence shows the full invariant group has generators
\(E,\omega_D\) and single relation \(4E=r\omega_D\).
No extra relation exists: its image in \(C_4\) first forces the
\(E\)-coefficient divisible by four, and the kernel is freely
generated by \(\omega_D\).
Choose \(a,b\) with \(ar+4b=1\), and define \(P=E^a\omega_D^b\).
Then \(P^4=\omega_D\), and its obstruction generates \(C_4\) with
sign \(\varepsilon=\pm1\). Its degree is one quarter the canonical
degree, so it is the primitive positive generator. This proves
uniqueness of its Picard class and absence of invariant geometric
torsion. It makes no assertion about infinitesimal fixed-group schemes.

Hurwitz gives
\[
g(D)-1=|Q|/20,\qquad \deg P=|Q|/40,\qquad
|Q|=\frac{5^{28}}4\prod_{i=2}^8(5^i-1).
\]
The last expression is divisible by eighty, so \(\deg P\) is even.
Choose a line of half this degree and correct its square using
surjectivity of multiplication by two on the Jacobian. An underlying
root \(R^2=P\) therefore exists.

## The root cocycle and actual Kummer cover

For the left action \(gR=(g^{-1})^*R\), put
\(\delta_g=gR-R\in A=\operatorname{Pic}(D)[2]\).
Expansion gives \(\delta_{gh}=\delta_g+g\delta_h\).
If it were a coboundary \(g\tau-\tau\), then \(R-\tau\) would be an
invariant square root of \(P\), impossible in \(\mathbf Z[P]\).
The span \(U_R\) is stable because
\(g\delta_h=\delta_{gh}-\delta_g\), is nonzero, and has zero
invariants since \(A^Q=0\).
Changing \(R\) changes this cocycle by a coboundary, but may change
its span.

Choose a basis \(\tau_i\) of \(U_R\), divisors \(E_i\) representing it,
and functions \(f_i\) with \(\operatorname{div}(f_i)=2E_i\).
The squareclasses \(f_i\) are independent: a square product makes
the corresponding divisor sum principal after dividing its divisor
identity by two. Conversely any principal sum gives a square product
up to a constant, and all constants in \(k\) are squares.
The field
\[
k(\widehat\Gamma)=k(D)(\sqrt{f_1},\ldots,\sqrt{f_r})
\]
therefore has degree \(2^r\) and deck group
\(N_R=\operatorname{Hom}(U_R,\mu_2)\).
Its projective normalization is connected and smooth.
Even valuations and invertible two make it unramified at every point,
equivalently a finite étale Kummer torsor.
Each class in \(U_R\) pulls trivially.
The standard Picard and Kummer facts can be found in
[Stacks, curves](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03RN)
and [Kummer theory](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03PK).

Stability of \(U_R\) makes every transformed \(f_i\) a product of the
\(f_j\) times a rational square and a constant square.
Hence every element of \(Q\) lifts to the ACTUAL curve.
Its full lift group has the asserted exact sequence and quotient
\(\mathbf P^1\).
The line \(\widehat M=\pi^*R\) is invariant: the difference under a
lift of \(g\) is \(\pi^*\delta_g=0\), and deck transformations fix it.
Étale canonical pullback supplies its eighth and sixteenth powers.

## Exact order, inertia and kernel

The \(Q\)-module \(\operatorname{Hom}(N_R,k^\times)\) is \(U_R\),
whose invariants vanish. The five-term sequence makes inflation
\(H^2(Q,k^\times)\to H^2(G_R,k^\times)\) injective.
Obstructions add under tensor powers and pull back through this
actual equivariant map, so
\[
2\beta=\varepsilon\,\operatorname{inf}\alpha_Q,\qquad8\beta=0.
\]
The right side has order four. Therefore \(\beta\) has exact order
eight, and \(10\beta=2\beta\).
If \(\varepsilon=-1\), the natural module has the inverse paired
multiplier; if \(\varepsilon=1\), its contragredient does.
A specified original calibrated \(P_D\) forces \(P=P_D\), and does
not permit a new choice of sign.

A stabilizer above a point \(x\in D\) meets \(N_R\) trivially.
For any element of \(Q_x\), lift it and uniquely translate its image
point back by a deck element. This proves surjectivity onto \(Q_x\),
hence a stabilizer isomorphism.
The finite étale map identifies completed local rings over the
algebraically closed residue field, so the actual weak break and
tame cotangent actions are unchanged.

The nonzero kernel is noncentral because \(U_R^Q=0\), so its dual
has nontrivial \(Q\)-action. A section would restrict the half-class
equation to an impossible halving of an order-four class in \(C_4\);
the extension is nonsplit.
Any prime-to-five quotient would kill wild inertia and give a
connected tame cover of \(\mathbf P^1\) with at most one branch
value, excluded by Hurwitz.
The accepted
[prescribed weak lift proof](weak_cyclic_reflection_representation_obstruction.md)
then supplies order-\(5,5,2\) generators and inverse conjugacy in
this SAME group.
The entire kernel is a noncentral two-group, so the accepted
[two-kernel rank theorem](../../../Theorems/quotient_geometry/local_actions/psl_eight_five_noncentral_two_kernel_rank_bound.md)
gives \(r\ge97,344\), without needing a particular wild Jordan class.

If the additional actual natural inertia classes are supplied,
pullback preserves them. Their natural and contragredient modules
are irreducible and retain the stated structural properties from
the accepted
[finite-tuple theorem](../../../Theorems/quotient_geometry/local_actions/actual_eight_dimensional_finite_tuple_existence.md)
only as properties of the full \(SL_8\) module, not as curve-existence
input. Any normalized linear-image subgroup surjecting onto \(Q\)
is full \(SL_8\): together with the center it generates \(SL_8\),
so its commutator subgroup already contains the perfect \(SL_8\).

## What is and is not transferred to the original source

The construction starts from the ACTUAL hypothesized \(D\); no
complex surface or abstract extension realizes it.
It produces some actual refinement with the half-class, not a
specified original \(\Gamma\).
If \(T\to D\) is already given, finite étale base change and a
connected component preserve both original étale endpoint maps by
composition on that SAME component.
The pulled original line and the new line may differ by two-torsion
even when their squares agree. No row-generation, carrier identity
or primitive degree-ten comparison follows.
No original endpoint map is put on a quotient curve.
