# Proof: reuse the accepted ordinary exponent-six fiber

[Statement](../../../Theorems/jacobians/ordinary_covers/main_exponent_six_ordinarity.md).

The [accepted backup exponent-six ordinarity theorem](backup_small_abelian_ordinarity.md) provides an ordinary smooth fiber of the same five-branch genus-two family at t=α, α³+α+1=0. Apply the GENERAL prime-to-five exponent-m assertion of [the family Hodge bound](genus_two_abelian_cover_families.md), with m=6. It supplies a NONZERO E∈F5[T] of degree at most4·6⁴+4=5188 outside whose zero set the maximal abelian exponent-six cover is ordinary. Hence a parameter of F5-degree greater than5188 is outside that zero set. The selected MAIN parameter meets this bound. No certificate is recomputed and no new ordinary fiber is claimed.

Every connected abelian exponent-dividing-six cover is dominated étale by this maximal cover. Cartier on a quotient cover is injective because pullback of differentials is injective and Cartier commutes with étale pullback. Thus it is ordinary. The stated five-group extension follows from the already used Crew ordinarity theorem for finite étale p-group covers of an ordinary curve.

In the [actual elliptic spin carrier](../../cartier_and_spin/actual_elliptic_spin_isogeny_carrier.md), a=3 or6 gives an ACTUAL connected cyclic étale degree-a cover Y′→Y and an ACTUAL separating map f:Y′→E′. The linear elliptic automorphism has order divisible by three, so E′ has j=0. Its invariant differential is Cartier-zero in characteristic five: for a j0 equation z²=x³+1 the Cartier scalar is the x⁴ coefficient of (x³+1)², which vanishes. The separating f pulls this form to a NONZERO Cartier-zero regular differential on Y′, contradicting its ordinarity. The accepted backup theorem supplies the same contradiction directly for BACKUP.

The extraction to Y′ uses actual intermediate quotients of the source preserving the endpoint maps as established in the carrier theorem. This argument neither replaces the original maps with arbitrary separable maps nor assumes a simultaneous Galois closure. Characters one and four are not decided here.
