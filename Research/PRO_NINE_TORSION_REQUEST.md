Decide the following exact torsion question for the specified curve:

    W_3(X,O) ∩ J(X)[9] = W_3(X,O) ∩ J(X)[3].             (*)

Give a proof of (*) or an exact counterexample of order nine. This is a
bounded subproblem in Litt's common finite étale cover investigation;
you are not being asked to solve that original problem. All essential
inputs are below; you have no access to our research files.

## Curve

Work over k=algebraic closure of F_5. Choose a∈F_25 with a²+4a+2=0.
Let X be the smooth projective model of y³=F(x), where

    F(x)=x^10+(4a+2)x^9+(a+4)x^8+(3a+1)x^7+3a*x^6
         +4a*x^5+(3a+4)x^4+a*x^3+(3a+3)x²
         +(4a+2)x+(2a+1).

F is squarefree, g(X)=9, and O is the unique point at infinity.
Write W_r(X,O)={ [D−rO] : D≥0, deg D=r }⊂J(X)(k).
All torsion statements concern geometric points. The eleven branch
points are O and P_i=(a_i,0), where F(a_i)=0.

## Proved inputs: use without rederiving

1. The pole semigroup at O is generated by3 and10. A basis of L(nO)
   is x^i y^j with i≥0, 0≤j≤2 and3i+10j≤n.

2. Every rational function on X of degree≤6 belongs to k(x).
   Consequently every nonzero class in W_3 has a UNIQUE effective
   representative of degree3. The degree3 representatives of zero
   are exactly the full x-fibers, including3O and the3P_i.

3. W_3∩J[3] has exactly276 classes: zero, the110 classes of2P+Q
   for distinct branch points P,Q, and the165 classes of three
   distinct branch points. These nonzero classes are distinct.

4. Every three-primary torsion point in W_3 is killed by9 and is
   defined over F_(25^12). Orders27 and higher are already excluded
   by proved divisor rigidity and the actual integral Frobenius module;
   no companion-matrix or lifting hypothesis needs to be checked.
   W_1∩J[9] is already exactly the eleven branch classes, all killed
   by3. The effective degree2 and degree3 cases are unresolved.

5. The following stronger rigidity lemma is available. Let
   ρ(x,y)=(x,ζy), with ζ a primitive cube root in F_25, and let π
   be25-power Frobenius acting on geometric points and divisor classes.
   For γ=ρ^iπ^(4j), i∈{0,1,2}, j≥0, and ξ∈W_3,

       (γ−1)²ξ=0  implies  (γ−1)ξ=0.

   These actions commute and fix every branch point individually.
   The lemma follows from the divisor γ²D−2γD+D and input2; it
   includes repeated support. This uses the action on points, not
   contravariant pullback of divisors under a Frobenius morphism.

## Equivalent bounded divisor problem

Prove that every effective degree3 divisor D with

    div(f)=9D−27O,  f≠0,

has branch support or is a full x-fiber; alternatively exhibit a D
violating that conclusion. Such a violation certifies exact order9
by inputs2–3, not merely divisibility by9.

Necessarily

    f=A(x)+B(x)y+C(x)y²,
    deg A≤9, deg B≤5, deg C≤2,

and its cyclic norm is

    N(f)=A³+B³F+C³F²−3ABCF=cR(x)^9,

where c≠0 and R records the finite part of D projected to the x-line,
with multiplicity; deg R=3−mult_O(D). This norm identity alone is
INSUFFICIENT: it can distribute zeros among different sheets. Retain
the actual divisor condition on X.

## Required scope and output

Cover D containing O, repeated points, branch points, and multiple
points in one x-fiber. At high multiplicity use valuations or Hasse
jets: ordinary derivatives lose fifth-power terms. Include infinity.
Do not discard the known276 solutions of order dividing3.

Prefer a structural proof using little coefficient-specific information,
but an executed exact computation with a checkable completeness
certificate is equally useful. A counterexample on a DIFFERENT curve
does not decide (*). Do not replace this explicit curve by a generic one.

Aim to decide (*), rather than stop with a proposed plan. If you cannot,
give your strongest proved NEW reduction and its exact residual obstacle.
Restating these finite equations, proposing an unexecuted enumeration,
or finding nothing over a few small finite fields is not a new reduction.
