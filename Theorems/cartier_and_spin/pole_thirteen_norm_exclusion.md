# Pole-thirteen supported norms are impossible

Version1, 25 September2026. On the fixed genus-nine X:y^3=P(x), there
is no rational function g with its sole pole of order thirteen at O
and every zero above a root of the specified quartic A.

This is a geometric assertion: coefficients are unrestricted in the
algebraic closure. It is proved by560 exact polynomial identities,
one for each support-multiplicity pattern, together with the complete
leading-coefficient boundary check. No finite-field point search is used.

For two actual finite etale maps to X with the same specified embedded
Cartier line, the established comparison norm has precisely this form
when its comparison pole has degree thirteen. That pole is therefore
excluded in every covering degree. Combining the prior poles below
twelve with the n=12,13 exclusions of
[the quartic trace theorem](quartic_trace_obstruction.md) proves
conditional line/tensor field recognition for every covering degree
at most THIRTEEN.

Comparison pole twelve at covering degree14 and above remains open.
Higher poles and shared-object extraction from an unmarked common
cover are not settled.
