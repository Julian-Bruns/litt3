# Bounded tame orders and integer tensor patterns

Let \(C\to\mathcal S\) be a representable finite étale atlas over an
algebraically closed field of characteristic \(p\ge3\), with \(C\)
smooth projective connected and \(h=2g(C)-2>0\). Assume the effective
smooth proper connected orbifold \(\mathcal S\) has coarse curve
\(\mathbf P^1\) and exactly two branch points: a wild point of inertia
order \(qt\) and different \(\delta\), and a tame point of inertia
order \(d\), with \(1<\delta/(qt)<2\).

There are effective bounds depending only on \(p,h\) for \(t,d\) and
for the weight, common positive zero order, and support size of a
nonzero regular tensor on \(C\) pulled back from a rational tensor on
\(\mathbf P^1\).

More precisely, put \(c=\delta-qt\), \(t=g_0t_0\), \(d=g_0m\),
\(\gcd(m,t_0)=1\), \(D=cm-qt_0>0\), so \(D\mid h\). For a
coordinate \(z\) placing the wild point at zero and the tame point at
infinity, the coarse map \(\pi\) gives
\[
s=\pi^*((dz)^d/z^{d+1}),\quad
\operatorname{div}(s)=eE,\quad e=g_0D,\quad |E|=u=hm/D,
\]
where \(E\) is the reduced wild fiber. The triples \((d,e,u)\) lie in
an effectively bounded finite set. An unbounded sequence of these
atlases therefore has a subsequence with one fixed integer triple;
this supplies neither a fixed tensor/divisor nor a bound for \(q\).
