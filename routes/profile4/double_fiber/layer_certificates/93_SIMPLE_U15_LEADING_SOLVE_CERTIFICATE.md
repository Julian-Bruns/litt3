# Simple \(x=0,u^{15}\) solve

## Status

**proved-text** as a local response calculation under the \(u^4\) leading
form in file 85. Its place in the tower is conditional on the incoming
repeated-\(u^{10}\) layer.

## Statement and proof

Work over \(k=\overline{\mathbb F}_5\). Assume the normalized entry-zero
equation has the \(u^4\) leading form displayed in file 85, with leading
scalar \(a\in k^\times\). On its simple branch \(w=-u+O(u^2)\), let

\[
 v=U_{12}(0),\qquad F_{23}=x(x-1)U_{12}(x).
\]

Apply [file 85](85_SIMPLE_FIVE_STEP_SOLVE_LEMMA.md) with \(m=3\).  The
\(u^{15}\) ODE equation is

\[
 R+2a^{-1}v=0,
\]

with \(R\) independent of \(v\). Since \(2a^{-1}\) is a unit, the equation
uniquely solves \(U_{12}(0)\) without imposing an equation on the older
variables. The formerly displayed coefficient \(2\) is the specialization
\(a=-\pi=1\), which requires the separate condition \(\pi=-1\).
