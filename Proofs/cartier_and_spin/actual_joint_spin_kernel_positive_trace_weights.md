# Proof: canonical infinity linearization and the actual coefficient directions

Version1,3 October2026. [Statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/actual_joint_spin_kernel_positive_trace_weights.md). Focused review pending. No computation or auxiliary source is used.

Every n∈N fixes each original X-field. In particular it preserves the reduced infinity fiber D_i=div(u_i) and acts trivially on the corresponding canonical pulled-back line h_i*O_X(∞). Choose the isomorphism L≅O_T(D_0) carrying u_0 to its canonical section. The canonical N-linearization on O_T(D_0) transports to an N-linearization on L fixing u_0. Since D_i is N-invariant, n*u_i is a scalar multiple χ_i(n)u_i. These scalars form characters. The retained identification L16≅ω_T and θ_i=u_i16 implies χ_i16=ONE: the identification is N-equivariant because it is already calibrated by the fixed nonzero section θ_0=u_016. Thus χ_i takes values in μ16.

The original q*J_Y has its canonical N-linearization, since N⊂G and qn=q. Transport it through q*J_Y≅L²⊗V, and cancel the chosen linearization on L². Each residual automorphism is a constant matrix on V: an endomorphism of L²⊗V is a matrix of regular functions on the smooth connected projective T, all of which are constant. The cocycle law gives an honest linear representation ρ:N→GL(V), not merely a projective representation.

The original map h_i*O_X(2∞)→q*J_Y is N-equivariant because both its source and its embedded image are pulled from the fixed original X-field. Under the identification with L² furnished by u_i², the source's canonical section goes to u_i², which has character χ_i². Its coefficient direction must therefore have the inverse character:
\[
\rho(n)v_i=\chi_i(n)^{-2}v_i.
\]
The prime-to-FIVE finite abelian group N acts semisimply on V. Nonzero eigenvectors with distinct characters are linearly independent, so at most r distinct χ_i² occur. ONE occurs for i=ZERO.

The characters χ_i generate the entire character group of N. Indeed an element annihilated by all χ_i fixes every spin ratio u_i/u_0, hence Γ, and fixes E; the retained primitive equality T=EΓ forces it to be the identity. For a finite abelian group over this algebraically closed field, a character subgroup with trivial common annihilator is the whole dual group.

For c∈C each χ_i(c) is ±ONE. Hence χ_i(c²)=ONE for every i, forcing c²=ONE by the preceding faithful-character assertion. Thus C is elementary abelian TWO. The dual group of N/C is generated by the χ_i². Those characters have order dividingEIGHT, and among them at most r−ONE nontrivial distinct generators occur. A group generated by r−ONE elements of exponent dividingEIGHT has order at most8^(r−ONE), proving the bound.

If the v_i span V, their eigencharacter kernels intersect in exactly kerρ=C. Any scalar ρ(n) fixes the nonzero v_0, so that scalar must beONE. This also identifies the projective kernel with C. Nothing bounds the rank of the elementary abelian kernel C or the dimension of an equal-character section space on an étale source; neither is inferred from the r-dimensional coefficient space.
