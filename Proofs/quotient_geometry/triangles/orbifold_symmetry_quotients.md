# Symmetry quotients of triangle orbifolds

[Statement](../../../Theorems/quotient_geometry/triangles/orbifold_symmetry_quotients.md).

The root construction is functorial for automorphisms preserving the
labeled divisor. Each indicated group G therefore acts on the source
stack X, and X->[X/G] is a representable finite etale G-torsor.
The quotient has coarse curve P1 and trivial generic inertia.

For the transposition, the two a-points form one orbit. Of the two
fixed points on the coarse line, one is the b-point and the other
is unmarked. Their quotient inertia orders are respectively2b and2.
The first stabilizer is cyclic and effective: in a root chart s^b=t
with the coarse involution t->-t, the lift s->zeta*s for a primitive
2b-th root zeta generates mu_(2b), including the original mu_b.
Thus the quotient is P1(2,a,2b).

A3-cycle has two unmarked fixed points and one orbit of the three
marked points. The quotient is P1(3,3,a). For S3, the marked orbit
has inertia2a; the other fixed points of transpositions form one orbit
of inertia2, and those of3-cycles one orbit of inertia3. The same local
chart proves effectivity at the marked orbit. Thus the quotient is
P1(2,3,2a). A tame effective stack curve with these cyclic stabilizers
and no generic inertia is precisely the corresponding root stack.

Composing finite etale maps proves the assertion about atlases.
