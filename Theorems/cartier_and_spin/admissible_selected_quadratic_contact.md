# Short quadratic contact at every selected degree-ten endpoint

Version1,1 October2026. Retain an ACTUAL primitive admissible degree-ten source, its annihilator $u$, and the short and finite frames of [critical quadratic incidence](admissible_critical_quadratic_incidence.md). Thus $w=W+a$, $a=Z/y$, and
\[
F=v\phi^2+\phi S+t^3,\quad \phi=T^5+q_s,\quad D=S',\quad U(W)=uD(W),\quad U^2-FQ=DV_2.
\]
Let $P$ be any of the nine selected finite endpoints. Its five selected sheets have $\operatorname{ord}W_i\ge1$, $\operatorname{ord}\phi_i=3$ and $\operatorname{ord}u_i=2$; every other sheet has $u_i$ a unit. Retain the actual primitive leading content $\operatorname{ord}_P v=\sum_{G\text{ above }P}g_i$, the universal jets $U_0\in r^3R$, $U_1\in r^2R$, $U_2\in rR$, and $U_5=v\rho$, $U_4=vm_5+s_4\rho$.

Then the constant coefficient of the SHORT quadratic satisfies
\[
Q_s(0)(P)=0.
\]
Equivalently, $Q_f(a(P))(P)=0$ for $Q_f(T)=Q_s(T-a)$. No vanishing of $Q_f(0)(P)$ is asserted.

This holds through zeros of $v$ and $s_4$, repeated nonselected root values, and repeated specialized critical roots. It uses no distinct selected slopes or function-field squarefreeness of $D$.

More precisely, write $F\bmod r=T^5H_0(T)$ and $U\bmod r=T^3L(T)$. Then $H_0(0)\ne0$, $D\bmod r=H_0'$, and every nonzero root $\lambda$ of $H_0$ of multiplicity $m$ satisfies $(T-\lambda)^{m-1}\mid L$. If $v(P)=s_4(P)=0$, then $\deg L\le0$ and $\deg H_0\le3$; the degree-one case is excluded by $H_0'(0)=0$.

These are local consequences on an already actual source. They do not reconstruct a source or supply an opposite map.
