# Small critical-root test and the remaining endpoint content

Version1, 27 September2026. In the constant degree140 family, at a
geometric square specialization the fixed x-content of the full norm
is supported only on the three distinct roots r_i of t, with
\[
c_W(x)=\prod_{i=1}^3(x-r_i)^{n_i},\qquad
0\le n_i\le3,\qquad \#\{i:n_i\ge2\}\le1.
\]
In particular deg c_W<=5. The spectral x-discriminant is identically
zero in the scale variable exactly when some n_i>=2. Such a fiber
must involve two or three DISTINCT simple-content points; higher
individual content and all common critical zeros are excluded.

There is a smaller local equation for those simple-content points.
Translate the primitive by L0 at p over r_i and put tau=t(x). Write
\[
f_\lambda=\lambda(W^5+\tau^3M)^2+
 (W^5+\tau^3M)(2aW^3+3bW^2+cW+d)+\tau^3N,
\]
where c is in(tau), d,M are units, and Md+N is in(tau^2).
Let a0,b0,d0,m0 be the residue values, c1=(c/tau)(p), and
ell0=((Md+N)/tau^2)(p). At a simple-content point of any possible
square specialization, b0 is nonzero, and content is positive iff
\[
J_p=b_0^5\ell_0-d_0c_1^5+2m_0b_0^4c_1^2=0.
\]
This equivalence allows a0=0 and retains all geometric parameters.
It gives a two-sheet incidence test before forming the degree140 norm.
It does not decide whether any such incidence satisfies the square equations.
