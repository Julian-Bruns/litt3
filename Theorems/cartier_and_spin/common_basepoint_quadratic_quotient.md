# Quotient multiplication for a common-base-point new-line plane

Version1,23September2026. Retain the actual rank-two new-line
orbit S subset B_Y of degree zero, with old radicals spanning
rank four. Its Frobenius evaluation has base2P_* and residual
simple Wronskian zeros Q1,Q2, distinct from P_*. Put C=Y^(1),
P1=F_Y(P_*), Qnu1=F_Y(Qnu), L=det S and omega=omega_C.

Multiplication of primitive representatives modulo S gives
\[
0\to\mathcal H=L^4\omega^{-2}(2P1)
\to\operatorname{Sym}^2S
\to(B_Y/S)(-P1)\to0.
\]
The kernel is a nowhere-zero binary quadratic form. Its discriminant
is zero if S is isotropic. Otherwise its exact divisor is
D_S+Q11+Q21, where D_S is the degree-two restricted-pairing
divisor and contains P1.

In the isotropic case there is a canonical saturated line
\[
\kappa=L^2\omega^{-1}(P1)\subset S,\quad\deg\kappa=-1,
\quad\operatorname{div}(F_Y^*\kappa\to\omega_Y)
=3P_*+2Q1+2Q2.
\]
For every actual orbit line, let E_i be its degree5d pairing-loss
divisor, R_i^circ=R_i-q^*P_*, and Delta_i the prescribed degree12d
old/new contact. Then
\[
\operatorname{div}(\lambda_i\wedge q^{(1)*}\kappa)
=(R_i^{\circ})^{(1)}+E_i,
\qquad
\operatorname{div}\langle\ell_i,q^{(1)*}\kappa\rangle
=\Delta_i+(R_i^{\circ})^{(1)}+2E_i.
\]
Every E_i avoids the common P1-fiber; its full-orbit sum descends
to an effective divisor of degree5M/8 on C. Individual E_i are
not proved to descend.

In the nonisotropic case the discriminant has an odd zero. Its
quadratic splitting field is ramified and cannot lie in the actual
etale source field. The modification loss of the degree9d HN line
at the common fiber equals mult_(P1)(D_S)*q^(1)*P1, with full
multiplicity. If D_S=2P1 its entire loss is2q^(1)*P1. If
D_S=P1+R1, the residual loss over R1 has degree between d and8d;
when R1=Qnu1 the total loss is at least11d.

Neither pairing case is excluded. In particular the actual lambda_i
are not asserted to factor the quadratic kernel, so its nonsplitting
is not a contradiction.
