# Canonical ring and reduced-marking spectrum of a cored span

Over an algebraically closed field of positive characteristic, let
X <-f- Z -g-> Y be an actual finite etale span of smooth projective
connected hyperbolic curves with a core. Let S=[W/G] be the effective
quotient constructed from a simultaneous etale Galois refinement, with
G generated by the TWO endpoint deck groups. Write B for its coarse
curve, n_X=deg(X/S), and (b_i,e_i,delta_i) for its local branch data.

The full shared canonical ring is naturally

    f*R(X) intersect g*R(Y)
      = direct_sum_(d>=0) H^0(B, omega_B^d(sum_i floor(d delta_i/e_i)b_i)).

Products are the ordinary products of rational tensors. In particular,
when B=P^1 its degree-d dimension is

    max(0, 1-2d+sum_i floor(d delta_i/e_i)).

ALL pairs of reduced endpoint divisors with f*D_X=g*D_Y are precisely
pullbacks of reduced divisors on S. Their possible degrees on X are

    m n_X + sum_(i in I) n_X/e_i,
    m>=0 an integer, I any subset of the exceptional points.

Consequently a canonically sized marking, deg D_X=2g(X)-2 and
deg D_Y=2g(Y)-2, exists if and only if

    deg(omega_S)=m+sum_(i in I) 1/e_i

for such m,I. In particular it cannot exist if
0<deg(omega_S)<1/max(1,e_1,...,e_r).

For either still-unexcluded large genus-nine atlas signature

    (e_w,delta_w,e_t,n_X)=(1000,1143,7,112000)
                      or (3000,3143,21,336000),

the canonical marking criterion fails: the smallest nonempty compatible
marking on X has 112 points, whereas 2g(X)-2=16. The first nonzero
shared canonical weights are respectively 7 and 21; the first weights
with at least two independent sections are 7000 and 21000.

These are conditional consequences IF a cored span has the stated
common-core quotient. The signatures are not asserted to be realized.
This makes a limitation of the marked-quotient route explicit; it is
not a new common-cover exclusion. Verification: major audit PASS,
`/root/cored_ring_normality_major_check`, 2026-09-06.
