# Higher-level opers do not remove the current obstruction

2026-09-09. Targeted literature check, not a new common-cover theorem.

Source: Wakabayashi, *Arithmetic liftings and 2d TQFT for dormant opers
of higher level*, [arXiv:2209.08528v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.08528v3),
revised2025-09-05. Read the proofs around8.17,8.21,8.28–8.34,9.2–9.9
and the discussion10.8. Here p>2 and rank is2.

## Two useful boundaries

1. A higher-level dormant oper can be ordinary only if its level-one
   reduction is ordinary (Proposition8.21). Thus adding higher differential
   operators cannot repair the nonordinary dormant companion on the
   ACTUAL common double in the AP residual case. This is distinct from
   ordinary pullback along an etale cover.

2. Merely demanding arbitrarily high level does not exclude any fixed
   level-one dormant oper. The proof of8.21 shows that the finite level-
   reduction morphism Op_N→Op_1 is flat; Op_1 is irreducible and Op_N
   is nonempty (8.17/8.28). Its image is therefore open, closed and
   nonempty, hence all of Op_1. For a fixed geometric level-one object,
   each level has a finite nonempty set of extensions. The finite-branching
   tree of their restrictions has an infinite branch. Consequently the
   object has a compatible extension through all finite levels. This does
   NOT say every level-N object extends, or that extensions are unique.

The distinction matters: such extensions on two endpoints need not MATCH
on their specified common source. No simultaneous higher-level existence
has been proved in this project. Arbitrary choices cannot be synchronized
by applying the preceding one-leg argument twice.

## Why the diagonal-lifting theorem is not the missing bridge

Theorem9.4 starts with a specified higher-level dormant oper on the special
fiber of an ALREADY CHOSEN W_N curve. It lifts that oper, assuming the
curve is N-ordinary; the proof uses invertibility at every lower level
(9.2–9.3). It does not canonically choose a curve lift from our single
level-one datum or identify the two actual sources. Remark9.7 explicitly
distinguishes this construction from the active p-adic Teichmuller lifts.

Remark8.34 states that the existence of a closed moduli point ordinary
at every level was not known to the author. Thus generic statements at
each finite level do not furnish such a point over bar(F5).

Keep admissible_two_leg_w2_lifts at W2: no W3 or full-Witt implication
is supplied by this literature. The higher-level route is parked unless
we obtain a genuine TWO-LEG lifting mechanism or another invariant of
compatible choices, not their individual existence.

## Generalized p-adic Teichmuller theory has the same existence boundary

Mochizuki's [2002 introduction](https://www.numdam.org/article/AST_2002__278__1_0.pdf),
Sections2.3--2.4, also treats nonordinary points of its perfect moduli
spaces Q_Pi. These points already PARAMETRIZE full Frobenius-invariant
crystals; their associated curve lifts are then recovered uniquely.
Theorem2.8 is an immersion theorem, not surjectivity from characteristic-p
nilpotent data. Theorem2.12 concerns closedness of a generalized ordinary
locus, not existence of a lift at our point. Neither supplies matching
crystals on our two endpoints. The stated binary existence theorem also
has a large-p hypothesis, not the p=5 case at hand. Read these sections
2026-09-09; no new result was extracted or claimed.

The strengthened etale_refinement_deformations v2 now puts the exact
joint problem in a complete local quotient of W(k)[[t1,t2,t3]] when one
endpoint has genus two. Nonliftability is p-nilpotence in this ring.
Once ONE full endpoint lift is fixed, the problem is W(k)/(p^e): there
is a unique diagram through each allowed Witt level. This removes
choice/compactness ambiguity, but does NOT bound e or make it infinite.

## Barsotti--Tate groups: stronger input, a real two-leg conclusion

See canonical compatible_bt_lifting, proved2026-09-09 from the full
proofs of Xia arXiv:1303.2954v1 Sections3--4, especially4.9--4.11,
and Krishnamoorthy arXiv:1711.04797v2 Corollary10.8. Compatible full
everywhere-versal height-two BT groups DO identify both lifted sources,
without indigenous ordinariness. Compatible BT_N groups identify the
sources through W_m for the safe range N>=m+2. Arbitrarily large
compatible truncations therefore force a full mixed-characteristic lift,
even if the choices and common etale refinements vary with N. This uses
the new joint deformation-ring criterion, not compactness of group choices.

Do not misread Xia's abstract: finite BT_N data gives only finite-range
UNIQUENESS of the curve lift. Theorem4.11 supplies a nonunique full
one-leg lift and is insufficient to identify two full sources. The
paper's Witt indexing is inconsistent; the canonical result deliberately
uses a conservative range.
