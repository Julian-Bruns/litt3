# Proof: a secant calculation and actual etale trace

This is a local continuation of the returned admissible-line report.
The [Sage reconstruction](../../scripts/arithmetic/admissible_trace_secant.py)
generates the complete one-variable remainders and Bézout multipliers;
the [independent standard-library verifier](../../scripts/arithmetic/verify_admissible_trace_secant.py)
checks them.
No independent-agent audit is claimed.

## The class and its seven coordinates

Write Z=sum_{P(r)=0}(r,0). Since div(y)=Z-10O, division by y
identifies O(12O-Z) with O(2O). Apply this identification to the
boundary of B|Z from
\[
0\to O(12O-Z)\to O(12O)\to O_Z(12O)\to0.
\]
Up to a common nonzero scalar, Serre duality identifies epsilon with
the functional on H^0(O(14O)) whose values on
(1,x,x^2,x^3,x^4,y,xy) are
\[
(m_0,m_1,m_2,m_3,m_4,0,0)=(14,20,16,15,23,0,0).
\]
Indeed a Cech representative is B/y; pairing with x^j theta reduces
to the x^{-1} coefficient of x^j B/P times the fixed residue factor
-3. The last two pairings vanish by cubic character. The Hankel matrix
H=(m_{i+j})_{0<=i,j<=2} has determinant [23], hence is invertible.

## Every length-three divisor is covered

It suffices to treat effective G_0 of degree exactly three, since a
smaller divisor can be enlarged. If epsilon dies after adding G_0,
its dual functional factors through evaluation of O(14O) on G_0.

First suppose G_0 contains O with multiplicity a. The five sections
1,x,...,x^4 have orders14,11,8,5,2 there. If a=1 or2, they contribute
nothing at O, and their moments are represented on a finite scheme
of length at most two away from O. Multiplication by x on that
scheme has a monic annihilating polynomial of degree at most two,
contradicting det H!=0. If a=3, only the x^4 section can contribute;
then m_0=m_1=m_2=m_3=0, again a contradiction. This includes all
mixed and nonreduced boundary divisors at infinity.

Now G_0 is affine. If the algebra generated by x in O_{G_0} had
dimension at most two, its monic annihilator D of degree at most
two would give epsilon(x^j D)=0 for j=0,1,2. This gives a nonzero
kernel vector for H. Hence O_{G_0}=k[x]/(D), with D monic cubic.
This argument also excludes the fat branch schemes on which x
is not a length-three coordinate. No assumption of distinct points
has been made.

Write D=x^3+t x^2+d_1 x+d_0. The relations epsilon(D)=epsilon(xD)=0
determine
\[
d_0=[4]+[20]t,\qquad d_1=[14]+2t.
\]
The functional on k[x]/D has the residue description
ell(a)=Res_{x=infty}(-N a/D)dx, where
\[
N=[14]x^2+([20]+[14]t)x+[19]+[18]t.
\]
The actual y-coordinate in O_{G_0} satisfies y^3=P(x).
The equations ell(y)=ell(xy)=0 say that the remainder of Ny
modulo D is constant: the first equation kills its x^2 coefficient,
and the second kills its x coefficient. Thus P N^3 must be constant
modulo D, including when D has repeated roots.

The exact positive-degree coefficients of that remainder are
\[
R_1(t)=[9]+[11]t+[20]t^2+[21]t^3,
\qquad R_2(t)=[17]+[19]t^2.
\]
They have the polynomial Bezout identity
\[
([11]+[19]t)R_1(t)+(3+t+[9]t^2)R_2(t)=1.
\]
They cannot vanish together over any extension of F25. This proves
(1) for all geometric divisors, not just rational divisors.

## An actual candidate supplies a killed class upstairs

For a saturated degree-zero line with adjunction 2Delta, the
[primitive reconstruction](admissible_line_reconstruction.md) gives
q=f+beta^5 and
\[
\operatorname{div}q=3(R-\Delta)-5G-10H,
\quad H=h^*O,\quad\deg G=n.
\]
This divisor identity does not require the line to be order five.
Put b=beta y. At a finite point of h^*Z outside G, regularity of q
forces b=B(x) on the fiber. If the point occurs in G with
multiplicity g, b has pole order at most g-1. At other finite points
its pole order is at most g. At an infinity point its pole order
is at most12+g: f has pole order7, whereas q has pole order at
most10+5g, so beta has pole order at most2+g.

Consequently b is a section of O(12H+G) lifting the image of
B|h^*Z after twisting by G. At a branch point in G both restrictions
are zero in the local O(G) frame: b has the stronger bound g-1.
The pulled-back boundary class therefore vanishes in H^1(O(2H+G)).

Finite etale trace maps O(2H+G) into O(2O+h_*G). This follows
pointwise from the sum of the unramified Laurent expansions; the
largest pole order is no greater than the sum of the multiplicities
in h_*G. The composite on the original O(2O) is multiplication by n.
Thus (2) implies n epsilon=0 in H^1(O(2O+h_*G)). For n<=3 this
contradicts (1). No averaging over a Galois closure was used.

## Stronger contact bound and stable modifications

For an arbitrary saturated L!=lambda_X in P_X, if its contact is
I+G_0 with I<=R_X^(1) reduced, the same primitive calculation gives
\[
\operatorname{div}(f+\beta^5)
=3(R_X-I^{\sim})-5G_0^{\sim}-10O+J,
\quad J\ge0.
\]
The extra effective divisor J adds no poles. The preceding local
b-bounds still kill epsilon after adding G_0^{sim}. Hence deg G_0
must be at least four. This strengthens the returned residual-two
bound without imposing a degree or torsion hypothesis on L.

For M saturated in Q_I, let L be its saturation in P_X. Unless
M=lambda_X, write M=L(-Z') where Z'<=I consists of transverse
modification fibers. Its residual contact has degree
9-|I|-deg M. The new bound gives deg M<=5-|I|. Comparing this
with deg lambda_X=-2 and slope(Q_I)=(7-|I|)/2 proves stability
for4<=|I|<=10 and semistability at3,11. The count is
sum_{m=4}^{10} binomial(13,m)=7722.

For a negative-degree rank-two bundle a nonnegative line is the
unique destabilizing line. After passing to a finite etale Galois
closure it would descend. Thus failure for a fixed modification
cannot be repaired by a further etale cover. This does not exclude
new, non-descending selected divisors on that cover.

## Trace with the maximum pole divisor

For G=sum_Q g_Q Q define D_h(G)=sum_P max_{Q over P}g_Q P.
The actual finite étale local branches identify their parameters
with a parameter downstairs. Trace is the sum of those Laurent
series and has pole order at most the maximum of their pole orders.
It therefore maps h_*O_S(2h^*O+G) to O_X(2O+D_h(G)).
Composing pullback and trace multiplies epsilon by deg h. If that
degree is prime to five and deg D_h(G)<=3, base nonvanishing gives
the asserted contradiction. No invariance of an individual G is
being assumed.
