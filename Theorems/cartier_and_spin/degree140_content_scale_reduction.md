# A positive-content square has one of four rational scales

Version1, 27 September2026. Work on a one-sheet content chart of the
normalized constant degree140 family, with selected p=(r,wv),
q=w^3=P(r)/v^3 and H=hw. Use the scaled source fhat=f_lambda/w,
mu=lambda/w, and the local parameter tau=t(x). Its translated critical
quadratic is a W^2+b W+c, with a,b,c the scaled coefficients and
c in(tau). Write d for its scaled cubic constant term and
ell=(M d+q^3 z_c^10)/tau^2, where z_c=y/w and M=(Q-L0^5)/t^3.

At p set A=a(p),B=b(p),D=d(p), m=M(p),
c1=(c/tau)(p), c2=partial_tau(c/tau)(p),
b1=partial_tau b(p), d1=partial_tau d(p),
m1=partial_tau M(p), ell1=partial_tau ell(p), and omega=-c1/B.
Then define the rational function
\[
\kappa=\ell_1+d_1\omega^5+2m_1B\omega^2
 +3m b_1\omega^2+2m A\omega^3+m c_2\omega.
\]
The derivatives hold H,q fixed and follow the chosen sheet of
z_c^3=P/q. B and m are nonzero at every selected positive-content
square. For the other sheets p_j=(r,w[11]^j v), j=0,1,2, define
A_j,B_j,D_j by evaluation of the same scaled a,b,d, with p0=p.

Every possible square on this chart has one of the four scales
\[
\mu=-\kappa/m^2,\qquad
\mu=\frac{A_j^3B_j^3+A_j^5D_j}{B_j^5}\quad(j=0,1,2; B_j\ne0).
\]
Keep mu nonzero and every previous open condition. Branches may coincide;
no simplicity assumption is made. If B_j=0, that large-critical branch
has no finite scale root at a possible square.

Thus the remaining one-sheet square decision reduces to at most twelve
rational-scale specializations over the three sparse curves. The scale
is no longer a free parameter in this incidence. These necessary scales
are not asserted to make the full residual square.
