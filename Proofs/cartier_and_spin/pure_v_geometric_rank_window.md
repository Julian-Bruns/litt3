## Exhaustive support from module identities

Use the established invertible coordinate matrix M of the quartic scroll,
and write v=Mu. Partition projective space by its first nonzero coordinate:
\(u_0=\cdots=u_{h-1}=0,u_h=1\), for \(0\le h\le5\).
For \(h<5\), take the cokernel of the 23 rows of T in a free module of
rank fifteen over the polynomial ring of that stratum. Its nonzero fibers
are exactly the rank-drop points. Every certified module identity remains
valid on every geometric fiber; no rational-point exhaustion is used.

On the main stratum write \(u=(1,a,b,c,d,e)\) and
\[
q_0=b-a^2,\quad q_1=d-ac,\quad q_2=e-bc,\quad
w=q_0^3\overline e_0.
\]
The exact certificate gives
\[
G(q_0)w=0,\quad (a,b,c,d,e)_i w=H_i(q_0)w,
\quad q_i^3\overline e_j=B_{ij}(q_0)w.
\]
Here G is monic, squarefree of degree44 with nonzero constant term;
the H and B polynomials have degree at most43. Their exact coefficients
are in the retained shape and containment files, and the verifier checks
all 51 module identities against the actual T rows.

Off the scroll some q_i is nonzero. The last identity makes the whole
nonzero fiber generated by w; hence w is nonzero and q_0 is nonzero too.
The first identities force a root z of G and coordinates
\((a,b,c,d,e)=(H_1(z),\ldots,H_5(z))\). Conversely every root gives ranks
(14,8), and \(b-a^2=z\), by exact field calculations. Thus these are
44 distinct points with no lost residue-field extensions.

On successive boundary strata, exact scalar annihilators give respectively:

- \(u=(0,1,[6],[17],[4],[4])\), the known extra F25 point;
- \(u=(0,0,1,0,0,u_5)\), a scroll line;
- \(u=(0,0,0,1,u_4,u_4^2)\), a scroll conic;
- no support on the next stratum;
- the final scroll point \((0,0,0,0,0,1)\).

Two polynomial kernel columns of T and two of Q on the dense scroll
chart establish rank bounds (13,7); closedness extends them to the whole
irreducible scroll. A sample attaining these ranks proves generic equality.
The degree44 polynomial factors into two irreducible quadratics and ten
irreducible quartics. Exact kernels and nonzero minors prove ranks (14,8)
at all their points, as well as at the additional F25 point.

For each isolated point, the tangent matrix
\(L(\partial T/\partial v_i)c\), with left and right kernels L,c, has
rank five on the affine projective chart. The maximal-minor scheme has
zero tangent space there and its local ring is its residue field, by
Nakayama's lemma. No scheme structure along the scroll is asserted.
All ten stability-boundary points have ranks (15,9).

The verifier replays 1,333 module membership identities with 480,763

## Local continuation: every rank-drop point is nonperiodic

The established reversible reconstruction identifies
\(\ker Q(v^{25})\) with actual invariant maps
\(F_{\rm abs}^{2*}R_v\to O_X(-O)\).
Because the coefficient matrices lie in F25, ranks at v and v^{25}
coincide. Every T rank-drop point therefore has such a nonzero map.
Its image is a negative-degree line quotient, so the Frobenius pullback
is not semistable.

Each nonzero R_v is semistable of degree zero. If any positive strict
period r existed, choose a multiple of r at least two and pull back this
negative quotient to that iterate. Frobenius is flat on a smooth curve;
the pulled-back quotient remains a negative line quotient. Identifying
the final iterate with R_v contradicts semistability. Thus every point
of the entire scroll-plus-45-point support has no positive period.

At a stable strict second return, the supplied actual Hom interpretation
requires rankT=14 and rankQ=9. The geometric support calculation excludes
that window. The rank-15 complement is excluded for SECOND returns by
this necessary condition, not for all future periods. Nor is any
conclusion drawn for the other thirteen extension coordinates.
