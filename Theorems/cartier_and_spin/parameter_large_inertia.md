# Large parameter inertia and Galois abelian quotients

Version1, 25 September2026. Work over the algebraic closure of F5,
with the fixed polynomials P,A and F25 convention of the
[fixed curve](../../Definitions/fixed_pair.md). Let S be a smooth
proper connected curve, with separating functions t,u,v and a scalar
epsilon!=0 satisfying
\[
A(v)=\epsilon^4t^{-13}A(u),\qquad
(dv/du)^3P(u)^2=\epsilon^{-17}t^{48}P(v)^2.
\]
Assume P(v)/P(u) is a cube. Each endpoint function is unramified away
from the roots of P and infinity, and has index1 or3 above those values.
Assume the fibers of t over0 and infinity are unramified. No degree
bound, Galois hypothesis, or field bound on epsilon is needed below.

There is at most ONE value of t admitting a point whose ramification
index belongs to
\[
\{4\}\ \cup\ \{e:e\ge7\}.
\]
This includes indices divisible by5. The claim does not restrict the
number of branch values with indices2,3,5 or6.

Consequently, if k(S)/k(t) is Galois, the prime-to-five part of its
abelianization has exponent dividing6. In particular, it has no cyclic
quotient of order4,9 or a prime at least7. This excludes every cyclic
parameter degree divisible by4 under these hypotheses, and proves the
complete cyclic-quartic exclusion in the actual pole-twelve comparison
problem. V4, D4 and S4 are not excluded by this group restriction.

There is also an actual-field conclusion. An admissible u has no second
A-preimage in k(S): A(w)=A(u) implies w=u. If in addition k(S)/k(t)
is Galois and k(S)=k(t,u)=k(t,v)=k(u,v), then
\[
k(S)=k(t,A(u))=k(t,A(v))=k(A(u),A(v)).
\]

The finite signature and boundary certificates are reused from the
returned cyclic-quartic proof. The large-index statement and general
Galois-quotient consequences are local continuations proved here.
Their hypotheses have not been derived for arbitrary higher comparison
branches or for an arbitrary unmarked common cover.
