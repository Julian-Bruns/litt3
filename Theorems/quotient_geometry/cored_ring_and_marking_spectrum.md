# Canonical rings and divisors on a cored quotient

Version2,2026-09-14.

Over an algebraically closed field of arbitrary characteristic, let
\(X\xleftarrow f Z\xrightarrow gY\) be an actual finite étale span of
smooth projective connected hyperbolic curves with a core.
Use its simultaneous étale Galois refinement \(W\), the group \(G\)
generated by both endpoint deck groups, and \(\mathcal S=[W/G]\).
Write \(\pi:W\to B=W/G\), \(n=|G|\), \(n_X=\deg(X/\mathcal S)\),
and \(R=\sum_P\delta_P[P]\), with branch data \((b_i,e_i,\delta_i)\).

The full shared canonical ring is naturally
\[
 f^*R(X)\cap g^*R(Y)
 =\bigoplus_{m\ge0}
 H^0\!\left(B,\Omega_B^{\otimes m}
       \left(\left\lfloor\frac{m\pi_*R}{n}\right\rfloor\right)\right),
 \qquad
 \left\lfloor\frac{m\pi_*R}{n}\right\rfloor
   =\sum_i\left\lfloor\frac{m\delta_i}{e_i}\right\rfloor[b_i].
\]
Products are products of rational tensors. For \(m\ge1\), its
dimension is \(g(B)\) if \(m=1\) and \(\pi\) is tame, and otherwise
\[
 (2m-1)(g(B)-1)+\sum_i\lfloor m\delta_i/e_i\rfloor.
\]

All compatible reduced endpoint divisors \(f^*D_X=g^*D_Y\) are
pullbacks of reduced divisors on \(\mathcal S\). Their degrees on \(X\)
are exactly
\[
 a n_X+\sum_{i\in I}n_X/e_i,\qquad
 a\ge0,\quad I\subseteq\{\text{exceptional points}\}.
\]
A compatible marking of canonical size exists exactly when
\(\deg\Omega_{\mathcal S}=a+\sum_{i\in I}1/e_i\).
In particular it cannot exist if
\(0<\deg\Omega_{\mathcal S}<1/\max(1,e_1,\ldots,e_r)\).

If the shared degree-one space is zero, then \(B=\mathbf P^1\) and
\(\sum_i\lfloor\delta_i/e_i\rfloor\le1\).
For characteristic \(p\ge3\), put \(h=2g(X)-2\). Either the quotient
is tame and \(n_X\le42h\), or it has one wild point with
\(1<\delta/e<2\) and at least one tame point. Three or more tame
points give \(n_X<2h\); two give \(n_X<6h\), except for tame orders
\((2,2)\), which require a positive divisor of \(h\) congruent to
\(-1\pmod{p-1}\). For a jointly minimal span, \(\deg(Z/Y)\le n_X\).

For either necessary genus-nine signature
\[
 (e_w,\delta_w,e_t,n_X)=(1000,1143,7,112000)
                 \quad\text{or}\quad(3000,3143,21,336000),
\]
the smallest compatible nonempty marking on \(X\) has112 points.
The first nonzero shared weights are7 and21; the first weights with
two sections are7000 and21000. These are conditional consequences
for the full core quotient; neither signature is asserted to exist.
