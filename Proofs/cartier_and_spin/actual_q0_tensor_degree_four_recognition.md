# Proof: every degree-four alternative is covered

Version1,3 October2026. Independently accepted in the [whole degree-four audit](../../Research/audits/Q0_TENSOR_DEGREE_FOUR_WHOLE_RECOGNITION_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). This proof combines their exact scopes; it contains no new calculation.

Let k(C0)=k(X1)k(X2) inside the ACTUAL original k(S), and take its smooth projective normalization. Ramification transitivity in the actual finite étale maps S→X_i makes C0→X_i finite étale. If [S:C0]=b, their degrees are d=FOUR/b∈{ONE,TWO,FOUR}. The tensor equality descends to C0 by injectivity of separating pullback. This auxiliary quotient does not presume that the original Y-map descends, and S together with that actual map is retained.

For d≤TWO use the [low-degree recognition theorem](actual_q0_tensor_low_degree_recognition.md), obtaining equal embedded X-fields. Suppose d=FOUR. The [elliptic joint-field alternative](actual_q0_tensor_elliptic_joint_field_cubic_alternative.md) rules out root index ONE below degree SIX. Hence the actual index is THREE, and the [cubic coarse reduction](actual_q0_tensor_cubic_coarse_curve_reduction.md) applies. Its joint x-curve B has genus at most TWO. The possible FOUR unramified infinity points would make z constant and lower the joint x-degree to at most TWO, so there is ONE common simple infinity point Q and two distinct triple poles.

If g(B)=ZERO, the [rational quotient theorem](actual_q0_tensor_degree_four_rational_quotient_exclusion.md) excludes all three μ6 partition types. If g(B)=TWO, the independently accepted [whole genus-two theorem](actual_q0_tensor_degree_four_genus_two_quotient_exclusion.md) excludes both Q positions and every common derivative class.

It remains to cover g(B)=ONE. The independently accepted [elliptic normal forms](actual_q0_tensor_degree_four_elliptic_hyperelliptic_form.md) retain exactly coprime and proportional odd numerators. The independently accepted [Weierstrass-Q theorem](actual_q0_tensor_degree_four_elliptic_weierstrass_pole_exclusion.md) excludes that entire position in both forms. At non-Weierstrass Q, the [whole proportional theorem](actual_q0_tensor_degree_four_elliptic_proportional_exclusion.md) excludes every proportional class, and the [whole coprime theorem](actual_q0_tensor_degree_four_elliptic_coprime_exclusion.md) excludes every coprime class. The latter includes separate trivial and marked-class deletions plus the final two-root quadratic packet; every sign and degree drop remains included.

Thus no jointly minimal degree-FOUR case exists. The embedded original X-fields are equal. Since both h_i induce that same extension of k(S), their identification differs by a k-automorphism of the fixed smooth X, namely an element of C3. Both maps stay on their original source S. No conclusion about arbitrary higher degree, a simultaneous Y-quotient or the unmarked common-cover problem has been inferred.
