# The small critical traces need only fixed endpoint residues

Version1,29 September2026. Use the constant degree140 critical family and
the notation of [the normalized trace theorem](degree140_normalized_critical_trace.md).
For m=0 or -1 and an affine regular function f on X, every coefficient of
Tr(f phi^m v) is a sum of residues at the two fixed infinity branches and
the points above the nine fixed endpoints t=0. No other moving point or
degree140 algebra inversion is required.

The endpoint contribution is zero above coefficient2 when m=0, and above
coefficient5 when m=-1. Thus the degree-nine trace T1 and degree-ten trace
Tx have only three endpoint-corrected coefficients each. All coefficients
of Q1,Qx,Qx2 are given by the same fixed-endpoint construction.

The formula below is an identity in the rational parameter field. It is
used directly where its local unit denominators are nonzero; on other
admitted charts its continuation must be taken as the actual normalized
function-field trace. This statement does not assert that a generic local
coordinate denominator is a unit at a normalization jump.
