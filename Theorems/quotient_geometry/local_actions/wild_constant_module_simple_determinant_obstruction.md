# A constant wild module cannot have a simple determinant defect without a target one-block

Version1,3 October2026. Whole independent review [PASS](../../../Research/audits/RANK_THREE_SOCLE_AND_ORTHOGONAL_ROW_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). No global field or bundle descent is inferred.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p>ZERO, A=k[[t]], and let σ generate the cyclic group C_p of orderp acting on A with σ(t)=t+O(t²). Let F be a free rank-n A-module with a compatible semilinear C_p action. Suppose its special fiber F₀ is a k[C_p]-module having NO Jordan block of sizeONE. Let W be an n-dimensional CONSTANT k[C_p]-module. Any equivariant A-linear map
\[
W\otimes_k A\longrightarrow F
\]
which is generically invertible has determinant order different fromONE.

Compatible projective actions may be used after their common restriction to this cyclic p-group has been normalized to honest actions. The normalization must be shared by source and target; arbitrary unrelated scalar choices do not suffice.

The conclusion excludes only a simple local determinant defect. It does not prohibit a perfect map, defects of higher order, or maps from a source with nonconstant semilinear fiber transport.
