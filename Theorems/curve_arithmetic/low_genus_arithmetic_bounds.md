# Explicit arithmetic counts in genera two and three

Use the [arithmetic reduction conventions](../../Definitions/arithmetic_curve_reductions.md).
Let S_h be the compact arithmetic complex curves of genus h, up to
isomorphism, and E_(h,p) their geometric potentially good reductions.
Including noncongruence lattices and all places, twists and residue
conjugates, for every prime p one has

| h | Upper bound for both cardinalities |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | <2^2000000 |
| 3 | <2^80000000 |

In fact |E_(h,p)|≤|S_h|: all reductions arise from one fixed p-adic
place applied to the Galois-stable set S_h.

The mod-two homology cover of an arithmetic genus-h curve has genus
G=1+2^(2h)(h−1), and its group lies in a maximal-order norm-one group.
For h=2,3 these cover genera are17,129. The original surface group
need not itself lie in such a norm-one group.

For any fixed smooth proper hyperbolic curve A over any algebraically
closed field of characteristic zero, fewer than2^80000000 genus-three
curves admit a common finite etale cover with A. If A is nonarithmetic,
the sharper bound2^17000 holds. No corelessness assumption is needed.
