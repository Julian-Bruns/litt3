with infinity O, and put W1={ [P−O]:P∈B }⊂J(B).

endomorphism whose square is[5].

its three-primary part is{0}. W1[24] and W1[36] also consist exactly
of the six Weierstrass classes. Every{2,3}-primary point of W1 is
F_(125^24)-rational and killed by144. This last assertion does not
classify the remaining mixed orders.
