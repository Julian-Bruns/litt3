# The actual sextic scalar cannot belong to F25

Version1, 27 September2026. For an actual pole18 comparison after the
established simultaneous cubic descent and mu29 normalization, its
scalar satisfies
\[
\epsilon\in\mathbf F_{5^8}^{\times}\setminus\mathbf F_{25}^{\times}.
\]
The exclusion of F25 follows already from the two fourth-trace endpoint
conditions. It retains all six-label multisets, repeated labels,
arbitrary phases and five-divisible multiplicities. No common-pole
profile or finite field of definition for the source is assumed.

More generally the same abstract endpoint contradiction holds for
multisets of cardinality seven if the identical two trace-membership
conditions are supplied. This does not assert that they have been
derived for every actual septic comparison.

The remaining E8 scalars, common-pole moments and actual sextic maps
are not decided. In particular this is not an unmarked common-cover
nonexistence theorem.
