# Full signed-cubic monodromy of the constant-family critical cover

Version1,29 September2026. At every allowed geometric ratio of the
constant-v degree140 family, the critical quadratic square class does
not descend through x, and its cubic norm is nonsquare in k(x).
The Galois closure of its degree-six critical map therefore has group
\[
(C_2)^3\rtimes C_3\simeq C_2\times A_4
\]
and degree24, throughout the original ratio chart.

This transfers the root-nine structural conclusion to the other
remaining degree140 family. Both auxiliary critical covers have full
signed-cubic monodromy; this is not the monodromy of a proposed actual
degree-ten étale source. Neither specialized square locus nor the
unmarked common-cover problem is decided by this theorem.
