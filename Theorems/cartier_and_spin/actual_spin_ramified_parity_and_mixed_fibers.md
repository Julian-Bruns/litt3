# Ramified actual spin images have even degree and mixed fibers

Version1,2 October2026. Substantial computation-free implication passed
[independent review](../../Research/audits/ACTUAL_SPIN_RAMIFIED_PARITY_MIXED_FIBER_AUDIT_2026_10_02.md).
The original common-cover problem is open.

Retain BOTH actual finite étale maps from the SAME source and its actual
sixteen-spin refinement q:T→Y, degq=8d. Let φ:T→Γ be the separating
normalization of the projective spin image, L=φ*M, degM=δ, κ=degφ,
d=κδ. Use the actual minimal normal quotient preserving both maps;
its projective action kernel K has order k=1or3.

Assume φ has higher-genus image and is ramified. The accepted critical
divisor table then says that its entire ramification divisor is ONE
reduced q-fiber q*P, with tame local index2.

Then κ is EVEN and κ≥4. Every target fiber containing ramification
also contains an unramified point. In particular the Galois closure
of φ cannot be an étale refinement of T.

If κ=4, then k=1 and the induced actual map Y→Γ/G has degree4. For
either selected endpoint its target is P1, and its complete branch
profile is one of:

- one fiber of type(2,1,1), three of type(2,2), one of type(4);
- one fiber of type(2,1,1), three of type(4).

These are necessary actual quotient profiles, not constructions or
exclusions of their remaining loci. Higher even κ remain unrestricted.
