# Actual root-fibre multiplicities bound the comparison scalar

Version1, 27 September2026. Work with two actual finite etale maps
h_i:T->X in the fixed line/tensor comparison normal form, of equal
degree n, and a nonconstant separating normalized comparison t with
scalar epsilon. Put u=x1, v=x2 and let alpha_0,...,alpha_3 be the A-roots.
No simultaneous cubic descent is assumed.

Let M_i be the NUMBER of zeros of t on T mapping to alpha_i under u.
These zeros are simple; their total is the comparison pole degree delta.
Put
\[
E8=\mathbf F_{5^8},\quad H=E8^*\mu_{29},\quad
g=\gcd_{i=0}^3(M_i-M_0).
\]
If g>0 and g'=g/5^v_5(g), then for any gamma^3=epsilon,
\[
\gamma^{-g'}\in H,\qquad
\epsilon^{\,g'\,29(5^8-1)/3}=1.
\tag{1}
\]
This is a bound from integer multiplicities; they are not first reduced
modulo five. If all M_i are equal, g=0 and this argument yields no bound.

For comparison pole EIGHTEEN, the proved polynomial supported norm gives
M_i=3m_i, sum m_i=6. It follows in every case that
\[
\epsilon^6\in H.
\]
After the permitted mu29 rescaling of t, one may normalize
\[
\epsilon^6\in E8^*,\qquad \epsilon\in\mathbf F_{5^{48}}.
\tag{2}
\]
The normalized multiplicative group has order6(5^8-1)=2343744.
This applies BEFORE cubic descent, for either field index1 or3.

If a simultaneous cubic quotient at pole eighteen is separately proved,
the same argument on that quotient uses multiplicities m_i instead.
It gives the sharper normalized restriction
\[
\epsilon^2\in E8^*,\qquad \epsilon\in\mathbf F_{5^{16}}.
\tag{3}
\]
Neither(2) nor(3) establishes cubic descent, a source field bound, or
nonexistence. Both actual maps are used to control the entire root fibres.
