## Checks and reasoning

The normalized trace projector is valid for the actual finite etale map, without Galoisness. On horizontal regular ten-differentials its Frobenius compatibility follows from Tr(q^5)=Tr(q)^5 and d^5=d in F5. Its source and target trace-zero spaces have the same dimension. The determinant derivative commutes with trace by etale-local splitting (equivalently, trace commutes with the local differential operator). Equal defects therefore make the complementary derivative invertible. For the semilinear Psi, relative Frobenius and projection formula give the same commuting projector with the twists retained. The established identification of indigenous defect with the corank/kernel dimension of Psi supplies the required dimension comparison. Bijectivity is asserted only on the trace-zero complement, over the perfect field k.

The fixed-curve argument is stronger than tangent equality alone: the trace-zero nonlinear equations vanish identically on the pulled-back affine space. They have the form A(x,y)y with A(0,0) invertible, so their completed ideal is exactly (y). The remaining equations are the lower determinant equations. The inherited scalar model explicitly supplies polynomial coefficient identities over nonreduced parameter algebras, so taking fifth-power differential bases here does not discard infinitesimal parameter directions. The whole completed germ claim follows.

For the Witt step, I checked the input and indices against Sections 1–2 of the audited forced_canonical_witt_endpoint proof. Data through W_(n-1), relative to C_n, determine the inverse-Cartier obstruction in H1(T_C) for a chosen C_(n+1). A reference lower curve lift exists; its original etale cover gives the reference upper curve. Subtracting this from the supplied compatible upper lift gives a marked torsor element xi_T. Naturality and the variation formula put Psi_T(xi_T) in the pulled-back summand. Complementary bijectivity forces xi_T=h*xi_C. Thus the supplied curve itself descends, rather than a replacement curve with the same obstruction class. Pullback injectivity then gives lower Hodge-obstruction vanishing.

Uniqueness of Hodge-line lifts uses H0(T_C)=0; graded identifications are unique projectively. The previous filtered tuple, graded comparison and square-trivial twist are retained in the induction. Two-torsion lifts uniquely over these nilpotent thickenings since 2 is invertible. At N=2 the canonical initial dictionary supplies the base case; the step n to n+1 extends the flow from W_(n-1) to W_n, as the statement requires. Marked curve and map uniqueness makes the finite-level identifications compatible. Proper algebraization and the henselian finite-etale equivalence produce the original map h on the algebraized tower. No claim about all Artinian mixed-characteristic bases is inferred merely from Witt-valued points.

In the corollary the inherited actual Galois defect-one factorization gives a genus-three bad double C and a prime-to-five defect-preserving map Z→C, while corelessness forces the connection on X to be ordinary. Pulling the canonical X flow along the original f gives the full upper input needed above: at W2 the projective initial data agree, and all subsequent data are filtration-compatible by pullback. Descent is along Z→C. The resulting characteristic-zero span has the same source as the original f-cover lift; neither the original map C→Y nor a simultaneous Galois closure is assumed to lift. The established characteristic-zero fixed-genus partner finiteness and stable specialization argument then apply. Finite connection choices on X, finite involution quotients of each C, and the established family multiplicity bound yield the stated qualitative finiteness.
