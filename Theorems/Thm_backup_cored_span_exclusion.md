# The small backup has no cored common cover with the fixed X

Version 1, 2026-09-11. Coherent assembly and small-packet audit PASS;
the named inherited inputs retain their own verification scopes.

Let k=bar(F5), let X be the fixed genus-nine curve, and put

    B: v^2=u(u-1)(u-2)(u-3)(u-alpha),  alpha^3+alpha+1=0.

There is no smooth proper connected effective Deligne–Mumford curve S
with representable finite etale atlases X->S and B->S.

Consequently every actual finite bi-etale span X<-Z->B, with Z smooth,
projective and connected, is coreless: the two endpoint function fields
inside k(Z) intersect in k. This statement allows wild orbifold
stabilizers and non-Galois original legs, and is over the full algebraic
closure without a coefficient-field bound on either map.

The exhaustive common-orbifold reduction has 24 tame, 12 small-wild and
2 large-wild profiles. Every profile is excluded. The degree-two row
uses the fixed X leg essentially; B itself has a hyperelliptic map.

This theorem does not exclude coreless common covers, and does not
assert nonexistence of a full simultaneous lift of a coreless span.
The original unmarked common-cover problem remains unresolved.
