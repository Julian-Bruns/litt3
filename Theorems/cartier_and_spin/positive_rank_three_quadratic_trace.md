# Exact quadratic trace in the positive rank-three radical case

Version1,23September2026. Retain the actual isolated positive
rank-three case: E subset B_Y has rank3 degree2, A=E-perp has
degree0, V=E/A has determinant omega_(Y1), and every containing
plane P_i is h_i^*P_X. Write C=Y^(1).

There is a canonical injection
\[
j:\mathscr H=A^2\operatorname{Sym}^2V\otimes\omega_C^{-1}
\longrightarrow E,\qquad \deg\mathscr H=0.
\]
Its canonical Veronese lines are the actual lambda_i. The complete
unsaturated lambda trace is EXACTLY j(H); its saturation is E.
The lambda orbit has rank3, and the defect has length2:
one unit at each simple zero of R_A, or elementary divisors(1,1)
at its double zero. These are distinct torsion-sheaf structures.
The lifted orbit lines generate H fiberwise, so their quotient
directions in V have at least three distinct values at every point
of the actual source.

Primitive squaring gives A^2->V. If R_A=P+Q is split its image
is saturated; if R_A=2P its saturation is A^2(P1), P1=F_Y(P).
Consequently
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
P+Q:&0\to A^2\to V\to\omega_C A^{-2}\to0,\\
2P:&0\to A^2(P1)\to V\to\omega_C A^{-2}(-P1)\to0.
\end{array}
\]
The previous contact divisors are the restrictions of the same
canonical quotient to the actual lines P_i/q^*A. In the double
case their fixed fiber has residual degree d. Both displayed
line bundles in the double case have degree1, so V is strongly
semistable.

More generally, for rank2 degree2 on a genus-two curve in
characteristic five, the all-height bound
mu_min(F^{r*}V)>=7*5^r/8 is equivalent to semistability of F^*V.
That first semistability already implies the stronger lower bound
24*5^r/25. Iterating those slope tests therefore adds no obstruction.

Neither the split nor double case is excluded. No X-component for
H or its canonical quotient, and no actual common cover, is produced.
