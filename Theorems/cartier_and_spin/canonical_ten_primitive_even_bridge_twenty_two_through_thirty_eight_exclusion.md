# Primitive even bridges of degrees twenty-two through thirty-eight are impossible

Version1, 3 October 2026. [Independent whole audit PASS](../../Research/audits/OCT03_PRIMITIVE_RANGE_AND_THREE_TEN_BLOCKS_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md).

Let F=k(t) over k=bar(F5), and let E,A be actual finite separable curve fields over F contained in a smooth curve field K, with AE=K. Require K/E étale, [K:A]=10 and S10 monodromy of K/A. Suppose
\[
n=[E:F]\in\{22,24,26,28,30,32,34,36,38\},\qquad
\frac{2g(E)-2}{n}\ge16,\qquad
\frac{2g(A)-2}{[A:F]}\le8.
\]
Then the single normal closure of E/F cannot have primitive monodromy.

The finite-group input is the complete Sims primitive-group classification through degree fifty. The exact degree-specific lists in these nine even degrees show that only A_n and S_n have order divisible by10!. The arithmetic verification for n22 and n24…38 is recorded in the linked source and certificates. The conductor proof includes the possible wild-five inertia at n30.

In particular this excludes the primitive sector of every disjoint-infinity nonsplit index-one retained-source bridge of joint degree eleven through nineteen. The theorem is about the actual compositum AE; it does not replace either original finite étale endpoint leg.
