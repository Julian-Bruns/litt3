# Cartier-line and tensor recognition through covering degree seven

Version 1, 24 September 2026. Use the fixed genus-nine X and the
specified Cartier line lambda and tensor tau from
[the comparison normal form](new_line_comparison_normal_form.md).
Let h_1,h_2:T->X be actual finite etale maps from the same smooth
projective connected curve. Their degrees are equal by Hurwitz.
If this degree is at most seven, then
\[
h_1^{(1)*}\lambda=h_2^{(1)*}\lambda
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
h_2^*\tau\in k^\times h_1^*\tau
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
h_1^*k(X)=h_2^*k(X).
\]
Consequently the two maps differ by the specified cubic automorphism
group of X. The equality of lines is equality of the embedded
saturated lines in the actual Cartier bundle on T.

The new step excludes the entire degree-seven rational simultaneous
quotient. Its exact certificate covers all 6,468,160 normalized
boundary configurations. All 958 exceptional contact configurations
are checked separately and are excluded by the necessary opposite-sheet
pole equation; no remaining denominator case is discarded.
Together with the preceding elliptic exclusion and genus bound,
this closes degree seven.

The statement is geometric, over the algebraic closure; it is not a
bounded search over possible coefficients of T. Recognition in
higher covering degrees, extraction of a shared line or tensor
from an arbitrary unmarked span, and both original common-cover
problems remain open.

For a non-jointly-minimal source, replace k(T) by the compositum of
the two embedded X-fields. Both intermediate maps remain etale,
and their degrees can only decrease. The established recognition
through degree six and the new degree-seven exclusion then apply.
