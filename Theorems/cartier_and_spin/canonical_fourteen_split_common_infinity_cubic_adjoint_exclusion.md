# Actual split degree fourteen with one to four common infinity points is impossible

Version1, 3 October2026. [Independent whole audit PASS](../../Research/audits/CANONICAL_FOURTEEN_SPLIT_COMMON_INFINITY_CUBIC_ADJOINT_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md), with no mathematical correction. Both actual source legs are retained.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic5. Use the fixed genus-nine X:y³=P(x), monic squarefree degree10, infinity O, θ=dx/y² with divθ=16O, and q₀(x)=(x+1)²+d, d≠0. Let h₁,h₂:C₀→X be TWO actual finite étale maps of degree14 from the SAME smooth projective connected curve. Retain their actual pullbacks xᵢ,yᵢ,θᵢ,Hᵢ and assume the actual split cubic-index-one identities
\[
k(C_0)=k(t,x_1,x_2),\quad q_0(x_2)=t^6q_0(x_1),\quad
\operatorname{div}(t)=H_1-H_2,\quad\theta_1=\kappa t^{16}\theta_2,
\qquad\kappa\ne0.
\]
Suppose t is nonconstant and J=min(H₁,H₂) has degree c with1≤c≤4. Then this configuration is impossible, for every distribution of J between the two conic boundary sections and every permitted ramification of t at its common poles.

In a retained-source split carrier where14−c≥10 is independently known, this deletes EVERY positive-overlap degree-fourteen profile. The disjoint c=0 case is excluded by the separate [exact-residue theorem](canonical_fourteen_split_disjoint_infinity_residue_exclusion.md). Together they exclude every split degree-fourteen comparison in a retained source where14−c≥10. This statement alone does not treat nonsplit comparisons.

Both actual endpoint maps stay on C₀, and any original endpoint maps from T stay on their SAME T. The proof uses their actual cubic coordinates, exact canonical identity and normalization conductor; auxiliary conic curves and polynomial normal forms are not substituted for them. Nonsplit comparisons, larger overlaps without the retained residual-degree hypothesis, higher degrees and the unmarked common-cover problem are outside the conclusion.
