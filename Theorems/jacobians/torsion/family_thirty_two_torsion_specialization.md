# Thirty-two torsion Abel points in the genus-two family

Version1,2October2026. Let C_t:y^2=x(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)(x-t)
over F5-bar, with t^5-t!=0 and infinity O.

There are at most479318 geometric parameters t for which C_t
has a non-Weierstrass point P with [32](P-O)=0 in its Jacobian.
The exceptional parameters form a Frobenius-stable set. Therefore
if degree(t/F5)>479318, the thirty-two torsion Abel points are
exactly the six Weierstrass points.

The same assertion holds for Y_S:V^2=(X^4-1)(X-S), under
x=X-1 and t=S-1, with unchanged infinity. In particular it applies
to the selected main partner, whose parameter degree exceeds
(336000)^2. It does not apply automatically to the fixed low-degree
backup partner.

The proof uses one complete geometric polynomial-jet specialization,
not a finite-point search. No claim about an actual common-cover
source or descent of its X-field follows from this endpoint theorem.
