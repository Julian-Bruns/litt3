# Tame rank-three sources force a second projective Frobenius return

Version1,26 September2026. Let K be the actual degree-one rank-two
bundle on X over the algebraic closure of F5. Let R be an irreducible
rank-three finite etale coefficient, with finite monodromy of order
prime to five. If Hom(R,K) is nonzero, its projective monodromy is
conjugate to a subgroup of the projective Hessian group of order216.
There is a finite character line T such that
\[
F_{\rm abs}^*R\simeq R^\vee\otimes T,\qquad
F_{\rm abs}^{2*}R\simeq R\otimes T^4.
\]
Thus every surviving tame rank-three source has projective Frobenius
period exactly two: period one is excluded by the established shifted
first-Frobenius theorem. This does not assert a strict period two.

The group classification is combined with the accepted exclusions of
reducible, imprimitive, and quartic-semi-invariant cases. The Frobenius
identity is an elementary calculation on the Hessian generators in
characteristic five; no reduction of a characteristic-zero invariant
ring is required.

This is a necessary condition on a possible coefficient source. It does
not construct such a source or identify it with the fixed-determinant,
fixed-kernel second-return family currently being computed. Twisting R
also twists the target of its map to K. Wild monodromy, other ranks and
the unmarked common-cover problem remain open.
