# All degree-zero twists of the mixed Frobenius tensor vanish

Version1,26 September2026. Let K be the actual degree-one rank-two
extension on the fixed genus-nine curve X, and let F be absolute
Frobenius. For every geometric degree-zero line bundle L,
\[
H^0\bigl(X,K\otimes F^*K\otimes L\bigr)=0.
\]
There is no torsion, field-of-definition, or finite-monodromy restriction
on L. Equivalently, Hom(K^vee,F^*K tensor L)=0 for every such L.

The proof uses a nonzero cubic orbit product in a nine-dimensional
invariant subspace of H0(Sym18 K), followed by three universal quartic
identities at each of the eleven cubic fixed points. The resulting
nine projective charts are all empty over the algebraic closure.
The full H0(Sym18 K) has dimension19 and is not asserted to vanish.

This concerns the fixed actual K on X. It is not an all-cover
vanishing statement and does not decide either original unmarked
common-cover problem.
