# Leading boundaries and geometric lines in the degree-ten square locus

Version1, 25 September2026. In the eleven affine7 coefficient spaces
of [the two-profile theorem](admissible_degree_ten_two_profiles.md),
use F25(alpha), alpha^4+[7]alpha^3+[6]alpha^2+[2]alpha+[5]=0.
The exact pole conditions and kappa!=0 are retained. Put
\[
\epsilon=[24]+[4]\alpha+[23]\alpha^3,\qquad
\eta=[11]+[18]\alpha^2+[20]\alpha^3.
\]
Define a=[x^4](D2)_0 for v=1 and a=[xy]D2 for linear v, and
\[
b=\epsilon\kappa=[x^{12}y]N_3,\quad c=[x^{15}]N_3,
\quad d=[x^{19}]N_4,\quad e=[x^{12}y^2]N_4,\quad f=[x^{16}y^2]N_5,
\]
\[
z=2d/b,\quad U=[8]\kappa z^5+[24](d^2/b+f),\quad
V=\eta\kappa+[24](cz^2+ez),\quad M=3a^3b^8.
\]
Then on every geometric point of the stated open spaces,
\[
[x^{144}]R_0=(MU)^3,\qquad U=0\Longrightarrow[x^{143}]R_0=(MV)^3.
\]
Thus U=0,V!=0 is excluded from the nonzero-square locus. Every possible
square of degree<144 has U=V=0 and d!=0. These leading equations alone
do not specify its smaller degree; the subsequent
[degree-floor theorem](degree_ten_lower_square_floor.md) sharpens that
boundary. The quadratic critical polynomial is separable
everywhere on these open spaces, so forcing a repeated critical root
cannot produce a square point.

For each fixed v, one explicit entire geometric affine line, obtained
by varying N5 by s y^2 P t^2, has empty square locus. Exact Bezout
identities cover all parameters with leading coefficient nonzero, and
the omitted point has residual degree143. This does not exclude other
parallel lines. All eleven spaces also contain explicit degree142
points with U=V=0 and nonsquare residuals. Hence that lower even-degree
boundary is real, not an empty formal case.

Neither the full square locus nor any actual etale realization is
settled. A square norm alone would not construct the requested cover.
