# Audit: simple Jacobians and bidegree-two bi-etale correspondences

**Verdict:** **PASS in every characteristic.**  No breaking mathematical
objection was found.

**Auditor and date:** /root/x_elliptic_quotient_maps/m9_incidence_profiles,

## Scope checked

The audit checked:

- existence and freeness of the deck involutions, including in
  characteristic two;
- finiteness and the dihedral presentation of the group generated by the
  two involutions;
- finite kernel of Jacobian pullback via the norm--pullback identity;
- Riemann--Hurwitz with effective different in wild characteristic;
- the one or two reflection conjugacy classes and their free action;
- etaleness of the rotation-quotient double cover \(E\to Q\); and
- the deduction that a common deck involution makes the product image a
  graph rather than a reduced bidegree-\((2,2)\) curve.

All were found correct.

## Breaking objections

None.
