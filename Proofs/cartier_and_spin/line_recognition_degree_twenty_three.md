# Combining actual pole exclusions with the supported norm bound

The [normal form](new_line_comparison_normal_form.md) supplies a
separating function t with simple poles of degree delta<=deg h_i,
unless the embedded X-fields already agree. Its actual norm on X
has pole delta and zeros among the twelve points over A=0.
The [supported norm theorem](bounded_supported_norms.md) makes it
a polynomial in x when delta<=26. Hence delta is divisible by three.

For delta<=23 the only possibilities are3,6,9,12,15,18,21. The first
five are the established actual lower-degree comparison exclusions;
the [pole18 theorem](pole_eighteen_complete_exclusion.md) and
[pole21 theorem](pole_twenty_one_complete_exclusion.md) close the last
two. Thus t is constant. Its divisor is h2^*O-h1^*O, so the normal
form's independently proved theta-recognition lemma identifies the
two embedded X-fields. Both original maps are preserved throughout.
