# Proof: coverage of the actual kernel and distinguished fiber alternatives

Version1. [Statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/main_degree_twelve_ramified_spin_exclusion.md).

The projective kernel has order k=1or3. If k=3, the effective degree is four and the entire case on BOTH endpoints is excluded by [the cubic-kernel theorem](kernel_three_effective_four_exclusion.md), including its quadrilateral signature.

If k=1, the [complete tame classification](tame_ramified_spin_degree_classification.md) gives exactly signatures(2,3,12),(2,4,6),(3,3,4). For an ordinary distinguished coarse value, the first two are excluded by [the ordinary spin theorem](tame_high_degree_ordinary_spin_exclusion.md), and the third by [the sparse cube-fiber theorem](degree_twelve_334_ordinary_spin_exclusion.md). These inputs hold on BOTH endpoints.

For a distinguished cone value, the actual genus-two map to the coarse rational base has degree twelve and at most three branch values: all ramification lies over the three triangle cones, since the special spin ramification introduces no fourth value. On MAIN this contradicts [the bounded three-branch theorem](../quotient_geometry/main_bounded_three_branch_exclusion.md). This use is confined to MAIN.

On BACKUP the cone case is left open. The classification gives 12=t(2+u), with u≥1, and t must be a nontrivial inertia in its signature. Thus the possibilities are precisely(2,3,12):t=2,3; (2,4,6):t=2,4; (3,3,4):t=3,4. This list records unexcluded cases, not witnesses.
