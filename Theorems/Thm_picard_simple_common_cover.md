# Absolutely simple, Hom-zero genus-three endpoints with a common cover

Let k=bar(F5), choose a^2+2=0, and let C_plus,C_minus be the smooth
projective models of

    C_plus:  w^3=t(t-1)(t-a)(t-a-2),
    C_minus: z^3=x(1-x)(1-a*x)(1-(a+2)*x).

They have genus3. Their Jacobians are absolutely simple, with

    Hom_k(J(C_plus),J(C_minus))=Hom_k(J(C_minus),J(C_plus))=0.

Nevertheless there is a connected smooth projective curve T of genus7
and two finite etale Galois maps T->C_plus, T->C_minus of degree3.
The source has function field

    k(t)(u,v), u^3=t, v^3=(t-1)(t-a)(t-a-2).

The first map uses w=uv. The second uses x=1/t, z=v/(u*t).
The span is cored with field k(t); no coreless span is asserted here.
Both Jacobians have p-rank2 and are not ordinary.

Thus absolute simplicity of BOTH Jacobians and geometric Hom-zero,
even together with two prime-to-five Galois etale legs of degree3,
do not exclude a common cover. No conclusion about the fixed genus9/
genus25 pair or all coreless correspondences follows.
