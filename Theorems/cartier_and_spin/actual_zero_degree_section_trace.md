Retain TWO actual finite etale maps $X\xleftarrow hS\xrightarrow gY$
from the SAME smooth connected projective source and their canonical
Cartier-bundle identifications. Let $L\subset B_X$ be a line
subbundle with nonzero section s, whose zero divisor is Z. Let R
and K be the actual first traces of its raw section and L. Suppose
$\deg R=0$. For every opposite point Q, let $m(Q)$ be the largest
multiplicity of a zero in Z whose actual source lift maps to Q,
or zero if there is none. Then
\[
\boxed{\sum_{Q\in Y}m(Q)\le\deg K.}
\]
This uses the ORIGINAL source images; it does not replace supported
divisors by complete fibres or infer a clump.

For the selected genus-nine X and its original q0 section, L is
$O_X(2O)$ and $Z=2O$. Consequently
\[
\#g(h^{-1}O)\le\lfloor\deg K/2\rfloor.
\]

there are exactly EIGHT distinct generic q0 image lines on an
actual one-leg Galois closure. There is an actual intermediate

\[

\]

global exact form on $Z'$.
