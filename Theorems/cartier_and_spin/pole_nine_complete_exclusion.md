# Complete exclusion of comparison pole degree nine

Version 1, 24 September 2026. For the two actual finite etale maps
in the specified new-line comparison normal form, comparison pole
degree delta=9 is impossible. This excludes the entire range of
covering degrees 9<=n<=125, not only a chosen degree or ramification
profile. No global Galois assumption is made.

More precisely, the cubic intermediate field supplied at delta=9
cannot satisfy even the two normalized tensor identities, their
endpoint poles, and the common-pole conditions. All cubic local
fiber types are retained, including a double-ramified triple pole.

The proof uses an exhaustive finite linear-algebra certificate on
88,883,028 normalized endpoint pairs, followed by an exact norm
contradiction in the sole linearly consistent case. The unknown
scalar and curve coefficients are not assumed to lie in a bounded
finite field; the proof first eliminates them geometrically.

Together with [the small supported-norm result](marked_divisor_relation_lattice.md),
distinct actual tensor comparisons have delta>=12. This is
conditional recognition, not extraction of a tensor from an
arbitrary unmarked common cover.
