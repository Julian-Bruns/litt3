# Weak cyclic two-branch curves constrain every irreducible reflection module

Version1,3 October2026. Whole scoped PASS in the [independent audit](../../../Research/audits/WEAK_CYCLIC_REFLECTION_MODULE_CONSTRAINTS_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). The reflection eigenspace hypothesis is essential and is not supplied by arbitrary higher-rank spin or trace modules.

Let k be algebraically closed of odd characteristic p, and let a finite group G act faithfully on a smooth connected projective curve Z with Z/G=P¹ and exactly TWO branch values: cyclic Cp of lower break ONE and cyclic C2. Let V be an irreducible projective G-module of dimension n≥TWO on which the original tame involution has eigenspace dimensions ONE and n−ONE. The representation need not be faithful.

Then n≤p. The prescribed cyclic lift supplies a generating opposite-class tuple a,b,c; its normalized unipotent matrices A,B are regular Jn blocks. Writing the repeated eigenvalue of AB as ρ gives ρ^n=−ONE. This irreducible matrix tuple is linearly rigid and is realizable over the smallest finite field F_q containing ρ. Its entire projective image lies in PSL_n(F_q).

If n is EVEN, inverse conjugacy imposes
\[
4\mid\frac{q-1}{\gcd(n,q-1)},
\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad
v_2(q-1)\ge v_2(n)+2.
\]
The stronger-looking condition 4n|(q−ONE) is equivalent only when n|(q−ONE); that divisibility is not automatic because ρ can lose odd factors in its order.

In characteristicFIVE the only possible dimensions are THREE andFIVE: dimensions TWO andFOUR violate the displayed condition. For n=THREE, ρ=−ONE gives a realization over F5 with an invariant nondegenerate symmetric form; either primitive-SIXTH-root value of ρ gives a realization over F25 with an invariant nondegenerate F5-Hermitian form. For n=FIVE, ρ=−ONE is forced, and the realization is over F5 with an invariant nondegenerate symmetric form. Thus odd dimension does NOT uniformly imply prime-field realization or symmetric self-duality.

No rank bound for arbitrary modules, finite coefficient extraction, actual X-atlas or unmarked common-cover exclusion follows. Both actual source maps must still be retained in any application.
