# The four wild140 coarse pairs have an auxiliary Hermitian spin realization

Version1, 3 October2026. Scoped one-leg construction; independent review pending. This does NOT construct the original common cover.

Take any of the four degree140 coarse maps β:Y→P¹ constructed by the [moving-family theorem](wild140_moving_coarse_map_family.md), with its exact-pole hypothesis q⁵≠4s. Let P be its unique additional ramification point of index two. After scaling β by a nonzero constant, there is an actual representable finite map
\[
Y\longrightarrow[H/A_7],
\]
where H is the Hermitian genus-ten curve and A₇ is the accepted subgroup of PSU₃(5). This map is ramified of index two at P; Y is NOT an étale atlas of that stack. Its normalized pullback U→Y of H→[H/A₇] is an actual finite étale A₇ torsor, possibly disconnected. Every connected component U₀ maps separably to H with different exactly q₀P, reduced and of index two, and
\[
\varphi_0^*\omega_H\simeq q_0^*\mathcal O_Y(P).
\]

There also exist an auxiliary connected finite étale source T′→Y, an étale degree-four cover H′→H, a separating map φ′:T′→H′, and line bundles M on H′ and L=φ′*M with
\[
M^8\simeq\omega_{H'},\qquad
L^8\simeq q'^*\mathcal O_Y(P),\qquad
L^{16}\simeq\omega_{T'},\qquad
\varphi'^*\omega_{H'}\simeq\mathcal O_{T'}(q'P).
\]
The Y leg can be made Galois by a further finite étale refinement. All ramification of φ′ has index two above q′P.

No map to the fixed X, descended infinity sections, basepoint-free spin series, primitive different generator, or faithful projective carrier action is constructed. The abstract spin-root identities and global one-leg Hermitian realization therefore do not supply those missing hypotheses. The unmarked common-cover problem remains UNSOLVED.
