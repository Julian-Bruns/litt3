# An all-degree admissible source energy in a fixed space

30 September2026. Use the fixed genus-nine curve X:y^3=P(x), its
point O, the reduced divisor R_X=O+x^*(A=0), and primitive f=Q/y^5
with div(df)=2R_X-10O. Let h:S->X be an actual connected finite
etale cover of arbitrary degree N, generated by b, with
\[
\varphi=h^*f+b^5,\qquad
\operatorname{div}\varphi=3E-5G-10h^*O,
\quad E\le h^*R_X\text{ reduced},\quad G\ge0.
\]
Assume the uniform norm hypotheses, so the monic source polynomial is
\[
F_b(W)=(W^5+f)H(W)+\tau,\qquad \tau\ne0.
\]
Impose the additional, coordinate-stable hypothesis
\[
\deg\bigl(H\bmod(W^5+f)\bigr)\le3.
\]
Write gamma for the cubic coefficient of that remainder. Then
\[
\mathcal Q^\sharp=
\operatorname{Tr}_{k(S)/k(X)}\frac{db^2}{\varphi}
+df\,d(\gamma/\tau)
\]
satisfies the uniform conclusion
\[
A\mathcal Q^\sharp\in H^0(X,\omega_X^2(8O)).
\]
With omega0=dx/(3y^2), its scalar coefficient relative to omega0^2
belongs to L_X(40O), of dimension32 and basis
\[
x^i\ (0\le i\le13),\qquad
x^iy\ (0\le i\le10),\qquad
x^iy^2\ (0\le i\le6).
\]
The space is independent of N, of the multiplicities of G, and of
the marked norm occupancies. If a product t of d selected factors
of A contains all finite support of h_*E, one may replace A by t
and 8O by (3d-4)O, with the natural interpretation when this divisor
is negative.

For N=10 the accepted trace-zero profiles satisfy the remainder
hypothesis, and their sharper infinity moments give the earlier
L_X(36O) condition with d=3. In general the quartic-remainder sector
is not covered. The energy can be zero; this is a uniform necessary
condition, not a proof of emptiness or of common-cover recognition.
