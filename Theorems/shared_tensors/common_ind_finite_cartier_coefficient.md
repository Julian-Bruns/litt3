# An actual span supplies an endpointwise ind-finite Cartier coefficient

ID: `common_ind_finite_cartier_coefficient`. Version2, 2 October2026.
Author proof; focused root review PASS in
[the audit](../../Research/audits/COMMON_IND_FINITE_CARTIER_AUDIT_2026_10_02.md).

Let $k$ be algebraically closed of characteristic $p>0$ and let
$X\xleftarrow hS\xrightarrow gY$ be two ACTUAL finite étale maps
from the same smooth projective connected curve. Choose a geometric
source point. Write $B$ for the Cartier bundle, with the actual
identifications $B_S=h^{(1)*}B_X=g^{(1)*}B_Y$. Begin with any nonzero
finite-dimensional space $V_0\subset H^0(X^{(1)},B_X)$.

There is a common coefficient $(\mathcal V_X,\mathcal V_Y)$ such
that each endpoint bundle is a filtered union of finite
étale-trivializable bundles, their source pullbacks are isomorphic,
and their compatible maps to the common Cartier bundle are nonzero.
It contains the given $V_0$ on the X-side. No covering degree is
inverted. In particular this construction also applies when $p$
divides either original degree.

More precisely, let $\widetilde S$ be the pointed universal pro-étale
cover of $S^{(1)}$. It is also the pointed universal pro-étale cover
of both endpoints. Let $G_X,G_Y$ be their deck groups and let $G_S$
be the common open subgroup. The underlying coefficient vector space
is the span $V$ of the finite $G_X,G_Y$ words applied to the pulled
$V_0$ inside $H^0(\widetilde S,B_{\widetilde S})$. Its restrictions
to BOTH profinite endpoint groups are locally finite continuous
representations. Evaluation descends to the asserted compatible maps.

This construction is EXACTLY the colimit of the canonical minimal
finite domains of alternating actual trace. On the common pro-cover,
a minimal X-domain is a finite $G_X$-stable section space $E_i$;
its minimal opposite domain is $F_i=\operatorname{span}_k(G_YE_i)$,
and its minimal return is
$E_{i+1}=\operatorname{span}_k(G_XF_i)$. Thus
$E_i\subset F_i\subset E_{i+1}$, and the return inclusions descend
to compatible endpoint finite-coefficient injections in ALL degrees.
They are not defined by dividing the ordinary adjunction unit by a
covering degree. When that degree is divisible by $p$, these new
inclusions need not lift through the corresponding raw induced
coefficient before minimalization.

These are objects in the fiber product of the TWO endpoint ind-finite
categories. They are not asserted to be filtered unions of common
FINITE coefficients. Under common finite-coefficient vanishing, $V$
has no nonzero finite-dimensional subspace invariant under both deck
groups, and its nonzero evaluation cannot factor through a finite
common coefficient quotient. Thus the endpointwise ind-finite
construction supplies no finite extraction or common-cover decision.

Every open subgroup $H\subset G_S$ has finite-dimensional invariants
$V^H$: they embed in $H^0(D^{(1)},B_D)$ for its corresponding finite
étale cover $D\to S$. The representation is generated by the finitely
many original vectors under the group generated by the two endpoint
deck groups. These additional finiteness properties do not imply a
finite common coefficient by the present argument.
