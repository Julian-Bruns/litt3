# The two actual cubic coordinates cannot become proportional after the spin weight

Version1, 3 October2026. New all-degree actual-field exclusion; [fresh independent whole audit PASS](../../Research/audits/CANONICAL_SPLIT_PROPORTIONAL_CUBIC_FIELD_EXCLUSION_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). No computation is used.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic five, and let X:y³=P(x) be the fixed smooth genus-nine curve with P squarefree of degree10, unique infinity O, and pole orders3,10 for x,y. Put q₀(x)=(x+1)²+d, d≠0, with no root in common with P. Retain TWO actual finite étale maps hᵢ:C₀→X from the SAME smooth projective connected source, their actual coordinates xᵢ,yᵢ, and reduced infinity divisors Hᵢ. Suppose a nonconstant t∈k(C₀) satisfies
\[
\operatorname{div}(t)=H_1-H_2,\qquad
q_0(x_2)=t^6q_0(x_1),\qquad
k(C_0)=k(t,x_1,x_2).
\]
Then for every η∈k×,
\[
\boxed{t^{10}y_1\ne\eta y_2.}
\]
There is no covering-degree bound and no disjoint-infinity requirement. Any original finite étale endpoint maps on a source T above C₀ remain on that SAME T. The argument neither supplies an auxiliary étale leg nor decides the unmarked common-cover problem.

In the actual split conductor-adjoint setup, let ρ be its nonzero actual conductor section, and let a,b be ANY global extensions with a|C=ρy₂ and b|C=t¹⁰ρy₁. Then b cannot be a nonzero constant multiple of a. This applies in particular to the [paired cubic-adjoint theorem](canonical_split_paired_cubic_adjoint_counterterms.md), regardless of its integral counterterms. It removes the globally proportional extension sector; it does not force a common factor or exclude every remaining split source.
