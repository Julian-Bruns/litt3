# Residue-degree obstruction to potential good reductions

Version 1, 2026-09-11. Author proof from stable-model uniqueness.

Let C be a smooth proper curve of genus at least two over a number
field F, and let v|p have residue degree f over F_p. If C has
potential good reduction at v, its smooth geometric reduction has
moduli Frobenius orbit length dividing f. Finite ramified extensions,
twists, and further residue extensions used to obtain good reduction
do not enlarge this geometric moduli orbit.

In particular, the backup B, whose geometric moduli Frobenius orbit
has length three, is not a potential good reduction of any curve
defined over Q, or over a quadratic number field, at any place above
5. More generally, any proposed defining field and place must have
residue degree divisible by three.

This excludes all Q-defined Shimura curves and their Q-defined
Atkin-Lehner quotients as possible generic curves reducing to B.
It does not assert that every arithmetic genus-two curve is defined
over Q, over its trace field, or over a quadratic field.
