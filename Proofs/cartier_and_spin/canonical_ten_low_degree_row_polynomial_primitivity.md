# Proof: finite root-group interpolation forces a forbidden pure power

Version1,3 October2026. Pending independent review; see the [actual-field statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/canonical_ten_low_degree_row_polynomial_primitivity.md). No computation or representation classification beyond the accepted natural coefficient image is used.

Put K=k(Γ_R) and F=k(C). For m≥ONE let U_m be the k-linear subspace of homogeneous degree-m polynomials in the FOUR row components whose evaluated section is pulled back from Γ_R. Include the zero evaluation in U_m. In a rational Q^(3m) frame, this means simply that its coefficient belongs to K. Such a rational descended coefficient is regular whenever its pullback section is regular, since finite surjective pullback detects the absence of poles.

The subspace U_m is stable under the projective coefficient group R: the actual row and Q are equivariant, and scalar choices do not affect stability of a linear subspace. The coefficient Frobenius twist has the same finite matrix image PSL₄(F_q), since FIFTH powers permute F_q. Thus the image contains every elementary root-group matrix I+tE_ij for t∈F_q.

Its action on homogeneous degree-m polynomials has degree at most m in the parameter t. Modulo U_m, the orbit of any element of U_m is consequently a vector polynomial of degree at most m that vanishes for all q parameter values in F_q. If m<q, its coefficients vanish identically. Therefore U_m is stable under I+tE_ij for EVERY t∈k, and hence under the full algebraic group SL₄(k), generated by these elementary matrices. This is why the bound is m<q rather than an unbounded claim about finite-group submodules.

Any nonzero algebraic-SL₄-stable subspace of homogeneous degree-m polynomials contains a pure power. Indeed translate a nonzero polynomial so its value at the first coordinate point is nonzero. Its coefficient of z₁^m is then nonzero. Under the torus diag(t³,t⁻¹,t⁻¹,t⁻¹), the monomial z₁^m has the unique highest character t^(3m); every other monomial has character t^(4j−m) with j<m. Torus-weight projection therefore extracts a nonzero multiple of z₁^m in the stable subspace. Algebraic SL₄-transitivity then supplies EVERY pure power of a linear form. This argument works in characteristicFIVE: distinct torus characters remain distinct over the infinite algebraically closed field.

If U_m were nonzero, it would in particular contain the mth power of the linear form corresponding to the accepted original primitive row component b_i. In its rational Q³ frame let β_i∈F be that primitive function. We would have β_i^m∈K.

Write m=5^sℓ with FIVE not dividingℓ. Since F/K is separable, β_i^ℓ cannot give a nontrivial purely inseparable subextension, so β_i^ℓ∈K. The extension K(β_i)/K is then a Kummer extension of degree dividingℓ: the algebraically closed constant field contains all ℓth roots of unity. Its degree is prime toFIVE. This contradicts the actual primitive degreeTEN of β_i. Thus U_m=ZERO.

Every nonzero homogeneous polynomial of degree m<q therefore evaluates outside K, and in particular not toZERO. Full S10 monodromy supplies no proper intermediate field, so every such evaluated coefficient generates F over K. Changing its rational Q^(3m) frame only multiplies by a nonzero element of K and does not alter that field.

The proof excludes constant low-degree equations and constant low-degree descent. It says nothing about K-linear dependence of the FOUR components: a relation with coefficients in K is not an element of U_m, which is a space over the constant field only.
