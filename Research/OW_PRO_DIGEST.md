## New useful limitation: changing embeddings can preserve the defect

In the common maximal unramified field L, let r be the shared connection,
G_C=Gal(L/k(C)), G_B=Gal(L/k(B)), H=G_C intersect G_B, and
Gamma=<G_C,G_B>. Actual r-preserving automorphisms sigma give alternative
joint fields K_sigma=k(C)sigma(k(B)), hence actual finite etale witnesses.
Conversely an alternative matched witness embeds in this way after taking
its joint normalization. This is an actual field construction, not an
unproved realization of an abstract fundamental-group commensuration.

The returned conditional tree argument is elementary and useful as a
warning: IF the bipartite coset graph Gamma/H is a tree, then every
sigma in Gamma gives a K_sigma containing a G_C-conjugate of the original
joint field. Indeed G_C intersect sigma G_B sigma^-1 fixes both endpoint
vertices, hence their geodesic and its first edge delta H with delta in
G_C. Fixed fields give delta(k(Z_0)) subset K_sigma. The resulting actual
etale map pulls back any nonzero indigenous tangent defect. Consequently
all these changed witnesses are nonordinary if Z_0 is.

The graph has NOT been shown to be a tree, and Gamma has NOT been shown
to exhaust Aut_k(L,r). Neither this conditional obstruction nor the field
description decides OW. No assertion of a simultaneous finite normal
closure is made.

The proposed positive construction requires a new embedding sigma(k(B))
inside the canonical-double/pro5 tower of C. This containment is unproved
and stronger than OW itself. It is not the next question to send again
under a different name.
