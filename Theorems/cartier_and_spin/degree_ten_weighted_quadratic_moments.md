# Weighted quadratic traces with the same small scale degree

30 September2026. Use the actual admissible degree-ten cover, v,t,
omega0 and fixed regular finite primitive w=b+B0/y from the
[fixed-frame theorem](degree_ten_fixed_frame_moments.md). Put
\[
J_j=\operatorname{Tr}_{S/X}\frac{w^j(dw)^2}{w^5+q},\qquad j=0,1,2.
\]
Every such actual cover satisfies
\[
t^2J_0/\omega_0^2\in L(61O),\quad
t^2J_1/\omega_0^2\in L(78O),\quad
vt^2J_2/\omega_0^2\in L(105O).
\]
These spaces have dimensions53,70,97. The full affine17 family and
arbitrary multiplicities of G remain in scope.

On a trace-zero profile the sharper bounds are
\[
t^2J_0/\omega_0^2\in L(46O),\quad
t^2J_1/\omega_0^2\in L(63O),\quad
vt^2J_2/\omega_0^2\in L((80+\deg_Ov)O).
\]
Here deg_O(v)=0 or3 for the constant and linear profiles. Retain
the separate corrected J0 condition in L(36O).

For a scale family F=lambda v phi^m+phi S+tau, with phi=W^p+q,
D=S_W of degree d and gcd(D,phi)=1, each weighted quadratic
moment Tr(w^j(delta w)^2/phi) has denominator dividing Delta=
Res(F,D) and numerator degree at most
d+floor(3/m), provided j+deg(S)+1-pm<=-2. In particular all
three displayed moments have scale-numerator degree at most four
for cubic D, and at most three for quadratic D in degree ten.
Multiple roots of D are retained without a discriminant inverse.

The weights1,W,...,W^(d-1) are the full dual set of polynomial
residue probes in the d-dimensional Artin algebra K[W]/D. Thus
they avoid discarding a critical direction by taking one scalar
trace. This is an algebraic statement, not an assertion that these
global regularity conditions characterize etaleness. Their joint
geometric coefficient locus is still undecided.
