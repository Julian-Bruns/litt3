# Multiplicity differences isolate the uniform eight-label exception

27 September2026. Let E=F_(5^8),K0=F_(5^14),B=F25 and H=E*mu29.
The full-fibre norm theorem, applied to multiplicities3m_i, gives
epsilon^g in H for g=gcd_i(m_i-m0)>0. It does not give a nonzero
exponent for four equal multiplicities. Integer phase balance follows from
[the independent regular-form theorem](unbounded_modular_phase_balance.md):
each root mass is at most nine, hence at most19. No bounded Fourier
anchor, cubic descent or small-phase search is required.

For sum m_i=8, every nonzero difference gcd divides8. For clarity,
the positive profiles with gcd>1 are(1,1,1,5),g=4 and(1,1,3,3),g=2;
the only zero-gcd profile is(2,2,2,2). If a root is absent, the gcd
divides8 directly. Hence epsilon^8 belongs to H unless the chosen
endpoint profile is uniform.

For sum m_i=9, absent-root profiles have gcd dividing9. Among
positive profiles the only gcd>1 profile is(1,1,1,6),g=5.
Fifth powers are bijective on H, so that case gives epsilon in H.
Consequently epsilon^9 belongs to H in every pole27 case.

Rescaling t by an element of mu29 removes the mu29 factor in either
power condition:4*8 and4*9 are both coprime to29. With q=5^8,
8(q-1) divides q^8-1 and9(q-1) divides q^9-1. The normalized scalar
is therefore in F_(5^64) for a nonuniform pole24 endpoint, and in
F_(5^72) at pole27.

## Intersect with the field forced by the actual traces

All endpoint sums and common moments belong to EK0=F_(5^56).
If epsilon lies outside this field, the first two trace equations
force C0,E0,Cinf,Einf all to lie in K0. The three nonconstant root
Fourier projections of C are nonzero, and every at most nine distinct
mu29 phases is F5-independent. Thus C_L in K0 forces the four
root counts at each phase to agree modulo five.

At cardinality eight this gives two complete four-root blocks.
At cardinality nine it gives one complete block plus a fivefold
singleton; the latter contributes zero to all four field sums.
Both endpoints would therefore give either the excluded two-block
system or the excluded one-block system. This is impossible. Every
remaining scalar consequently lies in F_(5^56), with no multiplicity
assumption beyond the stated cardinalities.

Intersecting F_(5^64) or F_(5^72) with F_(5^56) gives E in either
case. The argument may be applied after interchanging the two maps,
so one nonuniform endpoint suffices at pole24. If both endpoints
are uniform, no power bound was obtained: retain the whole F_(5^56)
possibility. The complete eight/nine moment-field scalar theorem removes
ALL of K0 in both cases; its intersection with E is precisely F25.
