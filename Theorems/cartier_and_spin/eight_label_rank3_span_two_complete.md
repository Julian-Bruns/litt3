# Both phase spans at most two exclude rank three

Version1,29 September2026. Use the genuine full-support eight-label
endpoint rows and coefficient fields of the
[distinct-pair theorem](eight_label_rank3_span_two_distinct.md).
If both endpoints have phase span at most two, their stacked matrix has
rank FOUR. Consequently every remaining rank-three pair has at least
one endpoint of phase span THREE.

This completes the span-two/span-two boundary, including adjacent and
opposite repeated pairs. It does not exclude span-two/span-three or
span-three/span-three pairs. Nor does it omit the subsequent actual
scalar-graph and common-moment conditions on a rank-three pair.
The unmarked common-cover problem remains unresolved.
