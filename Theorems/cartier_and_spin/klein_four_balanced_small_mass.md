# Balanced endpoint moments require residue mass at least nine

Version1, 26 September2026. For the fixed endpoint P,A, suppose the
two necessary Klein-four moment equations hold and both endpoint
multisets are balanced in the sense of
[the structural moment theorem](klein_four_structural_moments.md).
Let w_c in{0,1,2,3,4} be the residue of the integer pole weight m_c
modulo5. Then
\[
\sum_{c\in\mu_{29}}w_c\ge9.
\]
Equivalently, if j2 counts nodes of total weight6, then every actual
balanced comparison satisfies n>=21+5j2. In particular, no balanced
comparison exists in degrees14..20.

By the structural subfield theorem, any actual comparison in
degrees17..20 must have epsilon outside F_(5^14). No whole degree
in17..20 is excluded here. The inequality applies to higher degrees
as well; it is not a statement that their remaining profiles exist.

The result is a complete exact small-mass exclusion in all29 relative
phase classes, including nonreciprocal phases. Two independent
arithmetic implementations and profile enumerations agree.
