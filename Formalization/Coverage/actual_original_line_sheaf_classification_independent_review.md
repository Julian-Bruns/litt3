## Exact objects and hypotheses

This is the usual local-freeness input, rather than a divisor presentation or an abstract replacement for the classification conclusion. M belongs to the genuine category `X.Modules`. Local frames therefore act on every section module of every smaller open, not just on one stalk or one global vector space.

The geometric endpoint assumes `Field k`, `IsAlgClosed k`, `IsIntegral X`, an actual structure morphism `sX : X ⟶ Spec k`, `IsSmoothOfRelativeDimension 1 sX`, and `QuasiCompact sX`. Algebraic closedness is explicit; this review makes no arbitrary-field classification claim. Quasi-compactness is used to prove finite support, and no finite support is hidden in the line-sheaf condition. Neither properness nor a separate separation hypothesis appears in this endpoint. The generic embedding chain itself works on every integral scheme without the curve, smoothness or compactness assumptions.

The genuine divisor sheaf uses the literal convention v_x(f) ≤ exp(D(x)), equivalently ord_x(f) ≥ −D(x). Accordingly the constructed coefficient is **D(x) = −ord_x(g_x)**, where g_x is the derived rational image of a local frame's unit section. I checked this sign against the generator valuation and the final division argument; the positive and negative divisor conventions have not been interchanged.
