# First-derivative pole matching bounds every Klein-four quotient genus

Version1, 26 September2026. Under all actual same-source hypotheses of
[Klein-four pole shortening](klein_four_pole_shortening.md), use the
numerators and integers e,c_i,h_i,d_i of the
[simultaneous quotient](klein_four_simultaneous_quotients.md) and
[companion](klein_four_companion_constraints.md) theorems. Then
\[
E C_i\mid V_i-\epsilon t^{36}U_i. \tag{1}
\]
If e>=13, this implies the individual bound
\[
c_i\le16+2d_i,\qquad
h_i\le18+\lfloor c_i/2\rfloor. \tag{2}
\]
The numerical top case in (2) is incompatible with the endpoint
coefficient field. Consequently, for every e,
\[
g(S)\le50+j,\qquad g(S)\le79.
\]
Together with the [grid theorem](klein_four_grid_singularity_bound.md),
this excludes n=90,91 as well as92..182. The still possible actual V4
degrees in that interval are14..89. This is not a complete V4 exclusion,
does not close D4/S4, and does not solve an unmarked common-cover problem.

The proof reuses the already certified Fourier minors by finite Fourier
duality. A small four-dimensional F25 basis certificate excludes equality;
it covers all geometric endpoint labels without enumerating their roots
of unity or imposing a finite field on unknown curve coefficients.
