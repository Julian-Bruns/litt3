# Maximal abelian p-towers have unbounded birational spin series

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p>=5. Let C be an ordinary
genus-two curve, A=O_C(W) an effective spin, and 0!=e in H0(C,A).
Suppose eta=e^2 and Cartier(eta) are linearly independent.

For n>=1 put N=p^n. Let q_n:T_n->C be the maximal connected abelian
etale cover of exponent dividing N. Its group is G_n=(Z/N)^2. Then

    g(T_n)=N^2+1,   h0(T_n,q_n^*A)=N.

The complete spin series q_n^*A is base-point-free, separable and
BIRATIONAL. G_n acts faithfully on its projective section space.
For every subgroup H<=G_n of order p,

    h0(T_n/H,A|_(T_n/H)) = N/p.

The n=1 formula reads1 on every degree-p quotient. Thus every degree-p
subcover of T_1->C adds no spin sections, while their full compositum
has p sections and a birational complete spin map.

This applies to ALL SIX effective spins of the actual characteristic-five
family C_t, t^5-t!=0. For example n=2 gives degree625, genus626, and
25 sections with a birational complete spin map, uniformly in t.
These are ONE-LEG covers. No map to the fixed genus-nine endpoint is
asserted, and they are not counterexamples to the two-leg Pro question.
They rule out using ordinarity plus spin-birationality and unbounded
section growth alone as an exclusion.
