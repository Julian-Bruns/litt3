# Every actual common cover has an eight-index sixteen-spin series after étale refinement

Version1,2 October2026. Computation-free reusable implication; focused whole-argument review PASS. It does not solve either original common-cover problem or assert a common finite coefficient object.

Retain BOTH actual finite étale maps $X\xleftarrow hS\xrightarrow gY$ from the SAME smooth projective connected source, with the fixed genus-nine cyclic cubic $X$, its infinity $O$, $\omega_X\simeq O_X(16O)$, a selected genus-two $Y$, and degrees $n,8n$.

There is an actual connected finite étale refinement $T\to S$, with actual maps $q:T\to Y$ and $h_i:T\to X$ of degrees $8d,d$, such that $q$ is Galois and its deck group permutes the actual conjugate $X$ maps. On $T$ there is ONE degree-$d$ line $L$ with
\[
L\simeq h_i^*O_X(O),\qquad L^{16}\simeq\omega_T,
\]
for every conjugate map. The canonical sections $u_i\in H^0(T,L)$ have reduced zero divisors $h_i^*O$. Their span $W$ is basepoint free and is projectively deck invariant. Its dimension satisfies
\[
\boxed{8\mid\dim W,\qquad\dim W\ge8.}
\]
After constant rescaling, the actual theta pullbacks satisfy $h_i^*(dx/y^2)=u_i^{16}$ under the same canonical identification. The line is positive of degree $d$; no finite common coefficient or descent to both endpoints is inferred.

Under the exact noncyclic contact-eight hypotheses of the [branch-fiber theorem](contact_eight_noncyclic_branch_fiber.md), the actual evaluation orbit above its distinguished point $Q$ satisfies a stronger geometric constraint: each section of $W$ vanishing at a point of $q^{-1}(Q)$ vanishes there to order at least three. Therefore every projective hyperplane contains at most $d/3$ of the $8d$ actual labeled evaluation points, of density at most $1/24$, and $\dim W\le d/3+1$.
