## Old field obstruction, strengthened and reconnected

[No new large simple factor](ABELIAN_ETALE_TOWERS_ACQUIRE_NO_LARGE_SIMPLE_FACTOR.md)
extracts the factor-theoretic content of file95's audited proof. For
simple A of dimension d, End^0(A)=K and a0=[K intersect Qab:Q], a base
of genus h acquires no new A in any abelian étale cover if d>a0(h-1).
This includes all mixed primes and p-divisible degrees. Exact packet
multiplicity divisibility is also proved. Collaborative author proof.

For the fixed genus-nine JX, a0=2. The short discriminant argument was
checked PASS by /root/x_elliptic_quotient_maps, 2026-09-05. The
[arithmetic note and cheap certificate](EXPLICIT_GENUS9_ENDOMORPHISM_FIELD_ABELIAN_PART.md)
also give an S9 certificate for the real field. Thus Hom(JX,J(Y_n))=0
through every abelian tower over any genus-two base. This inequality
does not apply to the original genus-25 base.

[Fixed-corner lifting](FIXED_CORNER_CHARACTER_DESCENT_FOR_VARYING_TARGET_LIFTS.md)
and [norm-controlled moving strips](NORM_CONTROLLED_CHARACTER_DESCENT_OVER_MOVING_STRIPS.md)
