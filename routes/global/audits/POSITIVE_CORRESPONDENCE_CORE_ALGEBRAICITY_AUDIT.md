# Independent audit: a core and algebraicity of the positive return correspondence

Date: 2026-09-05. Auditor: `/root/positive_correspondence_core_algebraicity_check`.

Verdict: the proposed equivalence and Hankel criterion are sound for the
actual complex correspondence cycle category, with the two endpoints
treated as formally distinguished objects. No cohomological faithfulness
or lifting of a cohomological annihilator is used or established.

Scope: read `../BIETALE_REFINEMENT_BLOCKS_AND_POSITIVE_CORRESPONDENCE_CATEGORY.md`
in full and Theorem 1 of
`../CORELESS_GENERIC_GRAPH_FORCES_UNBOUNDED_PRIMITIVE_CORRESPONDENCES.md`.
Checked Definition 5.3, Lemma 5.4, and Proposition 5.10 in
[Krishnamoorthy, Correspondences without a core](https://msp.org/ant/2018/12-5/ant-v12-n5-p05-p.pdf).
After the initial check, read the complete new manuscript
`../CORELESSNESS_AS_TRANSCENDENCE_IN_THE_POSITIVE_CORRESPONDENCE_CATEGORY.md`.
Its explicit labelled-endpoint convention addresses the qualification
below, and its proof includes the direct path-source lifting argument.
The theorem as written in that inspected manuscript passes this check.

Let the connected joint-minimal finite bi-etale seed be
`Gamma: X <- C -> Y`, over an algebraically closed field, with smooth
projective hyperbolic endpoints. Put `P = Gamma* Gamma`. The following
are equivalent: the seed has a core; its two-object support-closed complex
linear category generated by the seed and its transpose is finite
dimensional; and `P` is algebraic over the complex numbers in the actual
cycle algebra `E(X,X)`.

Key checks:

- A core supplies finite maps `u: X -> S` and `v: Y -> S` with
  `u f = v g`. Every typed composition and every selected component
  preserves the common `S` coordinate. Each endpoint image is consequently
  an irreducible component of one of the four finite fiber products over
  `S`. There are finitely many such components. These fiber products may
  be ramified or nonreduced; only their reduced supports are needed for
  this finiteness argument. Only bi-etale components actually produced
  enter the category. Finite dimension implies algebraicity of `P`.
- In the coreless case, the primary graph criterion applies because `C`
  is hyperbolic. Its connected, locally finite, infinite bipartite graph
  has a blue vertex at distance exactly `2n` from the distinguished blue
  root for every `n >= 0`: finite balls and initial segments of shortest
  paths prove this directly.
- The support of `P^n` consists of the endpoint images of the length-`2n`
  path sources, with strictly positive integer multiplicities for every
  occurring component. Choose a vertex `q` at distance `2n`. The generic
  pair `(root,q)` lies on one such image. If that image occurred in
  `P^j`, `j<n`, its generic Omega-point would lift through the finite
  surjective map from a length-`2j` path-source component, since Omega is
  algebraically closed. This would give a length-`2j` walk to the same
  `q`, a contradiction. This direct lifting argument avoids ambiguity
  from different embeddings having the same endpoint image. Equivalently,
  such embeddings are conjugate over the rooted endpoint field and that
  conjugacy preserves rooted graph distance.
- Thus `P^n` has a basis image absent from all preceding powers. Its
  coefficient is a positive integer, retained in the complex coefficient
  field even in positive characteristic. The powers are linearly
  independent, excluding every nonzero polynomial relation.
- The coefficient-of-diagonal functional in Theorem 3 is faithful and
  positive: its basis norm is the degree of the left leg. Since `P*=P`,
  the matrix `H_n = [tau_X(P^(i+j))]_(0<=i,j<=n)` is the Gram matrix of
  `1,P,...,P^n`. Hence every `det(H_n)>0` precisely in the coreless case.
  In the cored case the determinants vanish for all sufficiently large
  `n`. No analytic completion is necessary.
- Replacing the seed by a connected refinement multiplies its cycle by
  its positive degree, and multiplies `P` by the square of that degree.
  Neither algebraicity nor the determinant vanishing criterion changes.

Wording qualification, addressed in the inspected manuscript: if `X=Y`
as curves, keep two labelled
copies in the generated category. In a literal one-object generated
category, arbitrary words such as `Gamma Gamma` are composable. A core
only supplies two potentially different maps `u,v: X -> S`, so the
fiber-product-over-the-core argument does not control these words.
The equivalence between having a core and algebraicity of `Gamma*Gamma`,
and the Hankel criterion, do not require this labelling qualification;
the asserted finite-dimensional generated category does.

The argument does not exclude a first coreless seed, establish Litt3,
or promote a Jacobian/cohomological polynomial relation to the actual
cycle algebra.

The manuscript's extra integrality and moment bound also check:
`tau_X(P^n)` is an effective integral diagonal coefficient, at most the
total left degree `(ab)^n`, where `a=deg(C/X)` and `b=deg(C/Y)`.
Consequently positive Hankel determinants are positive integers. The
stated identification of the coreless generated algebra with `C[t]`
follows from independence of all powers.
