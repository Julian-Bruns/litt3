# Intrinsic branch fibers force actual contact greater than 107/15

Version4,2 October2026. Version1 independent whole-argument audit PASS: [audit record](../../Research/audits/BRANCH_FIBER_UNIFORM_CONTACT_GAP_AUDIT_2026_10_02.md). The changed implications and constants passed a [focused extension review](../../Research/audits/BRANCH_FIBER_CONTACT_EXTENSION_REVIEW_2026_10_02.md). The unmarked common-cover problem remains unsolved.

For the selected MAIN pair, retain two actual finite étale maps $X\xleftarrow hS\xrightarrow gY$ from the SAME smooth connected projective source, of degrees $n,8n$. Let $I\simeq O_X^3\subset U\subset B_X$ be the original net and its degree-thirteen saturated hyperplane, and let its complete actual first trace $J\subset B_Y$ have degree one. Then
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{length}(h^*U/(h^*U\cap g^*J))>\frac{107}{15}n.}
\]
No positive q0 trace, strong semistability, finite projective monodromy or equal-line splitting is assumed.

If the length-three Cartier defect of $J$ is supported at at most two points, the stronger scoped bound is
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{length}(h^*U/(h^*U\cap g^*J))>\frac{143}{20}n=7.15n.}
\]
This assertion includes a cyclic triple point and double-plus-simple support. The general $107n/15$ bound includes three distinct simple points.

The geometric input is the intrinsic primitive-order-four line in the actual Cartier fiber. Every original cubic-branch net fiber contains it. At a cyclic target defect, all positive-contact branch quotient covectors annihilate its same two-dimensional preimage in the degree-one trace fiber, so lie in ONE projective line. Generic independence can survive only through determinant collisions. Moving-flat saturation and an aggregate pair-valuation budget bound generic collinear configurations. For three simple supports, infinity contributes at most one per sheet, so branch contacts require mass at least $(6+\epsilon)/8$ across three fibers; aggregate independent triple collisions contradict their degree budget. For at most two supports, two-slot convexity instead gives the stronger scoped bound.

The result excludes a uniform interval up to contact $(107/15)n$, and up to $7.15n$ for at most two target supports. Higher contact, noncyclic target defects allowed at contact at least eight, and the original unrestricted same-source problem remain unresolved.
