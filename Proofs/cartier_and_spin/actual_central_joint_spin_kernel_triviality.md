# Proof: a central actual joint-spin kernel is trivial

Version1,3 October2026. Pending independent review; scoped statement and retained actual maps are in the [theorem](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/actual_central_joint_spin_kernel_triviality.md).

Let ρ(h) be the genuine operator of h∈H on W, normalized by ρ(h)u_0=u_0. Choose any linear lift A_g of the projective G-action on W. If H is central in G, the projective operators commute. Therefore there is a scalar character ν_g:H→k* such that
\[
A_g\rho(h)A_g^{-1}=\nu_g(h)\rho(h).
\]
It is a character because ρ is a genuine representation of H. It is independent of the scalar chosen for A_g. Since H has exponent TWO, ν_g(h)∈{1,−1}. Comparing successive conjugations gives
\[
\nu_{g_1g_2}(h)=\nu_{g_1}(h)\nu_{g_2}(h).
\]
Thus g↦ν_g is a genuine homomorphism G→H∨, even though the G-action on W is projective. Its image has TWO-power order. The no-prime-to-FIVE quotient hypothesis makes this homomorphism trivial.

Consequently every A_g commutes with every ρ(h). Since u_0 is fixed by H, every original orbit line ku_i is fixed POINTWISE by ρ(H): if A_g u_0=c u_i with c≠0, then ρ(h)u_i=u_i. These lines span W, so H acts trivially on W and on the field generated by all ratios u_i/u_0. That field is the actual carrier Γ, where H acts faithfully. Hence H=ONE.

For the final rank statement, the orders of GL_a(F₂) for a=ONE,TWO,THREE are ONE,SIX,168, all prime to FIVE. The actual conjugation homomorphism G→GL(H) is therefore trivial if rank(H)≤THREE. This makes H central, and the proved implication gives H=ONE. No finite-group classification, computation, or enlargement of the actual source is used.
