# The actual fifth obstruction on the marked rank125 fixed line

Version3,2026-09-15. Original marked cover and canonical ordinary
reference. This is a geometric fifth-lift theorem in the specified
marked laboratory, not a common-cover theorem.

Use k0=F5[t]/(t³+t+1), k=bar F5 and field codes
[m]=m0+m1*t+m2*t², where m=m0+5m1+25m2. Retain the actual cover,
scalar Frobenius transport, completed kernel basis nu0,...,nu42,
periodicity line, preceding filtered object and graded identifications
of the [escape theorem](rank125_fourth_escape.md). Its actual
[fourth-reference origin](rank125_actual_fourth_reference.md) is

    Hstar=[101]nu1+[14]nu2+[122]nu11+[93]nu12
           +[117]nu15+[115]nu16+[83]nu27+[74]nu30+[58]nu31.

For every lambda in k put H_lambda=Hstar+lambda*nu39, where
nu39=s1³*s2⁴*s3⁴. The actual scalar fifth obstruction, independent of
all43 fourth-digit choices, is

    vartheta(lambda)=[56]+[25]*lambda
                    =(1+t+2t²)+t²*lambda.

Consequently exactly one third point of this line admits a compatible
fifth extension:

    lambda=[8]=3+t,
    H5=Hstar+[8]nu39.

Existence allows a fourth-digit translation and all terminal fifth
source and Hodge repairs. It does not assert that the particular
uncorrected fourth origin used to compute the scalar already extends
or that a sixth lift exists. Every
other geometric third point on this line is excluded at the fifth
stage, with all fourth choices allowed. The rest of the odd family,
the rank125 early descent/bootstrap and both unmarked candidate pairs
remain open.

There is a stronger exact result at H5. Let Gamma be the order-four
group generated by the two tame involutions of the escape theorem,
and let F be any perfect field containing k0. Its fixed scalar source
has basis nu1,nu2,nu11,nu12,nu15,nu16,nu27,nu30,nu31,nu39. The complete
relative fifth response satisfies, on F-points,

    ker(R_H5 | K^Gamma(F))=F*nu39,
    im(R_H5 | K^Gamma(F))={E in (R/(f))^Gamma(F):E100=0}.

Thus every compatible, Gamma-invariant fourth origin with E100=0
can be translated to admit W5 using coefficients in F. In particular
the present W5 tuple exists over W5(k0). Above a fixed invariant
fourth origin beta*, the fifth-admissible invariant fourth choices
form a torsor for F*nu39. This is a point/perfect-functor statement,
with no claim about reducedness of an unperfected parameter scheme.

For the default full-primary fourth origin in the proof, an explicit
admissible translation is

    Vstar=[93]nu1+[39]nu2+[82]nu11+[83]nu12+[90]nu15
           +[57]nu16+[10]nu27+[119]nu30+[28]nu31.

Here translating means adding the actual n(Vstar) to the fourth
source digit, with its whole regular second Hodge repairs. All
invariant admissible fourth choices relative to that default origin
are Vstar+tau*nu39, tau in F. The subsequent terminal fifth source
and regular primitives are obtained by the full six-row primary
solve, with coefficients remaining in F.

There is also an exact description without the second tame fixedness
condition. Let K_odd be the fixed space of the first tame involution
H(s)->-H(-s), and put delta=[117]nu32+nu33. Then, over every perfect
F containing k0,

    ker(R_H5 | K_odd(F))
       =F*delta + F*nu39 + F*nu40 + F*nu41.

In particular every such kernel direction lies in K9, equivalently
in original AS degree at most3. All odd fourth choices above H5
that admit W5 are exactly the prescribed default origin plus

    Vstar+x0*delta+x1*nu39+x2*nu40+x3*nu41,  xi in F.

This is an exact point/perfect-functor fiber, including possible
mixed-additive regular terms; a dimension count for an additive map
alone would not prove it. No claim about the even fourth directions
or a sixth lift on this larger family follows.
