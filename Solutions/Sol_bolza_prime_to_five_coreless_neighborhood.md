# Proof: prime-to-five arithmetic commensurations, then descend endpoints

[Statement](../Theorems/Thm_bolza_prime_to_five_coreless_neighborhood.md).
This constructs known characteristic-zero correspondences with controlled
good reduction. It does NOT lift an arbitrary given characteristic-five
two-leg span, or infer simultaneous lifting from good reduction of endpoints.

## 1. The fixed arithmetic seed

The Bolza curve B:y^2=x^5-x has good reduction at5. Its characteristic-five
fiber is isomorphic to C: choose a^6=2,r^2=2,b^2=-r and set

    s=t/a,  x=r(s+1)/(s-1),  y=bv/(s-1)^3.

Then r^5=-r and v^2=2(s^6-1), so both y^2 and x^5-x equal
-2r(s^6-1)/(s-1)^6. The Mobius map is invertible. This is a function-field
isomorphism, hence an isomorphism of smooth projective curves. The four
Cartier–Manin coefficients in (x^5-x)^2, at degrees4,3,9,8, vanish,
so C is superspecial. No uniqueness classification is invoked.

The precise arithmetic input is
[Katz–Katz–Schein–Vishne](https://u.math.biu.ac.il/~scheinm/text61.pdf),
Section4, Theorems1.4–1.5 and Propositions5.3,5.5. The compact surface
group Gamma uniformizing B is a congruence subgroup of the projective
norm-one group of a maximal quaternion order over F=Q(sqrt2). The algebra
is split at one real place and at every finite place except(sqrt2).
Gamma's level is supported at2; specifically it lies between principal
levels2 andsqrt2. These statements concern B itself, not a Jacobian
parametrized by another Shimura curve.

Let lambda be either prime over7. Then F_lambda=Q7, the algebra splits
there, and Gamma has the maximal compact condition atlambda. By
[Voight, Main Theorem28.5.3](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-56694-4_28)
choose g in the rational norm-one group integral at every finite place
other thanlambda, congruent to1 modulo2 at the dyadic place, and with
negative lambda-adic trace valuation. Indeed these conditions form a
nonempty open subset of the finite adeles, and the split real place
ensures strong approximation. Negative trace valuation gives a nonzero
translation length on the local tree. The cyclic subgroup generated by
any nonzero power of g is therefore unbounded.

For every integer n, Gamma intersection g^n Gamma g^-n contains a
projective principal lambda^m congruence kernel normal in Gamma, for sufficiently
large m depending on n. Outsidelambda conjugation preserves the specified
local conditions; atlambda sufficiently deep principal kernels lie in
both compact groups. The analogous containment holds on the conjugate
side. Both normal-core quotient orders consequently divide

    |SL2(O_F/lambda^m)|=336*7^(3(m-1)),

and are prime to5. Working projectively can only reduce this order.

## 2. The same property for a normal prime-to-five subgroup

We need the following elementary group fact. If L normal in Gamma and
L' normal in Gamma' have prime-to5 indices, and Gamma intersection Gamma'
has prime-to5 normal-core index on both sides, then L intersection L'
does too, relative to L and L'.

For one side let R=core_Gamma(Gamma intersection Gamma'), R0=R intersection L,
and S=R0 intersection L'. Then R0 is normal of prime-to5 index in L;
S is normal in R0, and R0/S embeds in Gamma'/L'. Intersect the conjugates
of S under representatives for L/R0. The intersection K is normal in L,
lies in L intersection L', and R0/K embeds in a finite product of groups
of order prime to5. Thus [L:K] is prime to5. Interchange sides.

Apply this with L normal in Gamma of arbitrary finite prime-to5 index,
L'=g^n L g^-n and Gamma'=g^n Gamma g^-n. The two maps defined by the
identity and g^-n from

    (L intersection g^n L g^-n) backslash upper-half-plane

to L backslash upper-half-plane are actual finite etale maps of compact
curves; their Galois closures have prime-to5 groups.

## 3. A sufficiently high power makes the span coreless

A compact surface lattice L has finitely many discrete overgroups.
Indeed the universal positive lower bound for hyperbolic orbifold area
bounds their indices. For an overgroup M with [M:L]=d, take
K=core_M(L). Then [L:K]<=d!, so the possible K belong to a finite list
because L is finitely generated. For each such
core K the normalizer N(K)/K is finite, being the automorphism group of
the compact hyperbolic curve K backslash upper-half-plane. This gives
only finitely many overgroups.

The normalizer N(L) is a finite extension of L. It is in the arithmetic
commensurator and is lambda-adically bounded: L is contained in a compact
local group and there are finitely many cosets. Thus the lattices
g^n L g^-n are pairwise distinct. Otherwise a nonzero power of g would
belong to N(L), putting its entire unbounded cyclic subgroup inside the
bounded group N(L), a contradiction.

Each of the finitely many discrete overgroups of L has only finitely
many subgroups with L's covolume, since the corresponding index is fixed.
Consequently, outside a finite set of n, the group generated by L and
g^n L g^-n is nondiscrete. A common nonconstant meromorphic function on
the upper half-plane would be invariant under this group. A sequence
of nonidentity elements tending to the identity has distinct images of
a suitable regular point accumulating at that point, contradicting the
identity theorem. Hence the endpoint fields intersect in constants.

## 4. Specialize one genuine two-leg diagram

Let D->C be any Galois connected etale cover with group order prime to5.
Start with the strict henselization R of a number-field valuation ring
at5 and the good Bolza model over R, whose geometric closed fiber is C.
[Stacks Lemma58.9.1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0A48) lifts this
GIVEN cover, with its Galois action, uniquely to a finite etale cover of
the proper model. Its generic fiber D0->B has surface group L normal in
Gamma of the same index. All this finite-presentation data descends to
an etale neighborhood of the original valuation ring, hence to a number
field with a place over5. Construct the characteristic-zero selfspan of
D0 in Sections2–3. Its curves and maps also descend to a number field
after finite extension: finite covers of the algebraic B descend to
Qbar, as do maps between the resulting hyperbolic curves. Fix the place
extending the one already chosen. Thus the prescribed special cover and
the arithmetic construction belong to one valuation-theoretic diagram.

Both leg closures have order prime to5, so
[Stacks Theorem58.30.3](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BUQ)
extends each leg to a finite etale cover of the fixed good model of D0
after a common finite extension of the valuation ring. The resulting
source models are smooth proper curves of genus at least2 with the same
specified generic fiber. Uniqueness of the stable model identifies them
by that generic-fiber identity. Thus BOTH original maps extend on ONE
smooth proper source. Geometric connectedness is retained.

[Krishnamoorthy, Lemma4.10](https://msp.org/ant/2018/12-5/ant-v12-n5-p05-p.pdf)
then implies that the special selfspan on D is coreless: a core in the
special fiber would give one in the generic fiber. Its prime-to5 Galois
closures also specialize etale, so the claimed monodromy bound survives.

## 5. Preserve any two prescribed endpoints in the family

By hypothesis there are actual spans C<-Wi->Ci (i=1,2) with both legs'
Galois closures of order prime to5. Take a connected compositum D of the
geometric Galois closures of Wi->C. Then D->C is Galois etale of prime-to5
order, and D->Wi->Ci are etale with prime-to5 monodromy by the tower
property below. Apply Section4 to D, giving D<-Z->D coreless.
Compose the left map with D->C1 and the right map with D->C2. The new
endpoint subfields are SUBFIELDS of the old ones inside k(Z), so their
intersection remains k. Both maps are still finite etale from the same Z.
Galois closures of these towers remain prime to5: their groups embed in
successive wreath products of the prime-to5 leg and endpoint closures.

Only D->C was lifted in Section4. The given maps D->Ci are composed on
the SPECIAL fiber; this proof makes no simultaneous-lifting assertion
about them. It covers the entire equivalence class in the definition,
not just curves admitting a map to C. The same argument, if the Wi->Ci
monodromy hypothesis is omitted, still constructs a coreless etale span,
but then makes no prime-to5 assertion for its final leg closures.

The actual Galois cover H->C has group C3^2 by
`hermitian_genus_two_test`. This proves the Hermitian assertion and the
statement for its prime-to5 Galois-closure covers by composition.

Do not replace this argument by arbitrary Cartesian endpoint extension:
that operation can create cored components. For example, extending both
endpoints of a coreless span to its own source gives a diagonal component
with two identity maps. The proof instead lifts the fixed seed cover,
constructs a coreless selfspan there, and then DESCENDS endpoints.
