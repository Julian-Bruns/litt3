# Picard group of a two-point effective orbifold with an actual atlas

Version1,3 October2026. [Independent whole-scope review: PASS](../../../Research/audits/TWO_POINT_WILD_ORBIFOLD_PICARD_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md).

Let k be algebraically closed, let Γ be a smooth projective connected curve, and let H act faithfully on Γ. Assume the coarse quotient Γ/H is P¹ and has exactly two branch values, with inertia orders e and m. Wild ramification is allowed. For S=[Γ/H], its Picard group has the presentation
\[
\operatorname{Pic}(S)=\langle U,D_1,D_2\mid eD_1=U,\ mD_2=U\rangle.
\]
Here U is the pullback of O_{P¹}(1), and D_i is the reduced stack divisor over the ith branch value, of degree1/e or1/m. Consequently the degree-zero subgroup is cyclic of exact order gcd(e,m).

This is a statement about the actual quotient stack, not an identification of a wild stack with a tame root stack. The canonical line has the actual different exponent at each branch value; no tame canonical-weight formula is substituted.
