## The canonical rational frame

Use the notation of the [statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/genus_two_global_liftable_cartier_kernel_witness.md).
The Frobenius pullback F*B_Y is the fourth infinitesimal diagonal
algebra modulo its constant subline. In the rational x-coordinate put
ε=x_second−x_first and f=1/w. The zero-diagonal infinitesimal integral
of η is
\[
\psi=f\varepsilon+\tfrac12 f'\varepsilon^2+
\tfrac16 f''\varepsilon^3+\tfrac1{24}f'''\varepsilon^4.
\]
Here primes differentiate in x. Its derivative in the second variable
agrees with the pullback of η through order three. This and its zero
diagonal value characterize it uniquely: differentiation on the
augmentation ideal through degree four has invertible coefficients
1,2,3,4. Thus the local expressions glue. Products ψ^j are taken in
the infinitesimal diagonal algebra before quotienting its constants;
the quotient itself is not regarded as an algebra.

Let D=w∂x and h=2B(Ax⁵−1). Direct differentiation, holding the second
factor fixed, gives ∇Dψ=−1+hψ⁴ before removing constants. Hence
\[
\nabla_D\psi=h\psi^4,\quad
\nabla_D\psi^2=-2\psi,\quad
\nabla_D\psi^3=-3\psi^2,\quad
\nabla_D\psi^4=-4\psi^3.
\]
To check h, write f=(Ax⁵+Bx⁴−1)²/w⁵. All w⁵ and x⁵ coefficients
are killed by ∂x; the x⁴ coefficient in the resulting expression is
2B(Ax⁵−1). Therefore w⁵f''''/24=h. The frame is fixed by η, and
these formulas are not a presumed arbitrary global splitting.

The native alternating form in this frame has P14=4,P23=3,
P32=2,P41=1, all multiplied by η⁵. Indeed its defining Cartier
formula pairs ψ^i and ψ^j by jη⁵ when i+j=5. This form is flat.

## Regularity, first jet and the condition at P

Away from P, η is a unit and ψ,…,ψ⁴ form a regular frame. The
ψ² coefficient of v is one, so v has no zero there. At P choose
ζ=x^−1/2 and write η=g(ζ)dζ, where
\[
g=\kappa\zeta^2(1+O(\zeta^2)),\qquad
\kappa=-2/\sqrt A.
\]
In the regular augmentation basis εζ,…,εζ⁴ the coefficients of v are
zero, g², gg'+ag³, and
g g''/3+(g')²/4+(3/2)ag²g'+bg⁴,
where a=a0+a2x² and b=b0+2a2xw. Their orders are at least
∞,4,2,1. The last coefficient has exact order one because a2≠0.
Consequently the rational map 1↦v defines a saturated O(P) line.

To test its first derivative at P, pass to the regular horizontal
primitive basis [ζ],[ζ²],[ζ³],[ζ⁴]. Write v=ζu0+ζ²u1+….
The coefficients found directly by expansion satisfy u0≠0 and
u1∈k u0. In the general polynomial family this condition is
a3=0 and b5=2a2; the displayed witness satisfies both. Therefore
the second fundamental form of O(P)⊂E vanishes at P. This is the
intrinsic first-order condition retained from an actual fold, rather
than an assertion of an entire degree-ten lift.

Evaluation is the ψ coefficient multiplied by η. Hence evaluation
of v is zero, and that of ∇Dv is −2η. The intrinsic first jet of
1↦v is therefore 2η². For O(P) this has divisor3P; for the
composite O(−P)→O(P) it has divisor5P. The composite has zeros2P.
At x=0, v still has ψ² coefficient one, proving the stated wild
fiber nonvanishing.

## Saturated degree and adjunction divisor

Compute the alternating annihilator u of the first three columns
by their signed three-by-three minors and the displayed native
pairing. Its entries are polynomial pairs f_i(x)+g_i(x)w. The
script checks that it annihilates all four columns.

On the affine curve the ideal generated by u_i has length six.
This is computed without numerical root approximations: as an
F125(ρ)[x] submodule of the affine coordinate algebra, its generators
are (f_i,g_i) and (H g_i,f_i), H=Ax⁵+Bx⁴−1. The monic gcd of
their two-by-two minors has degree six. For a full-rank submodule
of the rank-two free module over the PID k[x], that determinant
gcd measures the colength. Since the affine curve is smooth, this
is exactly the common-zero divisor D of the section u.

In the same regular augmentation basis at infinity, the four
coefficients of u have orders −10,−11,−11,−11. Therefore u has
order −11 and its saturated line has degree6−11=−5. The line is
F*Q, where Q=K^⊥. Thus degQ=−1. The alternating form on B_Y
takes values in ω_Y₁, so B_Y/K=Q*⊗ω_Y₁ and degK=2+degQ=1.

The norm of the adjunction coefficient u1, divided by the monic
affine common-zero determinant, has degree six and is squarefree.
This proves six simple affine adjunction zeros. At P the adjunction
coefficient has order −10 and the line generator has order −11,
so its zero is simple. Hence all seven adjunction zeros are simple.
Locally a horizontal line with an adjunction zero of order one has
leading primitive order two; it is different from the line orthogonal
to the entire evaluation kernel, whose leading primitive order is
four and whose adjunction zero has order three. Therefore the
evaluation of E is surjective everywhere. Equivalently this follows
by the local horizontal primitive pairing matrix. Its kernel H is
locally free of rank two.

Finally, the affine D ideal gives its hyperelliptic Mumford class.
The exact ideal-reduction algorithm reduces D−6P to a nonconstant
degree-two Mumford pair. Thus O(D−6P) is nontrivial. Since
F*Q=O(D−11P), it is not O(−5P). The determinant identity
detH=ω_Y^4⊗F*Q proves the final assertion.

## Descended effectivity and liftability

This additional check was executed after the seven-part witness audit.
Write g for the rational fourth coefficient of u in the flat primitive
basis [x_second^j]. Then u/g is horizontal, and the independently
executed gauge check verifies that all four flat ratios have derivative
zero. Since divu=D−11P, global sections of F*Q^-1 are represented by
functions in L(11P−D). The ten functions1,x,…,x⁵,w,xw,x²w,x³w form
L(11P). Imposing vanishing on D gives a four-dimensional space V.
The affine D condition is exact polynomial remainder modulo its
squarefree degree-six ideal, not a numerical root condition.

The induced canonical connection on F*Q^-1 sends a section represented
by f∈V to
\[
df-f\,dg/g.
\]
Indeed ∇u=(dg/g)u because u/g is horizontal. Cartier descent identifies
the kernel of this k-linear map with H0(Y₁,Q^-1). The source
`oct03_gram_four_descended_effectivity.sage` clears denominators and
computes its exact matrix on the four-space. Its rank is four, proving
h0(Q^-1)=0. This is a descended calculation, rather than the invalid
substitution h0(F*Q^-1)=4.

Since Q^-1 has degree one on a genus-two curve, Riemann–Roch gives
h1(Q^-1)=h0(Q^-1)=0. Applying Hom(Q,−) to
0→O_Y₁→F_*O_Y→B_Y→0 shows that every map Q→B_Y lifts to F_*O_Y.
The lift is unique because Hom(Q,O_Y₁)=H0(Q^-1)=0 as well. Thus this witness is unmarked and liftable, despite its saturated
O(P) line in H. The saturated O(P) line has a degree-TWO line quotient in H. A putative subline of degree at least THREE has zero map to that quotient, so factors through O(P); its map there is also zero. Thus no such subline exists. Since E/H=ω_Y has degree TWO, the same argument excludes every degree-at-least-THREE line of E. The marked bounded-primitive conclusion therefore cannot extend to arbitrary Q from liftability.
