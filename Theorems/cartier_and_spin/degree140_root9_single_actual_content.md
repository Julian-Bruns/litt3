family, a source defining an actual connected everywhere-étale

ONE geometric point of X_q. If nonempty, it is simple and lies above
one of the three roots of t.

of those endpoints. The new restriction compares the actual scales
forced at two different endpoints. All nine endpoint-pair/relative-sheet
charts are empty on the necessary nonzero-scale open.

norm on multiple-content ratios. The remaining single-point content
