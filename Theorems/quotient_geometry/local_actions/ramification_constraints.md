# First-break Swan divisibility and tame ramification constraints

Version1,2026-09-14.

Let \(k\) be perfect of characteristic \(p>0\), and let a nontrivial
finite \(p\)-group \(G\) act faithfully and \(k\)-linearly on \(k[[z]]\).
Use the lower ramification groups \(G_i\), and put
\[
 \epsilon=\sum_{i\ge1}(|G_i|-1).
\]
If \(b\) is the first positive break and
\(|G/G_{b+1}|=p^r\), then
\[
 p^{\lceil r/2\rceil}\mid\epsilon-b(|G|-1).
\]
The same statement applies to every subgroup, using its own lower
filtration \(H_i=H\cap G_i\).

Suppose the action extends to \(I=G\rtimes C_t\), with \(p\nmid t\).
Write its positive lower breaks as \(b_1<\cdots<b_\ell\), and put
\(r_i=\dim_{\mathbf F_p}(G_{b_i}/G_{b_i+1})\). Then
\[
 t\mid b_i(p^{r_i}-1),\qquad t\mid\epsilon,
 \qquad b_1\equiv\cdots\equiv b_\ell\not\equiv0\pmod p.
\]
These are necessary constraints on actual local actions.
