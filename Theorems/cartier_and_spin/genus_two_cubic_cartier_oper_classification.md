# Explicit cubic-Cartier oper coefficients and at most five actual horizontal pencils

Version1,3 October2026. Pending independent review. Fix a smooth monic quintic model Y:y²=Φ(z) with P=∞ Weierstrass, σ=dz/y, in characteristic FIVE. Put
\[
\Phi=z^5+qz^4+rz^3+sz^2+tz+u,\qquad
H=2qz^5+2qu+2rt+s^2.
\]
The complete coefficients E∈L(5P) satisfying the logarithmic dormant-oper equation ∂²E=3E²+2H, where ∂=y d/dz, are the following.

If q≠0, there are the TWO nonpolynomial coefficients
\[
E=qz^2+3rz+s\pm\sqrt q\,y,
\]
and the polynomial coefficients
\[
E=2qz^2+bz+\frac{b^2+2rb+4qs}{q},
\qquad b^3+2rb^2+3qsb-q^2t=0.
\]
Thus there are at most FIVE distinct coefficients; there is no generic reducedness assertion.

If q=0, the coefficients are E=±√(2rt+s²), and, when r≠0, also E=3rz+s. There are at most THREE distinct coefficients. No further coefficient functions occur.

For each fixed coefficient E which admits an F∈L(7P) of pole order FIVE or SEVEN with simple finite zeros and satisfying ∂²F=EF, the bounded horizontal space
\[
\{f\in L(7P):\partial^2f=Ef\}
\]
has dimension at most TWO over k. Therefore every actual low-degree F arising in [the large-wild carrier](large_wild_genus_two_small_packet_incidence.md) belongs to one of at most FIVE actual bounded horizontal pencils on fixed (Y,P,σ); its distinguished-wild subset lies in L(5P). This does not bound the number of pencil members or construct/exclude a global carrier. Both original finite étale endpoint maps and all inherited data remain on the SAME T.
