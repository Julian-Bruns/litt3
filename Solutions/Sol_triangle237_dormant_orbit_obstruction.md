# Dormant decoration removes the three primitive degree84 survivors

Version1,2026-09-10, root proof; focused audit PASS by
/root/audit_triangle237_dormant_decoration. This is a new partial
exclusion, not a proof that the degree84 row is empty.

## 1. A functorial Frobenius-orbit multiplier

Work over bar(F_p). Let C be defined over F_(p^s), with geometric moduli Frobenius orbit
exactly s. Suppose a finite set D(C) of intrinsic decorations is one
orbit of length h under Frob_(p^s), and every geometric automorphism
of C acts trivially on D(C). Let a normalized finite cover f with
source C determine d(f) in D(C), functorially for source isomorphisms
and coefficient Frobenius. Then the Frobenius orbit of the COVER
class of f has length divisible by sh.

Proof: all coefficients are algebraic over F_p, so the cover orbit is
finite. If f^(p^j) is isomorphic to f, their source curves are isomorphic,
so s divides j. Write j=sl. Both sources are now the identical displayed
F_(p^s) model, and an isomorphism of the two covers is an automorphism of
C. On decorations it must identify Frob_(p^s)^l d(f) with d(f). Since
automorphisms act trivially, h divides l. This argument does not require
the cover to be defined over F_(p^s), nor descent of its field of moduli.

## 2. A dormant connection supplied by every actual(2,3,7) map

On P1 with normalized branch values0,1,infinity put, in characteristic5,

    r_0(t)=2/t²+2/(t-1)²+3/[t(t-1)]
          =(2t²+3t+2)/[t²(t-1)²].

Exact rational differentiation gives r_0''-3r_0²=0. Its double-pole
coefficient at each of0,1,infinity is2; inversion is a Mobius change,
so has zero Schwarzian. If f:C->P1 is an actual separable tame map
with the complete uniform profile(2,3,7), its projective pullback is

    r_C(z)=(f'(z))² r_0(f(z)) - {f,z}/2.

This is a rational dormant connection by coordinate covariance of the
characteristic-five curvature test: E=r''-3r² is the oper-line
projection of p-curvature, so E(dt)^4 is intrinsic. The full fifth
companion iterate is given in [the scalar model](Sol_nilpotent_scalar_model.md),
Section1. The audit independently verified the universal Schwarzian
identity for E and the zero full p-curvature of r_0.

It is regular everywhere. At an actual tame point of index e=2,3,7,
choose a completed local coordinate with the corresponding base
uniformizer equal to z^e. This is possible because e is a unit. The
double-pole coefficient of the displayed pullback is

    2e²+(e²-1)/4=0 in F5.

A possible base simple pole pulls back to order e-2>=0, and the other
terms are regular. Away from the three branch fibers f is etale and
r_0 is regular. Thus d(f)=r_C is a genuine regular dormant projective
connection, not only a connection on a punctured curve. The assignment
is defined over F5 and is functorial for normalized cover isomorphisms.

## 3. The backup supplies h=5 and s=3

For C_alpha:v²=F(u) with alpha³+alpha+1=0, the complete dormant scheme
is reduced of length5 and is one orbit under Frob125. This is an
already audited input of backup_hermitian_atlas_exclusion, proved
from the original curvature equations in backup_genus_two_prepare.sage.
Its irreducible separator in b2 is

    z^5+(alpha+1)z^4+(2alpha²-2)z³-2alpha²z²
      +(-2alpha²+alpha+1)z+(2alpha²+2alpha-2).

The existing branch-point computations show moduli orbit3 and
Aut(C_alpha)=C2, generated by the hyperelliptic involution iota.
These same inputs occur in the degree24/48 exclusions.

The involution acts trivially on all regular projective connections.
Indeed H0(omega²) in genus2 has basis(du/v)²,u(du/v)²,u²(du/v)²,
fixed by iota. The affine space of connections is a torsor for this
space, so iota acts by a translation. Its square is the identity and2
is invertible, hence that translation is zero. In particular it fixes
every dormant connection. No arbitrary isomorphism can identify two
different members of the five-cycle.

Section1 therefore forces the orbit of any proposed cover to have
length divisible by15, not merely3.

## 4. Apply only to the existing primitive bucket

The checked degree84 census has155 simultaneous-conjugacy classes.
Its existing geometric exclusions leave42 hyperelliptic-factor classes
and THREE primitive classes46,55,90. Full tame specialization injects
actual characteristic-five covers into this census and preserves their
monodromy block systems, as in triangle238_frobenius_factor_obstruction.
Primitivity is intrinsic and preserved by coefficient conjugation.
All the existing source exclusions also hold for Frobenius conjugates.

Consequently a primitive f on C_alpha would yield at least15 distinct
primitive cover classes in a bucket of size3, a contradiction. The
PSL2(F83) identification is not needed. None of the three primitive
survivors can have source C_alpha.

The remaining42 classes are NOT thereby excluded. Their source curves
need not all be C_alpha conjugates; divisibility of42 by15 is not a
valid test. The new script checks the rational dormant identity/local
coefficients and reuses the existing hashed tables without regenerating
the census. Monodromy-order refinement was tested: all42 hyperelliptic
survivors have the SAME order

    1544820563565707308559000457954401938526818836607401984000000000.

It supplies no additional partition. Their geometric source comparison
remains open; a useful next direction is the dormant pullback equation
itself, not reconstruction of the already excluded primitive sources.
