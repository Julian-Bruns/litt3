# A monic degree-eleven combination with no moving endpoint denominator

Version1,30 September2026. Work on the primitive critical-family chart
of [the multiplied traces](degree140_endpoint_multiplier_traces.md),
with coefficient ring
\[
R=K[H,H^{-1},q,q^{-1},\Psi^{-1}].
\]
The ideal in R[mu] generated by the three multiplied traces, the three
positive traces and the three
[corrected companion traces](degree140_companion_trace_coordinate_correction.md)
contains a monic polynomial of degree eleven. Its construction uses
only additions, polynomial multiplication and inversion of existing
chart units. No exceptional parameter fibre is discarded.

More precisely the normalized multiplied trace with multiplier x*t
has degree twelve and unit leading coefficient
\[
\langle29601\rangle q^{15}H^{-33}.
\]
Reduce the other eight traces by this polynomial. Let N0,N2,N6 denote
the leading numerators in degree eleven of, respectively, the t,
x^2*t and t^4/phi trace rows. The exact coefficient certificate gives
polynomials W0,W2,W6 in K[H,q] such that
\[
W_0N_0+W_2N_2+W_6N_6=q^{16}.
\]
Multiplying each row by its known leading denominator and the
corresponding W, then dividing the sum by q^16, constructs the claimed
monic polynomial. The weights have H-degrees6,0,0 and q-degrees80,78,77.

Every allowed nonzero fully ramified fibre annihilates this polynomial.
This improves the denominator-free elimination system; it does not
improve the earlier degree-nine bound that allowed moving endpoint
denominators. No emptiness or common-cover decision is asserted.
