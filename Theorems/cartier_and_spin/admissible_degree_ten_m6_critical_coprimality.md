# Full critical denominator and a finite rational-root boundary in m6

Version1,1 October2026. Retain an ACTUAL nontrivial admissible
degree-ten source in the $d=10,m=6$ profile of
[the nonzero profile theorem](admissible_degree_ten_nonzero_profiles.md),
with critical cubic $D$, trace-dual numerator $U$, and short coordinate
$W$. The finite coordinate is $w=W+Z/y$.

The critical cubic is squarefree over $K=k(X)$ and
\[
\gcd(D,U)=1.
\]
In particular every rational critical root is uncanceled by the
annihilator numerator, and the reduced critical denominator has
degree three. This does not assert $D$ irreducible.

Any rational critical root $c$ must have exact short infinity pole5
and forces
\[
\delta_3=\kappa q,\quad q=([13],[18],[24]),\quad
c=\frac{([22]+[15]x)y^2+g}{q}-\frac Zy,
\quad\kappa\ne0,\quad
g\in L_{10}=\langle1,x,x^2,x^3,y\rangle.
\]
The other short critical coefficients have exact pole11 and16
for $\delta_2$ and $\delta_1$, respectively. The finite-frame
root $c_f=c+Z/y$ has exactly three simple finite
poles among the six unramified points above $x=[12],[16]$,
distributed as one and two, or two and one, on the two fibers,
and has no other finite pole. The complementary monic quadratic's
finite-frame linear coefficient has simple poles at exactly the
other three points. The fixed-field convention is $\beta^2=\beta+3$,
$[a+5b]=a+b\beta$.

These are necessary conditions on a hypothetical rational root
of an already actual source. The stronger
[critical irreducibility theorem](admissible_degree_ten_m6_critical_irreducibility.md)
now excludes this boundary by exact necessary source identities.
The normal form is retained as the geometric reduction used there.
