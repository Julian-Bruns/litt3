# Core-preserving refinements with fixed auxiliary degree

Version3,2026-09-14. The cover construction now uses Ore's theorem
and allows prescribed mixed inertia. The original core and primitive-weight
arguments retain their audited scope.

Let k be algebraically closed. Start with an actual finite bi-etale
coreless span X<-Z->Y of smooth projective connected curves of genus>=2.

## Compositum criterion

Let X'->X and Y'->Y be finite Galois covers whose inertia groups
normally generate their Galois groups. Suppose their completed extensions,
pulled back to Z, agree at every point. In the tame case it suffices that
the ramification indices agree point by point.

Every connected component of the normalized reduced fiber product
X' x_X Z x_Y Y' is a bi-etale coreless span X'<-W->Y'. If both auxiliary
groups are perfect, every primitive canonical weight prime to char(k)
is preserved. No restriction on the original leg degrees is needed.

## Prescribed tame inertia

Let G be a finite nonabelian simple group generated by two elements,
with order prime to char(k) when the characteristic is positive. On any
smooth projective connected curve C of genus>=2, at any nonempty finite
set of distinct points P_j, prescribe nonidentity elements sigma_j in G.
There is a connected G-Galois cover C'->C branched exactly there, with
inertia conjugate to <sigma_j>. It is genuinely ramified. If n_j is the
order of sigma_j, then

    g(C')-1=|G|(g(C)-1)+(|G|/2) sum_j(1-1/n_j).

In particular PSL2(F7), of order168, allows arbitrary mixed indices
from{2,3,4,7} in characteristic zero or any characteristic not dividing168.

In characteristic five, any clump in the original span therefore admits
compatible degree168 endpoint covers of a uniform index n in{2,3,4}.
The upper legs remain etale and coreless, their degrees grow by at most
a factor168, and every prime-to-five primitive weight is retained. For
a reduced branch divisor D,

    g(C')-1=168(g(C)-1)+84(n-1)deg(D)/n.

## Cartier-zero application

Suppose the shared canonical ring is k[s], where s has primitive weight
d prime to5 and div(s_C)=eD_C with D_C reduced. Suppose its eligible
generalized Cartier image vanishes. Put E=e/d in F5. If E=0, use the
identity covers. Otherwise E is1,2 or3; choose n=3,2 or4, respectively.

On the resulting bi-etale coreless span, s still has primitive weight d,
and its zero multiplicity e'=n(e+d)-d is divisible by5. Its associated
dormant projective connection is regular, with distinguished horizontal
Borel everywhere transverse to the oper Borel. Both structures match on W.

The auxiliary degree is independent of the tensor weight, original leg
degrees and clump size. The endpoint genera still grow with the clump,
and the endpoints have changed. This construction neither supplies a
clump nor excludes the resulting transverse dormant data.
