# The actual spin rank bounds an endpoint-preserving source

Version2,3 October2026. Both the recurrence bound and the sharper geometric bound passed [independent whole review](../../Research/audits/ACTUAL_SPIN_CARTIER_RECURRENCE_RANK_BOUND_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). The original common-cover problem remains UNSOLVED.

Work over k=F₅bar, with the fixed genus-nine X, θ=dx/y², and absolute Cartier C. Retain BOTH actual finite étale maps from the SAME source to X and a selected genus-two Y. On its actual Galois sixteen-spin refinement q:T→Y let G be the deck group, deg q=8d, and let h_i:T→X be the original conjugate maps of degree d. Use the actual basepoint-free infinity series W⊂H⁰(T,L), L¹⁶≅ωT, with h_i*θ=u_i¹⁶. Put r=dim W and let m count the distinct section lines ku_i. Set
\[
n_r=r\binom{r+2}{3},\qquad B_r=\frac{25^{9n_r}-1}{24},\qquad
M_r=\min\{B_r,2350000^r\}.
\]
Then:

1. For ANY two actual finite étale maps to X from one connected smooth projective source, proportional pullbacks of C²θ imply proportional pullbacks of θ, and hence identify their actual embedded X-fields. The scalar of proportionality belongs to F₂₅.
2. The original orbit size satisfies m≤M_r. The sharper bound 2350000^r also bounds the number of distinct lines in ANY r-dimensional W⊂H⁰(T,L) whose sixteen-power is an ACTUAL pulled fixed theta form, for any separating map T→X, provided L¹⁶≅ωT. It does not require a G action or étaleness of those extra maps.
3. There is an ACTUAL smooth connected quotient source Z of T retaining finite étale maps to BOTH X and Y, with
\[
\deg(Z/X)=bm/8\le3M_r/8,\qquad \deg(Z/Y)=bm\le3M_r,
\qquad b\in\{1,3\}.
\]

This is a bound for every FIXED spin rank r. No uniform bound on r, on the original source degree, or on whole alternating transport is supplied. No simultaneous Galois closure, common coefficient, or atlas X→[T/G] is asserted. The sixteen-power image used here is the actual multiplication image of W⊗Sym³(W^(5)); no projective normality or surjectivity onto H⁰(T,ωT) is assumed.
