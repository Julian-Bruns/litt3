# Odd trigonal pencils, two-primary W3, and three-point tensors

Let \(C\) be a smooth projective connected curve over an algebraically
closed field of characteristic different from 3, and let
\(x:C\to\mathbf P^1\) be a degree-three map with trace-zero bundle
\(\mathcal O(-a)\oplus\mathcal O(-b)\), \(a\le b\). Every rational
function outside \(k(x)\) has degree at least \(b\). If all local
ramification indices of \(x\) are odd and \(2r<b\), then for any
\(O\in C(k)\), \(W_r(C,O)\) is disjoint from every translate by a
nonzero element of \(J(C)[2]\).

Let \(A\) be an abelian variety over a finite field of odd
characteristic and \(W\subset A\) a closed Frobenius-stable
subvariety containing zero and disjoint from its nonzero
\(A[2]\)-translates. If a Frobenius power \(M\) satisfies
\(M-I=2U\) on \(T_2A\), with \(U,I+U\) invertible, then
\(W\cap A[2^\infty]=\{0\}\). More generally, if
\(M-I=2^sU\), \(s\ge2\), with \(U\) invertible, then
\(W\cap A[2^\infty]\subset A[2^s]\).

For the [fixed curve](../Definitions/Def_fixed_pair.md), the trace-zero
splitting is \((-4,-7)\), and
\[
W_3(X,O)\cap J(X)[2^\infty]=\{0\}.
\]
In particular, let \(g_0\) be a power of two and let
\(0\ne s\in H^0(X,\omega_X^{3g_0})\) have divisor
\(16g_0E\), with \(E\) reduced of degree 3. Then
\(E=x^*(c)\) for some finite \(c\in k\) with \(F(c)\ne0\), and,
up to a nonzero scalar,
\[
s=s_0^{g_0},\qquad s_0=(x-c)^{16}\theta^3.
\]
Set \(d=3g_0\), choose \(1\le r\le4\) with \(rd\equiv1\pmod5\),
and put \(n=(rd-1)/5\). Its generalized Cartier image is nonzero:
\(C_{X,n}(s^r)\ne0\).

Thus this divisor shape cannot occur for a primitive coreless shared
canonical generator (whose Cartier image would have to vanish).
It also excludes the entire two-branch single-wild-jump signature
family \((q,j,t_0,m,D)=(5,2,1,3,16)\), in the notation
\(t=g_0t_0\), other tame order \(d=g_0m\),
\(D=(\delta-qt)m-qt_0\), and lower jump \(j\).
Other supports and weights are not excluded.
