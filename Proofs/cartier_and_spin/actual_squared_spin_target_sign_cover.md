The actual square-source theorem gives E=Γ0·X_i and T=Γ·E, with [T:Γ]=[E:Γ0]=TEN. Thus E and Γ are linearly disjoint over Γ0. The monodromy of E/Γ0 contains the monodromy of its base change T/Γ, which is S10. Being a subgroup of S10 on its TEN sheets, it is itself S10. Its deck group is therefore trivial: the normalizer of a point stabilizer S9 in S10 is S9.

The normalized squared infinity series on E is basepoint-free and defines its actual target Γ2, with hyperplane line pulling back to F. Any σ∈Aut(E/Γ2) fixes the pullback hyperplane sections. Comparing the canonical pullback linearization on F8 with ωE gives ONE constant scalar cσ on every original θ_i. The constants form a character. Actual theta recognition forces cσ∈μ3, and if cσ=ONE then σ fixes EVERY original X-map. Since E is their compositum, σ is the identity. Hence Aut(E/Γ2) embeds in C3.

The extension Γ0/Γ2 is genuinely normal elementary TWO. Every σ in that cubic deck group preserves Γ0, by normality, and its restriction to Γ0 belongs to a TWO-group. The restriction is therefore trivial. It lies in the trivial deck group of E/Γ0 and is the identity. This proves Aut(E/Γ2)=ONE. Its subgroup coming from G/H has trivial kernel as asserted.

For the ramification support, use projective coordinates indexed by the original infinity sections. On the torus where all those coordinates are nonzero, coordinatewise squaring is finite étale in characteristicFIVE. The inverse image of the squared curve is a union of sign translates of the original curve. Normalization commutes with this étale base change, so the normalized map Γ→Γ2 is étale wherever all original infinity sections are nonzero. It can ramify only at their zero divisors. Its elementary TWO deck group has tame cyclic local inertia; the only nontrivial cyclic elementary TWO group is C2, so each nontrivial index is TWO. Since Γ→Γ0 is étale, these are also the indices and support of Γ0→Γ2.

At a point of q*P the original φ has indexTWO. A nonzero descended infinity section can vanish there only with even order after pullback through φ. But every original u_i has reduced zero divisor h_i*∞ because h_i is étale. Therefore none of the original infinity sections vanishes at q*P. These points lie in the torus just considered, so Γ0→Γ2 is étale at their images. At an original infinity point φ is unramified, and so is E→Γ0; all additional ramification is consequently indexTWO inherited from Γ0→Γ2. The different tower gives precisely the old reduced q_E*P plus the pullback of this possible additional infinity-supported reduced different. No other ramification appears.

The trivial source deck alone does not bound the sign degree. A Galois cover of the target can have deck transformations that do not lift through the degree-TEN source extension. The actual normalized action and branch-group argument below are needed.

For the final normalized-action clause, the original G action permutes the original infinity section lines. Thus it preserves Γ2=k(r_i²), because a transformed ratio square is a constant times a ratio of other original squares. It consequently normalizes the entire elementary TWO deck group Gal(Γ/Γ2), and induces the asserted normalization of Δ_s on Γ0. An element in (G/H)∩Δ_s lifts to an automorphism of E fixing Γ2, which was just proved trivial. Thus the intersection is ONE.

If a=rank(Δ_s)≤THREE, conjugation defines a homomorphism G/H→GL_a(F2), whose order is prime toFIVE. The no-prime-to-FIVE-quotient hypothesis makes that homomorphism trivial. Hence Δ_s centralizes G/H and acts on B=Γ0/(G/H). It fixes each of the TWO unique branch values, since their inertia types differ. Its map to Aut(B) is injective: an element trivial on B belongs to the full Galois group G/H, whose intersection with Δ_s is ONE. A finite elementary TWO subgroup of Aut(P1) fixing TWO points has order at mostTWO, so |Δ_s|≤TWO.

If Δ_s=C2, its nonidentity δ induces the sign involution of a coordinate on B and fixes exactly those TWO branch values. Any fixed point of δ on Γ0 would therefore lie above one of them. Above the tame-TWO branch it would give a second commuting order-TWO inertia element, since δ centralizes G/H and is not in it. A tame curve-point stabilizer is cyclic, so this is impossible. Above the weak-FIVE branch, choose a uniformizer t linearizing δ as δ(t)=−t. A nonidentity g of its wild inertia has breakONE, hence g(t)=t+αt²+O(t³), α≠0. The equality δg=gδ forces α=−α, impossible in characteristicFIVE. Thus δ has no fixed point, and Γ0→Γ2 is actually étale. This excludes ramification for a≤THREE without asserting the étale double itself impossible.
