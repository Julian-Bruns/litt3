# Positive critical traces have only finitely many possible ratios

Version1,30 September2026. Retain the primitive ratio chart and the three
global polynomials P0,P1,P2 in
[degree140_global_positive_trace_equations](degree140_global_positive_trace_equations.md).
On H*q*Psi !=0, the projection to q of their common geometric zero locus
is finite. In particular no component of the necessary trace locus
dominates the q-line. The entire geometric fibre q=3 is empty.

This assertion concerns all algebraic extensions of the coefficient
field, not only rational parameter values. The exceptional finite set of
q-values has not yet been enumerated. The full primitive square locus,
and the unmarked common-cover problem, remain undecided.
