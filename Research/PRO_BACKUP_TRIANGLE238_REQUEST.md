# One degree-48 map on a specified genus-two curve

Please decide ONE existence question. This is a bounded subsidiary lemma
for the common-cover problem, not a request to solve that whole problem.
The degree-24 case is already settled; do not redo its census or proof.

## Target

Work over k=algebraic closure of F5. Choose alpha with
alpha^3+alpha+1=0, and let C be the smooth projective curve

    C: v^2=F(u)=u(u-1)(u-2)(u-3)(u-alpha).

Does there exist a finite separable degree-48 map f:C->P1 with exactly
three branch values, normalized to0,1,infinity, and COMPLETE fibers

    f^*(0)=2D0,    f^*(1)=3D1,    f^*(infinity)=8Dinf,

where D0,D1,Dinf are reduced pairwise disjoint divisors of degrees24,16,6,
and f is unramified everywhere else?

The map need not be Galois. All inertia is prime to5, but the geometric
monodromy group may have order divisible by5. The question is over ALL k,
not just maps with coefficients in F125. A branch-cycle tuple alone is
not a map on the specified C.

## Established inputs — take these as given

1. C has genus2 and Aut_k(C) consists only of the hyperelliptic involution.
   Its geometric moduli Frobenius orbit over F5 has length3.
2. J(C) is ordinary and geometrically simple, with geometric endomorphism
   algebra the quartic CM field generated by a root of

       Z^4-8Z^3+182Z^2-1000Z+15625.

   Its Rosati-fixed real field is Q(sqrt21). In particular there is no
   nonconstant map C->elliptic curve and no Rosati-symmetric square root
   of5 in End^0(J(C)).
3. Every connected cyclic etale cover of C of degree1,2,3 or6 has ordinary
   Jacobian. This is NOT asserted for arbitrary finite etale covers.
4. The five regular dormant projective connections on C are reduced.
   For EVERY such connection r and EVERY chi in Pic(C^(1))[3] or[4],
   H^0(C^(1),V_r tensor chi)=0, where V_r is the rank-two Cartier descent
   of the connection q''=r q on the first-jet bundle J^1(omega_C^2).
   Thus V_r has degree2, is stable, and its global sections are precisely
   the dormant tangent vectors. These complete twist checks are done.
5. C has no uniform tame maps with the following complete profiles:

       degree16: (2,4,8);       degree24: (3,3,4);
       degree24: (2,4,6);       degree36: (2,3,9);
       degree6:  (2,2,3,3);     degree12: (2,2,2,3).

   Nor does C have a representable etale atlas to the full Hermitian
   quotient [H/PGU_3(5)], H:X0^6+X1^6+X2^6=0. All cubic determinant
   twists were included. Do not conflate this with arbitrary common
   etale covers of C and H.

For an ACTUAL genus-two map with the degree12(2,2,2,3) profile, our
stronger general result constructs either a nonhyperelliptic involution
or an INTEGRAL Rosati-symmetric T with T^2=[5]. This may be used if an
actual intermediate map on C is produced; it is not a statement about
arbitrary intermediate curves or abstract group representations.

## Useful information specific to the NEW profile

Use the projective-connection convention z''=r z, with pullback

    f^*r=(f')^2 r(f)-{f,u}/2.

The target-line connection for(2,3,8) is

    r0(t)=(2t^2+3t+2)/(t^2(t-1)^2).

It is DORMANT, not active. Explicitly put

    h=t^2(t-1)^2,    z0=(t+2)/(t^3(t-1)^3).

Then h''/h=r0, r0''-3r0^2=0, z0'=h^(-2), and
r0=-{z0,t}/2. These rational identities have been checked in F5(t).
The prescribed tame indices make the pulled-back connection regular
at all points of C. Thus f determines one of the FIVE given dormant r.
The rational developing function z0 is NOT an etale map and cannot be
used as an etale-cover contradiction.

Here is a small bundle constraint, supplied with its verification so it
need not be reinvented. Write K_C for a canonical divisor. Hurwitz and
the two principal fibers give

    K_C ~ D0+2D1-9Dinf,
    2D0 ~ 8Dinf,    3D1 ~ 8Dinf,
    4Dinf ~ 12K_C.

Hence chi=O(Dinf) tensor omega_C^(-3) lies in Pic(C)[4].
The rational horizontal quadratic

    q0=f^2(f-1)^2(df)^2

has divisor6D0+10D1-50Dinf. Its horizontal first-jet vector gives,
after Cartier descent, a SATURATED line

    omega_C(-Dinf)=omega_C^(-2) tensor chi^(-1) -> V_r,

with the harmless Frobenius twists transported in this display. Locally,
the minimum order of(q0,q0') is5 at D0,10 at D1,-50 at Dinf, and0
elsewhere; division by5 gives D0+2D1-10Dinf~K_C-Dinf.
Thus one obtains a nonzero section in the eight-dimensional space

    H^0(C^(1),V_r tensor omega_(C^(1))^2 tensor chi_1).

The dimension8 follows from stability, duality and Riemann--Roch. This is
only a bounded pool, NOT an existence/equivalence theorem. In particular
the un-twisted-by-omega^2 vanishings in input4 do not exclude it.

If using monodromy, full tame specialization allows all transitive pairs
a,b in S48 with types2^24,3^16,(ba):8^6, up to simultaneous conjugacy,
as an upper bound for the characteristic-five possibilities. Preserve
the branch labels and all nonnormal cases. The inclusions of triangle
groups Delta(3,3,4) or Delta(2,4,8) into Delta(2,3,8) do NOT imply that
an arbitrary surface subgroup lies in one of them. Base changing to a
cover of C is not an intermediate factorization of the original f.

## Deliverable and why it matters

A nonexistence proof removes the next degree48 row from the smaller
backup pair's remaining list. An explicit f, with all divisors checked,
settles the opposite verdict and identifies the actual orbifold to test
against the other endpoint. Either verdict changes our next action.

Use whichever geometry or finite computation actually decides this ONE
case. Exact computation is welcome if its completeness and its effect
on the specified characteristic-five curve are proved. Include executable
source and a verification transcript if computation is used; distinguish
an executed certificate from proposed code or a characteristic-zero tuple.

Do not replace the target by another qualitative finiteness result,
repeat the earlier degree24 result, or provide a catalogue of weaker
necessary conditions. If undecided, identify the exact remaining step
briefly, and separate any genuinely new result from the supplied facts.
