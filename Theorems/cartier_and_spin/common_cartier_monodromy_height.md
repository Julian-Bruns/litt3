# A clumpless span forces unbounded infinitesimal monodromy height

Version3,3October2026. Let k be algebraically closed of positive
characteristic p. Retain two actual finite etale maps
X<-Z->Y of smooth proper connected hyperbolic curves, and suppose
there is NO clump. The category of common vector bundles with the
specified pullback comparisons is a neutral Tannakian category,
using evaluation at any chosen point of Z.

More generally, let E be a finite commutative algebra object in this
common category, whose algebra at ONE endpoint's generic point is
a field. Write A for its fiber algebra and H for its algebraic
monodromy group. For every point \(\epsilon:A\to k\), the orbit
coaction is injective:
\[
(\epsilon\otimes1)\rho:A\lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow k[H].
\]
Thus the orbit has the WHOLE fiber as its scheme-theoretic image,
including all nonreduced structure. Any characteristic-primary
nilpotence visible in A is also visible in k[H]. No Frobenius or
genus divisibility assumption is needed for this general orbit lemma.

Use ABSOLUTE Frobenius to regard
\[
\mathcal A_r=F_{\mathrm{abs}*}^{,r}\mathcal O
\]
as a common algebra object on the SAME span for every r. This keeps
the scalar Frobenius action; it does not identify relative twists
as k-curves. Let H_r be the algebraic Tannaka group of the full
tensor subcategory generated by A_r, and let h_r be the least
nonnegative integer with
\[
x^{p^{h_r}}=0\quad
\text{for every }x\in\sqrt{0}\subset k[H_r].
\]
The orbit of the unique geometric point of Spec(k[t]/t^(p^r)) is
the WHOLE Frobenius fiber scheme-theoretically. More precisely, the
orbit coaction embeds its coordinate algebra into k[H_r]. Thus
\[
k[t]/(t^{p^r})\ \lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow\ k[H_r],
\qquad t\longmapsto a_0,
\qquad a_0^{p^r-1}\ne0.
\]
In particular
\[
h_r\ge r.
\]
There is no genus or covering-degree divisibility exception. Even the first
Cartier algebra has nonreduced common monodromy, and every further
height forces a larger lower bound on infinitesimal height.

No tensor category generated by ONE finite-rank common bundle can
contain all the A_r. Equivalently, the common algebra tower cannot
be accommodated by a single affine algebraic monodromy group of
finite type. The assertion allows arbitrary covering degrees and
uses the actual two-map common category.

This is a necessary condition on a hypothetical no-clump span.
Unbounded infinitesimal height is not a contradiction: the
[full coordinate-group tower](truncated_coordinate_algebra_model.md)
provides the relevant algebraic model. This theorem neither produces
a clump nor solves either candidate common-cover problem.
