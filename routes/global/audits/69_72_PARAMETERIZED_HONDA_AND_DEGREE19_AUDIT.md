## Checks performed

### File 69

- Checked the Honda screen from the CM type of
  \(Y_\ell:z^2=1-t^\ell\).  The \(p\)-decomposition group is
  \(H=\langle p\rangle\), \(p\) splits completely in
  \(E=\mathbf Q(\zeta_\ell)^H\), and the valuations of
  \(F^f\) at the primes of \(E\) are the coset counts \(m(aH)\).  Their
  denominator lcm is the Honda--Tate Schur index.  Trivial translation
  stabilizer of the valuation vector really gives
  \(\mathbf Q((F^f)^N)=E\) for every \(N\), because \(E/\mathbf Q\) is
  abelian.  The resulting simple factor has dimension
  \([E:\mathbf Q]f/2=(\ell-1)/2\), so it exhausts the Jacobian and stays
  simple over every finite constant extension.
- Checked the cyclic-orbit multiplicity argument.  If the line generated
  by \(a^*\) were stable, the induced order-\(r\) element in the division
  algebra would generate \(E(\zeta_r)\), of degree \(r-1\) over \(E\).
  The intersection \(E\cap\mathbf Q(\zeta_r)=\mathbf Q\) is valid for the
  distinct primes \(r,\ell\).  On a primitive right
  \(\mathscr D\)-space of dimension \(m\), the field embedding into the
  central simple endomorphism algebra gives \(r-1\mid fm\), hence exactly
  the stated multiple of \((r-1)/\gcd(r-1,f)\).  The invariant projection
  of a cyclic module is generated by one vector, while the nonzero norm
  makes it one copy rather than zero.
- Recomputed the norm and Lefschetz identities.  For \(j\ne0\), the
  correspondence \(a_*\beta^{j*}a^*\) has Lefschetz number \(I_j\), so its
  \(H^1\)-trace is \(2M-I_j\).  Substitution gives (69.29)--(69.30) with
  no missing factor.  Etale-locally over \(C\), the normalization branches
  of the image in \(C\times Y\) are smooth graphs.  The conductor on each
  branch is the sum of its intersections with the other branches, so the
  ordered coincidence divisors give conductor degree \(\sum I_j\) and
  delta invariant \(\frac12\sum I_j\), as stated.
- Checked the individual-cross-image adjunction formula.  For bidegree
  \((d,d)\), self-intersection is
  \(2d^2-\langle w,w\rangle\), canonical intersection is
  \(4d(h-1)\), and the etale normalization has genus
  \(d(h-1)+1\).  Their difference is exactly (69.34).
- Checked the full-order coefficient envelope prime by prime.  Away from
  \(p\), coefficient extraction from an operator preserving the rank-one
  \(\mathcal O_K\)-lattice gives the inverse relative different, including
  at the ramified prime \(\ell\).  At an intermediate slope, condition
  (69.11) makes the fractional valuations of the \(f\) graded terms
  distinct, so cancellation is impossible.  The slope-zero Tate lattice
  gives the integral bound, and Rosati adjunction gives the slope-one
  bound.  These are precisely the valuations defining \(\mathcal I_s\).
  Cross terms have nontrivial cyclic grade and reduced trace zero, while a
  diagonal term has trace
  \(p^s\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbf Q}(x_s\bar x_s)\), verifying
  (69.40).  The adjoint valuation calculation also verifies the claimed
  isometry \(\mathcal I_s\simeq\mathcal I_{f-s}\).
- Checked the finite-difference argument.  Intersections with the graphs
  of \(\rho^b\) and \(\iota\rho^b\) give the two inequalities
  \(2d\mp t_b\ge0\).  Multiplying the inverse-different coefficient by
  \((\zeta_\ell-1)^f\) puts it in
  \((1-\zeta_\ell)\mathcal O_K\), whose field trace lies in
  \(\ell\mathbf Z\).  Hence the reduced sequence is a polynomial function
  of degree at most \(f-1\).  The value-set bound, injectivity of reduction
  on \([-2d,2d]\), the identity \(\sum_b\rho^b=0\), and nondegeneracy of
  the cyclotomic trace pairing give the asserted vanishing of the
  \(K\)-component.  Orthogonality of the grading then makes the lattice
  minimum contradiction in Theorem 69.12 valid.
- Reran
  `67_CUBIC_CROSS_ROSATI_GAP_CERTIFICATE.sage`, which is the numerical
  specialization used in the last paragraph of file 69.  It reproduced
  the slope multiset
  \((0,0,1,1,1,2,2,2,3,3)\) and both exact weighted minima \(106\).
  The comparison \(106>2\cdot3(3+14)=102\) is correct.

### File 72

- Reran `72_DEGREE19_REDESIGN_CERTIFICATE.sage` with SageMath 10.9.  It
  passed all assertions: smoothness and the three point counts of the
  explicit plane quartic, its Weil polynomial, the exact ratio resultant,
  irreducibility of the two nontrivial resultant factors, and the coset
  data for \(Y_{71}\).
- Independently checked the Honda data.  The displayed count sequence has
  no nonzero cyclic period and contains values prime to \(5\), so both
  hypotheses of Proposition 69.1 hold.  Theorem 69.4 forces primitive
  multiplicity to be a positive multiple of 16.  At \(M=19\), one block
  has dimension \(16\cdot35=560\) and two would have dimension 1120,
  exceeding the 608-dimensional Prym; hence the multiplicity is exactly
  16.
- Checked all dimension counts in Corollary 72.2: \(g(C)=39\), the norm
  image has dimension 35, \(c^*J(X)\) has dimension 3, and the vanishing
  of \(c_*h\) gives Rosati orthogonality, hence a codimension-one sum in
  \(J(C)\).
- Recomputed both Castelnuovo numbers.  For a degree-38 curve in
  \(\mathbf P^{14}\), \(37=2\cdot13+11\) gives bound
  \(13+22=35<39\).  For degree 19 in \(\mathbf P^{17}\),
  \(18=1\cdot16+2\) gives bound 2.  Generic projection in the first case
  preserves degree and birationality.
- Checked the full-span quadratic contradiction.  Since \(2\nmid19\), it
  is the quadratic-core branch.  Castelnuovo gives \(g(B)\le2\), so the
  invariant map from the simple threefold \(J(X)\) into \(J(B)\) must
  vanish.  Signed pullback rigidity then forces \(X\) to be hyperelliptic,
  contradicting the hypothesis.
- Checked the explicit quartic's absolute-simplicity certificate.  The
  point counts give the stated reciprocal degree-six polynomial.  In the
  ratio resultant, \((v-1)^6\) accounts for equal Frobenius roots; the two
  remaining factors are irreducible and their primitive integral
  associates have leading coefficient 125, so neither is cyclotomic.
  Thus no quotient of distinct roots is a root of unity, all powers of
  Frobenius retain six distinct conjugates, and the Jacobian stays simple
  over every finite extension.

## Non-breaking suggestions and objections

- File 69 jumps from Proposition 69.4 to Proposition 69.7.  This is only a
  numbering gap.
- In Proposition 69.1, one sentence explicitly invoking the
  Honda--Tate fact that normalized local invariants are unchanged after a
  constant-field extension would make the passage from (69.9) to absolute
  simplicity easier to verify.
- In Proposition 69.10, “locally free of rank one” should be read after
  localization at each factor of
  \(\mathcal O_K\otimes\mathbf Z_{\ell'}\).  Stating this explicitly would
  avoid any impression that a global freeness assertion is being used.
- Proposition 69.11 could display the two graph-intersection formulas
  \(\Gamma\cdot\operatorname{Graph}(\rho^b)=2d-t_b\) and
  \(\Gamma\cdot\operatorname{Graph}(\iota\rho^b)=2d+t_b\).  They are the
  immediate source of (69.44).
- The final strategic table in file 72 predates file 74.  Under the same
  hypotheses on \(X\) and \(Y\), Corollary 74.5 now eliminates **every**
  quadratic coefficient row at \(M=19\), without the scalar-window
  condition.  Thus lines 340--358 are stale as a description of the
  current route, but this does not weaken or invalidate any theorem proved
  in file 72.
