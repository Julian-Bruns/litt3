# Comparison pole degree six forces a single degree-55 pattern

Version 2, 24 September 2026. Let two actual finite etale maps to the
fixed X share its specified line or tensor, have distinct embedded
fields, and jointly generate the source field. Assume that the
comparison function has pole degree six. Use the quadratic quotient
S and normalized s,epsilon of
[the genus-bound theorem](new_line_degree_six_genus_bound.md).

Then their common degree is EXACTLY n=55. The quadratic quotient has
3<=g(S)<=16. Among the 29 possible finite common-pole values:

- four values have no pole;
- eight have one unramified simple common pole;
- ten have two unramified simple common poles;
- seven have one ramified common pole of order three.

The minimal denominator has degree32, with seven double roots.
There are exactly seven possible normalized weight rows, and they
form one arithmetic-conjugacy orbit. Taking the endpoint indexing
of the squarefree certificate, one representative has endpoint
indices (0,58;31,89) and weight row
\[
(2,3,0,1,3,1,0,3,2,3,2,1,2,2,2,3,1,0,1,1,0,1,3,2,2,2,1,2,3).
\]
Weights record total pole order in the quadratic fiber; weight3
means a ramified pole of order3, not three different sheets.
The comparison scalar is determined by the endpoint and moment data.
These necessary conditions are inconsistent: the later
[complete exclusion](pole_six_complete_exclusion.md) proves a nonzero
third-order differential obstruction for the single arithmetic class.
The present reduction is retained as its finite input.

As a corollary, equality of the specified line or proportionality
of the tensor recognizes the same embedded X-field for EVERY pair
of actual covers of common degree at most EIGHT. This uses joint
minimalization, the complete pole-degree-three exclusion, and
delta in <3,10>, delta<=n. At n<=8 the only positive possibilities
are delta=3,6; neither can occur.
