# Unmarked saturated annihilators and the original net's two-point incidence

Version1, 3 October2026. Fresh independent bounded
[audit PASS](../../Research/notes/oct03_ten_hour/unmarked_net_cohomology_audit.md).
This applies in **every original coefficient-source dimension** under
the stated rank, integral-net, and original-sheet hypotheses. No specified
eight-dimensional module or Gram rank is an assumption. It is an endpoint
classification and actual-source necessary condition; it does not decide
the unmarked common-cover problem.

Let $Y$ be the fixed ordinary genus-two endpoint in characteristic five,
$Y_1=Y^{(1)}$, $F=F_Y$, and
$B=F_*\mathcal O_Y/\mathcal O_{Y_1}$. Let the actual rank-three
degree-one $K\subset B$ be **saturated**, with alternating annihilator
$Q=K^\perp$ of degree minus one. Retain the actual rank-four degree-one
$J\subset B$ with integral quotient $J/K=\mathcal O_{Y_1}$.
Write $D_J$ for its determinant-defect divisor on $Y_1$ and $R_Q$ for
the degree-seven adjunction zero divisor of $F^*Q\to\omega_Y$ on $Y$.
All support intersections below use the canonical bijection of underlying
points under relative Frobenius; no line class is transported merely from
its degree.
For every original-source assertion retain BOTH actual finite étale maps
$q:T\to Y$ and $h:T\to X$ on the SAME smooth connected projective $T$,
with degrees $8d$ and $d$. Write $q^{(1)}:T_1\to Y_1$ for the relative
twist of this map, reserving $q_j$ for the original three net directions.
The maps $\mathcal O_{T_1}^3\to q^{(1)*}J\to B_T$ must be those of the
actual pulled original net, and their composite with
$q^{(1)*}(J/K)=\mathcal O_{T_1}$ must be the actual nonzero constant
row $\ell$, with $\ell(q_0)=0$.

1. For every degree-minus-one line $Q$ on $Y_1$,
   $\dim\operatorname{Hom}(Q,B)=4$. Its maps lift through
   $F_*\mathcal O_Y$ with obstruction in $H^1(Q^{-1})$.
   If $Q^{-1}$ is ineffective, all four dimensions lift uniquely.
   If $Q^{-1}=\mathcal O(S)$, the liftable maps form a dimension-three
   hyperplane. Thus a nonzero lifting obstruction forces
   $Q=\mathcal O(-S)$ for an actual point $S$, without identifying it
   with the fixed source point $P_0^{(1)}$.

2. The embedded $J$ is exactly the inverse image of a nonzero
   $\sigma\in H^0(\omega_{Y_1}Q^{-1})$; this space has dimension two,
   $D_J=\operatorname{div}\sigma$, and its actual extension
   $e_J=\partial\sigma\in H^1(K)$ is nonzero. Retain BOTH original
   finite étale legs on the SAME original source $T$ and their actual
   constant quotient row $\ell$. If $\ell(q_1)\ne0$ and the accepted
   infinity support ledger puts the canonical wild pair
   $R_+^{(1)}+R_-^{(1)}$ in $D_J$, then
   $Q^{-1}=\mathcal O(S)$ for the residual degree-one point $S$.
   The accepted nonzero-quotient theorem supplies saturation under
   those exact hypotheses.

3. Instead assume $\ell(q_1)=0$. Retain the actual étale $G$-Galois
   $q:T\to Y$ of degree $N=8d$, an actual étale $h:T\to X$ of degree
   $d$, and the original three-section net and its actual maps into
   $q^{(1)*}J$. Assume its quotient values are the retained constants
   $\ell(q_j)$, with $\ell(q_0)=0$ and $\ell(q_2)\ne0$.
   Retain the accepted fixed-$X$ ledger: primitive orders $11,8,2$
   at infinity; ten reduced finite cubic branches with rank-two
   raw net image containing the intrinsic primitive-order-four line;
   every constant two-form plane has saturation degree at most four.
   Then at least $9d$ distinct actual source cubic-branch points map
   into $\operatorname{supp}D_J\cap\operatorname{supp}R_Q$.
   In particular that intersection has at least TWO distinct points.

If $Q^{-1}$ is ineffective, $|\omega_{Y_1}Q^{-1}|$ is a base-point-free
degree-three pencil. The last conclusion says that at least two
distinct adjunction zeros must lie in a single fiber of this exact
descended pencil. Absence of that incidence excludes the saturated
$\ell(q_1)=0$ source for that embedded annihilator.

In particular, an ineffective inverse annihilator with pairwise distinct
images of its adjunction-zero support under this descended pencil is
excluded in BOTH quotient branches, provided the stated original net and
nonzero-$q_1$ infinity-support antecedents are retained. This is a
conditional source exclusion for that actual embedded annihilator, not
an assertion that every annihilator satisfies the distinct-image test.
Saturation is an explicit assumption in the zero-$q_1$ branch.
