# The backup's maximal exponent-four and exponent-six covers are ordinary

Version2,24 September2026. The full character calculations are retained;
the later unit-root theorem covers the old monodromy exclusion.

Let B be the genus-two backup with alpha^3+alpha+1=0. Its maximal
abelian exponent-four and exponent-six finite etale covers both have
ordinary Jacobians. Their degrees are respectively 256 and 1296.

Every connected abelian finite etale cover of B with group exponent
dividing four or six is therefore ordinary, as is every connected
finite etale five-group cover of such a curve. This is Jacobian
ordinarity, not indigenous ordinarity. The stronger actual-span
monodromy exclusion is in the
[unit-root theorem](../isogeny_sieves/five_by_abelian_unit_root_exclusion.md).
