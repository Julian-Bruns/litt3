# Doubled phase pairs cannot occur in rank three

Version2,30 September2026. Use the genuine full-support eight-label
endpoint rows of
[the rank-three boundary](eight_label_rank3_span_two_distinct.md).
Suppose that at one endpoint each of the four unordered phase pairs is
doubled: its pair at root i is
{a_i,a_i}. Then its stacked matrix with
ANY genuine full-support second endpoint has rank FOUR.

There is no restriction on the second endpoint's phase span or repeated
pair pattern. Combining this with the completed span-two theorem, every
remaining rank-three configuration has a span-three endpoint and neither
endpoint has four doubled pairs. The mixed span-two/span-three
and general span-three/span-three cases remain open, as do their scalar
graph and shared-moment requirements. No common-cover decision follows.
