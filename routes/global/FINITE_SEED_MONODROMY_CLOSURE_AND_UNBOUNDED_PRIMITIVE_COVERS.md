# Finite seed monodromy closure and unbounded primitive covers

**Status:** proved structural test, 2026-09-05. The first result strengthens
[the generic-path theorem, Theorem 3](CORELESS_GENERIC_GRAPH_FORCES_UNBOUNDED_PRIMITIVE_CORRESPONDENCES.md).
The second concerns covers of one base only; it supplies no second etale leg.
Throughout, base curves are over an algebraically closed field.

## 1. The exact closure invariant supplied by finite seeds

For finite groups `G_X,G_Y`, let `C` be the smallest isomorphism-closed
class of finite groups containing them and closed under subgroups,
quotients, and extensions. An extension means that `N,Q in C` and
`1 -> N -> E -> Q -> 1` imply `E in C`.

Every leg monodromy group of an alternating path source generated by a
fixed bi-etale seed with leg monodromies `G_X,G_Y`, and of every joint
minimalization of that source, belongs to `C`.

**Proof.** Base change a Galois closure with group `G_i` and select a
connected component. Its group is a subgroup `D <= G_i`. The selected
component of the original non-Galois cover has monodromy
`D/core_D(J)` for its stabilizer `J`. Thus a step monodromy is a **section**
(quotient of a subgroup) of `G_i`; a literal subgroup assertion is
unnecessary and need not hold.

For a tower of finite separable extensions, the Galois closure group
embeds into an iterated permutation wreath product of the step monodromy
groups. For two steps the containing wreath product has the form
`A^d semidirect B`, where `B` permutes the `d` embeddings of the lower
extension and each copy of `A` controls a conjugate upper extension.
Finite direct products and these wreath products belong to `C` by
extension closure; their subgroups do as well. Build the path from either
endpoint to prove the assertion on both legs. Passing to an intermediate
endpoint field gives a quotient of the path monodromy group: its normal
closure lies in the normal closure of the path field. This proves the
claim. The argument applies equally to any finite list of seeds.

There is an equivalent concrete description. Let `S` be the finite list
of finite simple groups that occur as sections of subgroups of `G_X` or
`G_Y`. Then

\[
 C=\{G:\text{ every composition factor of }G\text{ belongs to }S\}.
\]

Indeed, every member of `S` belongs to `C`, and extensions reconstruct
every group with those composition factors. Conversely `S` is itself
closed under taking simple sections of subgroups: sections of sections
are sections. Intersecting a composition series with a subgroup shows
that its composition factors are simple sections of subgroups of the
original simple factors, hence remain in `S`. Quotients and extensions
also retain these factors. This proves the equality.

This controls simple-group types, not their multiplicities, and is
stronger information than prime support in general. Using just the
composition factors of `G_X,G_Y` would be incorrect: a subgroup of a
nonabelian simple seed can introduce additional simple factor types.
Membership in `C` is a necessary invariant of seed-generated paths;
it is not a claim that every member of `C` is geometrically generated
by the chosen seed correspondence.

## 2. Fixed closure types allow unbounded primitive covers of one base

Fix any smooth projective curve `X` of genus `g >= 2` in characteristic
five. For every integer `r >= 3`, put

\[
 n=2^{r-2},\qquad V=(\mathbf F_{3^n},+),\qquad
 H=\langle\zeta\rangle\cong C_{2^r},\qquad G_r=V\rtimes H,
\]

where `zeta` is a primitive `2^r`-th root in `F_(3^n)` and acts by
multiplication. Then `X` has a connected non-Galois finite etale cover of
degree `3^n` with monodromy `G_r` and no proper intermediate cover.
All these groups belong to the single closure class generated by `C_2,C_3`.

**Arithmetic and irreducibility.** For `t >= 1`, elementary induction
using difference of squares gives
`v_2(3^(2^t)-1)=t+2`; for odd `m`, `v_2(3^m-1)=1`.
More generally for even `m`, this valuation is `2+v_2(m)`.
Consequently `ord_(2^r)(3)=2^(r-2)=n`. The smallest field containing
`zeta` over `F_3` is therefore `F_(3^n)`. An `H`-invariant additive
subspace is stable under `F_3[zeta]=F_(3^n)`, so is zero or all of `V`.
Thus the action is faithful and irreducible.

**Two generators and primitivity.** Multiplication by `zeta` and
translation by `1` generate `G_r`: their conjugates supply translations
by all powers of `zeta`, which span the field over `F_3`. The affine
action on `V` is faithful and transitive. If `H <= K <= G_r`, then
`K=(K intersect V)H`; its intersection with `V` is `H`-invariant.
Irreducibility makes `H` maximal, proving the action primitive. The
stabilizer `H` is nonnormal, so the resulting cover is non-Galois.

**Geometric realization.** The prime-to-five geometric fundamental group
of `X` is the prime-to-five completion of the genus-`g` surface group.
Its presentation has generators `a_i,b_i` with relation
`product [a_i,b_i]=1`. Send `a_1,a_2` to the two generators just given,
all `b_i` and the other `a_i` to `1`. This gives a surjection onto `G_r`,
whose order `3^n 2^r` is prime to five. Hence there is a connected finite
etale `G_r`-Galois cover `W_r -> X`. The quotient `W_r/H -> X` has degree
`[G_r:H]=3^n`; the subgroup calculation excludes every proper intermediate
cover. Its Galois closure is `W_r`, since the affine action is faithful.
The fundamental-group input is the prime-to-characteristic comparison
theorem (SGA 1, XIII, Corollary 2.12); see also
[Harbater's survey, Sections 2.2 and 7](https://www2.math.upenn.edu/~harbater/abhsurvey.pdf).

Since `G_r` is an extension of elementary abelian `3`-group `V` by cyclic
`2`-group `H`, its composition factors are only `C_2,C_3`. Its derived
length is at most two. The degrees are already:

| `r` | `n` | primitive cover degree `3^n` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 2 | 9 |
| 4 | 4 | 81 |
| 5 | 8 | 6561 |
| 6 | 16 | 43046721 |
| 7 | 32 | 1853020188851841 |

These values were checked by direct modular-order and integer-power
computation. The proof applies to every `r`, with no ordinarity assumption.

## 3. Boundary for generation claims

Even a fixed base, two abstract closure generators `C_2,C_3`, two group
generators, fixed simple factors, prime-to-five order, and derived length
at most two do not bound the degree of a permutation-primitive cover.
The quotient of a Galois refinement can therefore be indecomposable over
its base despite arising inside a group built by extensions from small
groups. Indecomposability is not the same as failing to arise by such
operations.

Here **permutation primitive** means no intermediate field between the
base field and the cover field. **Joint minimality** of a correspondence
means that its source field is generated by its two endpoint fields.
Neither condition should be substituted for the other. The construction
above proves no second etale map to a prescribed endpoint, no coreless
correspondence, and no completeness theorem for operations on a fixed
geometric seed. Those require additional geometric input.
