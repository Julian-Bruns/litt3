# Pole twelve is excluded for every actual tensor comparison

Version1, 27 September2026. Let X be the fixed genus-nine curve and
lambda,tau the specified second Cartier line and tensor of
[the comparison normal form](new_line_comparison_normal_form.md).
Let h1,h2:T->X be actual finite etale maps from the SAME smooth
proper connected curve, with equal embedded lambda pullbacks,
equivalently proportional tau pullbacks. Suppose their embedded
endpoint fields are distinct.

The associated nonconstant comparison function z cannot have pole
degree twelve. This holds for every covering degree and every
monodromy group, not only for Galois or Klein-four parameter maps.
The index-one branch is excluded by the established cubic-descent
theorem; the new four-trace contradiction excludes the entire
remaining quartic branch, including C4,V4,D4,A4,S4.

More generally, the four coefficient equations obtained from the
universal quartic traces have no solution with arbitrary four-label
endpoint multisets, x,y in F_(5^14) and nonzero epsilon in F_(5^56).
No V4-specific integer-weight restriction is needed.

Combining this result with the previous small-pole and pole-thirteen
exclusions, every jointly minimal counterexample has pole degree
at least FIFTEEN. Consequently conditional line/tensor field
recognition holds for every covering degree at most FOURTEEN:
\[
\deg h_i\le14,\quad
h_1^{(1)*}\lambda=h_2^{(1)*}\lambda
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
h_1^*k(X)=h_2^*k(X).
\]
The tensor/line premise is essential. This does not extract such
an object from an arbitrary common cover, decide the higher poles,
or resolve either original unmarked common-cover problem.
