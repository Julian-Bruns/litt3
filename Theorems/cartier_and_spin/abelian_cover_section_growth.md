# Section growth on maximal abelian p-covers

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p>0, let C be an ordinary
curve of genus g>=2, and let M have degree g-1 with h0(C,M)=1. For n>=1,
put N=p^n and let q_n:T_n->C be the maximal connected abelian etale cover
of exponent dividing N. Its group is (Z/N)^g, and

    g(T_n)=N^g(g-1)+1,   h0(T_n,q_n^*M)=N^(g-1).

## Genus-two spin series

Suppose further that p>=5, g=2 and M=A=O_C(W) is an effective spin.
If eta=e^2 and Cartier(eta) are linearly independent for 0!=e in H0(C,A),
the complete series q_n^*A is base-point-free, separable and birational.
Its projective section space carries a faithful action of G_n=(Z/N)^2.
For every subgroup H<=G_n of order p,

    h0(T_n/H,A|_(T_n/H))=N/p.

Thus each degree-p subcover of T_1->C has one spin section, while their
compositum has p sections and a birational complete spin map.

The spin conclusions apply to all six effective spins of the
characteristic-five family C_t whenever t^5-t!=0. These one-leg covers
supply no map to the fixed genus-nine endpoint; unbounded section growth
and spin-birationality alone cannot exclude them.
