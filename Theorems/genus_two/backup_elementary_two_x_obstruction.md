# No backup map from any elementary-abelian two-cover of X

Version 1, 2026-09-11. Author corollary of the audited complete Prym
certificate; no new finite enumeration is used.

Let X be the fixed genus-nine curve and B=C_alpha the small backup.
Let X_[2]->X be the maximal connected elementary-abelian two-cover,
of degree 2^18 and genus 2,097,153. Then

    Hom(J(B),J(X_[2]))=0.

In particular X_[2], or any intermediate curve over X, has no
nonconstant map to B. Consequently the X-leg of an actual common
finite etale span X<-Z->B cannot have elementary-abelian two-group
Galois closure. This includes the degree-one and degree-two cases
and does not require the span to be cored.

All new Prym factors of X_[2]->X are ordinary, although J(X_[2])
itself is not ordinary because J(X) is not. The statement does not
extend to a further five-group cover or to arbitrary two-groups.
