# A proper positive Cartier image excludes wild étale target atlases

Version1,3 October2026. Whole independent review **PASS** in the [audit](../../Research/audits/PROPER_CARTIER_ETALE_ATLAS_WILD_GENERALIZATION_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). This reusable generalization preserves the frozen [rankTHREE wild exclusion](rank_three_etale_row_depth_free_wild_reduction.md).

Let Y be either selected ordinary genus-TWO endpoint. Let q:C→Y be an actual finite étale R-Galois cover and ρ:C→D an actual finite étale R-equivariant map, with R acting faithfully on the smooth projective connected D. Let K_D⊂B_D be an R-stable integral locally free Cartier image of rank ONE,TWO orTHREE and degree g(D)−ONE. Require that its natural R-action is inherited from B_D and that its actual adjunction F_D*K_D→ω_D is everywhere surjective.

More generally Y may be ANY ordinary genus-TWO curve in characteristicFIVE with a Weierstrass reference O for which SIXTEEN-torsion Abel points are all Weierstrass.

Then D→D/R has NO wild inertia. Equivalently every nonidentity étale target atlas with these data is tame. No projective coefficient module, normalized spin-image hypothesis, marked determinant, source-to-X map or bound on the rank of a complete section space is required.

For an application to a common cover, every original endpoint map remains on its given source above C. The theorem excludes this target wild ramification; it does not infer an X-map on D or settle the common-cover problem.
