# Proof: the regular four-dimensional Cartier block has no proper evaluating subspace

Version1,3 October2026. Whole scoped review [PASS](../../Research/audits/RANK_THREE_SCALAR_TARGET_AND_TAME_IDENTITY_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md); see the [exact statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/proper_cartier_image_excludes_first_wild_break_one.md). No computation is used.

In a parameter t, the Cartier fiber has exact differential-coefficient basis dt,t dt,t²dt,t³dt. These are the evaluations of the four horizontal Cartier basis classes, and reduce modulo the first Frobenius-target uniformizer t⁵. If σ(t)=t+a t²+⋯ with a≠ZERO, its action is upper triangular in increasing differential order. The leading successive entries of σ−ONE are
\[
2a,\quad3a,\quad4a,
\]
all nonzero in characteristicFIVE. Terms of differential order FOUR cannot contribute: they are forbidden for a locally exact differential, and direct substitution also gives the cancellation by a factorFIVE. Thus σ acts as a regular unipotent Jordan block J₄ on the FOUR-dimensional Cartier fiber.

Every proper invariant subspace of a single regular block lies in its unique invariant hyperplane, here spanned by t dt,t²dt,t³dt. The fiber IMAGE of K→B_Z is σ-invariant and has dimension at mostTHREE, whether or not K is saturated there. It must lie in this hyperplane. Its zeroth differential evaluation therefore vanishes. This contradicts the assumed integral surjectivity of F_Z*K→ω_Z at the point.

This is a direct calculation on the actual Cartier fiber, not an inference from a pulled-back theta jet. For the descended scalar carrier the adjunction is surjective because it factors the original basepoint-free row; its invariant embedded lattice is retained by faithful étale pullback. No identification of its possibly much larger constant-module fiber with K's fiber is used.
