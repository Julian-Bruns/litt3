# Proof: the other-map regular values cannot have index two

Version1,3 October2026. Frozen pending independent review; see the [statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/actual_q0_tensor_degree_six_split_genus_two_local_opens.md).

Use the [exact split genus-TWO form](actual_q0_tensor_degree_six_split_square_class_reduction.md), with $u=a+a^{-1},v=a-a^{-1}$. Thus u²−v²=FOUR and
\[
x_2=(uA+vY)/2,\quad x_1=(vA+uY)/(2t^3),\quad
Y^2=A^2-t^6+1.
\]
At t=ZERO both sheets are ordinary and Y is a unit. Up through order FIVE one can regard x2 as the composition of A(t) with the TWO branches of (uA+v√(A²+ONE))/TWO; the t6 correction does not affect orders at most FOUR. Their first derivatives cannot BOTH vanish: that would require u=ZERO and vA0=ZERO, hence A0=ZERO, contrary to the actual x1 own-triple open FOUR A0²+u²≠ZERO.

If A1=ZERO and A2≠ZERO, at least one sheet therefore gives local index TWO for x2, impossible. If A1=A2=ZERO, A is a polynomial in t³. The genuine automorphism t↦ωt,Y↦Y then fixes BOTH displayed xi and is nontrivial on B, contradicting B=joint x-field. Thus A1≠ZERO.

At infinity use the actual uniformizer s=ONE/t on each of its TWO ordinary sheets and put R=s³A(ONE/s)=A3+A2s+A1s²+A0s³. Then
\[
x_1=(vR+u\widetilde Y)/2,\qquad
\widetilde Y^2=R^2-1+s^6.
\]
The derivatives of the TWO outer branches cannot both vanish: that would force v=ZERO,A3=ZERO, contrary to the x2 own-triple open FOUR A3²+v²≠ZERO. If A2=ZERO, the already nonzero A1 makes R−A3 have order TWO and at least one branch gives local index TWO for x1. Hence A2≠ZERO.

Finally consider a zero of one outer derivative at t=ZERO. Its equation is uY0+vA0=ZERO. Squaring and using Y0²=A0²+ONE gives A0²=u². If v=ZERO that forces Y0=ZERO, contradicting the ordinary-sheet open. Otherwise the second outer derivative is v/(TWO Y0³), nonzero. Since A1≠ZERO, the actual x2 map would have index TWO. Thus A0²≠u².

The infinity argument is dual. Vanishing of vY∞+uA3 is equivalent to A3²=v². If u=ZERO this violates the ordinary-infinity open; otherwise the second outer derivative is−u/(TWO Y∞³), nonzero. Since A2≠ZERO, x1 would again have index TWO. Hence A3²≠v².

Only actual forbidden local indices or the exact original joint-field equality are used. No coefficient gate, fixed-P norm condition, original Y descent or whole split-class exclusion is inferred.
