# The backup Cartier bundle has exactly four nonisotropic degree-one planes

Version1,22September2026. Let k be an algebraic closure of F5, let
alpha^3+alpha+1=0, and put
\[
Y:\ v^2=x(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)(x-\alpha).
\]
Write F:Y->Y^(1) for relative Frobenius and
B_Y=F_*O_Y/O_(Y^(1)), with its canonical omega_(Y^(1))-valued
alternating pairing. Put
\[
t=\alpha^2+3\alpha+4,\quad s=\alpha^2+4\alpha+4,
\quad P_\pm=(t,\pm s),\quad y_\pm=F(P_\pm),\quad z=(\alpha,0)\in Y.
\]
There are exactly FOUR geometric saturated nonisotropic rank-two
subbundles E of B_Y with degree one. They are all defined over F125
and stable. They form two symplectically orthogonal pairs; the restricted
pairing vanishes at y_+ for one pair and at y_- for the other.

Every one has first Frobenius filtration
\[
0\longrightarrow\omega_Y^2(-z)\longrightarrow F^*E
\longrightarrow\omega_Y\longrightarrow0.
\]
The adjunction map is everywhere surjective, and the adjacent canonical
connection map has its sole simple zero at z. Its positive HN line has
degree three and its quotient degree two. This is a branched type, not
an unbranched dormant oper.

More precisely, there are exactly two saturated primitive line images
O(-y)->F_*omega_Y^(-2) whose nonzero quadratic norm has divisor 2y,
one for each y=y_+,y_-. Each yields the two Pluecker signs above. The
calculation exhausts all geometric points, including the six
Weierstrass points, and retains the relative Frobenius coefficients.
The count does not assert reducedness of the parameter scheme.

This is an endpoint classification. It supplies no actual common cover
with the fixed genus-nine X and does not resolve either original problem.
