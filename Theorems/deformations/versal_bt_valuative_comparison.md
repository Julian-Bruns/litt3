# Generic versal-BT comparisons extend at every level

Version2,3 October2026. Put $k=\overline{\mathbf F}_5$,
$R=k[[t]]$ and $K=k((t))$. Let $A,B$ be actual height-two,
dimension-one BT$_N$ groups over $R$, $N\ge1$, with ordinary
generic fibers. Assume their closed fibers are either both ordinary,
or both supersingular with simple Hasse zeros and continuous
Kodaira--Spencer maps that are isomorphisms.

Every supplied generic group isomorphism extends uniquely:
\[
\operatorname{Isom}_R(A,B)
\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Isom}_K(A_K,B_K).
\tag{1}
\]
No initial integral BT1 comparison or determinant normalization is
required. Any marking or determinant condition holding generically
is retained. This asserts integrality of a given comparison, not
its existence.

Consequently, on a smooth proper curve, (1) holds for actual
BT$_N$ groups whose BT1s are generically ordinary and everywhere
versal, with the SAME reduced supersingular divisor. In particular
it applies to marked extensions of a fixed such BT1 at every level.

For normalized marked BT2 groups, the ordinary invariant of the
[basic Kummer comparison](versal_bt_display_descent.md) is injective:
\[
\Delta_C:Q_C\hookrightarrow\Gamma(C-S,\mathcal O_C).
\tag{2}
\]
Thus the quotient has no subgroup supported only at supersingular
points. Nonzero images in (2) may still have poles.

The unmarked BT1 base case comes from the later
[generic-comparison calculation](generic_bt_comparison_etaleness.md);
its local matrix argument is now included in this proof. That later
theorem still owns the separate assertion that a generic comparison
forces an arbitrary second morphism to be etale.
