# Noncyclic degree-one contact eight concentrates an actual target fiber in finite branches

Version2,2 October2026. Computation-free scoped implication; the whole branch-fiber argument and focused unconditional-norm extension passed review. This is not a common-cover exclusion.

Retain the selected genus-nine cyclic cubic curve $X:y^3=P(x)$, $\deg P=10$, its infinity $O$, the reduced divisor $R$ of ten finite cubic branch points, and the original net $I\subset U\subset B_X$. Retain BOTH actual finite étale maps $X\xleftarrow hS\xrightarrow gY$ from the SAME smooth projective source, of degrees $n,8n$. Suppose the complete actual first trace $J\subset B_Y$ has rank four and degree one, has noncyclic Cartier defect, and its source contact has length exactly $8n$.

Its total-length-three defect must have one of these shapes: $(1,1)$ at a point $Q$ and a simple defect at a distinct point; or $(2,1)$ at one point $Q$. On every actual one-leg Galois source $q:T\to Y$ of degree $8d$ and every actual conjugate map $h_i:T\to X$ of degree $d$,
\[
\boxed{q^{-1}(Q)\subset h_i^{-1}(R)}
\]
as reduced divisors. Every raw source sheet above $Q$ is a FINITE cubic branch sheet, with contact one; no infinity sheet occurs there. Consequently
\[
D_i=h_i^*R-q^*Q\ge0,\qquad \deg D_i=2d.
\]
This is an actual same-source divisor concentration statement. It neither establishes a second saturation clump nor replaces either finite étale map by arbitrary separable data.

Unconditionally, put $F_0=\omega_Y(-2Q)$. Then $2D_i\sim4h_i^*O+q^*F_0$, and its norm $F_i=(h_i)_*D_i$ satisfies $F_i\sim2dO$. Writing $F_i=\sum_{r\in R}k_r r$, one has $0\le k_r\le d$, $\sum k_r=2d$, and $k_r\equiv2d\pmod3$ for every $r$. Indeed the accepted simple nine-dimensional Jacobian of X forces $\operatorname{Nm}_{h_i}\circ q^*:J(Y)\to J(X)$ to vanish for every genus-two Y. More generally $\operatorname{Nm}_{h_i}q^*O_Y(Q')=O_X(8dO)$ for every $Q'\in Y$. These actual norm constraints do not themselves exclude large covering degrees.
