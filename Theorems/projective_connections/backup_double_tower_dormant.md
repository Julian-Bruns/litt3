# Simultaneous dormant theta separation on backup double covers

Version2, 16 September 2026. The returned global argument passed its
bounded audit and all fifteen exact certificates replayed.
The arithmetic and enlarged monodromy consequences are local additions.

Let \(Y:v^2=u(u-1)(u-2)(u-3)(u-\alpha)\), with
\(\alpha^3+\alpha+1=0\), and let \(\mathcal V_0,\ldots,\mathcal V_4\)
be its five actual dormant tangent bundles on \(C=Y^{(1)}\), in the
[fixed theta conventions](backup_dormant_theta_divisors.md).
For EVERY connected finite étale double \(q:D\to Y\),
\[
\bigcap_{i=0}^4\{L\in J(D^{(1)}):
 H^0(D^{(1)},q^{(1)*}\mathcal V_i\otimes L)\ne0\}
=\varnothing.
\tag{1}
\]
This controls every geometric twist and every coordinate boundary,
including the full Prym direction and its translates.

Choose the standard model of D over \(\mathbf F_{125^2}\).
Then all five section spaces vanish for every L defined over
\(\mathbf F_{125^{2r}}\) with \(5\nmid r\). In particular they vanish
for EVERY \(\{2,3\}\)-primary torsion line, without an exponent bound.

Consequently all five dormant connections stay infinitesimally rigid
on every actual cover of Y whose Galois-closure group G has a normal
five-group P such that
\[
|G/P|=2^a3^b,\qquad
G/P\text{ has an abelian subgroup of index at most two}.
\tag{2}
\]
This includes dihedral, generalized quaternion and generalized
dihedral \(\{2,3\}\)-groups of unbounded order, followed by normal
five-group refinements. It includes Version1's two-step doubles
and dihedral group of order eight.

The proof uses actual double-cover pushforwards and the
[singular determinant-cut theorem](genus_two_determinant_cuts.md).
It does not need the 960-twist enumeration or the
base-Jacobian slice.

These are dormant-tangent results. They imply neither Jacobian
ordinariness nor the corresponding active statements, and do not
force a dormant match on an arbitrary common source. Neither
common-cover candidate is resolved; (2) is not a reduction of arbitrary
covering monodromy to the groups listed there.
