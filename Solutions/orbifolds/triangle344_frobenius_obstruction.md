The characteristic-five use is an UPPER bound, not a claim that every
complex class has good reduction. A tame cover of the smooth marked
line lifts over its fixed marked lift (P1_W(k),0,1,infinity), together
with its Galois closure and group. The primary input is
[Wewers, Deformation of tame admissible covers of curves](https://www.uvm.edu/~cvincen1/files/teaching/wewers.pdf),
Cor3.1.3, printed p265, and Prop4.3.3, p277. Sections3.2.2--3 lift local
tame roots, glue by etale descent, and algebraize. The INERTIA, not the
whole group, must be prime to5. This is only lifting ONE tame cover.

Nonisomorphic special covers cannot give isomorphic geometric generic
covers: after finite DVR extension, a generic source isomorphism extends
by [stable-model uniqueness](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0E8C),
and equality of maps to the fixed line extends by separatedness.
Thus the table also bounds the characteristic-five classes in each
fixed monodromy-order part. No simultaneous lift of an arbitrary
common-cover diagram is asserted.

## 2. Each order576 cover has an actual elliptic double quotient

The native verifier finds a preserved partition into SIX PAIRS for
each of the three order576 representatives. Try {1,j} for 2<=j<=12
and close its images under a,b; a successful closure consists of six
disjoint pairs. In fact, its emitted representatives all preserve

    {1,5}, {2,6}, {3,4}, {7,11}, {8,12}, {9,10}.

On these blocks the inertia cycle types are (3,3), (4,2), (4,2).
The verifier checks the equivalent genus-one cycle-count identity.

Let W be the ACTUAL Galois closure, G its group, H a sheet stabilizer,
and J the stabilizer of its pair block. Then H<=J and [J:H]=2.
The intermediate field k(W)^J supplies

    C=W/H -> E=W/J -> P1,    degrees 2 and 6.

The second map is tame with the displayed quotient cycles, so Hurwitz
gives 2g(E)-2=-12+(4+4+4)=0. Its smooth projective model has genus one.
The degree-two deck involution of C->E is not hyperelliptic, and commutes
with the central hyperelliptic involution. Hence |Aut(C)|>=4.
Norm-pullback for C->E also puts an elliptic isogeny factor in J(C).

## 3. Frobenius forces the cover into that part

Fifth-power Frobenius fixes the normalized branch values and permutes
the finite characteristic-five cover classes, preserving group order.
Forgetting the map is Frobenius-equivariant. Every orbit of covers
above C has length divisible by the geometric moduli orbit length of C.

If that length is at least three, every part of size one or two in the
table is impossible. Only order576 remains; Section2 supplies the
actual elliptic double quotient. The argument uses the orbit of the
ISOMORPHISM CLASS, not merely a chosen coefficient field.
