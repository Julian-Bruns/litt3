# Proof: cyclic cubic divisor rigidity and ramified-adic Frobenius

[Statement](../Theorems/Thm_cyclic_cubic_low_abel_torsion.md).
Work geometrically over an algebraic closure unless a field is specified.

## 1. A second difference cannot move a low-degree divisor class

Every nonzero alpha in W_r has a UNIQUE effective representative D of
degree r in the class alpha+rO. Otherwise a nonconstant function with
pole divisor bounded by D has degree<=r and thus belongs to k(x).
Its degree is a positive multiple of3. Since r<=3, necessarily r=3
and D is a full x-fiber. But a full fiber is linearly equivalent to3O,
which would make alpha zero.

Assume alpha!=0 and (gamma-1)^2 alpha=0. There is a function with divisor

    gamma^2 D-2gamma D+D.

Its degree is at most2r. The pencil hypothesis makes it a function of x,
so its divisor is rho-invariant. Commutation therefore gives

    (gamma-1)^2 (rho-1)D=0

in the FREE abelian group of divisors. The gamma-orbit of this divisor
is finite. On its rational span gamma has finite order, so its minimal
polynomial is squarefree over Q; hence (gamma-1)(rho-1)D=0. Therefore
gamma D-D is rho-invariant. Its coefficients at ramification points
vanish, since gamma fixes those points individually. Any nonzero positive
or negative part is consequently a sum of full unramified cubic fibers,
and has degree at least3.

For r<3 this is impossible. For r=3 it forces D and gamma D to be full
fibers with no common part, again making alpha zero. Thus gamma D=D,
which proves (1). This deals with repeated points and possible common
support without a reduced-divisor assumption.

## 2. Cubic torsion classes and the ramification subgroup

Norm to the rational quotient is zero on J, so 1+rho+rho^2=0. In
particular lambda^2=-3rho. If3alpha=0, then (rho-1)^2alpha=0.
Apply Section1 with gamma=rho. Every nonzero representative D is then
rho-invariant. For degree<=3 it is supported on ramification points
unless it is a full unramified fiber; the latter represents zero.
Conversely each ramification point P satisfies3(P-O)=0.

Riemann--Hurwitz gives n=g+2 ramification points. In degree3 the n
divisors3P all represent zero. Every2P+Q with P!=Q and every triple
of distinct branch points gives a nonzero class, and these classes
are distinct by the uniqueness proved in Section1. This proves (2).

We also need the FULL kernel of lambda, not just its small effective
part. It is generated by the classes of ramification points relative
to O. Here is a direct proof. Write the cyclic function field as
y^3=R(x), with the finite branch valuations a_i in{1,2}. If a principal
divisor is supported on ramification points, its defining function f
satisfies rho(f)/f in k^*, hence is an eigenfunction y^j h(x), j=0,1,2.
Its finite branch valuations modulo3 are therefore (j a_i)_i.
The only F3-linear relation among the n-1 finite branch classes is
the one with coefficient vector(a_i), furnished by div(y) after
removing multiples of full fibers. Their span has dimension n-2=g.
It lies in ker(lambda). Finally lambda^2=-3rho implies deg(lambda)=3^g;
lambda is etale because3 differs from the characteristic. Its geometric
kernel has exactly3^g points, proving equality.

## 3. Use the ramified cubic order on the ACTUAL Tate module

Let T=T_3J. The rho action makes T a free rank-g module over the discrete
valuation ring R=Z_3[rho], with uniformizer lambda=1-rho, residue field
F3, and lambda^2=-3rho. Indeed R is the ring of integers of Q_3(zeta_3),
and T is torsion-free over it. Frobenius commutes with rho.

All ramification points are fixed by M=pi^D. By Section2, M fixes
J[lambda]; equivalently M-1=lambda U for an R-integral endomorphism U
of T. Expanding its cube gives

    M^3-1=lambda^3 U V,
    V=U^2-rho^2 lambda U-rho^2.                      (5)

In particular M^3 is the identity on J[3]. The determinant valuations
in the statement are lengths of cokernels over R: the residue field
has3 elements. Hence

    v_lambda det_R(U)=a,
    v_lambda det_R(V)=b.

Neither determinant vanishes, since Frobenius has no root-of-unity
eigenvalue. Both a,b are nonnegative. Adjugates show that lambda^a U^-1
and lambda^b V^-1 are integral. Crucially, their denominator bounds
need not be added: U and V commute, and

    V-U(U-rho^2 lambda)=-rho^2

is a unit. Dividing this identity by UV expresses (UV)^-1 as a unit
multiple of U^-1-(U-rho^2 lambda)V^-1. Thus
lambda^max(a,b)(UV)^-1 is integral. Equation (5) proves that the kernel
of M^3-1 on J[3^infinity] is killed by lambda^(3+max(a,b)).

To apply this bound to W_r, use the finite-field translation lemma:
if a Frobenius power fixes J[3], any nonfixed three-primary point has
a power-conjugate differing by a nonzero3-torsion point; that power
fixes the difference. This is Boxall--Grant, Proposition2.3(iii), with
its proof in Section2. The ensuing second-difference relation contradicts
Section1, since our Frobenius powers fix all ramification points. Thus
every three-primary W_r point is fixed by M^3. This proves (3).

Source: [Boxall--Grant, Singular torsion points on elliptic curves,
Section2](https://boxall.pages.math.cnrs.fr/pages-web-mathematiques-de-john-boxall/wwwMaths/SingularFinal.pdf).
Their proposition is stated for arbitrary commutative algebraic groups;
the low-Abel-image and ramified cubic refinements above are separate
arguments, not an assertion that all W_r points are almost rational.

## 4. Fixed-X certificate

The audited independent-pencil bound in `two_primary_w3` is7, so it
applies to every function of degree<=6. The ten finite branch roots
of F_10 have F25 factor degrees1,1,4,4. Their lcm is4; O is rational.
For the actual Weil polynomial P_X retained in `fixed_pair_arithmetic`,
exact resultants give

    v_3 Res(P_X,T^4-1)=10,
    v_3 Res(P_X,T^12-1)=30.

With g=9 this is a=10-9=1, b=30-27-1=2. Equations (2)-(3) give
276 classes of order dividing3 and an initial bound lambda^5. The short certificate
`scripts/check_cubic_low_abel_torsion.sage` checks these arithmetic
inputs directly, without recomputing the curve's Weil polynomial or
making a companion-matrix assumption.

We improve the exponent using the first Frobenius layer. Let K=F_(25^4),
Q=25^4, and order the roots a_1,...,a_10 of F. Fix a primitive cube root
zeta in F25. For each i define elements of F3 by

    zeta^(t_j)=(a_i-a_j)^((Q-1)/3), j!=i;
    t_i=-sum_(j!=i) t_j.

In the basis e_i=[P_i-O],1<=i<=9, with e_10=-sum e_i, the reduction
Ubar of U=(M-1)/lambda has column i equal to (t_j-t_10)_(1<=j<=9).
Here and throughout, Frobenius acts on geometric points and divisors,
not by the contravariant divisor pullback.

To justify this formula, use [Arul, Theorems2.1 and2.29](https://arxiv.org/html/1810.07299v4#S2.SS4).
Choose cube roots r_j^3=a_i-a_j, with r_i=0. Arul constructs a class
beta_i with lambda beta_i=e_i. Frobenius sends r_j to zeta^(t_j)r_j.
Theorem2.29, applied with exponent vector -t of sum zero, gives

    (M-1)beta_i=sum_j t_j e_j=Ue_i.

Section2 of that source works in characteristic different from3. The
intrinsic expression sum_(j!=i) t_j(e_j-e_i) also shows independence
from the exponent assigned to the zero root. The source proof's global
branch section has a harmless coordinate typo: (-r_1^n,0) is the correct
section, specializing to (0,0) on r_1=0; the étale-section proof is intact.

The exact computation `scripts/check_cubic_frobenius_layer.sage` gives

    rank(Ubar)=8,       nullity(Ubar^2-I)=2.

Together with v_lambda det U=1 and v_lambda det V=2, this proves that
the nonunit Smith exponents of U are (1), and of V are (1,1): Vbar is
Ubar^2-I since rho reduces to1. Thus lambda U^-1 and lambda V^-1 are
integral. The Bézout identity in Section3 implies lambda(UV)^-1 is
integral. From M^3-1=lambda^3UV its three-primary kernel is killed by
lambda^4=9rho^2. All three-primary W3 classes lie in that kernel by
Section3, proving the bound9 over F_(25^12).

For W1 this finishes the classification: 9(P-O) principal gives a
function in L(9O)=span(1,x,x^2,x^3), hence a function of x. A divisor
9P-9O of such a function forces P to be ramified. Conversely 3(P-O)=0
at each branch point. Their eleven classes are distinct since a
function with divisor P-Q for distinct points would have degree one.

## 5. Isolate one primary component without leaving W_r

Let P,N be as in assertion4 and gamma=pi^N. Write alpha=sum alpha_ell
for its primary decomposition. The congruence gamma=I modulo ell
(modulo4 for ell=2) implies that the gamma-orbit of alpha_ell has
ell-power length d_ell. If alpha_ell is not fixed, put

    m=product_(p in P, p!=ell) d_p,       tau=gamma^m.

Then tau fixes all other components and still moves alpha_ell, since
m is prime to ell. Put Q=(tau-I)alpha_ell, of exact order ell^s.
The elementary finite-field translation calculation gives

    tau^(ell^(s-1))-I=ell^(s-1)(tau-I)E,

where E is integral, invertible, and commutes with tau. Explicitly, for
n=ell^(s-1), factor the binomial expansion as

    E=I+sum_(j=2)^n (binomial(n,j)/n)(tau-I)^(j-1).

The inequality v_ell(binomial(n,j)/n)>=-v_ell(j) makes every summand
after I divisible by ell: use (j-1)-v_ell(j)>=1 for odd ell, and
2(j-1)-v_2(j)>=1 for ell=2, since tau-I is then divisible by4.
Thus E=I modulo ell. This is the elementary Boxall translation proof,
also recorded in Section1.1 of the retained Prüfer-torsion proof.

Set sigma=tau^(ell^(s-1)). Its difference on the FULL alpha is
ell^(s-1)EQ, nonzero of order ell; the other primary components stay
fixed. Sigma fixes this difference. Hence (sigma-I)^2 alpha=0 while
(sigma-I)alpha!=0. All powers here fix the branch points, so Section1
contradicts this. Therefore gamma alpha=alpha. No projection alpha_ell
has been asserted to belong to W_r.

For fixed X take N=684=lcm(342,12). The independently checked
two-adic input in `two_primary_w3` is

    pi^171=I+2U_2,       U_2 and I+U_2 invertible.

Writing C=U_2(I+U_2), one gets

    pi^342=I+4C,       pi^684-I=8C(I+2C).

Its two-primary kernel is therefore killed by8. At3 put A=pi^12-I.
Since A is divisible by lambda^3, the factorization

    (I+A)^57-I=3AB,
    B=19I+sum_(j=2)^57 (binomial(57,j)/3) A^(j-1)

has B integral and B=19I modulo lambda, hence a unit. Indeed every
nonconstant term has lambda-valuation at least3(j-1)-2>=1.
Section4 gives lambda^4 A^-1 integral, so lambda^6(pi^684-I)^-1
is integral. Since lambda^6=-27, its three-primary kernel is killed
by27. The Frobenius pi^684 fixes J[4], J[3] and every branch point;
the general mixed-prime assertion places all the indicated W3 points
in its kernel. Their order divides8*27=216, proving (5).
