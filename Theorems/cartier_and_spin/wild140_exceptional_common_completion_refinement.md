# Wild140 exceptional family: common completion leaves four normalized pairs

Version1, 3 October2026. Scoped implication; [independent whole review PASS](../../Research/audits/WILD140_EXCEPTIONAL_COMMON_COMPLETION_REFINEMENT_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). This is not a wild140 exclusion.

Work over an algebraically closed field of characteristic five. Retain BOTH actual finite étale endpoint maps from the SAME source and all actual-carrier hypotheses of the [ordinary canonical wild140 reduction](canonical_wild_degree_140_ordinary_spin_reduction.md). Restrict to its BACKUP exceptional mixed degree-at-most-one family. In its translated model write
\[
y^2=w^5+qw^4+s,\qquad F=w(y+v),\qquad q,s,v,c\ne0,\qquad dG=cF^3\frac{dw}{y}.
\]
The actual full wild fiber consists of seven distinct zeros of F. Assume G is nonzero at these zeros and the SAME-BASE completed-extension condition
\[
\frac{(yF')^5}{G^2}=\text{one constant at all seven zeros of }F,
\qquad F'=dF/dw.
\]
This condition is an explicit additional hypothesis here. Equality of inertia groups or different exponents alone does not supply it. The actual-carrier specialization of the [weak completed-extension invariant](../quotient_geometry/weak_local_completed_extension_invariant.md) supplies it when the actual quotient coordinate is β=G⁷/F²⁰ and the retained common source identifies those completions.

Then, up to the nonzero scale c, only FOUR pairs remain. More precisely,
\[
v^2=2s,\qquad
G=c\left[4w^9+2qw^8+(4s+2v^2)w^4
+yv\left(3w^4+\frac{3w^5}{q}+\frac{3(v^2+s)}q\right)\right]
+\frac{4cs^2}{q}+\frac{3cs}{q}w^5
+\left(C-\frac{3c}{q}\right)w^{10},
\qquad C^2=\frac{c^2q^3}{s}.
\]
Thus v and C each have two choices. A choice with zero leading coefficient of w¹⁰ must still be deleted if an exact pole20 is required. No existence, primitive-characteristic relation, descended X-section compatibility, or whole exclusion is asserted for the remaining pairs. Mixed degree-at-least-two and cone profiles remain outside the statement.
