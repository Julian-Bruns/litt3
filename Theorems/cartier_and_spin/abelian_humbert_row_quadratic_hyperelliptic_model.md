\[
X_0^2+X_2^2,\quad X_1^2+X_3^2,\quad X_0X_2,\quad X_1X_3.
\]
They define a finite degreeEIGHT map τ:P³→P³. Their restriction to C is exactly the quotient C→E followed by the hyperelliptic map E→P¹ and its complete cubic embedding as a twisted cubic D. Scheme-theoretically C=τ⁻¹(D). Consequently the row's Hilbert–Burch matrix can be chosen as the catalecticant TWO-by-THREE matrix of a twisted cubic with linear coordinates replaced by these invariant quadrics.

The line identities are
\[
Arow^8\simeq\omega_C^3,\qquad P^8\simeq\omega_C,
\qquad Arow\simeq P^3,\qquad P^2\simeq\pi^*H,
\]

\[
\bigl(M\otimes\mathcal O_Y(-W)\bigr)^4\simeq\mathcal O_Y.
\]
Thus the actual positive determinant lies in a finite torsion translate in Pic¹(Y). M is a line class, not an asserted effective point; the accepted Abel-curve point-torsion exclusions do not delete this finite class list.
