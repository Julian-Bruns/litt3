# Fifth-power norm forces a constant Frobenius remainder

30 September2026. Let K be a one-variable function field over a perfect
field of characteristic p, let f be in K but not K^p, and let L/K be
a finite separable extension generated by b. Let F be the monic
minimal polynomial of b, of degree N. If
\[
\operatorname{Nm}_{L/K}(f+b^p)\in K^{\times p},
\]
then N>=p and there is a unique polynomial H and nonzero tau in K with
\[
F(W)=(W^p+f)H(W)+\tau.
\]
Conversely such a presentation implies the norm condition. The result
also holds for a separable polynomial and its finite source algebra.

For the fixed characteristic-five admissible primitive, the
[uniform norm theorem](uniform_admissible_norm.md) therefore yields this
presentation in every primitive source degree. Its low weighted trace
identities and centered energy are given by
[the general remainder theorem](frobenius_remainder_centered_energy.md).
This is an algebraic restriction on the actual source polynomial, not
an existence or nonexistence theorem for the source or for a common cover.
