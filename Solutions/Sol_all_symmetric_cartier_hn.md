# Proof: all symmetric-power Cartier HN polygons

[Statement and audit metadata](../Theorems/Thm_all_symmetric_cartier_hn.md).
Keep its notation F:C→C1, s=g−1, B=F_*O_C/O_C1, M=(p−1)n and
W_n,E_n,Q_n,H_n. The graded source input below was separately checked by
/root/graded_alpha_p_symmetric_power_sources against Troesch and Touzé.

## 1. The graded derivation calculation

Let V have basis e_1,…,e_(p−1) of weights1,…,p−1 and
N(e_i)=i e_(i−1), with e_0=0. Extend N as a derivation to Sym(V);
then N^p=0 and N lowers weight by one. Write J_l[a] for a graded
Jordan block of length l with top weight a. We prove

    Sym^n(V) = free length-p blocks, together with
      J_1[M]       if n≡0 (mod p),
      J_(p−1)[M]   if n≡1 (mod p),
      no extra block otherwise.                              (1)

The source input concerns W=⟨e_0,…,e_(p−1)⟩ with the same derivation.
[Troesch, Theorem3.1.3 and §3.3](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/aif.2133.pdf)
shows that Sym^j(W) is free over R=k[N]/(N^p) unless p|j, in which
case it is free plus one trivial module of weight(p−1)j.
Equivalently see [Touzé, Remark9.2 and Theorem9.4](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/asens.2160.pdf):
the differential is the derivation extending the Jordan shift, with
cohomological grading (p−1)j minus our weight. A finite p-acyclic complex
has only length-p blocks; the coresolution contributes the trivial block.
This is the primitive derivation action, not a diagonal cyclic-group action.

Multiplication by the invariant e_0 gives graded R-modules

    0 → Sym^(n−1)(W) → Sym^n(W) → Sym^n(V) → 0.               (2)

Graded free R-modules are both injective and projective. If neither n
nor n−1 is divisible by p, (2) splits with free quotient. If p|n, its
free source splits off, leaving precisely the trivial block at M.
If p|(n−1), cancel free summands in the source. The remaining quotient
is the cosyzygy of the trivial module at h=(p−1)(n−1), plus free modules.
Its injective envelope is J_p[h+p−1], with the trivial module at the
bottom, and its quotient is J_(p−1)[M]. Graded Krull–Schmidt proves
(1); n=0 is immediate.

A free block of bottom weight b has character z^b(1+⋯+z^(p−1)).
Subtracting the exceptional character E_n from W_n therefore proves
that Q_n=(W_n−E_n)/(1+⋯+z^(p−1)) has nonnegative integral coefficients.

## 2. Differential saturation is intrinsic

Evaluation identifies F^*B with I=(α) in O_C[α]/(α^p). The quotient
connection, not a restriction of the connection on that algebra, is

    ∇_∂(α)=0,   ∇_∂(α^i)=−iα^(i−1) for 2≤i<p.               (3)

The full-algebra derivative of α is constant and disappears modulo
the pulled-back unit. Powers of I have intrinsic graded lines ω_C^i.
Consequently Sym^n(I)=F^*Sym^n(B) has an intrinsic total-weight
filtration V_(≥w), whose weight-q piece is a sum of ω_C^q.
In its étale-local monomial frame the connection is ∂−N.

Let G_w be the smallest connection-stable O_C-submodule containing
V_(≥w). Locally,

    G_w = ∑_(j=0)^(p−1) ∇_∂^j(V_(≥w)).                       (4)

It is stable since ∇_∂^p=0, and coordinate-independent by its defining
minimality. The monomial frame identifies it with O_C tensored with
the N-submodule generated by weights≥w. Thus G_w and all successive
quotients are locally free. Cartier descent gives G_w=F^*E_w for
subbundles E_w of Sym^n(B).

A homogeneous Jordan decomposition COMPUTES G_w/G_(w+1): it contains
exactly the blocks whose top weight is w. No chosen Jordan summands
are asserted to glue. All blocks at a fixed top have the same length
l=p, except the unique exceptional top M, of length1 or p−1. The
weight-M multiplicity is one, so no free block coexists there.

If m blocks have top w and length l, the induced filtration on the
pullback of E_w/E_(w+1) has pieces

    (ω_C^q)^m,   w−l+1≤q≤w,                                  (5)

and connection isomorphisms between neighboring pieces after tensoring
the lower piece by ω_C. The graded subspaces used in this computation
are intrinsic: a coordinate change acts on the weight-q monomial graded
frame by the scalar (dt_new/dt)^q, preserving every such fixed subspace.

## 3. The slope argument and the formula

A bundle E on C1 whose pullback has (5) with those connection
isomorphisms is semistable of slope (2w−l+1)s/p. To see this, intersect
a horizontal F^*A for a subbundle A⊂E with the filtration. Its graded
ranks satisfy

    r_(w−l+1) ≥ r_(w−l+2) ≥ ⋯ ≥ r_w,

because the connection maps inject the higher graded parts into the
lower ones tensored by ω_C. Each part injects into (ω_C^q)^m, so

    p deg A ≤ ∑_q 2qs r_q ≤ (2w−l+1)s rank A.

The last inequality says that decreasing nonnegative ranks have
average increasing weight at most the unweighted midpoint.
If m=1 and A is proper nonzero, its rank sequence has strictly smaller
average weight, proving stability.

Apply this to Section2. The length-p slopes (2w−p+1)s/p strictly
decrease with w. The shorter exceptional block, if present, has the
unique highest top M and strictly larger slope than all other factors.
Thus the descended filtration IS the HN filtration. Each free block
of bottom b contributes p z^(2b+p−1), while the exceptional block
contributes z^(2M) or (p−1)z^(2M−p+2). This is precisely the statement's
formula H_n=p z^(p−1)Q_n(z²) plus its case-dependent exceptional term.

Everything commutes with finite étale base change, including degrees
divisible by p. Duality Γ^n(B)=(Sym^n(B^∨))^∨ and
B^∨≅B⊗ω_C1^(-1) give the divided-power and line-twist assertions.
For example p=5,n=5 gives

    z^40+5(z^34+z^32+z^30+z^28+z^26+2z^24+z^22+z^20+z^18+z^14),

of rank56. Its slope8s line agrees with the pth-power inclusion
F_abs^*B→Sym^p(B). Prime-field saturation tests for p=5,n=1,…,12
are checks; the argument proves all n and odd p without averaging
over S_n or using the [full-tensor theorem](Sol_all_tensor_cartier_hn.md).
Neither theorem computes arbitrary Schur subquotients or extension
classes, supplies a shared differential for two actual maps, or excludes
a common finite étale cover.
