# Cored comparisons of the new Cartier line recognize the fixed X-field

Version1,23September2026. Let h_1,h_2:T->X be finite etale maps
from the same smooth proper connected curve to the fixed genus-nine
X. Suppose their embedded new-line pullbacks agree, equivalently
their weight13 tensors are proportional. Then
\[
\operatorname{trdeg}_k\bigl(h_1^*k(X)\cap h_2^*k(X)\bigr)=1
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad h_1^*k(X)=h_2^*k(X).
\]
Thus any counterexample to unrestricted new-line recognition must
be CORELESS. The same conclusion holds whenever an actual finite
etale refinement is Galois over both endpoint copies, in particular
if both h_i themselves are Galois.

No simultaneous Galois closure is assumed for a coreless span.
Neither its nonexistence nor unrestricted recognition is proved.
