# The degree-seven comparison cannot have elliptic simultaneous quotient

Version1, 24 September 2026. Work with the fixed genus-nine X, its
specified Cartier line and tensor, and two actual finite etale maps
\(h_i:T\to X\) with proportional tensor pullbacks. Suppose the distinct
embedded X-fields jointly generate k(T), and both maps have degree seven.

Their simultaneous cubic quotient \(S\) cannot have genus one.
Together with
[the higher-cover genus bound](new_line_degree_six_genus_bound.md),
any such comparison would necessarily have rational simultaneous
quotient, comparison pole degree six, and a single common point in
\(h_1^*O\cap h_2^*O\).

The genus-one exclusion is geometric, not limited to a chosen finite
field. A complete exact certificate covers6,356,452 normalized boundary
configurations, comprising all219,188 two-end orbit representatives
and all29 possible normalized common-pole values. There are no residual
denominator cases. The surviving726 cases after the first algebraic
tests are all excluded by the necessary opposite-sheet cancellation.

The separate [degree-seven recognition theorem](degree_seven_new_line_recognition.md)
now excludes the rational quotient as well. Higher degrees and both
original unmarked common-cover problems remain open. This statement's
own scope remains the elliptic-quotient exclusion.
