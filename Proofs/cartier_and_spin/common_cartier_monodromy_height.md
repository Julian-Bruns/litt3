# Proof: the actual Frobenius field forces a full infinitesimal orbit

[Statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/common_cartier_monodromy_height.md).
The field is perfect and the full common category includes the
specified comparison on the original source.

## The common category and its finite tensor subcategories

For a common morphism, the rank-drop locus and any torsion in the
cokernel pull back to the same finite set on Z. If nonempty, that
set is a clump. Under the present hypothesis they are empty.
Kernels, images and cokernels are therefore vector bundles, and
evaluation at a point of Z is exact and faithful. Duality, tensor
products and the unit are preserved; the unit has endomorphism
field k. Thus this is a neutral Tannakian category. This uses no
preexisting simultaneous Galois cover or common connection.
The reconstruction assertion is [Deligne--Milne, Theorem2.11](https://www.jmilne.org/math/xnotes/tc2022.pdf);
the finite-type tensor-generator criterion used next is Proposition2.20.

The full tensor category generated by a finite-rank object has an
affine algebraic Tannaka group: the object gives a faithful closed
embedding in its general linear group. On the subcategory generated
by A_r, the group preserves its ACTUAL multiplication and unit.
After choosing a coordinate on the smooth Frobenius fiber, it acts
by automorphisms of k[t]/t^q, q=p^r. Its fiber representation is
therefore a closed subgroup H_r of the full coordinate group G_r.

Absolute Frobenius is used only to keep these objects on one common
category. Each original etale map commutes with it and gives the
usual base-change algebra isomorphism. On a geometric fiber the
scalar action is twisted, but perfection of k gives the stated
truncated coordinate algebra. No scalar Frobenius is replaced by
a k-linear operation on a relative twist.

## A generic field algebra has the full scheme-theoretic orbit

Let E be the finite common algebra in the general statement. There
is no nonzero proper common ideal subobject: at the endpoint with
generic algebra a field, its positive rank would be smaller than
the rank of E, whereas a field has no such ideal.

Let A be its fiber algebra, R=k[H], and write the action coaction as
rho:A->A tensor R. Choose any geometric point with augmentation
epsilon:A->k. The orbit morphism has coordinate map
\[
phi=(epsilon\otimes1)rho:A\longrightarrow R.
\]
Set J=ker(phi). It is a proper ideal, and is an H-subrepresentation:
coassociativity gives
\[
(phi\otimes1)rho=\Delta phi.
\]
Hence rho(J) is contained in J tensor R, since tensoring vector
spaces over k is exact. Tannakian reconstruction carries this
subrepresentation to a common subbundle ideal of E. The preceding
generic-field argument forces J=0. Thus phi is injective. Its value
on every nilpotent of A retains that nilpotent's exact power order.

For E=A_r the generic algebra is the FIELD k(C), with scalar
embedding the p^r-power map. Its fiber is k[t]/t^q and has unique
augmentation t->0. Here phi(t) is the constant coordinate coefficient
a_0, so
\[
a_0^q=0,\qquad a_0^{q-1}\ne0.
\]
For any h<r, also a_0^(p^h) is nonzero. This proves h_r>=r.
It concerns the nonreduced orbit SCHEME; the geometric point set
of this fiber still consists of a single point.

Finally suppose one finite-rank common object tensor-generated a
category containing all A_r. Its single algebraic group H preserves
all these algebra objects. The same ideal argument gives injections
k[t]/t^(p^r)->k[H] for EVERY r. But the nilradical of the Noetherian
ring k[H] has a finite nilpotence exponent. This is impossible.

No subgroup is claimed to be normal, and the argument does not assert
that an alpha_(p^r) subgroup embeds in H_r. A faithful coordinate-ring
map from the orbit is precisely what is needed for the bound.

## Character lifting across nilpotents

The independent character lemma used in Version1 remains useful.
Let H be any affine algebraic group over k whose nilradical is killed
by \(p^h\)-th powers. If a character chi restricts to \(\lambda^{p^r}\)
on \(H_{\rm red}\), then
\[
\chi^{p^h}=\psi^{p^r}
\]
for a character psi of H. Lift lambda to a unit u in k[H].
The defects of its group-like equation and of
\(\chi=u^{p^r}\) lie in nilpotent ideals killed by \(p^h\).
Thus \(\psi=u^{p^h}\) is group-like and has the asserted power.
No quotient by the reduced subgroup is assumed.

For the Frobenius algebra the full-orbit argument already gives
the stronger genus-independent bound, so the old determinant
application is unnecessary. Unbounded height remains a necessary
condition, not a contradiction to proalgebraic monodromy.
