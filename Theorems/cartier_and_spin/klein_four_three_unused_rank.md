Version1,26 September2026. Retain the actual same-source hypotheses and
notation of [the uniform V4 theorem](klein_four_uniform_pencil_gap.md).
If e<=26, at most one character satisfies
\[
c_i\ge13+2d_i.
\]

\[
g\le46+j\qquad(e\le26).
\]
More generally, regardless of e, a constant character with
c_i>=e-13 has its whole word defined over M=F_(25^7), up to scalar.

with the characters just described.

The exact new linear-algebra ingredient is this: on every
two-dimensional word space with c=15+2r,0<=r<=5, no three distinct
complementary nodes give proportional mixed evaluation functionals.
Two-node collisions do occur, and are retained.

and neither original unmarked common-cover problem is decided.
