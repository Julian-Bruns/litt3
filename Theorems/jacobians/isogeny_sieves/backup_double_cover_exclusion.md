# Complete two-primary Prym exclusion for the backup

Version2. The complete exact finite sieve has a scoped assembly audit;
its elementary-abelian consequence uses only character decomposition.

Let X be the fixed genus-nine cyclic trigonal curve and B=C_alpha the
fixed genus-two backup, alpha^3+alpha+1=0 over characteristic5.

For EVERY nontrivial geometric two-torsion line L on X, the Prym of its
connected finite etale double has no geometric isogeny factor J(B).
Consequently no connected smooth projective W admits finite etale maps

    W→X of degree2, and W→B of degree16.

In particular, X has no degree16 orbifold map to the SAME six-point
P1(2,2,2,2,2,2) of B's hyperelliptic map. The split-double case is
already excluded by Hom(JX,JB)=0. Thus the backup degree2 atlas row
is completely excluded over the algebraic closure.

More generally, let \(X_{[2]}\to X\) be the maximal connected
elementary-abelian two-cover. Its group is \((\mathbf Z/2)^{18}\),
its degree is \(2^{18}\), and its genus is \(2{,}097{,}153\). Then
\[
\operatorname{Hom}(J(B),J(X_{[2]}))=0.
\]
Hence \(X_{[2]}\), and every intermediate cover over \(X\), has no
nonconstant map to \(B\). In particular the \(X\)-leg of an actual
common finite étale span \(X\leftarrow Z\to B\) cannot have
elementary-abelian two-group Galois closure. Every new Prym factor
of \(X_{[2]}\to X\) is ordinary; \(J(X_{[2]})\) is not ordinary because
the factor \(J(X)\) is not. This does not address a further five-group
cover or arbitrary two-groups.

Every actual model, quadratic twist, multiplicity and possible geometric
