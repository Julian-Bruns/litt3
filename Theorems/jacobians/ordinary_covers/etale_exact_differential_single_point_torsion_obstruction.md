# An exact differential with one pulled-back zero divisor forces an exceptional Abel point

Version1,3 October2026. [Root whole-scope review PASS](../../../Research/audits/RANK_THREE_SCALAR_TARGET_AND_TAME_IDENTITY_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md).

Let Y be an ordinary smooth projective connected genus-TWO curve over an algebraically closed field of odd characteristic, and let O be a Weierstrass point. Let f:Z→Y be ANY finite étale map of degree h from a smooth projective connected curve. If a NONZERO regular exact differential dv on Z satisfies
\[
\operatorname{div}(dv)=2f^*Q
\]
for a point Q of Y, then [TWO h](Q−O)=ZERO in J(Y), and Q is NON-Weierstrass.

Consequently, if the Abel curve W₁(Y,O) meets J(Y)[TWO h] only in its six Weierstrass classes, no such f and dv exist. The cover need not be cyclic or Galois, and h need not be prime to the characteristic. No ordinarity of Z is assumed.
