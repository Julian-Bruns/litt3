# The two endpoints exclude the last constant-character boundary

Version2,26 September2026. Retain the actual same-source V4 hypotheses of
[the uniform pencil theorem](klein_four_uniform_pencil_gap.md). Every
character satisfies
\[
d_i=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad c_i\le13,
\]
including full common-pole support e=29. No rationality hypothesis on
the unknown curve or its coefficients is imposed.

Consequently actual V4 comparisons in degree88 are impossible. Combined
with the preceding exclusions, their remaining degrees are14..87;
the conditional recognition cutoff remains13. The proof also gives
g<=47+j for every still-possible V4 comparison. The last numerical
relaxation leaves75 scalar profiles at86 and18 at87; these are only
necessary profiles, not realizations.

The new endpoint ingredient is a finite coefficient-plane rigidity
statement. Put M=F_(25^7), and let (r,s) be any forced first jet with
nonzero leading denominator. If 1,r,s are M-linearly independent,
the plane span_M(1,r,s) determines the M-line of r in the quotient
by M. Thus two such endpoint jets spanning the same plane have
proportional first-coordinate classes modulo M.

More generally put u=29-e, the number of unused locations. For any
constant character with c+u>=14, both normalized endpoint jets and
all coefficients of its normalized word F belong to the quadratic
extension of M inside K0*M. In particular their spans with1 have
M-dimension at most two. This generalization uses the same endpoint
rigidity and interpolation, without another finite enumeration.
