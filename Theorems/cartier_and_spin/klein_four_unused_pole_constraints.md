# Independence of two unused pole constraints

Version1, 26 September2026. Retain the actual same-source hypotheses and
notation of [the uniform pencil gap](klein_four_uniform_pencil_gap.md).
If the common pole support has size e<=27, at most one of the three
characters can satisfy c_i=14+2d_i. Consequently
\[
g\le47+j,
\qquad g\le46+j\quad\text{if all three }c_i\text{ are even}.
\]
The first bound improves g<=48+j precisely when at least two of the
29 allowable pole locations are unused. It reduces the seven necessary
degree88 profiles to four; it does not exclude degree88 or any smaller
remaining degree.

The proof establishes independence of two mixed evaluation constraints
on a three-dimensional cyclotomic word space. A stronger proposed
single-node nonvanishing statement is false in48 orbit cases, all retained
in the certificate. Each minimum word has at most one exceptional node,
which is sufficient for the independence assertion.
