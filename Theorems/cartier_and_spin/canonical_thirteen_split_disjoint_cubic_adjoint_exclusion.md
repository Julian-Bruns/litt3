# Actual split index-one degree thirteen with disjoint infinity is impossible

Version1, 3 October2026. [Independent whole-proof audit PASS](../../Research/audits/CANONICAL_THIRTEEN_SPLIT_DISJOINT_CUBIC_ADJOINT_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md), with no required corrections. No computation is used.

Work over k=F̄₅ with the fixed genus-nine curve X:y³=P(x), P monic of degree10, its unique infinity O, and θ=dx/y² with div(θ)=16O. Let q₀(x)=(x+1)²+d with d≠0.

Let h₁,h₂:C₀→X be TWO actual finite étale maps of degree13 from the SAME smooth projective connected curve. Write xᵢ=hᵢ*x, yᵢ=hᵢ*y, Hᵢ=hᵢ*O and θᵢ=hᵢ*θ. Assume H₁ and H₂ are disjoint and there is a nonconstant actual function t with
\[
k(C_0)=k(t,x_1,x_2),\qquad q_0(x_2)=t^6q_0(x_1),
\qquad\operatorname{div}(t)=H_1-H_2,
\qquad\theta_1=\kappa t^{16}\theta_2\quad(\kappa\in k^\times).
\]
These are the actual split cubic-index-one hypotheses, including jointly generated field and original cubic coordinates. Then the configuration is impossible.

The proof treats a potentially singular conic image through its actual conductor and canonical differential. It does not assume that y₂ descends to the image or that the degree-twelve extension vanishing persists. Both actual maps remain on C₀, and any original finite étale endpoint maps from T retain their SAME T. Auxiliary surface divisors do not replace either map.

Common infinity, split degrees14 and higher, nonsplit comparisons and the unmarked common-cover problem are outside this conclusion.
