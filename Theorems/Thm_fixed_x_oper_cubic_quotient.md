# A cubic quotient enumerates the remaining fixed-X dormant opers

For the system in [fixed_x_dormant_equations](Thm_fixed_x_dormant_equations.md):

1. The equation a10=c4^2 holds scheme-theoretically.
2. The closed slice c4=0 has length330 and reduced support EXACTLY the
   55 invariant points A=C=0. Each has slice length6 and full length8.
3. On the complementary open set, put t=c4, lambda=t^3,
   C=t Chat and A=t^2 Ahat. Both Chat (degree4) and Ahat (degree10)
   are monic. Quotienting by t->zeta_3 t gives a finite scheme of length9645.
   The original open set is recovered by t^3=lambda, a finite etale
   degree-three map. Local multiplicities are unchanged.

The quotient has an explicit presentation in JUST the14 lower coefficients
of Chat,Ahat. The reconstructed lambda is already a unit: lambda=0 would
force B to be one of the55 invariant opers and the corresponding linear
C-block to kill a monic polynomial, whereas that block is injective at
all55 points. Thus adjoining an inverse variable is unnecessary even
scheme-theoretically. The polynomials B and lambda are reconstructed
uniquely. There are44 coefficient equations,
of maximum degree16, generated by
[scripts/normalized_oper_quotient.sage](../scripts/normalized_oper_quotient.sage)
with --msolve-input. The unit certificate is reproduced by
[scripts/normalized_oper_lambda_unit.sage](../scripts/normalized_oper_lambda_unit.sage).

Thus one can enumerate the remaining original solutions by solving this
smaller system, retaining local multiplicities, and taking all three
cube roots of lambda for each point. These triples are DISTINCT solutions,
not duplicates to discard.

Status: author proof plus exact computations, not independently audited.
This is the reduction used by the now-completed
[oper census](Thm_fixed_x_oper_enumeration.md). Neither result excludes
a common cover.
