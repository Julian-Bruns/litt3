# Compute the next Witt obstruction along an explicit defect line

Compute the next higher-Witt obstruction along the unique defect direction
of the explicit etale double below. The goal is to determine which of its
one-parameter family of compatible W3 lifts can continue to W4. We have
already computed its nonlinear characteristic-five germ, including a
nonzero cubic coefficient; this gives a concrete starting point.

## The curve, connection, and reference lift

Work over k=bar(F5), with t^5-t!=0, and W_n=W(k)/(5^n). Set

    G=u(u-1)(u-2)(u-3), F=G(u-t), Y:v²=F, eta=du/v,
    A=(t+1)²G, a=A/F², r=3a''/a+(a'/a)².

Derivatives here are in u. The scalar convention is U''=rU, with
the projective Schwarzian transformation law. This r is regular,
active, admissible and indigenous-ordinary on Y. Its normalized
square-Hasse tensor is s=A eta^4, and J(Y) is ordinary.

Let C be the smooth projective curve with field

    k(C)=k(u,v,kappa), kappa²=u(u-3), gamma=v/kappa.

Thus gamma²=(u-1)(u-2)(u-t). The original map pi:C→Y is connected
finite etale of degree2; g(C)=3. Pull r and s back to C.

The canonical ordinary lift of (Y,r), together with the unique lift
of this original etale cover, gives a specified FULL reference curve
C^0/W and its pulled-back filtered projective inverse-Cartier data.
Use its reductions C2^0,C3^0 as the reference; this fixes the origin of
the following affine parameter. The square-trivial previous-flow twist
on Y is O(W_t-O). Its lifted pullback is part of these data.

## The one polynomial to compute

Let V=H1(C,T_C), and let Psi:V→V be the Frobenius-semilinear
higher-Hodge variation map, with its relative Frobenius twists retained.
For this C it has a five-dimensional bijective part and a zero line.
Its dual twisted Cartier kernel is generated by the regular quadratic

                        phi=gamma*eta².

Normalize the unique v0 in ker(Psi) by the Serre pairing <v0,phi>=1.
For z in k form the marked curve lift

                        C3(z)=C3^0+z*v0

above the FIXED C2^0. The Hodge obstruction for this first step is zero
for every z. Its unique lifted Hodge line and projective graded
identification therefore supply the complete previous-flow tuple over
W2 for the next inverse-Cartier transform.

Choose any marked W4 curve C4 lifting C3(z). Let rho_z(C4) in V be the
obstruction to lifting that previous Hodge line in the resulting flat
bundle over C3(z). Define

                         P_t(z)=<rho_z(C4),phi>.

The variation formula rho_z(C4+xi)=rho_z(C4)-Psi(xi) makes this
independent of C4. Its zero set is exactly the set of z admitting a
filtration-compatible W4 step. The reference gives P_t(0)=0, and
the double involution gives P_t(-z)=-P_t(z).

TARGET: determine this function P_t and its complete geometric zero
set for the family. A formula up to a nonzero factor depending only
on t is sufficient. Give an exact finite-precision construction or
certificate that determines the function, including its degree bound.
An explicit additional exceptional polynomial in t is acceptable.

## Completed geometric calculations to use

The full regular quadratic basis on C is

                  (1,u,u²,kappa,u*kappa,gamma)*eta².

For E(R)=R''-3R² and N(R)=-(E')²-3E(E''+3RE), write N(R)(du)^10
in the fifth powers of this basis. These six coefficient equations
define the fixed-curve nilpotent scheme, with its full scheme structure.

The three invariant linear directions have determinant
4t(t+2)(t+3)(t+4)(t+1)^8. The two kappa directions have invertible
Cartier block of determinant(t+3)(t+4)(t+1)^4 before coefficient fifth
roots. The remaining direction is phi. Eliminating the five invertible
directions gives the completed local algebra k[[w]]/(w³). Explicitly,

    N(r+w*gamma/F+w²*(u-t)/((t+1)F))
                =3(t+1)w³*(gamma/F)^5 + O(w^4).

This identity was executed symbolically over F5(t). There is also a
genuine transverse branch deformation: replace3 by c in G and in
kappa². The derivative at c=3 of the formerly zero linear coefficient
is3(t+1)². Thus the single defect has nontrivial cubic and transverse
geometry. Here w is a connection-deformation parameter; the target z
is the curve-lift displacement defined above. P_t is the new arithmetic
invariant.

The relevant higher construction is Lan--Sheng--Yang--Zuo,

## How the answer advances the two-map problem

In the already reduced Galois one-defect branch of our actual common
cover problem, the joint source is a prime-to5 Galois cover of one of
these bad doubles. Its only possible canonical W3 mismatch is pulled
back from z*v0. The other ordinary endpoint gives a compatible W4
step upstairs, so its next endpoint obstruction class is zero. By
etale naturality that class is the pullback of the obstruction on
C3(z). Prime-to5 trace is a left inverse on the Psi cokernels, so the
class downstairs must be zero as well. Consequently the actual mismatch
parameter must be a root of P_t. This argument retains both original
maps and uses the actual intermediate cover.

If zero is the only root, this removes that mixed W3 mismatch in the
reduced branch. Additional roots would identify the alternatives for
the next stage; identically zero would show that this particular
arithmetic test imposes no further restriction. The task is the explicit
obstruction function on C, so the geometric construction and calculation
can be carried out directly from the data above.
