# An ordinary genus-two endpoint has no nontrivial odd uniform atlas

Version1,3 October2026. New wild extension of the accepted tame endpoint argument, awaiting independent whole review.

Let Y be either selected ordinary genus-two endpoint. An ACTUAL separating finite map Y→C with every branch fiber complete and uniform, all ramification indices dividing its ODD degree d, and ONE UNIFORM GALOIS COMPLETED LOCAL EXTENSION TYPE throughout each branch fiber, must have degree ONE. In particular the local different is uniform. This applies to an actual representable finite étale orbifold atlas, whose full local inertia action is retained. No assumption of tameness, solvability or a bounded wild group is imposed.

Consequently the actual canonical-different extraction in a ramified spin source has étale upper degree one even in the wild branch. Its different coefficient is primitive over its carrier. Both actual endpoint maps remain on the same source. This does not solve the original common-cover problem.
