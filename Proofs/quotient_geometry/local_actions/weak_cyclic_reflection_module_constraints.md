The prescribed smooth weak-Cp Kummer lift and formal patching in the [reviewed rank-(p−ONE) theorem](weak_cyclic_reflection_representation_obstruction.md) are independent of module dimension. They supply a,b,c generating the SAME G, with abc=ONE, orders p,p,TWO, the original tame class c, and b conjugate to a⁻¹. This preliminary construction requires only odd p and the stated curve data; its proof does not use p≡ONE moduloFOUR until the final determinant obstruction.

Lift a,b uniquely to unipotents A,B and put C=(AB)⁻¹. The repeated C-eigenvalue λ has multiplicity n−ONE, and its other eigenvalue is −λ. Since detC=ONE, λ^n=−ONE. The linear tuple remains irreducible even if the projective G-module has kernel.

Scott's elementary inequality on End(V) bounds its sum of THREE centralizer dimensions by n²+TWO. The C centralizer has dimension (n−ONE)²+ONE, and every n-by-n matrix has centralizer dimension at least n. These bounds attain the Scott limit exactly. Thus A and B each have centralizer dimension n and are single regular unipotent Jn blocks. Their pth powers are ONE, so the block size cannot exceed p. This proves n≤p and shows that neither p-generator image was killed.

The same equality on Hom between any two irreducible product-ONE tuples in the same THREE classes proves simultaneous rigidity, exactly as in the reviewed theorem. Put ρ=λ⁻¹, the repeated eigenvalue of AB. It satisfies ρ^n=−ONE and is algebraic over F_p. Entrywise q-Frobenius fixes the exact THREE classes for the smallest finite field F_q containing ρ; rigidity and GL_n Lang descent give A,B,C∈SL_n(F_q). The projective image is generated by the TWO p-elements, so its determinant modulo nth powers quotient, of order gcd(n,q−ONE) prime to p, is trivial. Thus it lies in PSL_n(F_q).

Write AB=ρ(I−TWO E) with E the rank-ONE simple-eigenspace projector and put M=ρB⁻¹. Then M−A has rankONE and detM=ρ^n=−ONE. The common companion-basis construction in the reviewed proof is valid for every n≥TWO. In that basis the B-cyclic volume is

(-1)^{n(n-1)/2}\rho^{n(n-1)/2}(\det M)^{-(n-1)},
\]
while the A⁻¹-cyclic volume is (−ONE)^(n(n−ONE)/TWO). Centralizer determinants are nth powers. Therefore the inverse-conjugacy determinant class is

\rho^{n(n-1)/2}(-1)^{n-1}
\quad\text{modulo }(\mathbf F_q^\times)^n.

For EVEN n it equals −ρ^(n(n−ONE)/TWO). Set κ=ρ^(n/TWO), so κ²=−ONE and κ has exact orderFOUR. Because n−ONE is odd, this class is either κ or −κ and has orderFOUR. Inverse conjugacy in the entire PSL image requires it to be an nth power. The nth-power subgroup has order (q−ONE)/gcd(n,q−ONE), proving the stated necessary divisibility. Since ρ^n=−ONE, the TWO-adic order of ρ is exactly 2^(v₂(n)+ONE), so v₂(q−ONE)>v₂(n). The equivalent TWO-adic formulation follows. No assertion that all odd factors of n divide q−ONE is made.
