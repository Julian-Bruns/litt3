# An oriented scalar chart for remaining Klein-four comparisons

Version1, 26 September2026. Retain the actual same-source V4
comparison hypotheses and canonical coefficient conventions of
[the fourth traces](klein_four_fourth_endpoint_trace.md). Let
K=F_(5^14), F=F_(5^56), and pi_lam be the sigma-eigenprojection
for lam in {1,2,3,4}. Put c_lam=pi_lam(c(alpha)).

Every remaining actual comparison has the following properties.

1. Both endpoint multisets have at least two distinct phases.
2. Both odd characters are nonzero at each endpoint. The determinant
   D_R=C_R,3 U_R,2-C_R,2 U_R,3 is nonzero for R=Q,H.
3. At least one of pi3(epsilon),pi3(epsilon^-1) is nonzero. By
   interchanging endpoints one may therefore assume pi3(epsilon)!=0.

In that orientation write
\[
\epsilon=\kappa r,\qquad
r=c_3+a_0+a_2c_2+a_4c_4,
\qquad\kappa\in K^*,\quad a_0,a_2,a_4\in K,
\quad(a_0,a_4)\ne(0,0).
\]
The two pole moments are forced:
\[
y=\frac{\pi_3(rC_Q)}{\eta c_3},\qquad
x=\left(\frac{\pi_3(rU_Q)}{\eta c_3}\right)^{5^{10}}.
\]
Thus the remaining finite coefficient decision has only the three
normalized scalar coordinates and its nonzero scale, besides the
two endpoint multisets. All four original trace equations and the
integer pole-weight test still have to be imposed. The chart is
exhaustive for the remaining comparisons, not a nonemptiness or
emptiness theorem for it.
