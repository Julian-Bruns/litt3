# Exact height for one short abelian factor and reduction to balanced covers

Version1,21 September2026. Focused independent audit PASS.

Let $H/C$ be an actual genus-two BT1 from the
[F625 existence-descent counterexample](etale_p_witt_obstruction.md),
with its original first periodic datum. For EVERY actual connected
finite etale cover with Galois group $C_{5^a}\times C_5$, $a\ge1$,
\[
\boxed{\max\{N:q^*H\text{ admits an actual BT}_N\}=a+2.}
\]
Equivalently the exact maximal compatible marked Witt length is
$a+3$. No such cover gives a full prolongation, regardless of the
chosen intermediate repair digits.

More generally, for an actual abelian FIVE-GROUP cover with group
$C_{5^a}\times C_{5^b}$, $a>b\ge1$, full potential effectivity
is equivalent to that on its actual balanced quotient of type
$(C_{5^b})^2$. A supplied full upper group descends through the
unbalanced part. Thus any abelian five-group full repair must already
occur on a balanced cover $(C_{5^b})^2$ with $b\ge2$. Such balanced
covers are unique as quotients of the maximal geometric abelian
five-cover; their effectivity is not decided here.

These conclusions include all actual normalized extensions and the
finite-character realizations of the fixed projective datum. They do
not cover a prime-to-five part, nonabelian covers, or either original
common-cover problem.
