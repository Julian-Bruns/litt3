# Integral wild-subgroup jumps bound two-branch atlas degrees

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p>=3. Let a smooth
projective curve X, with h=2g(X)-2>0, be a finite etale atlas of degree
n of an effective proper orbifold with coarse P^1 and exactly two
branch points, one wild and one tame. At the wild point put e=qt,
q=|I_1|, p not dividing t, c=delta-e, and assume 0<c<e.

If all upper jumps of the local wild-subgroup extension L/L^(I_1)
are INTEGERS, n has an effectively computable bound depending only
on (p,h). This is the numbering for I_1, not the full inertia extension.
The hypothesis holds for abelian I_1 by Hasse--Arf; it is not asserted
for arbitrary nonabelian I_1.

For p=5,h=16 there is at most one positive jump, q=5, and n<=2240.
This is an atlas theorem with the displayed two-branch hypotheses,
not an unconditional statement about coreless covers.
