# The Hessian pairing in characteristic five

This proves the [tame rank-three reduction](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/tame_rank_three_second_projective_return.md).
Use the accepted [low-degree twist vanishing](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/low_degree_twist_vanishing.md)
and [first projective-return exclusion](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/shifted_first_frobenius_vanishing.md).
Every nonzero map from an irreducible rank-three finite coefficient to K
is surjective. The former theorem excludes imprimitive sources and any
source having a semi-invariant in Sym^m R with m<=4.

## The classification input and its geometric scope

[Dolgachev--Martin, Theorem2.6, p8](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/idolga/wp-content/uploads/sites/1334/2024/08/autodelPezzoOdd.pdf)
lists the conjugacy types of finite subgroups of PGL3 in odd
characteristic. At characteristic five, its Lie-type groups and the
exceptional A5/A6/A7 types are not tame. Among primitive irreducible
tame types there remain PSL2(7) and subgroups of the Hessian group216.
The PSL2(7) type preserves a Klein quartic, as discussed in its
section4.2. Apply the same classification to the dual representation
to obtain the quartic semi-invariant in Sym^4 R rather than merely in
Sym^4 R^vee. The accepted vanishing excludes this type.

For clarity, the abstract GL2 quotient in item(13) of that theorem is
not alone a proof of reducibility. The underlying original
[Bloom paper, section7, pp176--177](https://paperzz.com/doc/7654041/the-subgroups-of-psl-3--q--for-odd-qc-)
places these matrices, up to conjugacy and inverse transpose, in the
parabolic with first-row entries c12=c13=0. They preserve a plane or
a line. Its preceding diagonal cases either preserve an eigenspace
or are monomial. The same geometric description is used in the proof
of Dolgachev--Martin Theorem4.4. This retains the case where the normal
p-subgroup is trivial. The original Bloom full-text transcription was
read; the AMS-hosted PDF was inaccessible during this review.

Consequently the only possible projective image is contained, after
conjugation, in the standard Hessian group. This is a classification
input, not an assertion that an abstract subgroup of GL2 has no
irreducible three-dimensional representation.

## The character-valued Frobenius pairing

Let w^2+w+1=0, w!=1, in F25. The standard generators in
[Artebani--Dolgachev, section4, pp9--10](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/idolga-new/wp-content/uploads/sites/1467/2024/08/hesse09.pdf)
are
\[
C=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&1\\1&0&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
D=\operatorname{diag}(1,w,w^2),\quad
H=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&1\\1&w&w^2\\1&w^2&w\end{pmatrix},\quad
E=\operatorname{diag}(1,w,w).
\]
Since w^5=w^2, direct multiplication gives
\[
C^{(5)t}C=D^{(5)t}D=E^{(5)t}E=I,\qquad H^{(5)t}H=3I.
\]
The identity A^{(5)t}A=c(A)I is preserved by products. A scalar
multiple changes c by its sixth power. Therefore it holds for every
matrix in any finite scalar lift of the projective Hessian group.
Uniqueness of c shows that c is a multiplicative finite character.

For the associated representation W, the bilinear pairing
W^{(5)} tensor W -> k_c is nondegenerate and equivariant. Here the
first factor is the coefficient-Frobenius representation, so the
formula is genuinely k-bilinear. On the associated coefficient
bundles it gives
\[
F^*R\simeq R^\vee\otimes T.
\]
This agrees with absolute Frobenius pullback: after an etale
trivialization the descent matrices are constants and their entries
are raised to the fifth power. Applying F once more yields
\[
F^{2*}R\simeq (F^*R)^\vee\otimes T^5
\simeq R\otimes T^{-1}\otimes T^5=R\otimes T^4.
\]
Conjugating the initial projective image simply transports the
nondegenerate pairing. For the dual representation the inverse
pairing has the inverse character, so the orientation choice in the
quartic argument causes no problem.

The previously proved first-projective-return exclusion makes the
projective period exactly two. No triviality of T^4 is asserted.
Nor may one normalize this character by twisting R without tracking
the map R->K, its determinant, and its degree-minus-one kernel.

## Small exact verification

The [standalone verifier](../../scripts/arithmetic/hessian_frobenius_pairing.py)
uses only arithmetic in F5[w]/(w^2+w+1). It checks the four identities,
enumerates the216 normalized projective matrices generated by them,
and checks the pairing and the coefficient25-power identity on every
one. The symbolic product/scalar argument above covers arbitrary
finite scalar lifts, which need not be defined over F25.
The executed receipt is in the
[external evidence directory](../../../litt3-computation-data/overnight_three_replies_20260926/).
This verification supports the displayed matrices; it does not
re-prove the finite-subgroup classification.
