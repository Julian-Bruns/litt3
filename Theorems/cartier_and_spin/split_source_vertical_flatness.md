# Higher-order flatness at vertical critical content

Version2,30 September2026. Let R=k[[r]], with k algebraically closed
of odd characteristic p. Let
\[
F(W)=(W^p+q)^2+(W^p+q)S(W)+\tau\in R[W],
\qquad \deg S\le p-1,\quad \tau\in R^\times.
\]
Suppose F splits as a product of 2p monic linear factors in R[W],
and S' is zero modulo r. Let F0 be the constant-coefficient lift of
the reduction of F. Then
\[
F-F_0\in r^{(p+1)/2}R[W],\qquad
S'\in r^{(p+1)/2}R[W].
\]
If the quadratic (Z+bar q)^2+bar S(Z+bar q)+bar tau has a double
root, the stronger inclusions with r^p hold. Here bar S is constant,
as follows from its degree bound and the derivative hypothesis.

For p=5, an actual split integral degree-ten source of this form
cannot have a vertical common-critical point with first or second
order coefficient content. Its coefficients of W^4,W^3,W^2,W
must all vanish to order at least three; the double-root reduction
forces order at least five. This conclusion is local and independent
of any discriminant-square computation.

A unit leading coefficient in front of (W^p+q)^2 is allowed by first
dividing F by that coefficient and normalizing S and tau. A nonunit tau,
nonintegral source roots, or a derivative of larger degree is outside
the statement. No new whole endpoint family is excluded here.
