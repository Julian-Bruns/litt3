# Every nonpolynomial supported unit uses all three cubic characters

Version1, 27 September2026. On the fixed genus-nine curve X:y^3=P(x),
let O be its unique infinity and let Z be the twelve points over A=0.
Let g be nonzero, regular outside O, with every finite zero in Z.
Write its unique affine expression
\[
g=U(x)+V(x)y+W(x)y^2.
\]
If g does not belong to k(x), then U,V,W are ALL nonzero. There is
no restriction on the pole degree, zero multiplicities or geometric
coefficient field. Equivalently the span of the three conjugates of
a supported function has dimension one or three, never two.

The proof combines a general ramification bound for a reduced
two-character witness with finitely many exact, independently
reconstructed Hermite rank certificates through pole28. It is not
an extrapolation from a bounded search. Common polynomial content
and arbitrary fifth powers are removed in the proof.

This restricts the norm of any actual comparison in every degree,
but does not imply that such a norm is polynomial, or exclude
functions with all three characters. It does not decide the original
unmarked common-cover problem.
