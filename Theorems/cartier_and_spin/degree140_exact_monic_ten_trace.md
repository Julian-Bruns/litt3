# An exact monic degree-ten relation in the global trace ideal

Version1,30 September2026. On the primitive degree140 chart, let
R=K[H,H^-1,q,q^-1,Psi^-1]. The nine actual trace polynomials in
[the degree-eleven construction](degree140_monic_eleven_trace_combination.md)
generate an ideal of R[mu] containing a monic polynomial of degree TEN.
No new parameter is inverted, and no exceptional fibre is discarded.

This follows from full, untruncated polynomial operations. After reducing
by the established monic degree-eleven element, write the degree-ten
coefficient of row i as u_i*N_i/D_i, with u_i,D_i units of R and
N_i in K[H,q]. An exact certificate gives
\[
\sum_{i=0}^8 W_iN_i=H^6q^4.
\]
The corresponding linear combination of the entire trace rows is monic
after division by H^6q^4. Every admitted entirely ramified nonzero
critical fibre annihilates it.

This is a relation in a necessary trace ideal, not a proof that its
common zero locus is empty. It is distinct from the earlier withdrawn
fixed-cutoff computation; that computation is not used.
