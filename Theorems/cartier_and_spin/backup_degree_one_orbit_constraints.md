# Linked divisors and an étale section requirement in the degree-one orbit

Version1,23September2026. Retain the explicit backup genus-two Y,
fixed X, and the actual first-Y-Galois orbit of a jointly minimal
coreless étale span. Write deg h_i=d, deg q=8d, and N for the number
of distinct embedded X-fields. Suppose the radical orbit saturates
to a nonisotropic degree-one plane E on Y^(1).

The four possible E determine the same nonzero logarithmic line
\[
k\beta_Y=k(u+1)\,du/v,\qquad
\beta_Y=(3\alpha^2+4)(u+1)\,du/v.
\]
For their associated P=P_+ or P_- and z=(alpha,0), O_Y(P-z) is
nontrivial five-torsion and remains nontrivial under every finite
étale pullback.

Let H_X=ker(F_X^*U->omega_X) and K_X=H_X(-24O), stable of rank2
and degree1. The actual orbit forces saturated inclusions
epsilon_i->h_i^*K_X for two-torsion epsilon_i. After one connected
finite étale refinement trivializing them all,
\[
0\longrightarrow O\longrightarrow\widetilde h_i^*K_X
\longrightarrow O(\widetilde h_i^*O)\longrightarrow0 .
\]
This is a necessary condition, not a proved impossibility.

The degree6d projection divisors C_i have the exact decomposition
\[
C_i=5B_i+\Delta_i+2Z_i,
\]
where B_i is reduced over the common modification fiber q^*P,
Z_i is reduced over q^*z, and Delta_i is reduced and avoids q^*z.
Here Delta_i may meet B_i. One has 8|N and, at each point of q^*P,
at most floor(3N/20) indices belong to B_i.

The fixed bundle has an explicitly certified further pullback
\[
0\longrightarrow O_X(118O)\longrightarrow F_{\rm abs}^*H_X
\longrightarrow O_X(127O)\longrightarrow0 .
\]
Its first displayed line is generated by the unique section after
twisting by -118O; the twist by -119O has no section. No HN or
strong-semistability assertion follows from this alone.

Projecting the fifth pullbacks of the actual degree24d lines gives
effective divisors C_i^[1] of degree7d, with
\[
O_T(C_i^{[1]}-C_i)\simeq h_i^*O_X(O),\quad
\sum_i C_i^{[1]}=q^*\Gamma^{[1]},\quad
\deg\Gamma^{[1]}=7N/8 .
\]
They have empty common support. None of these identities supplies
X-saturation of the effective divisors or descent of beta_Y.
The degree-one case and both original common-cover problems remain open.
