# Etale quotients of a hyperelliptic curve

Let C be a smooth projective hyperelliptic curve of genus at least two
over an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from two.

- There are at most three geometric isomorphism classes of curves Y
  admitting a finite etale map C→Y of degree two.
- Every connected finite etale Galois quotient C→Y has group trivial,
  C₂ or C₂×C₂. In particular its degree is at most four.

Consequently any smooth projective genus-three curve in this
characteristic has at most three genus-two etale quotients: the
existence of one already forces the genus-three curve to be hyperelliptic.

The Galois hypothesis in the second assertion is essential to the
argument; no bound on non-Galois etale quotient degrees is asserted.
