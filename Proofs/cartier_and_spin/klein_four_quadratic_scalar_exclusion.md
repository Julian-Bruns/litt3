## Phasewise vanishing

A nonzero F5-linear relation on at most four distinct29th roots is
impossible: powers of five provide the consecutive exponents4,5,6,7,
and the corresponding square Vandermonde minor is invertible.
For five terms, use also22,23,24,25. The two four-row matrices differ
by column factors xi^18. Their common one-dimensional kernel would
force every xi^18 equal, contrary to gcd(18,29)=1.

Thus T_lam(R;m)=0, for any nonzero m, means that the coefficient
sum vanishes separately at every phase. This criterion is independent
of m. Both odd characters2 and3 vanish exactly when each type has
the same multiplicity as its opposite type. Counts lie in0..4, so
the equalities modulo five are actual integer equalities.

If just one character vanishes, phase groups have at least two
occurrences. Two phase groups would both be opposite pairs, hence
would kill both characters. In a single group of four, either the
weights split into opposite pairs, or their only zero-sum pattern
is three copies of u and one of2u. The latter is the claimed
adjacent-type pattern. Repeated labels are retained throughout.
