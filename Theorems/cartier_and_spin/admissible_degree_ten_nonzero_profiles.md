# Nine remaining actual nonzero-first-moment infinity profiles

Version1,1 October2026. Use the actual admissible degree-ten hypotheses
of [reconstruction](admissible_line_reconstruction.md), with primitive
$b$, reduced $E$ of degree fifty, and the order-five condition retained.
Let $v\ne0$ satisfy $\operatorname{div}(v)=h_*G-10O$ and write
$d=\operatorname{pole}_O(v)$. In the short frame $W=b+L_0/y$, put
\[
F_s=v(W^5+q_s)^2+(W^5+q_s)S_s+t^3,\qquad
S_s=s_4W^4+s_3W^3+s_2W^2+s_1W+s_0.
\]
Assume $s_4\ne0$, and put $m=\operatorname{pole}_O(s_4)$.
Then the only possible infinity profiles are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
d&m&\text{positive }G\text{-multiplicities over }O&
\text{five large }W\text{-poles}\\\hline
0&12&(10)&(12,2,2,2,2)\\
3&12&(7)&(9,2,2,2,2)\\
6&12&(4)&(6,2,2,2,2)\\
9&12&(1)&(3,2,2,2,2)\\
10&3,6,9,10,12&\varnothing&(2,2,2,2,2).
\end{array}
\]
The last row denotes five separate profiles. In particular $m<12$
forces $v$ to have exact pole ten. The annihilator's scale and all
geometric coefficient values remain unrestricted.

On a four-sheet pole-two cohort, the leading residues are either
four distinct nonzero values or two opposite pairs. On a five-sheet
cohort, after the established exclusion of the fully concentrated
case, they have at least four distinct values. These are restrictions
on leading residues, not assertions that the underlying sheets coincide.

No one of the nine profiles is asserted to occur. This theorem does
not exclude the entire nonzero sector or construct an actual cover.
