# A central actual joint-spin kernel is trivial

Version1,3 October2026. New scoped argument pending independent review. Both original finite étale endpoint maps remain on the SAME source; no common-cover decision follows.

Work over an algebraically closed field of characteristic FIVE. Retain an actual source T, its finite group G, the G-orbit of ORIGINAL infinity section lines ku_i in a basepoint-free space W⊂H⁰(T,L), and the normalized ratio carrier Γ with field k(u_i/u_0). Require that G preserves L and W projectively, that these lines form ONE G-orbit, and that the action of a subgroup H⊂G on Γ is faithful. Suppose H is normal and elementary abelian TWO. Give L its genuine H-linearization normalized by fixing u_0; in particular the H-operators on W are genuine commuting involutions. These hypotheses hold in the retained canonical degree-TEN joint-source construction after the accepted [elementary-TWO character reduction](canonical_degree_ten_prime_to_five_quotients.md).

Assume G has NO nontrivial quotient of order prime to FIVE. If H is central in G, then H is trivial.

Consequently, in this actual setup every nontrivial H has rank at least FOUR, hence |H|≥SIXTEEN. For rank at most THREE the conjugation quotient G→GL(H) is prime to FIVE, so H would be central and therefore trivial.

The conclusion concerns the ACTUAL joint kernel with its faithful original ratio carrier and transitive original label orbit. It does not apply to an arbitrary central étale kernel on an unrelated auxiliary cover, nor does it bound the rank of a noncentral kernel.
