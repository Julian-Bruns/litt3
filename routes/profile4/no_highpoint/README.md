# No-highpoint route

The branch is open. Read
[file 170](170_NO_HIGHPOINT_RESIDUAL_GATE.md) and
[Task 03](../../../tasks/03_NO_HIGHPOINT_EXCLUSION.md) only.

There are two independent missing steps in the residual strategy:

1. prove that every no-highpoint profile-4 pair enters the displayed residual
   ansatz;
2. eliminate the full residual family, not just its previously tested small
   subfamilies.

Ramification in step 2 must be checked on the normalization. A common zero of
the plane equation and one partial derivative may be merely a singular image
point whose normalized branches are unramified. Thus an empty resultant locus
is a sufficient stronger condition, while a nonempty resultant locus is not
an obstruction without a smoothness or branch calculation.

The cited small-family source notes and executable certificates are absent.
Their summaries are not a substitute for either step, and their recorded
resultants do not include the normalization checks just described.
