## Primitivity and tensor indecomposability

If W₈ has an invariant block system with2 or4 blocks, the permutation image lies in S₂ or S₄, a prime-to-five quotient of G. It is trivial, contradicting irreducibility. With8 one-dimensional blocks, the order-five element a either permutes a five-cycle and fixes three lines, or fixes every line. Its normalized monomial action therefore has type J₅⊕J₁³ or J₁⁸. Neither is an allowed wild type. Thus no block system exists.

Every nontrivial tensor factorization of dimension8 has a factor of dimension2. In a2×4 factorization the factor sizes are distinct, so their projective actions are preserved separately. In a2×2×2 factorization the factor-permutation image is a quotient inside S₃, hence is trivial by the same prime-to-five hypothesis. Irreducibility of the whole tensor forces irreducibility of every factor. A projective two-dimensional factor cannot kill the tame image: that would make the generated projective image cyclic of order dividing5 and supply an invariant line. Its nontrivial tame involution instead has1+1 eigenspaces, contradicting the accepted characteristic-five reflection-module theorem in dimension2. Thus no such tensor factorization exists.

## Scalar classes prohibit all defining-characteristic forms

Coefficient Frobenius5ʳ multiplies the scalar class α by5ʳ; duality negates it. A projective isomorphism W₈≅(W₈^[5ʳ])* would therefore require
\[
(5^r+1)\alpha=0.
\]
