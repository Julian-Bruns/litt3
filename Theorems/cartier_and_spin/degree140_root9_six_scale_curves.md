# The six genus-ten leading-scale curves are excluded

Version2, 29 September2026. Use the fixed root-nine degree140 family,
its ratio coordinates q,u,s and free scale mu, and the normalized
square tails Cn=[T^n]a(T)^63 from the
[universal square model](universal_degree140_linear_square_model.md).
Define
\[
U_0=\frac{[24]^6q^7}{s^3u^9},\qquad
V_0=\frac{2\epsilon^6q^7}{u^9},\qquad
\sigma_0=\frac{[24]^2}{3\epsilon^2}=\langle112400\rangle.
\]

\[
\deg_\mu C_n\le\lfloor3n/4\rfloor\quad(n\le124),\qquad
[\mu^{54}]C72=2(U_0V_0)^5(U_0-V_0)^2(U_0+V_0)^6.
\]
Its zero set is exactly s^6=sigma0^6. The six s-values, in the
fixed K-code, are
\[
(112400,246025,215500,360225,164100,272625).
\]
For each sigma, the projective normalization of
\[
f_\sigma(u)=(a_0(q)-\sigma d(q))u^3+b(q)u^2+c(q)u+e(q)=0
\]
is geometrically irreducible of genus10. Its degree-three q-map has
24 simple finite branch values and is unramified at infinity, where
u has pole orders2,2,4. The six branch divisors are disjoint.

The full square locus on these six curves has no point on a_sigma=0
or e=0, where a_sigma=a0-sigma*d. This is an all-scale, all-geometric
exclusion with nilpotents retained: twelve a_sigma-boundary ratios
and96 e-boundary ratios are excluded by twelve complete algebra
certificates. Thus a_sigma and e become units in the square quotient.

Together with the [full discriminant theorem](degree140_root9_full_discriminant_exclusion.md),
the remaining six curve questions can be posed on the finite etale

\[
B_\sigma[u]/(f_\sigma/a_\sigma),\qquad
B_\sigma=K[q,1/(q d a_\sigma e F_\sigma
(q-\langle10149\rangle)(q-\langle64426\rangle))],
\]
where F_sigma=Disc(f_sigma); append the independent unit mu.
Here u is automatically a unit. No other coefficient polynomial is
inverted.

On EACH of these six whole cubic algebras, with the independent scale
mu inverted, the received exact certificates now prove
\[
(C71,C72,C73)=(1).
\]
Thus the entire six-curve square locus is empty, over the algebraic
closure and with nilpotents retained. The computation retains all three
ratio roots; it neither transfers a square condition between roots nor
specializes the unknown scale to the coefficient field.

Consequently s^6-sigma0^6 is a unit in the full root-nine square
coordinate ring. On its remaining ratio chart, C72 has degree54 in mu
with unit leading coefficient. Before inverting mu, its monic equation
defines a finite free rank54 algebra over that ratio chart; the remaining
square scheme is a subscheme of its mu-nonzero open. Every geometric
scale fibre has length at most54. This does NOT by itself assert that
the mu-nonzero open is finite over the ratio base.

The discriminant-nonzero complement s^6!=sigma0^6 remains undecided,
as do the other P-root families and the actual common-cover problem.
