# A full degree-twenty cubic extension excludes its conductor pole fiber

Version1, 3 October2026. Fresh independent whole argument review PASS; canonical presentation review pending. No computation is used.

Use the fixed characteristic-five genus-nine curve X:y³=P(x), its unique infinity O, θ=dx/y² with divisor16O, and q₀(x)=(x+1)²+d, d≠0. The polynomial P is monic squarefree of degree10, its roots avoid the q₀ roots, and the coefficient p₉ of U⁹ in P(U−1) is [22]≠0.

Retain TWO actual finite étale degree20 maps hᵢ:C₀→X from the SAME smooth projective connected source, their ACTUAL cubic coordinates, disjoint reduced infinity divisors Hᵢ, and
\[
k(C₀)=k(t,x₁,x₂),\quad q₀(x₂)=t⁶q₀(x₁),\quad
\operatorname{div}(t)=H₁-H₂,\quad θ₁=κt^{16}θ₂.
\]
Both original endpoint legs stay on the SAME original T whenever C₀ is an actual descended source.

On the actual conic image C⊂S:u²−v²=d(t⁶−1), normalized by C₀, put u=x₂+1,v=t³(x₁+1), D=D₊+D₋, L=D+3F. Let R=C−6L be the effective EXACT conductor-adjoint with defining section ρ. Let the SAME source-derived paired extensions satisfy
\[
a,b\in H⁰(S,O_S(3D+10F_\infty+R)),\quad
a|C₀=ρy₂,\quad b|C₀=t^{10}ρy₁.
\]
Retain their general paired cubic identities, including possible errors.

If ENTIRE ρ divides a globally, then F∞ is NOT a component of R. If ENTIRE ρ divides b globally, then F₀ is NOT a component of R.

The full-factor boundary already forces C∼10L,R∼4L,δ=120,degΔ=240 and vanishing of the corresponding individual error. This result excludes a conductor component at that fully extending endpoint's actual pole fiber. It does not exclude the whole actual degree-twenty boundary, force the other individual factor, discard its error in H⁰(2L), or solve the unmarked common-cover problem. Simultaneous full factors are also not excluded by the present theorem.
