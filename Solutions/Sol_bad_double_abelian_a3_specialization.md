## Passage to all balanced abelian covers

Use the general actual-cover quotient/norm theorem of
[abelian_p_defect_node](Sol_abelian_p_defect_node.md), not its separate
genus-two specialization. Compatible free presentations over the
balanced tower leave one scalar relation f_infinity in
Lambda=k[[e1,e2,e3]], defined up to a unit. Its reduction modulo
(e1^5,e2^5,e3^5) is the finite relation above. Hence its entire4-jet
is the one just computed: that quotient ideal has no term of degree<5.

The transverse Hessian block is invertible. Formal implicit elimination
and completion of the two squares split off a nondegenerate quadratic
form in two variables. The remaining one-variable series has leading
coefficient3a+3a² in degree4. Since2 and4 are units, a formal fourth
root of its unit factor absorbs all subsequent terms. Over k the
quadratic form is split, giving formal typeUV+W4.

For q=5^n, every invertible formal coordinate change preserves the
ideal generated by the q-th powers of a system of parameters. This
follows from characteristic-five Frobenius and the inverse coordinate
change; no division by q occurs. Thus the defect length is

                 length k[[U,V,W]]/(UV+W4,Uq,Vq,Wq).

Straightening UV=-W4 gives the basis W^c and U^aW^c,V^aW^c with
1<=a<q and 0<=c<min(q,4(q-a)); for W^c alone,0<=c<q.
The extra cutoff is exactly the reduction of Uq V^(q-a) and its
V-analogue. These monic straightening relations give independent
standard monomials. Hence the length is

                 q+2 sum_(j=1)^(q-1) min(q,4j).

Writing q=4m+1 evaluates this to28m²+14m+1=(7q²-3)/4.
Pullback of sections of E_r into any further actual etale cover is
injective, proving the final lower bound without supplying a second map.
