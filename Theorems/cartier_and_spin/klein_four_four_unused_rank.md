# Four unused locations improve the genus bound

Version1,26 September2026. Retain the actual same-source hypotheses and
notation of [the uniform V4 theorem](klein_four_uniform_pencil_gap.md).
If e<=25, at most one character satisfies
\[
c_i\ge12+2d_i.
\]
Consequently
\[
g\le45+j\qquad(e\le25).
\]
The three-dimensional lower interpolation spaces have exactly one
four-node rank exception, up to rotation and coefficient Frobenius.
Its two-dimensional upper kernel consists of linear multiples of a
single pair, whose endpoint ratio is incompatible with the actual maps.
The exception is retained and excluded, not discarded by a generic-rank
argument.

Together with previous bounds the necessary scalar relaxation has52
profiles at86 and13 at87. No further full degree is excluded; degrees
14..87 and both original unmarked common-cover questions remain open.
