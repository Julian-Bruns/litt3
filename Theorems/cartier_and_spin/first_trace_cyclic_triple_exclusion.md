# A degree-one original trace has no singleton cyclic triple defect

ID: `first_trace_cyclic_triple_exclusion`. Version1,2 October2026.
[Focused whole-implication review](../../Research/audits/CYCLIC_TRIPLE_FIRST_TRACE_DEFECT_AUDIT_2026_10_02.md) PASS.

Retain two actual finite etale maps X<-h-S-g->Y from the SAME smooth
connected projective source, with the fixed genus-nine X and a smooth
genus-two Y, of degrees n and8n. Let I be the original three-section
image in B_X, U its saturated hyperplane, J the complete actual trace
of I in B_Y, and H the complete actual trace of U. If deg J=1, then
B_Y/J cannot be a cyclic length-three module supported at a single
point. This holds without contact-seven equality, ordinariness,
corelessness or a simultaneous Galois closure.

The argument uses the actual source primitive orders and local I/U
Smith exponents, the finite-coefficient positivity of J quotients,
and the accepted deg H>=3 bound. The split defect shapes and the
whole common-cover problem remain open.
