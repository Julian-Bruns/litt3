# Three-scale bound for actual étale degree-ten covers

Version2,29 September2026. Fix an allowed geometric ratio in either
the constant-v degree140 family or the fixed-root-nine linear-v
degree140 family. There are at most **three distinct nonzero scales**
whose source defines an actual connected everywhere-étale degree-ten
cover of the fixed curve. At a positive-content root-nine ratio there
is at most one such scale. The constant family's positive-content
locus is already excluded.

The assertion is about actual étaleness, stronger than the necessary
square-norm condition. It does not assert a length bound for a scheme
of actual covers.

Let K=F_(5^8), q=w^3, and use H=h*w in the constant chart and H=h/w
in the root-nine chart. The normalized scale is mu=lambda/w. If
d=[K(H,q):K], an actual cover with nontrivial geometric
discriminant-half class in J(X_q)[2], where X_q has equation
q*z^3=P(x), necessarily satisfies
\[
57\mid d.
\]
In particular, when57 does not divide d, every actual candidate has
geometrically square discriminant on X_q. That square alternative,
and arbitrary ratio fields with degree divisible by57, remain open.

On the positive-content root-nine locus the stronger condition is
\[
171\mid d.
\]
Here the unique content sheet over a fixed K-rational endpoint identifies
X_q over K(H,q) with a cubic twist already defined over K. The single
possible actual scale is defined over that same ratio field. Thus the
cubic-twist ambiguity that weakened the general bound disappears.

This does not extract a shared tensor from an arbitrary two-map span
or decide the unmarked common-cover problem.
