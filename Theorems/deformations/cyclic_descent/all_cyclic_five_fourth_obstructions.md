# All six cyclic-five repairs have a nonzero fourth obstruction

Version2,24 September2026. Exact integral computation on the remaining
arithmetic orbit, with an independent finite-field contraction check;
the all-height dictionary supplies the actual BT consequence.
The all-parameter implication uses the previously audited secondary
trace theorem.

Use the original marked genus-two datum over $k_0=\mathbf F_{625}$ in
[the explicit obstruction example](../explicit_genus_two_witt_obstruction.md),
with $\tau^4+4\tau^3+\tau^2+4\tau+3=0$ and $\mu=4+4\tau$.
For EVERY actual connected cyclic etale cover $q:D\to C$ of degree
five, the pulled original second tuple admits compatible third
solutions, and NONE admits a compatible fourth solution. Its exact
marked Witt height is three.

The six geometric covers form arithmetic orbits of sizes one and
five. On the rational orbit, the normalized secondary trace is the
previously established $3/\mu$. On the other orbit it is an element
$E\in\mathbf F_{625^5}$ of exact degree five over $k_0$, with
\[
N_{\mathbf F_{625^5}/k_0}(E)=3/\mu
=4+2\tau+2\tau^2+4\tau^3\ne0.
\]
In both cases the value is independent of EVERY geometric choice of
the preceding third repair. There is no division by the cover degree.

Consequently every actual connected cyclic etale $5^a$ cover of this
base, with ANY degree-five quotient, has exact compatible marked Witt
height $a+2$. This statement retains the original periodic tuple and
does not concern arbitrary connections on these source curves.

By the [all-height dictionary](../all_height_bt_hodge_dictionary.md),
every actual connected cyclic étale \(5^a\)-cover of this base has
maximal actual BT level \(a+1\) for the specified pulled-back BT1,
including all normalized markings and finite-character realizations.
At degree five each of the six covers has a two-dimensional marked
BT2 extension family, none with a BT3 extension. Unnormalizing the
determinant does not evade the bound. Other BT1s, noncyclic covers and
both original unmarked common-cover candidates remain outside the
statement.
