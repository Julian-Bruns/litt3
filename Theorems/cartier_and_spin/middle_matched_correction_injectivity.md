# Global injectivity of the matched middle correction block

Version1,26 September2026. Use the actual extension and the geometric
twenty-fifth-power source coordinates from
[middle-character correction rigidity](middle_character_correction_rigidity.md).
Write its necessary residual tensor as
\[
H(b)(p,A)=P(b)p+Q(b)A,
\qquad H(b):k^{15}\longrightarrow k^{30},
\quad p\in k^8,\quad A\in k^7.
\]
Here b=(a1^25,...,a5^25,b1^25,...,b5^25) is the ten-coordinate
source block, indexed from zero in the matrices.

For EVERY nonzero geometric b, the map Q(b) is injective. On the
normalized strata whose first nonzero coordinate has index0,1 or2,
there are polynomial left inverses of degrees at most4,4 or3,
respectively. No additional parameter is inverted.

Consequently, for fixed b!=0 and p, there is at most one matched
correction A satisfying the necessary equations. On each of those
three strata they are scheme-equivalently
\[
A=-L(b)P(b)p,
\qquad (P(b)-Q(b)L(b)P(b))p=0.
\]
In particular, a nonzero actual map with f=yp and polynomial-only
correction cannot have p=0. These conclusions do not require stability,
the full Hom-rank window, or finite monodromy. The polynomial-only
qualification is essential: the theorem does not remove unmatched
corrections without the hypotheses of the earlier rigidity theorem.

This proves uniqueness of a correction, not existence of a lift or
emptiness of the residual incidence. It does not decide a strict
Frobenius return or either unmarked common-cover problem.
