# Explicit norm coordinates for the degree-two carrier search

Version1,2026-09-11. Focused audit PASS, /root/audit_degree2_kummer_dictionary,
with no mathematical correction. This is a construction
and a finite system, not an exclusion of the fixed-X degree-two row.
The existing audited A4/Prym reduction remains unchanged.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic5 and X:y^3=F(x), with
F squarefree of degree10. The unique point O at infinity has pole
orders3,10 for x,y, and g(X)=9. For the fixed X use precisely its
canonical polynomial over F25.

## Complete bounded Kummer representation

For every nonzero L in J(X)[2], Riemann--Roch gives an effective
divisor D of degree9 with [D-9O]=L. Thus there is a function f with
div(f)=2D-18O. Since the affine curve is smooth and

 L(18O)=<1,x,...,x^6,y,xy,x^2y>,

we have f=P(x)+Q(x)y, degP<=6, degQ<=2. Its norm down to k(x) is

                  P^3+F Q^3=R^2,       degR<=9.             (1)

The norm divisor is even; constants are squares over k. Here Q is
nonzero: Q=0 and (1) would make P a square and L trivial.
Conversely, a bounded solution with Q!=0 cannot make f a square on X.
Such a square root would lie in L(9O)=<1,x,x^2,x^3>, whereas f has a
nonzero y coefficient.

There is an exact extra local condition for (1) to be UNRAMIFIED.
Let g=gcd(P,Q), and let g_odd be the squarefree product of the roots
at which g has odd order. Then

        X(sqrt(P+Qy)) -> X is etale iff g_odd divides F.     (2)

At a root of F there is one cubic place, and its valuation of f is
the valuation of Norm(f)=R^2, hence even. The same applies at infinity.
At a finite nonbranch point, put m=min(ord P,ord Q). At least two of
the three cubic sheets have valuation exactly m; at most one has a
larger valuation. Their sum is even by (1). Therefore all three are
even exactly when m is even. This proves (2), including common-root
and lower-pole-order cases. No coprimality chart has been silently
assumed. In a necessary-system search one may omit (2), but any
surviving solution then requires this test before it represents a cover.

## An actual quartic carrier

Choose zeta of order3, set f_i=P+zeta^i Qy, and choose a_i^2=f_i with
a_0 a_1 a_2=R. The V4 extension is generated by two of these roots;
the third is their product inverse times R. Its cyclic order3 action
permutes a_i. The four even-sign sums have equation

               z^4+4P z^2+2R z+2P^2=0.                  (3)

For every solution satisfying (2) and Q!=0, its smooth projective
normalization is the actual R=U/C3 carrier from the audited reduction:
degree4 over x, eleven complete (3,1) fibers, genus8, and
J(R) is isogenous to Prym(X(sqrt(f))/X). This is not a map R->B.
Nontriviality of the three Kummer classes follows from
1+rho+rho^2=0 on J(X)[2], so the geometric Galois group is A4.

Independent symbolic checks give discriminant

                3(R^2-P^3)^2=3F^2 Q^6.

The standard cubic resolvent, under u=3P-v, is
-v^3+R^2-P^3; after v=Qy it recovers y^3=F. The factor Q^6 is
apparent polynomial-model discriminant and must not be interpreted as
new ramification of the normalized carrier. Initial guessed constants
in this draft's discriminant and resolvent were rejected by the exact
checks before any statement was promoted; the displayed formulas are
the independently expanded ones.

## Residue-coordinate alternative, to benchmark rather than presume faster

In the etale algebra k[x]/F, every pair P^3=R^2 has a unique S with
P=S^2 and R=S^3: at each root take S=R/P if P!=0, and S=0 otherwise.
Thus use10coefficients of degS<10, put P_full=S^2 modF and R=S^3 modF,
and impose the three coefficients of P_full of degrees7,8,9 equal0.
Then impose (1), with P the degree<=6 part and Q of degree<=2.
All three nonzero-Q charts can be normalized by setting its leading
coefficient to1: scaling S by c scales P,Q by c^2 and R by c^3.
These charts cover all nontrivial geometric double covers, including
the lower-pole-order theta-characteristic cases.

Keeping Q's coefficients as variables may be much sparser than
substituting their high-degree expressions. The actual construction
and matrix support costs, not the variable count alone, will decide.

## Executed checks and remaining work

scripts/check_degree2_kummer_carrier.py checks the even-sign quartic,
discriminant, translated resolvent and pole-semigroup basis. It also
constructs the smooth degree10 playground

 F=2x^10+3x^6+3x^4+x^2+4,
 P=x^6+1, R=x^9+4x^3+x, Q=1,

checks F squarefree and gcd(P,F)=1, and independently normalizes its
quartic function field: genus8, total1.153s. This is not the fixed X
and is only a test of the uniform construction. The fixed-X norm systems
are implemented in scripts/prepare_degree2_norm_system.py. Construction
and bounded solving are not enumeration. Actual torsion/carrier
enumeration and the geometric JB factor test remain open.
