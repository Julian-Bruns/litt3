# A finite-flat Dirac obstruction, with the exact positivity boundary

Author proved-text, consolidated2026-09-07; not independently audited.
The finite-flat formulation below retains the dualizing line explicitly.
It specializes to isogenies without ordinarity and to the ordinary
algebra-trace argument. No Litt3 application or Dirac existence on an
arbitrary principally polarized abelian variety is asserted.

## 1. The finite-flat theorem

Let v:X→Y be finite flat Gorenstein over an algebraically closed field k.
Fix y∈Y(k), x0∈K(k) for K=v^(-1)(y), and write

    H⁰(K,O_K)=∏_(x∈K(k)) C_x.

Each C_x is Artinian local Gorenstein with one-dimensional socle
Soc(C_x)=Ann(m_x). For a line bundle T on X, call a global section
socle-Dirac if it vanishes on every K_x except K_x0, and on that factor
is a nonzero element of Soc(T|_K_x0). This is intrinsic: changing a
line-bundle generator multiplies by a unit and preserves that socle.

Let N be a line bundle on Y whose global sections all vanish at y.
Then T has NO socle-Dirac section whenever

    R=v^*N⊗ω_v⊗T^(-1)

is generated by global sections at the reduced point x0.
Here ω_v is the relative dualizing line; it cannot generally be omitted.

Proof. Put E=v_*O_X. Finite duality identifies
v_*ω_v=Hom_OY(E,O_Y), with counit ε(φ)=φ(1) and
(aφ)(b)=φ(ab). Finite projectivity gives exact fiber base change

    Hom_S(D,S)⊗_S S′ ≅ Hom_(S′)(D⊗_S S′,S′).                (1)

On each C_x, a generator σ of the dualizing module yields a perfect
pairing λ(ab), λ(a)=ε(aσ). If0≠z∈Soc(C_x0), then λ(z)≠0:
otherwise λ(bz)=b(x0)λ(z)=0 for every b in that factor, and products
with other factors are zero, contradicting perfection.

If s were socle-Dirac, choose t∈H⁰(X,R) nonzero at x0. On K_x0,
t has a unit coefficient, so st is a nonzero socle section of
(v^*N⊗ω_v)|_K_x0, zero on the other factors. Projection formula and
the counit give a global section u∈H⁰(Y,N) whose value at y is
nonzero by (1) and the preceding pairing. This contradicts the base
point of N. No division by the fiber degree occurs.

For reduced K the condition is ordinary pointwise Dirac support.
For nonreduced K_x0, a nonzero socle section has value ZERO in the
reduced point fiber; these are different conditions. Set-theoretic
support at x0 alone is insufficient.

## 2. Isogenies and the ordinary algebra-trace specialization

An isogeny v:A→B is finite faithfully flat Gorenstein. For example,
a fiber is cut out in a regular local ring of A by the pullbacks of
a regular parameter system on B, a regular sequence.
Duality gives

    ω_v≅ω_A⊗(v^*ω_B)^(-1).

Choose nonzero invariant top forms η_A,η_B. Their ratio is a global
nowhere-vanishing generator σ of ω_v, so the theorem's condition becomes
generation at0 of v^*N⊗T^(-1). The section v^*η_B here belongs to
the pulled-back LINE BUNDLE v^*ω_B. It is not its image under the
differential map to ω_A, which can be zero for an inseparable isogeny.

The induced τ_σ:E→O_B satisfies

    E≅E^∨,   a↦(b↦τ_σ(ab)).

It may have τ_σ(1)=0 while its pairing remains perfect. Changing invariant
forms only rescales it by a nonzero constant.

When v is étale, use the canonical algebra trace instead. On a split
fiber it is∏_h k→k, (a_h)↦∑_h a_h. If s is pointwise Dirac and
t(0)≠0, then Tr_v(st)(0)=s(0)t(0)≠0, the same contradiction.
The fibers of v^*N are canonically N_0; summing values of s alone
would have no meaning without those identifications. Even if the
degree is divisible by p, trace of1 being zero does not make this
sum functional zero. This argument applies to any finite étale map
with a split fiber and the displayed base-point/evaluation hypotheses.

For duality use [Stacks, finite morphisms, especially Remark48.11.5](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0AWZ),
[Gorenstein fibers](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0C02),
[the dualizing line](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0C14) and
[composition of dualizing sheaves](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0C10).
For algebra trace see [Stacks §49.4](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BSY)
and [the split-fiber calculation](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02DU).

## 3. Principal polarizations and the sharp numerical bound

Assume char k=p>0, (A,L) is principally polarized of dimension g≥1,
K⊂A[p] is finite flat maximal isotropic, and v:A→B=A/K has a
principal ample line bundle M with v^*M≡L^p.
This compatible polarization is an EXPLICIT hypothesis in the general case.

It is automatic for ordinary A and the reduced subgroup K=`A[p](k)`:
K is constant of order p^g, not the full nonreduced A[p].
Its pairing for pλ_L takes values in μ_p and is trivial on the reduced
K×K. Polarization descent gives degree p^(2g)/(p^g)²=1 on B,
hence a principal M with the required class
([van der Geer–Moonen, Proposition11.25](https://www.math.ru.nl/~bmoonen/BookAV/PolWp.pdf)).
This descends the polarization, not an assumed linearization of L^p.

Since h⁰(B,M)=1 and its theta divisor is nonempty, a translate M_0
has its unique section vanishing at0, still with v^*M_0≡L^p.
Here use [Riemann–Roch and ample vanishing, Theorems9.11/9.14](https://www.math.ru.nl/~bmoonen/BookAV/CohomLB.pdf).
Thus any permitted M_0 with v^*M_0⊗T^(-1) generated at0 excludes
socle-Dirac sections on K.

In particular T≡L^m, 1≤m≤p−2, is excluded: the residual is
L^(p−m)⊗P with P∈Pic⁰(A), hence a translate of L^(p−m).
The polarization isogeny is surjective on algebraically closed points,
even if inseparable, and powers L^n for n≥2 are globally generated
([Genestier–Ngô, Theorem2.2.3](https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~ngo/Shimura.pdf)).
For p=2 this positive range is empty.

Both boundaries m=p−1 are genuinely attained:

- Reduced: on a product of ordinary elliptic curves E_i take
  D_i=∑_(0≠h∈`E_i[p](k)`)[h]. The exterior product of their divisor
  sections is pointwise Dirac for T=⊠O_Ei(D_i)≡L^(p−1).
- Nonreduced: for relative Frobenius of a supersingular elliptic E,
  K has algebra k[x]/(x^p). The divisor section of
  T=O_E((p−1)[0]) restricts to a unit times x^(p−1), a nonzero socle.
  Also F^*O_(E^(p))([0])=O_E(p[0]). Products give every dimension,
  with socle generator∏x_i^(p−1); the product Frobenius kernel is
  maximal isotropic for the compatible product polarizations.

The local dualizing functional for k[[y]]→k[[x]], y=x^p, can be
ψ(∑_(i=0)^(p−1)a_i(y)x^i)=a_(p−1)(y). Its pairing is anti-diagonal
with units, and on the fiber ψ(x^(p−1))=1 but ψ(1)=0.
Any dualizing generator wψ, w a unit, is still nonzero on that socle.
By contrast algebra trace on k[x]/(x^p) is identically zero.
The invariant-form generator need not equal this coefficient functional
in an arbitrarily chosen parameter.

## 4. Nonproportional positivity and the exact cohomological test

The method only needs generation of the actual residual R at0.
If its numerical class is a sum of two ample integral classes, R is
globally generated. Indeed write R=N_1⊗N_2 with both ample, absorbing
any Pic⁰ twist. For a fixed point x, each set of twists P for which
N_i⊗P has a section nonzero at x is nonempty open: translate an
effective divisor away from x, and use ample vanishing/base change.
The two relevant opens U_1 and−U_2 meet in irreducible Pic⁰(A).
Multiply the two nonvanishing sections. Thus the obstruction also
applies if p[L]−[T]=2[N]+[Q] with N ample and Q nef.

The evaluation sequence gives the exact test

    R generated at0
      ⇔ H¹(A,I_0⊗R)→H¹(A,R) is injective.                  (2)

When H¹(R)=0 this is equivalent to H¹(I_0⊗R)=0.
IT_0 for I_0⊗R is sufficient. For ample R it is equivalent to global
generation of R: all Pic⁰ twists are translates, and all their higher
cohomology vanishes. IT_0 for R alone is NOT sufficient; a principal
theta line through0 is an ample IT_0 counterexample.

## 5. The constrained residual Θ+q^*D is not automatically generated

Let q:A→C be a quotient by an abelian subvariety and
T=L^(p−1)⊗q^*O_C(−D). For a permitted M_0 the actual residual is

    R=L⊗q^*O_C(D)⊗P_0,   P_0=v^*M_0⊗L^(-p)∈Pic⁰(A).        (3)

The twist P_0 is constrained by theta(M_0) containing0.
Even ample D downstairs has only nef pullback and is not a second
ample factor upstairs. For A=E_1×E_2 with product principal L and
q=pr_1, the ample IT_0 bundle L⊗q^*O(D) still has an
O_E2([0]) factor: all sections vanish on E_1×{0}.

Put K′=ker q, L′=L⊗P_0 and G=q_*L′. Fiberwise ampleness makes G
locally free, with higher direct images zero and
G_0=H⁰(K′,L′|_K′). Evaluation of (3) factors as

    H⁰(C,G⊗O_C(D)) → H⁰(K′,L′|_K′)⊗O_C(D)_0 → R_0.        (4)

The exact condition is that this composite be nonzero. If L′|_K′
has a base point at0, (3) fails for EVERY D. If both L′|_K′ and
G⊗O_C(D) are generated at0, the composite is nonzero.

Equivalently, for Z={Q∈Pic⁰(B)(k):theta(M⊗Q) contains0}, the method
excludes socle-Dirac whenever there EXISTS Q∈Z for which

    0∉Bs(v^*(M⊗Q)⊗T^(-1)).

Existence of a socle-Dirac section forces the opposite base-point
condition for EVERY Q∈Z. This is only a necessary condition for
Dirac existence, not a converse. Duality does not remove the constrained
twist, change that quantifier, or settle the unresolved evaluation problem.
