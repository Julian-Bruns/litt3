# Eight- and nine-label traces have no scalar in the whole moment field

Version1,27 September2026. In the standard four common-moment trace
system, with both endpoint label multisets of cardinality eight or
both of cardinality nine, there is no nonzero scalar in
K0=F_(5^14). Labels may repeat and fivefold characteristic-five
blocks are retained.

This strengthens the F25 scalar exclusion to the entire moment
field. In particular, the uniform-root pole24 exception cannot use
a scalar in K0. It does not exclude all scalars in F_(5^56), nor
all actual pole24 or pole27 comparisons.
