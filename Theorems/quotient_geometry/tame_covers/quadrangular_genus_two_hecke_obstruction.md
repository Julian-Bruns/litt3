# Two small uniform covers force elliptic quotients or real multiplication

Version3. The monodromy counts are published; explicit representatives
and the integral correspondence identity have exact finite verification.

Let C be a smooth projective genus-two curve over an algebraically closed
field of characteristic different from2 and3, with Aut(C)=C2,
generated by its hyperelliptic involution. Consider an ACTUAL tame map
f:C->P1 with precisely four branch values and a complete uniform profile.

1. Profile (2^3,2^3,3^2,3^2), degree6, is impossible. The only monodromy
   not already contradicted by Aut(C)=C2 would give an actual degree-two
   map from C to a genus-one curve, hence an additional involution.

2. Profile (2^6,2^6,2^6,3^4), degree12, forces an INTEGRAL endomorphism
   T of J(C), symmetric for its canonical Rosati involution, satisfying

                          T^2=[5].

   T is induced by an actual self-span
   C<-W->C whose TWO maps are finite etale of degree5 and whose common
   source W has genus6. This span has an actual common quotient P1.
   The construction uses an off-diagonal component of C x_(P1) C.

In particular both profiles are excluded for the backup C_alpha. Its
Aut=C2, geometrically simple ordinary Jacobian, and geometric endomorphism
field with real subfield Q(sqrt21) are recorded exact inputs. A symmetric
square root of [5] would instead embed Q(sqrt5) in that real subfield.

The proof counts paths in the icosahedron as integral correspondences:
A^2=5 Delta+2A+2B and B=iota A. The finite checker verifies all9 and39
distinct monodromy classes and their quotient and correspondence data.
