# A proper Cartier image with surjective evaluation has no first-break-one inertia

Version1,3 October2026. Whole scoped review [PASS](../../Research/audits/RANK_THREE_SCALAR_TARGET_AND_TAME_IDENTITY_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md).

Let Z be a smooth curve in characteristicFIVE and let a finite group act on it. Let K⊂B_Z be an invariant integral Cartier subbundle of rank at mostTHREE, with everywhere surjective Frobenius adjunction F_Z*K→ω_Z. Then no point stabilizer contains an automorphism σ with
\[
\sigma(t)=t+a t^2+O(t^3),\qquad a\ne0.
\]
Equivalently every nontrivial first wild ramification group has first lower break at leastTWO. This requires neither a finite constant coefficient module of dimensionFOUR nor equality of K with a scalar-line tensor product.

In particular it applies to the actual descended rankTHREE scalar carrier of [étale line descent](etale_frobenius_line_descent_prime_to_characteristic.md), and to its actual cyclic canonical-discrepancy trivializer. It deletes first-break-one target inertia; it does not bound subsequent ramification depth or imply the full rankFOUR étale carrier exclusion.
