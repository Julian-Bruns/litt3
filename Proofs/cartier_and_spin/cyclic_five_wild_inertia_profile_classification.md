# Proof: the tame character and an integral area equation

Version1. [Statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/cyclic_five_wild_inertia_profile_classification.md). No endpoint map is replaced. Only the accepted actual two-branch ledger and its even effective degree are used.

The wild subgroup C5 has one positive lower break j. Thus Δ=5h−1+4j. Conjugation by a generator of the tame inertia quotient multiplies its first wild coefficient by the j-th power of a primitive h-th root. That coefficient lies in a one-dimensional F5-vector space. Hence h divides4j. Write4j=hL, with L a positive integer.

Set g=gcd(h,m), h=gH and m=gM, so gcd(H,M)=1. The tame integer m is prime to five. The accepted relation n=lcm(5h,m) gives n=5gHM. Its area equation becomes
\[
4j-1=(5H+1)/M,\qquad H(gLM-5)=M+1.
\]
Put c=gL. Then cH=4j and H(cM−5)=M+1. In particular cM>5. Since H≥1,
\[
M(c-1)\le6.
\]
For c=1, integrality of j forces H≥4, and the last area equation gives M≤7 with M>5. M=6 would give H=7, contrary to4|cH; M=7 gives H=4. For c≥2 the displayed bound forces c≤7 and M≤6/(c−1).

The resulting elementary integer check is fully recorded below. Require H=(M+1)/(cM−5) integral, gcd(H,M)=1 and4|cH. These conditions leave exactly:

|c|M|H|j=cH/4|
|---|---|---|---|
|1|7|4|1|
|2|3|4|2|
|4|2|1|1|
|6|1|2|3|

For each row choose g dividing c, put h=gH and m=gM, and impose m≥2,5∤hm and n=5gHM even. This produces the nine profiles in the statement, with Δ=5h−1+4j. This small finite arithmetic is a consequence of the infinite-tail bound, not a bounded search on possible common covers.

No assertion that these local data are globally realizable is made. The tangent character restriction is necessary even when some tame elements centralize C5; faithfulness of the tame action on C5 was not assumed.
