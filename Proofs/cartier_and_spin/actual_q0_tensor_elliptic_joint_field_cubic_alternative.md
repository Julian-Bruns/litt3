The audited invariant-field and tangent-surface clarifications are explicit below. This proof uses actual field composites, never a presumed simultaneous Galois closure. Both original maps stay on S; C0 is an auxiliary analysis of their joint image only.

The source S maps finitely to the smooth normalization C0 of its image in X×X. Every projection C0→X is separating, since its differential pulls back to the nonzero differential of the original étale map. In the tower S→C0→X the local different exponents are nonnegative and their sum, with the ramification multiplier, is zero. Thus C0→X is genuinely étale. Hurwitz gives the same degree d0 for both maps and g(C0)=8d0+1. Its field is exactly the composite of its two X-fields.

Apply the [actual cubic-root construction](actual_q0_tensor_exact_root_self_span.md) to C0. Its root C0′ has genus30d0+1 and degree-d0 étale maps to X′. Put q_i=q0(x_i), P_i=P(x_i). The root is generated by the two X′ fields: it is already k(C0)(w1), and the specified w2 lies in that field. Its two root forms are proportional,
\[
w_1^8\,dx_1/y_1^2=c\,w_2^8\,dx_2/y_2^2,
\qquad c\in k^\times.
\]
The actual elliptic joint field D contains x1,x2,w1,w2. The ratio dx1/dx2 also belongs to k(D), since both xi are separating rational functions on D. Rearranging gives
\[
(y_1/y_2)^2=c^{-1}(w_1/w_2)^8(dx_1/dx_2)\in k(D),
\qquad (y_1/y_2)^3=P_1/P_2\in k(D).

Their quotient shows y1/y2∈k(D). Hence C0′=D(y1), and y1³=P1 gives degree one or three. In the latter case the cubic Kummer extension is Galois. Its generator scales y1 and y2 by the same ζ and fixes each xi,wi.

The specified w-deck action scales BOTH w1 and w2 by ζ and fixes BOTH original y-fields. One may see the common phase either directly from the construction, or from w2=(η/(δθ2))²/q2⁵: scaling w1 by ζ scales η by ζ² and therefore w2 by ζ. It is nontrivial there because it scales y1. This is an ACTUAL symmetry over the jointly embedded x-functions, not a pointwise automorphism over either X.

In index ONE, C0′=D. Put z=w2/w1 and B0field=k(x1,x2,z). The common w-deck fixes B0field. Moreover k(C0′)=B0field(w1), since w2=zw1 and D is the elliptic joint field. It remains to prove the stronger genus bound in the index-one alternative. In this case C0′ itself is the normalization of the integral image curve B⊂E×E. The two projections have degree r=3d0. Let A,B0∈E be the two q0-root points. The zero divisor of either root η is ten times the reduced pullback of {A,B0}, so proportionality gives equality of these reduced pullback divisors. The projections to E are unramified along them: X′→E is unramified at the q0-root points, and C0′→X′ is étale. Thus their common divisor has 2r distinct points.

Consequently B meets the four boundary curves {A,B0}×E and E×{A,B0} only at the FOUR points of {A,B0}×{A,B0}. Write n_ij for the number of normalization branches at each of these four points. Every branch is smooth, since either projection has local order one. The row sums and column sums are r, and Σn_ij=2r. The local delta invariant is at least binom(n_ij,2): every two distinct smooth branches have intersection multiplicity at least one, irrespective of tangency. Hence
\[
\sum\delta\ge\sum_{i,j}\binom{n_{ij}}2
\ge \frac{r^2}{2}-r.
\]
The second inequality follows from Σn_ij²≥(Σn_ij)²/4=r².

On the abelian surface E×E, the canonical class is trivial. If F1,F2 are the two fiber classes, H=F1+F2 is ample, H²=2 and B·H=2r. The Hodge index theorem gives B²≤2r², and adjunction gives arithmetic genus p_a(B)=1+B²/2≤r²+1. Subtracting the actual normalization delta invariants yields
\[
g(C_0')\le \frac{r^2}{2}+r+1.
\]
Together with g(C0′)=30d0+1 and r=3d0, this implies d0≥6. For degrees at most five only the actual cubic alternative remains.

At degree SIX, r=18 and g(C0′)=181=r²/2+r+1. Every inequality just used is therefore equality. Hodge equality gives B numerically equivalent to18H. Equality in the local delta bounds forces every pair of branches to have intersection multiplicity ONE; the nine branches at each marked pair are therefore smooth with pairwise distinct tangents. No delta invariant remains for any other singularity. This proves the exact configuration, without proving that it is impossible.
