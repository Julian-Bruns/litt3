# A wild orthogonal row has no simple full-rank Gram defect without a target one-block

Version1,3 October2026. Whole independent review [PASS](../../../Research/audits/RANK_THREE_SOCLE_AND_ORTHOGONAL_ROW_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). The source need not be a constant module.

Let A=k[[t]] in odd characteristic p, and let the cyclic group C_p of orderp act on A, on a free rank-n module F and on a free module U, all compatibly. Suppose F has a perfect equivariant symmetric pairing valued in a rankONE module. Assume the special fiber F₀ has NO Jordan block of sizeONE. Let U→F be equivariant and have generic Gram rank n. Quotient U by the saturated generic Gram radical to obtain a free rank-n module Q with a regular generically nondegenerate induced form.

Then the determinant of that induced form has order different fromONE. This rules out only a simple full-rank Gram defect; it asserts neither generic row independence nor a similar constraint for smaller generic Gram ranks.
