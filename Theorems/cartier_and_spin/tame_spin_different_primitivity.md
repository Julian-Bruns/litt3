# The canonical different is primitive in every selected tame ramified spin branch

Version1,2 October2026. New computation-free consequence of the actual different extraction, tame degree bound and selected endpoint exclusions; passed [independent whole-implication review](../../Research/audits/TAME_SPIN_DIFFERENT_PRIMITIVITY_AUDIT_2026_10_02.md).

In the setup of [the tame ramified degree classification](tame_ramified_spin_degree_classification.md), put E=k(Γ)(a), where a is the canonical different section in a rational Γ frame. Then E=k(T): the canonical different generator is PRIMITIVE of degree κ overΓ on BOTH selected endpoints.

More generally the accepted actual extraction gives an étale upper map T→E of odd degree e dividing the number r of ramified points over one branch image. In the tame setting e≤42, the actual faithful quotient C=E/G gives a complete uniform tame map Y→C of degreee, totally ramified over the distinguished point P. Every e>1 is excluded by the selected endpoint arithmetic and automorphisms.

This does not construct an étale X atlas of the common quotient stack. The higher-degree spectral and étale spin-image branches of the common-cover problem remain open.
