# Norm and occupancy obstructions at seven-point support

Version 1, 24 September 2026. Use the fixed X, f and thirteen-point R_X
of [admissible reconstruction](admissible_line_reconstruction.md).
For every reduced six-subset D of R_X,
\[
V_D=H^0(X,\omega_X(R_X-3D)),\qquad V_D\cap C^{-1}(V_D)=0.
\]
All1716 cases are certified geometrically by exact matrix ranks.

Let h:S->X be an actual connected finite etale cover of degree n and
q a primitive with dq=h*df and
\[
\operatorname{div}q=3E-5G-10H,\quad H=h^*O,\quad
E\le h^*R_X\text{ reduced},\quad\deg E=5n,\quad\deg G=n,
\quad E\cap G=\varnothing.
\]
If h(E) has exactly seven points, then Norm(q) is a fifth power and
\(h_*E=5D_E\) with integral effective D_E. This conclusion allows ALL
monodromy groups. On the primitive quotient k(X)(q), its degree m is
seven or at least ten; with prime-to-five normal closure it is seven
or at least eleven and is prime to five.

Under the actual nontrivial order-five line hypothesis, support arithmetic
also gives trivial norm for that line,
\(2(D_E-nO)\sim0\), and \(h_*G-nO\sim D_E-nO\).
At actual degree seven, only the six supports O plus two complete cubic
fibers remain. This last assertion must not be applied to a primitive
quotient whose descended line may have acquired prime-to-five torsion.

No cover or embedded line is constructed or excluded in every degree.
[Proof and exact scope](../../Proofs/cartier_and_spin/seven_point_norm_cartier.md).

The later [single finite-point theorem](finite_missed_point_cartier.md)
extends norm vanishing beyond seven-point support. The
[degree-seven trace obstruction](admissible_degree_seven_exclusion.md)
excludes all six actual-degree-seven cases left by this reduction.
