# Endpoint content is simple and occurs on at most one cubic sheet

Version1,29 September2026. Use the original root-nine ratio chart and
regular scale quadratic e(mu) on X. At every geometric ratio carrying
a polynomial-square cubic norm, its fixed affine content is a reduced
divisor supported over the three roots of t. Above each root of t it
contains at most one of the three points of X.

In the endpoint coordinates of
[the local scale theorem](root9_content_etale_scale.md), B is nonzero
at every content point. The residual e(mu) has fixed order exactly
one there; its coefficient of the first local power is a quadratic
polynomial in mu with nonzero leading coefficient. On either of the
other sheets the value at the endpoint is a nonzero affine-linear
polynomial in mu, possibly constant.

The complete two-sheet incidences have1110 retained marked points
for each of the three endpoints. All are excluded for arbitrary
geometric scale by exact polynomial unit identities. All three
B=C1=0 exceptional incidences, each of length36, are separately
excluded. These are full geometric computations with actual ratio
coordinates, not finite-field point searches. The marked-branch and
off-endpoint contents were excluded previously.

The possible single-sheet content curves remain open. Their existence
as necessary coefficient loci does not construct an actual cover.
