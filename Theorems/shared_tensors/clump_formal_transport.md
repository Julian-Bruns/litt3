# Infinite formal transport and algebraization of its line invariants

Version2,24September2026. Retain an ACTUAL finite étale span
$X\xleftarrow f Z\xrightarrow gY$ of smooth projective connected
curves of genus at least two. Let S be a finite connected component
of its physical fiber graph. Its edges identify the completed local
discs at their endpoint vertices. Fix a vertex y and let
$\Lambda_y\subset\operatorname{Aut}_k k[[t]]$ be the group of
formal return maps along all loops based at y.

## Finiteness of whole germs is equivalent to a core

The following are equivalent:

1. The span has a core, with the two given embeddings of function fields.
2. The group $\Lambda_y$ is finite.
3. Only finitely many distinct formal path germs start at y.

This holds in every characteristic. In particular a coreless clump
has INFINITE formal transport, even when its tangent transport is
finite. A bound on the number of distinct whole germs would prove a
core, rather than merely construct or explain the clump.

## Its invariant meromorphic line sections nevertheless algebraize

Continue over $\overline{\mathbf F}_p$ with a coreless span and a
clump, in ANY characteristic and genera. Let L be ANY common
line-bundle triple.
Restriction identifies its global common rational sections with its
compatible meromorphic sections on the formal completions at S.
These spaces are nonzero precisely when
\[
L\simeq\mathcal O(cS)\quad\text{as a common line, for some }c\in\mathbf Z.
\tag{1}
\]
In that case they are one-dimensional, generated by the canonical
section of $\mathcal O(cS)$. Thus completion introduces no new common
line sections, although its return group is infinite.

For canonical tensors, the entire formal meromorphic invariant algebra
is exactly $k[s,s^{-1}]$, with the SAME primitive global tensor s.
Its regular part is $k[s]$. In particular formal completion does not
lower the primitive canonical weight.

For a characteristic-five genus-two clump of size at least two with
no shared regular one-form, every compatible formal meromorphic projective
connection along S is the restriction of a global regular common
projective connection. The sets of such connections are affine spaces
under the common weight-two tensors. On either selected pair this is
a singleton except in the branch $R=4,e=1$, where it is an affine line.
The line-section assertion has no shared-one-form or prime-to-p
torsion hypothesis: inseparable roots are detected by an actual local
parameter, while prime-to-p roots use finite étale clump descent.
This is an algebraization statement for these PARTICULAR formal
invariants, not for arbitrary representations, bundles or group germs.

The theorem supplies a core criterion and formal line-section
algebraization at an existing clump. It does not produce a clump or
exclude an arbitrary common-cover span.
