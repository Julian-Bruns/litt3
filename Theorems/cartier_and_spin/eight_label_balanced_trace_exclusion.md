# Two complete root blocks at both endpoints are impossible

Version1, 27 September2026. In the standard four-trace endpoint system,
let EACH endpoint be the union of two complete four-root blocks, whose
two phases are arbitrary elements of mu29 and may coincide. There is
no solution with nonzero geometric scalar epsilon and common moments
X,Y in F_(5^14).

This is an all-scalar endpoint exclusion. The old traces first force
epsilon into F_(5^14); exact norm elimination then covers all2,175
phase-pair representatives. All2,118 possible surviving scalar roots
fail a fourth trace. A separate field representation and explicit
Euclidean verifier checks the complete root lists and every case.

It excludes this sector of actual pole24 comparisons once endpoint
balance and the direct traces are supplied. Other eight-label shapes,
and the unmarked common-cover problem, are not decided here.
