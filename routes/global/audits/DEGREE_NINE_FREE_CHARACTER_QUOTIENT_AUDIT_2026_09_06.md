## Primary source and auxiliary groups

Checked directly [SGA 1, Expose XIII, Corollaire 2.12 and proof,
printed pp. 290–291 (PDF pp. 306–307)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0206203#page=306).
It explicitly realizes every finite prime-to-p group generated by surface
generators satisfying the surface relation. Taking no punctures, three
a-generators and trivial b-generators gives exactly the required connected
etale covers of any genus-at-least-three curve. Expose X, Corollaire 3.10
(PDF p. 233) also states the projective prime-to-p presentation directly.
Thus no claim about arbitrary prime-to-p-generated groups of p-divisible
order is being smuggled into the argument.

For n=6m+3, m>=0, Q=2^n is 2 or 3 modulo 5 and 8 modulo 9.
Hence |PSL2(Q)|=Q(Q-1)(Q+1) is prime to 5 and divisible by 9.
For even Q its center is trivial; its nonsplit norm-one torus is cyclic
of order Q+1. These groups are nonabelian simple for Q>=8, have strictly
increasing orders, and therefore supply the required arbitrarily large
nonisomorphic groups. The displayed three generators work: squaring
permutes F_Q^*, so the diagonal conjugates of U(1) include every upper
root element; interchange supplies all lower root elements. Elementary
matrices generate SL2(Q).

## Equivariant endpoint realization and actual order-nine lift

Taking conjugates under Gal(K/k(r^9)) still gives a kernel embedded in a
product of C16's. The full zero fiber is invariant under this larger group.
At zero each conjugate has the same tame degree-16 completion, and off
zero all these extensions are locally split over K. Consequently the exact
F20 infinity field, different 55, and local indices 16 and 20 remain intact.
Over k(r), the quotient by the abelian 2-kernel remains A9, preserving the
absence of an F20 quotient. The original sign choice x=-r, y=r is retained.
The singleton's corelessness and both etale-leg arguments therefore apply.

Normality of A over k(x^9) and B over k(y^9) makes N normal over M^s:
an embedding restricts to a power of s on M and preserves each endpoint.
At O, x is a uniformizer and s(x)=lambda*x, so the seed quotient has tame
index 9. The completion of N/M has index 16. Thus the local Galois group
has order 144, is tame cyclic, and its unique order-nine subgroup maps
isomorphically onto the seed C9. This produces one simultaneous lift of
order exactly nine. Its restrictions to both endpoints have order nine
because their rational coordinates already do.

The Riemann–Hurwitz bound is valid even if some candidate fixed points
have only stabilizer three: summing e_P-1 is at most eight times the
number of points above zero and infinity. Its stated value 63q/80 is correct.

## Connectedness, simultaneous extension, and both quotient legs

The intersection with a Galois closure is a Galois intermediate field of
the simple-group cover, so the group-order bound gives disjointness.
After base change to N, nonisomorphic simple groups have no common
nontrivial quotient, giving Gal(U/N)=Hx times Hy. The two invariant-field
calculations and finite orbit polynomials then give A1 intersect B1=k.

A useful nonbreaking elaboration of the simultaneous lift is the field
presentation

    U = k(Vx) tensor_{k(X0)} N tensor_{k(Y0)} k(Vy).

The proved disjointness says this tensor algebra is a field. Acting by
identity, sigma, identity on its three factors is well-defined since sigma
fixes both base fields. It has order nine and commutes with both deck
groups. This addresses explicitly the fact that the auxiliary covers have
different bases; pairwise prescriptions alone need not generally suffice.

Every nonidentity power of delta has a nonidentity projection hx^j on Vx
and hy^j on Vy. Deck transformations of these etale torsors have no fixed
geometric points. Freeness therefore holds on both endpoints and on the
same connected source Z1. For each leg the quotient square is Cartesian:
Z1 is the pullback of Z2 along the endpoint C9-torsor. Finite etaleness
descends along that torsor. Connectedness and positive canonical degree
also survive the free quotients. Endpoint invariant fields are subfields
of A1 and B1, so their intersection in U^delta remains k.

## Canonical ring and Tango descent

In every nonnegative degree m, dividing a shared tensor upstairs by
alpha^m gives a function in A1 intersect B1. Thus the common ring is
exactly k[alpha], and equivariant etale pullback identifies the downstairs
common ring with its invariants. The character has exact order nine,
giving k[alpha^9] with primitive weight nine.

The horizontal connection defined by alpha is regular because all zero
orders are 15, and is unchanged when alpha is scaled by lambda. Its
canonical linearization descends along the free etale quotients to the
canonical line bundles. Regularity, vanishing p-curvature, and compatibility
descend faithfully flatly. Cartier vanishing is etale-local; upstairs every
horizontal form is a fifth-power multiple of the exact alpha. Hence the
descended connections are regular Tango structures. The ramified-quotient
warning is consistent: the displayed orders 7 and 39 are not divisible by 5.

The result disproves the stated primitive-weight-one implication. It does
not assert ordinary endpoints or settle the common-cover conjecture.
