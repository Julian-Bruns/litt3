## From divisors to an eight-dimensional integer kernel

The cubic automorphism gamma(x,y)=(x,[11]y) satisfies pi^4(R_j)=gamma(R_j).
Each complete cubic fibre is linearly equivalent to3O. Consequently
Q(pi)kappa=0 for Q=T^8+T^4+1. The homomorphism
\[
\mathbf Z[T]/Q\longrightarrow\Gamma,\qquad H\longmapsto H(pi)\kappa
\]
is onto by definition. If a degree-zero divisor supported on Z union{O}
has coefficients n_j at R_j, its class is represented by
\[
h=(a_0,\ldots,a_3,b_0,\ldots,b_3),\quad
a_i=n_i-n_{i+8},\quad b_i=n_{i+4}-n_{i+8}.
\]
It is principal exactly when h belongs to the actual kernel I. It is
the divisor of a function in k(x) exactly when h=0: these and only
these divisors are integral combinations of the four complete cubic
fibres minus3O. This remains valid with signed multiplicities.

The exact point-order calculation started with the candidate integer
\[
348845748669743914287634301453156614528955729501515119047979680559316323399234377575817619.
\]
This integer was checked directly to annihilate kappa, then reduced
prime by prime in the actual Jacobian, giving the exact squarefree
order m in the statement. Its discovery through a Weil-module
calculation is not needed to justify the direct group check. At each
prime, the last nonzero multiplication proves the remaining factor
occurs in the actual point order.

## One actual relation and matching indices

Since Gamma is generated by the Frobenius translates of kappa, its
exponent is also m. For each ell dividing m its primary component is
an F_ell-vector space generated as an F_ell[pi]-module by kappa_ell.
For each of the three large primes, the point order alone gives
primary rank at least one. Actual group arithmetic gives rank at
least four at3 and at least two at13, as follows.

Multiply kappa by the candidate annihilator with its ell-primary
factor removed. Directly check that the resulting eta is nonzero
and killed by ell. Compute its Frobenius translates. At3, match two
halves of81 group elements each: there are exactly81 relations among
all3^8 coefficient tuples, so their image has3^4 elements. At13,
the13+13 half tables have only the zero relation, giving13^2 distinct
elements. Every collision tuple is retained. These lower bounds need
neither a Weil polynomial nor an assumed minimal Frobenius polynomial.
Coefficient Frobenius on the function-field ideals is calibrated
against the actual next point(alpha^25,rho^25). Hence
\[
|\Gamma|\ge3^4\,13^2\,(16907427139)(22797284179)
 (18385365305430963493897)=351m.
\]

Now let G have ascending coefficient row
\[
(-652173,104828,-133365,8040,4980,-494720,-65556,30252).
\]
An independent Horner calculation in the actual Jacobian checks
G(pi)kappa=0. Thus the ideal(G) in Z[T]/Q lies in the true relation
kernel. The determinant of the eight rows T^jG modulo Q is, in
absolute value, exactly351m. Therefore
\[
|\Gamma|\le|\mathbf Z[T]/(Q,G)|=351m.
\]
The upper and lower bounds agree, proving I=(G) and the exact group
order. The small-primary ranks are consequently exactly4 and2, and
the three remaining ranks are exactly one.

The congruences in the statement are another presentation of this
same lattice. Each of the eight generator rows satisfies every
congruence, whose combined independent index is351m. Equality of
indices proves equality of lattices. The exact integer verifier also
checks equality with the LLL basis used for the two minimizations
below. Thus no new enumeration is needed when replacing the original
Weil-based discovery by this shorter proof. The LLL row basis and
rational inverse are retained in the exact receipt.

The three-primary multiplicity explains why the order of kappa alone
would not describe Gamma. The actual abstract group has invariant
factors3,3,39,m. No prime from the larger Weil module is retained unless
it occurs on the actual marked point.

There is also a complete multiplicative description of the supported
units. Let D_G=sum_(j=0)^7 G_j R_j-(sum G_j)O, and choose a rational
function u with divisor D_G. It exists by the direct G check. Let u_j
be its coefficient-Frobenius conjugate with divisor pi^jD_G,0<=j<8.
Then every rational function whose divisor is supported on Z union{O}
has the unique expression, modulo a nonzero constant,
\[
\prod_{i=0}^3(x-alpha^{25^i})^{c_i}\prod_{j=0}^7u_j^{d_j},
\qquad c_i,d_j\in\mathbf Z.
\]
Indeed the images of the latter eight divisors are the integral basis
T^jG of I. After subtracting their uniquely determined combination,
the remaining divisor has h=0, hence is a unique combination of the
four complete-fibre divisors. Properness makes a function with zero
divisor constant. The construction asserts exact existence and gives
all relations and generators modulo constants; it does not expand u
as a large rational polynomial or claim any two-map realization.

## Exact least pole of a nonpolynomial supported function

For fixed h=(a,b), all rootwise triples are(a_i+c_i,b_i+c_i,c_i).
Their least nonnegative choice is c_i=max(0,-a_i,-b_i). Thus define
\[
N(h)=\sum_{i=0}^3\bigl(a_i+b_i+3\max(0,-a_i,-b_i)\bigr).
\]
If h is a nonzero element of I, this effective divisor minus N(h)O
is principal and gives a nonpolynomial function. Conversely, removing
complete cubic fibres from any such function gives this least
representative. Therefore the desired minimum is exactly
min_(0!=h in I)N(h), and its minimizing vectors give all minimizing
effective divisors.

This is computed by exact rational lattice enumeration. Starting with
the minimum of N on signed LLL basis rows gives U=1,617,894. If N(h)<=U,
then ||h||_2^2<=2U^2: h is a sum of N(h) coefficient vertices, each
of Euclidean norm at most sqrt(2). Exact Gram--Schmidt and rational
Fincke--Pohst enumeration in that closed ball visit31,184 nonzero
vectors. Their least gauge is1,617,894, attained at twelve vectors.
The radius is not shrunk during enumeration, so all minimizers are
retained. No floating-point exclusion or finite-field function search
enters this calculation.

The twelve vectors form one orbit of multiplication by T modulo Q.
Their effective triples are uniquely determined. The first is displayed
in the theorem; its class is also checked directly by Horner evaluation
of H(pi)kappa in the actual Jacobian, independently of the primary
matrices and lattice assembly. Principality suffices for geometric
existence without writing a million-degree function. The same divisor
determines its function up to a constant on the proper connected curve.

## Finite poles: a simple second gauge

For a signed representative put S=sum n_j. Its total pole degree is
delta=max(sum max(n_j,0),sum max(-n_j,0)); the O coefficient is -S.
Let A,B,C denote respectively the sums of the largest, middle and
smallest entries of the four triples(0,a_i,b_i), and put
u=A-B>=0,v=B-C>=0. The exact least degree of a signed representative is
\[
\delta(h)=\left\lceil\frac{\max(2u+v,u+2v)}3\right\rceil
         =\left\lceil\frac{\max(N(h),N(-h))}3\right\rceil.
\]
For completeness, an optimum has -2<=S<=2. If S>=3, subtracting one
from c_i in a triple with a positive entry drops the positive sum by
at least one, raises the negative sum by at most two, and keeps S>=0;
this strictly improves the degree. The negative case is symmetric.
For each allowed S congruent A+B+C modulo3, start c_i at the negative
largest entry and allocate D=(S+2A-B-C)/3 increments. Across the four
triples there are u increments of cost one, v of cost two, and then
unbounded cost three. This gives the exact convex minimum. Taking S
of the appropriate sign attains the displayed ceiling, including ties.

A degree-delta divisor gives h/delta as a difference of convex
combinations of the thirteen coefficient vertices
\[
0,\quad e_i,\quad e_{i+4},\quad-e_i-e_{i+4}\quad(0<=i<4).
\]
Their squared diameter is five. Hence ||h||_2^2<=5delta^2. The same
rational enumeration with this certified radius visits7,172 nonzero
vectors and gives minimum788,052, with twelve minimizing relation
vectors. This is the least degree OUTSIDE k(x), not the least degree
of all nonconstant supported functions; x-alpha_i already has degree3.
