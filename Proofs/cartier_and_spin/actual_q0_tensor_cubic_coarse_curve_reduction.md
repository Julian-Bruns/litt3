# Proof: the cubic alternative has a low-genus simultaneous cube-map quotient

Version2,3 October2026. Pending independent review. Version2 adds the actual étale converse. No computation is used.

The actual y-deck A scales both yi and fixes xi,wi; the actual w-deck C scales both wi and fixes xi,yi. They commute and generate a genuine C3×C3 action on C0′. Their common invariant field is B, since the C-invariants are precisely C0 and B=C0/A. Both t=y2/y1 and z=w2/w1 are invariant. Let B0=k(x1,x2,t,z). The two X′ fields generate C0′, so C0′=B0(y1,w1). Its degree over B0 is at most NINE, while its degree over B is exactly NINE. Since B0⊂B, this proves B0=B. All displayed cube and differential identities follow on these actual fields.

The differential ratio dx2/dx1 belongs to the joint x-field Bx, since xi are separating functions there. From t²/z⁸∈k(Bx) and z³∈k(Bx), one obtains (t/z)²∈k(Bx). Its cube also belongs to k(Bx), so t/z∈k(Bx). Consequently B=Bx(z) has degree ONE or THREE over Bx. The index divides either map degree d, proving the coprime conclusion.

In the actual tower C0→B→P¹_xi, the total ramification indices are only ONE or THREE because C0→Xi is étale. Dividing by the index of the tame cubic C0→B gives the same restriction for B→P¹; its branch values are necessarily among the eleven branch values of Xi. Thus the q0 roots are unramified, and their inverse image consists of2d distinct points. A is free on the original common q0-zero divisor because yi is a unit there, so this is exactly the SAME set for both maps on B.

Now D=B(w1) is the actual cyclic cubic cover w1³=q0(x1). It branches at those2d points. At infinity a ramified point of B→P¹ has index THREE and q0(x1) has valuation−6; the root is unramified there. An unramified infinity point has valuation−2 and contributes one branch point. There are no other branch points. Thus the branch count is2d+u and tame Hurwitz gives g(D)=3g(B)−2+2d+u. Using the second map gives the same formula, so u is common. The infinity multiplicities sum to d and are ONE or THREE, giving u≡d modulo THREE and0≤u≤d.

The actual joint field D has two elliptic projections of degree d, unramified at their common marked divisor of degree2d. Its image in E×E therefore has four marked boundary singularities with row and column sums d. The same Hodge-index and delta calculation as in the [joint-field cubic alternative](actual_q0_tensor_elliptic_joint_field_cubic_alternative.md) gives g(D)≤d²/2+d+1. Combining the two formulas gives the stated bound on g(B). Separately, Hurwitz for C0→B, with g(C0)=8d+1, gives branch count8d−3g(B)+3.

For d=FOUR or FIVE, each individual map B→P¹ has monodromy generated by three-cycles. Its sign quotient is unramified over P¹, hence trivial. The transitive subgroup is A4 for degree FOUR and A5 for degree FIVE. For degree FOUR this follows directly because a transitive proper subgroup of A4 has no three-cycles. For degree FIVE, a transitive subgroup of A5 has order divisible by FIVE. Its Sylow-five count is ONE or SIX. In the first case it lies in the dihedral normalizer of order TEN and has no three-cycle. In the second its order is at least THIRTY; the only proper possibility has index TWO, excluded by simplicity of A5. This is an individual monodromy argument, not a presumed Galois completion of both maps together. The genus bounds and u-refinements now follow by substitution and integral rounding; in particular d=5,u≥2 gives g(B)≤2, rather than the weaker bound obtained by omitting u.

Both original X-maps remain on C0 and on the original source above it. The quotient B is used to formulate an exact simultaneous cube-map problem; it is not an étale replacement source for either X or Y.

For the converse, form the connected tame cubic field k(C0)=k(B)(y1), y1³=P(x1). The noncube assumption ensures degree THREE. The cube identity gives the second embedded X-field using y2=t y1. At a finite P-root, a point of B has x_i ramification index e=1 or3 and radicand valuation e. At infinity its radicand valuation is−10e, with the same alternatives. If e=1 the normalization C0→B has tame index THREE; if e=3 it is unramified. In both cases the total index of C0→P¹ is THREE, exactly that of Xi→P¹. Away from the eleven branch values, B→P¹ and its cubic root are both unramified. Thus every relative index of C0→Xi is ONE. All maps are separating because xi are separating functions on B. Over algebraically closed residue fields an index-one extension of these completed smooth-curve local fields is trivial; hence both maps are genuinely étale. Their degrees are d by the degree tower.

The differential identity gives θ2/θ1=c⁻¹z⁻⁸, while q2/q1=z³. Therefore τ2/τ1=c⁻³. Since B=k(x1,x2), the two X-fields together contain B and y1 and generate the entire constructed source. For d=FOUR or FIVE the forward alternative necessarily has this joint-x property and cannot have cubic index ONE, so the construction and reduction have exactly the claimed two-X scope. Nothing in this converse supplies any morphism to the original Y.
