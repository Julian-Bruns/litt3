# The backup has only its hyperelliptic tame uniform atlas

Version1,21September2026. Let $k=\overline{\mathbf F}_5$,
$\alpha^3+\alpha+1=0$, and
\[
Y:\quad v^2=u(u-1)(u-2)(u-3)(u-\alpha).
\]
Suppose $a:Y\to\mathbf P^1_k$ is finite separable, every branch fiber
is complete and uniform, and every ramification index is prime to five.
Then $\deg a=2$, and a is the hyperelliptic quotient up to an
automorphism of the target.

Equivalently, the only representable finite etale atlas from Y to a
tame genus-zero effective orbifold is its hyperelliptic atlas to
$\mathbf P^1(2,2,2,2,2,2)$.

The theorem is over the entire algebraic closure. It does not impose
a field bound or require the fixed genus-nine endpoint. It consolidates
the twenty-three Y-only exclusions in the complete tame-profile table.
It does not exclude a characteristic-zero quotient map whose reduction
is inseparable, nonuniform or wildly ramified.
