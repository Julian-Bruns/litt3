# Actual root-nine degree140 covers have no fixed content

Version1,29 September2026. In the root-nine degree140 family of the
[two actual profiles](admissible_degree_ten_two_profiles.md), no
allowed geometric ratio and nonzero scale with positive fixed affine
coefficient content can give an everywhere-etale degree-ten cover.

The exclusion covers all three endpoint-content curves, their
coefficient and denominator boundaries, and the previously excluded
marked cubic branch. It does not impose a bound on parameter field
degrees or assume that the discriminant-half class is trivial.

Consequently every remaining actual cover in this root-nine chart has
ZERO fixed content. Together with the stronger
[constant-family content theorem](degree140_positive_content_exclusion.md),
both remaining degree140 actual-cover families may be treated on their
primitive parts.

This result uses actual local etale splitting to select one rational
scale on each content curve. It does **not** exclude all other
polynomial-square norm points on those curves, nor all content-free
actual covers, the other degree-ten profiles or the original unmarked
common-cover problem.
