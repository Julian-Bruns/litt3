# Small odd parts of the genus-two cover group are excluded

Version1,21September2026. Let $X/\mathbf F_{25}$ be the fixed
genus-nine endpoint. Let $Y/\mathbf F_{25^b}$ be ANY smooth proper
genus-two curve, with $19\nmid b$. In an actual finite etale span
$X\leftarrow Z\to Y$, let $G$ be the Galois-closure group of the
original $Y$-leg. Its odd part satisfies
\[
\boxed{|G|_{\rm odd}\notin\{1,3,5,7,9\}.}
\tag{1}
\]
Thus $|G|_{\rm odd}\ge11$. There is no bound or restriction on the
two-primary part. Neither original map need be Galois, and no
normality of a Sylow subgroup is assumed.

This applies to BOTH selected partners: take $b=3$ for the backup,
and $b=r$ for the high-prime-degree partner. It is an actual
same-source exclusion, not a character-degree condition. It does
not settle general mixed-prime groups or either common-cover problem.

The intermediate arithmetic assertion is useful separately. Let
$C\to Y$ be an etale cover of odd degree $d\le9$, and suppose that
the COVER has a model over $\mathbf F_{25^c}$ with $19\nmid c$.
Then no geometric cover of $C$ with two-group Galois closure has
the fixed $J(X)$ as a factor. The arithmetic condition on the
cover is part of this assertion; it is proved for the Sylow
quotients needed in (1), not assumed for arbitrary covers.
