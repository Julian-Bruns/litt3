# Proof: the two-triple polar budget removes all higher split genera

Version1,3 October2026. Frozen pending independent review; see the [statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/actual_q0_tensor_degree_six_split_square_class_reduction.md).

The [disjoint-profile theorem](actual_q0_tensor_degree_six_two_triple_disjoint_reduction.md) gives B=Bx,g(B)≤FIVE and div z=TWO D1−TWO D2. In this theorem's SPLIT case t²=z on B, hence div t=D1−D2 and deg t=TWO. Both divisors consist of TWO distinct points, so this separable degree-TWO map is unramified over ZERO and infinity. Center and scale as before so q(xi)=xi²+ONE. The exact q relation becomes
\[
F_+F_-=t^6-1,\qquad F_\pm=x_2\pm t^3x_1.
\]
At D1, t³x1 and x2 are regular. Elsewhere away from D2 both functions are regular. At each point of D2 both x2 and t³x1 have poles of order at most THREE, so each F± lies in L(THREE D2). Their product has exact pole order SIX there, so BOTH factors have exact order THREE at BOTH points of D2. No extra finite pole is present.

For genus g≥ONE write the degree-TWO model Y²=Φ(t), with squarefree polynomial Φ of degree TWO g+TWO. This even degree is required by the TWO ordinary infinity points. Every function in L(THREE D2) has the form
\[
A(t)+YB(t),\quad\deg A\le3,\quad\deg B\le2-g.
\]
Indeed a function regular away from infinity is in the affine hyperelliptic ring k[t,Y], and Y has order g+ONE at each infinity point. The pole bound on BOTH points prevents cancellation of a larger even/odd term on just one sheet from enlarging the space.

If g≥THREE, F± are both in k(t). Then x2=(F++F−)/TWO and x1=(F+−F−)/(TWO t³) lie in k(t), contradicting B=Bx and [B:k(t)]=TWO. Thus g≤TWO. The rational genus-ZERO case is already below the bound and is not assigned a positive-genus model.

At g=TWO write F+=A1+b1Y,F−=A2+b2Y with deg Ai≤THREE and constant bi. Both constants are nonzero: if one vanished, the rational product identity forces the other factor into k(t) also, giving the same joint-field contradiction. Comparing the odd product coefficient gives A1b2+A2b1=ZERO. Choose a with a²=−b1/b2, and replace A=A1/a,Y by b1Y/a. This gives EXACTLY
\[
F_+=a(A+Y),\qquad F_-=a^{-1}(A-Y),\qquad
Y^2=A^2-t^6+1.
\]
The zero-even-part boundary A=ZERO is retained; it does not make xi constant in general. Write A3,A0 for the leading cubic coefficient and constant coefficient of A, allowing A3=ZERO. Since ZERO and infinity are unramified for t, the opens are
\[
A_3^2-1\ne0,\qquad A_0^2+1\ne0,
\]
and the degree-SIX polynomial A²−t6+ONE has nonzero discriminant. Both original maps have the required triple poles at BOTH points of their own divisors precisely only if the following necessary leading-product opens hold:
\[
4A_3^2+(a-a^{-1})^2\ne0,\qquad
4A_0^2+(a+a^{-1})^2\ne0.
\]
The first is the product of the TWO x2 leading coefficients at infinity, up to a nonzero constant. The second is minus the product of the TWO numerators of t³x1 at ZERO, up to a nonzero constant. These retain allowed finite behavior of the OTHER map at each divisor. No extra symmetry, original Y descent or full fixed-P cube condition is asserted.

This confines the split class to genera at most TWO and gives the exact genus-TWO necessary normal form. It leaves the nonsplit class and all low-genus split models open, with both original maps retained on their SAME source.
