# Remaining projective monodromy of rank-two finite coefficients

Version2,26 September2026. Let K be the actual rank-two degree-one
bundle on the genus-nine curve X over the algebraic closure of F5.
Let R be a rank-two bundle trivialized by a connected finite etale
cover of X. If Hom(R,K) is nonzero, then R is irreducible as a finite
coefficient, the map is generically an isomorphism, and its projective
monodromy is conjugate to one of
\[
\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbf F_{5^a}),\qquad
\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbf F_{5^a})\quad(a\ge2).
\]
In particular, cyclic, dihedral, p-semi-elementary, tetrahedral,
octahedral and q=5 projective images are excluded. This uses the
established all-geometric Pic0-twist vanishing for Sym^m K,1<=m<=5,
the new [mixed-tensor theorem](mixed_frobenius_tensor_twist_vanishing.md),
and the finite subgroup classification in
[Faber, Theorems B/C and Remark2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1112.1999).

The S4 and q=5 groups are now excluded by a character-valued
Frobenius-dual pairing. No all-twist Sym^6 vanishing is needed or
asserted. The successful mixed-tensor argument retains a special
subbundle and its fiber norms inside Sym18 K, whose full section
space has dimension19.
More generally chi(Sym^m K)=(m+1)(m-16)/2 is positive for m>=17.
Thus the q+1-point orbit products for q>=25 cannot be excluded by
blanket symmetric-power vanishing; their special structure would have
to be used.

The groups with q>=25 remain, as do unrestricted higher-rank coefficients.
This classification of a necessary rank-two source is neither a
finite-monodromy realization nor a common-cover decision.
