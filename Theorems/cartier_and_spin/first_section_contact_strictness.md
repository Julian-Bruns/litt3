# The first section contact has a uniform lower bound

Version5,2 October2026. [Focused whole-argument review](../../Research/audits/FIRST_SECTION_CONTACT_BOUND_AUDIT_2026_10_02.md) PASS, including the geometric net certificate, BOTH former equality configurations, and the simpler contact-seven strengthening using stability of the actual J. The independent earlier proofs are retained.

For the fixed genus-nine curve $X$, EVERY plane generated generically
by a two-dimensional constant subspace of its three global exact forms
has saturation of degree at most FOUR in its Cartier bundle $B_X$.
This statement covers all geometric constant subspaces, not merely
subspaces defined over the displayed finite field.

Retain BOTH actual finite etale maps
$X\xleftarrow hS\xrightarrow gY$ from the SAME smooth connected
projective source, where $Y$ has genus two and the degrees are $n,8n$.
Let $I\subset U\subset B_X$ be the original three-section image and
its rank-three saturation, and let $J\subset B_Y$ be the COMPLETE
first actual trace of $I$. Suppose $\deg J=1$. Put
\[
L=h^*U\cap g^*J,\qquad
t=\operatorname{length}(h^*U/L).
\]
Then the original-source contact bound is
\[
\boxed{t\ge7n.}
\]
In particular $t>33n/5$: both former equality configurations are completely
excluded in every covering degree. No corelessness, prescribed
radical-orbit rank or simultaneous Galois closure is required for this
conditional degree-one result.

The primary bound uses the fixed net calculation to exclude
degree-zero quotients when $t<9n$. Then J is stable of slope one
quarter, and its actual source hyperplane intersection has degree
at most $6n$, giving $t\ge7n$. Stability is retained only on the
explicit range $t<9n$; it is not asserted for larger contacts.

Every constant original ONE-form line has saturation degree at most
TWO. Consequently a degree-zero vector-bundle quotient $Q$ of the
actual $J$ has rank at most two and imposes
\[
\operatorname{rank}Q=1\Longrightarrow t\ge9n,
\qquad\operatorname{rank}Q=2\Longrightarrow t\ge11n.
\]
These inequalities concern the original unsaturated trace and require
no additional saturation or shared-object hypothesis.

If the actual Frobenius-kernel intersections have a common descended
line of degree one, its horizontal closure is a degree-zero plane
$W\subset J$ contained in every source hyperplane. This branch forces
$t\ge9n$. In particular the COMMON-line equality sector at
$t=33n/5$ is excluded, without assuming $W$ saturated in $B_Y$.

The NONCOMMON triangle equality configuration is also excluded:
three independent pair-intersection lines split the rank-three
Frobenius evaluation kernel, forcing the canonical line to be a
common horizontal summand of all actual source bundles. Frobenius
descent would make its degree two divisible by five, impossible.

The separate [contact-seven theorem](contact_seven_equality_exclusion.md)
now excludes equality for MAIN, while giving an actual bounded replacement
for BACKUP. Larger contacts, the degree-one trace itself, and the unmarked
common-cover problem remain open. Stability on the original curve alone
still does not imply stability after every etale pullback.
