# The order-five rank-three power lattice is automatic

Version1,23September2026. Let h:S->X be an actual finite etale
map of degree n to the fixed genus-nine X, let C=S^(1), and
let P=h^(1)*P_X in B_S. Suppose A in P is a saturated degree-zero
line with A^5=O_C and F_S^*A=O_S. Suppose its adjunction has
divisor2 tilde D, where D is reduced of degree8n and lies in
h^(1)*R_X^(1). Put E=A^perp and J=A+E(-D), as an image sheaf.

Let H_A be the inverse image of A in F_{S*}O_S. Multiplication
in this actual algebra induces an isomorphism onto its image
\[
\operatorname{Sym}^3H_A/O_C\!\cdot1^3
\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ J\subset B_S.
\]
In particular J has degree zero, determinant A, and F_S^*J=O_S^3.
In a global frame its canonical connection is
\[
\nabla e_1=\nu e_1,\quad
\nabla e_2=2\nu e_2-2\zeta e_1,\quad
\nabla e_3=3\nu e_3-3\zeta e_2,
\]
where nu is the normalized connection form of F_S^*A and zeta
is its adjunction differential. Evaluation is (zeta,0,0); its
image is omega_S(-2 tilde D), its kernel is trivial of rank two,
and the determinant connection form is nu.

These are automatic individual properties, not an additional
compatibility between two such lines. No such A exists when n=1.
On any cover its adjunction differential cannot be pulled back
from a regular differential on X. General existence on etale
covers and uniqueness of two character classes remain open.
