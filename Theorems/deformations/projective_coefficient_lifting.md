# A projective coefficient gives a lift or a singleton clump

Version1,20 September2026. Let $k=\overline{\mathbf F}_5$ and let
$X\xleftarrow f Z\xrightarrow gY$ be an actual finite étale span
of smooth projective connected hyperbolic curves, with $g(Y)=2$.
Degrees are arbitrary. Let $K/\mathbf Q_5$ be any finite extension.

Suppose the endpoints have oriented orthogonal rank-three
$K$-coefficient $F$-isocrystals, with a specified orientation-preserving
isometry on the SAME source. Equivalently these are compatible
$\mathrm{PGL}_2$ coefficients. Suppose the Newton polygon is
nonconstant, with normalized generic slopes $(-\delta,0,\delta)$,
where $0<\delta\le1$. Relative twists, coefficient components and
the actual Frobenius arrows are part of the data.

Then the following alternative holds:
\[
\boxed{\text{the ORIGINAL span lifts over a complete mixed-characteristic DVR}
\quad\text{or}\quad f^*B_X=g^*[y]\text{ for some }y\in Y(k).}
\]
Here $B_X$ is effective, necessarily reduced, and has degree
$g(X)-1$. The second alternative is an actual clump consisting of
one whole $g$-fiber. It does not merely assert a zero of an unrelated
projective connection on $Y$.

In particular, excluding singleton clumps forces the full lift of
both ORIGINAL maps. Both selected candidate pairs exclude singleton
clumps. Hence the main pair has NO such common projective coefficient;
for the backup such a coefficient forces the still-open liftable branch.

The central obstruction is retained. There is a common integral
projective crystalline connection model with positive nonhorizontal
Harder--Narasimhan section. Its actual branch divisors satisfy
\[
f^*B_X=g^*B_Y,\qquad \deg B_Y\in\{0,1\},\qquad
w_2(P_C)=\deg B_C\pmod2.
\]
Only after the branch vanishes do these models give honest rank-two
coefficient $F$-isocrystals on the original endpoints. A finite étale
refinement of the SOURCE handles their remaining comparison torsors;
the established refinement theorem descends the resulting lift.

No global spin lift, simultaneous Galois source, polarized abelian
realization or compatible endpoint Dieudonné models are assumed.
Coefficient extension alone is not claimed to kill geometric $w_2$.
The theorem does not construct a common coefficient from a bare span.

Returned Pro proof, integrated with the existing linear lifting and
source-refinement theorems. Focused author checks of central parity,
projective uniqueness and source descent; no independent audit claimed.
