# A pole-gap criterion for paired polynomial coefficients

Version1, 25 September2026. Let a valued field contain the roots of
\[
F(W)=(W^m+f)(W^m+a_1W^{m-1}+\cdots+a_m)+c.
\]
Suppose exactly m roots have pole orders p_1>=...>=p_m>s, and the
other m roots have pole order at most s. Put h=pole(f), P=sum p_i.
For 1<=j<m,
\[
\operatorname{pole}(a_j)\le P+js-h.
\]
If p_j>p_{j+1}, then necessarily
\[
p_{j+1}+\cdots+p_m\ge h-js.
\]
These assertions hold in arbitrary characteristic; they need neither
coefficient computations nor a monodromy assumption. A zero coefficient
has pole order minus infinity.

For the actual trace-zero degree-ten primitive at infinity, m=5,
s=1,h=7,p_i=2+m_i, with m_i the five unselected G-multiplicities.
Exactly four positive m_i are impossible. This excludes every
degree-two polynomial denominator in the established degree-ten list.
It does not exclude the remaining three-positive-multiplicity profiles.
