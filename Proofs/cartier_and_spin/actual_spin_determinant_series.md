The raw image P is generated by W⊗O_T. Its determinant is A=ωT⊗L²(-q*D), and determinants of generating pairs generate A integrally. Thus their span C is basepoint free.

Let c be the scalar cocycle of the projective deck action on L. Functoriality of first jets gives the same cocycle on P and cocycle c² on its determinant A and its invariant section span C. If s=dimC, the line Aˢ⊗(detC)* has genuine deck linearization: the two scalar cocycles cancel. It descends through the actual étale torsor q. Its degree downstairs is
\[
\frac{s\deg A}{8d}=s\left(\frac94-\deg D\right).
\]
Integrality, since degD is an integer, forces4|s. This uses actual descent and no presumed finite simultaneous coefficient.

Fix one actual infinity section u₀. Its zero divisor is reduced because the X map is étale. The determinant map W→C taking u to the divided Wronskian Wr(u₀,u) is linear. Its kernel consists of u with d(u/u₀)=0. Over the perfect constant field in characteristic five, such a ratio is a fifth power in k(T). Yet every pole of u/u₀ has order at most one, since u is a regular L section and div(u₀) is reduced. Fifth-power pole orders are multiples of five. There are therefore no poles, and projectivity makes the ratio constant. The kernel is exactly ku₀. Consequently s≥r−1≥7. Combined with4|s, this gives s≥8.
