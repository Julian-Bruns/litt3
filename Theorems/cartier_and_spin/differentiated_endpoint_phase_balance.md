# Differentiated traces balance endpoint phases without a collision search

Version1, 27 September2026. Consider the fixed genus-nine curve and
ACTUAL same-source finite etale comparison normal form with t nonconstant.
Suppose the
norms of t and t^-1 are polynomials in the respective x-coordinates
of degree m>=1. Thus t has3m simple zeros and poles, with m_alpha
branches on each cubic sheet over each root alpha of A, and
sum m_alpha=m at each endpoint.

If at each endpoint at least one root has m_alpha<=2, all three
sheetwise phase multiplicities at every root agree modulo five.
In particular this conclusion holds whenever m<=11.

If m<=9, those multiplicities agree as INTEGERS. Hence all four
direct common-source trace equations apply, with m-label endpoints,
without a cubic-descent assumption. This includes poles24 and27
once their supported norms are known to be invariant.

The proof uses the constant and linear coefficients of traces of
regular one-forms. The two phase moments occupy the two complementary
degree14 Frobenius orbits in mu29. It replaces the earlier bounded
phase-sum collision searches, which fail at length eight.

This theorem assumes the stated actual norm and comparison data. It
does not assert the endpoint equations are inconsistent in these
higher degrees, or extract a shared tensor from an unmarked cover.
