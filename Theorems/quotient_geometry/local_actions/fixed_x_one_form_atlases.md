# Every one-form orbifold quotient of the fixed curve is trivial

Version2,22September2026. Let X be the fixed genus-nine curve over
k=F5bar. Its Cartier-bijective six-space separates first jets at
EVERY geometric point. Consequently there is no nonconstant rational
function A on X with dA regular and pole divisor at most10P for a
single point P.

More strongly, let q:X->S be a representable finite etale atlas of an
effective smooth proper orbifold. If a nonzero rational one-form on
its coarse curve pulls back regularly to X, then q has degree one:
S is X itself. In particular there is NO such rational coarse quotient.

The proof reduces the rational case to degree160, one wild D10 branch
of lower break3, and sixteen points above it. Cubic invariance then
makes its primitive explicit. A local Galois invariant and a certified
rank-three polynomial matrix exclude the remaining case over the
ENTIRE algebraic closure. The certificate is an explicit identity
among ten minors, verified independently without a Groebner basis.
The primitive-group classification is not needed for this exclusion.

This theorem controls actual quotients carrying a one-form. It does
not produce a shared one-form on either original common-cover problem,
both of which remain open.
