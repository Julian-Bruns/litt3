# A shared curve deformation forces a clump, and genus two forces dormancy

Version5,2026-09-09. Independent medium audit PASS for the main mechanism
and version2 scope, /root/audit_joint_tangent_clump,2026-09-09. The NEW
intrinsic identification also passed a FRESH bounded medium audit,

Let k=bar(F5), and let X<-f-Z-g->Y be an ACTUAL coreless finite etale
span of smooth projective connected curves of genus at least two. Set

    T_joint=f*H^1(X,T_X) intersect g*H^1(Y,T_Y) in H^1(Z,T_Z).

Use the [pointed extension conventions](../Definitions/Def_marked_curve_deformations.md).
Then:

1. T_joint!=0 implies that the span has a nonempty clump. In particular,
   a no-clump span is infinitesimally rigid: T_joint=0.

2. If g(Y)=2 and T_joint!=0, the two endpoints have regular dormant
   projective connections with equal ACTUAL pullbacks to Z. For each
   nonzero shared tangent class, its two extension bundles E_i are
   semistable but not normalized strongly semistable. Their common first
   Frobenius-destabilization index n is finite and >=1. At that stage
   the maximal lines N_i have

       deg N_i=(5^n+1)(g(C_i)-1),
       N_i²=omega_i^(5^n+1).

   Their second fundamental maps for the canonical Frobenius connections
   are everywhere isomorphisms; these are the matched dormant opers.
   Projection N_i→omega_i^(5^n) has a nonempty REDUCED zero divisor Delta_i,
   with equal pullbacks. In particular |Delta_Y|=5^n-1 and
   |Delta_X|=(5^n-1)(g(X)-1); there is no additional multiplicity parameter.

3. More precisely, if T_joint!=0, the constructed dormant connection is
   the INTRINSIC r_s of the primitive common tensor s. The distinguished
   horizontal section projects to q_i in E_i^(n)/N_i; the second
   fundamental isomorphism gives q_i² a shared tensor of weight5^n-1.
   Its scalar expression u² yields r=u''/u=r_s.
   Since q_i has simple zeros, the primitive generator has precisely

       (weight, zero order) = ((5^n-1)/2,1) or (5^n-1,2).

4. Consequently EVERY span with a shared active nilpotent connection
   and a genus-two endpoint has T_joint=0. This includes ALL85 active
   choices on the current Y, even when both canonical doubles split:
   r_s is then the ACTIVE midpoint, not either dormant companion.
   A possibly nonrigid span must instead have a positive generator
   whose intrinsic connection is regular and dormant.

5. Put P=5^n. Two distinct branches with equal tangent of an actual
   joint image preserving the pointed extensions have intersection order

       I=0 mod P away from Delta_X times Delta_Y;
       I=2 mod P over Delta_X times Delta_Y.

   In particular off-clump tangencies have order at least P, not merely5.
   The same assertion applies to reduced unions of such preserving
   images and hence to the relation generated by the original span.
   Contact2 at the clump is NOT excluded, so this does not itself force
   a core by the existing contact-budget theorem.
