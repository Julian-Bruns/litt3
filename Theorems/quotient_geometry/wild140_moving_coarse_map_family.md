# A moving degree140 coarse-map family with common weak completions

Version1, 3 October2026. Scoped coarse-map theorem; [independent whole review PASS](../../Research/audits/WILD140_MOVING_COARSE_MAP_FAMILY_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md). This does NOT construct a common source, a canonical spin carrier, or either original endpoint map.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic five and t∈k∖F₅. Put
\[
Y_t:y^2=z(z-1)(z-2)(z-3)(z-t),\quad P=(t,0),
\quad r=t+1,\quad K=r^4-1,
\quad q=4r^3/K,\quad s=1/(r^5K).
\]
The change of coordinates
\[
x=1/(z-t),\qquad w=x-4/r,\qquad y_0=yx^3/\sqrt K
\]
gives y₀²=w⁵+qw⁴+s, with P at infinity. Choose c≠0, v²=2s and C²=c²q³/s, and define F=w(y₀+v) and G by the four-pair formula in [the common-completion refinement](../cartier_and_spin/wild140_exceptional_common_completion_refinement.md).

If q⁵≠4s, then all FOUR pairs define separating maps
\[
\beta=G^7/F^{20}:Y_t\longrightarrow\mathbf P^1
\]
of degree140 with the following complete ramification ledger: seven poles of index20 and different23, twenty zeros of tame index7, and one further ramification point P of index2. There is no other ramification. The seven weak completed extensions over the SAME pole coordinate β have one common scalar invariant, hence are mutually isomorphic over that coordinate.

The condition q⁵=4s cuts out only the degree-at-most20 polynomial
\[
r^{20}-(r^4-1)^4=0.
\]
In particular the construction applies to any parameter of degree greater than20 over F₅. It also applies to the exceptional BACKUP model q=[15],s=[9], since q⁵=[68]≠[21]=4s in F₁₂₅.

Thus a positive-dimensional family survives the coarse ramification and same-completion gates. Tame Hurwitz isolation cannot be imported to delete this family. Global Galois-carrier existence with its actual order16 spin root and BOTH original endpoint maps remains outside the conclusion.
