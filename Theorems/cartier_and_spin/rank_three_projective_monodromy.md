# Remaining rank-three monodromy is of linear or unitary type

Version1,26 September2026. Let R be an irreducible rank-three finite
etale coefficient on the fixed genus-nine X. If Hom(R,K) is nonzero,
its projective monodromy is one of the linear or unitary families
in items1--4 of the finite-PGL3 classification, with defining parameter
q=5^a and a>=2. This includes the stated index-three normal extensions
and does not assume that the actual scalar lift is defined over Fq.

In the notation of that classification, the remaining families are
PSL3(q),PGL3(q),PSU3(q^2),PGU3(q^2), and their listed index-three
normal overgroups. Here q is the exponent in the Hermitian involution;
the unitary matrices are over F_(q^2). This convention is explicit
because some sources instead denote these unitary groups by PSU3(q).

In particular, all exceptional projective types A6,A6.2,A7 are
excluded, including wild finite monodromy. The q=5 linear and unitary
types are excluded as well. Every tame irreducible rank-three source
was already excluded by the mixed Frobenius tensor.

This is a necessary monodromy classification, not a construction or
an exclusion of the remaining Lie-type groups. It gives neither a
rank bound for arbitrary finite coefficients nor their extraction
from an actual common cover. Both original unmarked problems remain open.
