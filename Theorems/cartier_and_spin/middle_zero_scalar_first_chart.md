# The first nonscalar middle chart with both scalar entries zero is empty

Version1,26 September2026. Use the actual30-by15 matched tensor
H(b) and its ten source coordinates from
[middle correction rigidity](middle_character_correction_rigidity.md).
For every geometric source with
\[
b_0=b_1=0,\qquad b_2\ne0,
\]
H(b) has rank15. All seven remaining source coordinates are arbitrary.
Equivalently, after b2=1 the rows of H generate the full free module
over F25[b3,...,b9]. No further localization or finite-field equation
is imposed.

Thus a nonzero matched middle kernel on b2!=0 requires
(b0,b1)!=0. This statement does not exclude arbitrary scalar
eigenvalues, the strata b2=0 with b3 or b4 nonzero, or the full
second-return problem. The two original common-cover questions remain
open.
