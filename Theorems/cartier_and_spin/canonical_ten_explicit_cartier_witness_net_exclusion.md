# The explicit global Cartier witness fails the original net incidence

Version1, 3 October2026. The new pencil calculation and its conditional
source interpretation passed [independent static review](../../Research/audits/OCT03_EXPLICIT_CARTIER_WITNESS_ACTUAL_NET_EXCLUSION_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md).
The earlier witness and descended-effectivity checks are separate
inputs. This excludes one embedded kernel under the hypotheses below;
it does not exclude the whole constant-first-jet family.

Let k=bar(F5), α³+α+1=0, A=1+4α, B=2+4α, and
Y:w²=A x⁵+B x⁴−1, with infinity P and η=dx/w. Put
F:Y→Y₁=Y^(1), B_Y=F_*O_Y/O_Y₁. Let K⊂B_Y be the **specific
embedded** saturated rank-three degree-one kernel of the
[global witness](genus_two_global_liftable_cartier_kernel_witness.md),
defined there by the cubic point and its differential closure. Put
Q=K^⊥. The relative target has equation W²=A⁵X⁵+B⁵X⁴−1.

The descended inverse Q^-1 is ineffective. Its complete degree-three
pencil |ω_Y₁ Q^-1| maps the seven simple zeros of the adjunction
F*Q→ω_Y to **seven pairwise distinct geometric points** of P¹,
using the underlying-point bijection of relative Frobenius. This
comparison uses the exact descended pencil, not a degree-three pencil
chosen from its degree.

Consequently this K cannot occur in either quotient branch of an
original source satisfying the following antecedents:

- BOTH actual finite étale maps q:T→Y and h:T→X lie on the SAME smooth
  connected projective T, with degrees 8d and d. Retain the original
  rank-four degree-one J⊂B_Y, its integral quotient J/K=O_Y₁, and the
  actual pulled original three-section net q₀,q₁,q₂ with its nonzero
  constant quotient row ℓ and ℓ(q₀)=0.
- In the branch ℓ(q₁)=0, retain actual étale G-Galois q of degree 8d,
  ℓ(q₂)≠0, and the exact fixed-X ledgers of the
  [unmarked net-incidence theorem](canonical_ten_unmarked_saturated_net_annihilator_incidence.md):
  primitive infinity orders 11,8,2; ten reduced finite cubic branches
  whose rank-two raw image contains the intrinsic primitive-order-four
  line; and saturation degree at most four for every geometric constant
  two-form plane. Saturation of K remains an explicit hypothesis.
- In the branch ℓ(q₁)≠0, retain the accepted original infinity-support
  antecedents putting the fixed wild hyperelliptic pair
  R₊^(1)+R₋^(1) in divσ=D_J. If automatic saturation is invoked, retain
  the native-carrier and Galois hypotheses of the
  [nonzero-quotient theorem](canonical_ten_rank_three_nonzero_quotient_saturation.md).

When applying the statement to an original endpoint model, require a
specified relative-Frobenius-compatible comparison with the displayed
Y,Y₁ that preserves P, the wild pair, and this embedded K and Q.
No such comparison is inferred from line degrees or a Mumford class.
The result applies in **any original coefficient-source dimension**
under the stated rank and source hypotheses.

The fixed witness remains a valid liftable global Cartier bundle.
Other embeddings with the same abstract line class, unsaturated
zero-q₁ kernels, other constant-first-jet kernels and the nonconstant
jet strata are not excluded here. The unmarked common-cover problem
remains unsolved.
