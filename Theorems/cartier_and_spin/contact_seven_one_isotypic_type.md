# Contact-seven equality has one isotypic line type

Version1,2 October2026. Computation-free theorem; [independent whole-branch audit](../../Research/audits/CONTACT_SEVEN_TWO_TYPES_AUDIT_2026_10_02.md) PASS. The unmarked common-cover problem remains unsolved.

Retain two actual finite étale maps $X\xleftarrow hS\xrightarrow gY$ from the SAME smooth connected projective source, of degrees $n,8n$, for the selected MAIN pair. Let $I\simeq O_X^3\subset U\subset B_X$ be the original three-section image and its rank-three saturation of degree thirteen. Let $J\subset B_Y$ be the actual first trace of $I$. Assume $\deg J=1$ and
\[
\operatorname{length}(h^*U/(h^*U\cap g^*J))=7n.
\]
On an actual one-leg Galois closure $q:T\to Y$ of degree $8d$, the accepted split-line reduction gives $q^*J$ as four equal-degree lines, each of degree $2d$, with one or two isotypic types. Then there is only ONE type:
\[
q^*J\simeq A^{\oplus4},\qquad\deg A=2d.
\]
In particular $\operatorname{End}(J)$ is finite-étale-trivializable. This conclusion does not assume that the complete trace of the positive q0 saturation has degree one.

More generally, without the MAIN low-degree witness exclusion, the two-type case forces exactly EIGHT distinct actual quotient directions. Quotienting the SAME one-leg source by the kernel of their field stabilizer gives an actual common witness of degrees $(e,8e)$ to $(X,Y)$, for $e\in\{1,3\}$. MAIN excludes these actual degrees by effective quotient descent.

The reusable source bound behind the exclusion is: every rank-two bundle $V$ inside an actual degree-$d$ étale pullback of $U$ satisfies $\deg V\le81d/10$. Consequently on the actual isotypic double cover $Y'\to Y$, the saturations of its two rank-two degree-one blocks inside $B_{Y'}$ have degree at most TWO. At most one original cyclic defect point can have its fiber kernel in a single isotypic block. Exact fiber counts then exclude all larger quotient-direction orbits.

This theorem closes the two-type equality branch. The remaining one-type equality has now been excluded separately by [the full contact-seven theorem](contact_seven_equality_exclusion.md). Higher contact, positive q0 saturation degree greater than one, and the original unrestricted problem remain open.
