# Rational two-triple degree-six spans have one-parameter odd maps and a joint polynomial packet

Version1,3 October2026. New substantial scoped necessary reduction with an actual two-X converse, frozen pending independent review. No computation or exclusion is claimed.

Retain a jointly minimal actual τ-preserving degree-SIX self-span C0→Xi, its cubic-root index-THREE alternative and rational B equal to its joint x-field. Suppose each xi has TWO triple infinity poles, with the two degree-TWO reduced pole divisors disjoint. Each xi has A6 monodromy and exactly FOUR branch values: infinity with profile(THREE,THREE), and THREE finite fixed P-values with profile(THREE,ONE,ONE,ONE).

For each map separately, choose a rational source coordinate v with its poles at ZERO and infinity. It has the complete form
\[
f=\kappa\left(2v^3+a v^2+(a^2+2b)v
+\frac{2a^2b-b^2}{v}+\frac{ab^2}{v^2}
+\frac{3a^2b^2}{v^3}\right)+m,
\quad ab\kappa\ne0.
\]
After v=a t it is a ONE-parameter shape λ=b/a², plus a nonzero target affine scale and shift. Its critical polynomial is t³+t²+λt−λ; λ≠ZERO,TWO. These two individual source coordinates are not presumed equal.

In ONE common coordinate u whose infinity is ordinary for all data, there are exact polynomials Ai,Bi,Ci,H,R and nonzero constants κi such that
\[
x_i=A_i/B_i^3,\quad \deg A_i\le6,\quad \deg B_i=2,
\quad\deg C_i=3,\quad\deg H=12,\quad\deg R=51,
\]
\[
A_1^2+B_1^6=A_2^2+B_2^6=H,
\quad A_i'B_i-3A_iB_i'=C_i^2,
\]
\[
P_{\mathrm{hom}}(A_i,B_i^3)=\kappa_i R C_i^3.
\]
Here q0 has undergone its permitted common centering/scaling to x²+ONE and P is the corresponding fixed degree-TEN polynomial; P_hom(S,T)=T10P(S/T). The Bi are squarefree with disjoint root sets, the Ci are squarefree and avoid their own Bi, H is squarefree and avoids every Bi, and R is squarefree and avoids every Bi,Ci,H. All displayed degrees and original local conditions are retained. The original two maps imply k(x1,x2)=k(u).

Conversely, such exact polynomials with these genuine opens and k(x1,x2)=k(u) give TWO actual degree-SIX finite étale maps from the SAME connected cubic source y1³=P(x1) to the fixed X, with proportional original q0 tensors. This does NOT construct an original Y-leg.

The packet is an equivalent rational two-X problem, not a solution of it or of the original common-cover problem.
