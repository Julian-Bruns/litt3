# Marked curve and correspondence deformations

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p>0. A base A is a local
Artinian W(k)-algebra with residue field k. A marked deformation of a
smooth proper k-curve C is a smooth proper A-curve together with a specified
identification of its special fiber with C. Isomorphisms preserve this
identification. Write Def(C)(A) for their isomorphism classes.

For fixed finite etale maps f_i:Z→C_i, Def((f_i))(A) also includes lifts
of ALL these maps on the SAME marked source. The lifted maps are required
to be finite etale. No target or source embedding may be changed when
comparing diagrams. All curves in the deformation statements have genus
at least two, so marked deformations have no infinitesimal automorphisms.

A refinement is a fixed connected finite etale h:W→Z, replacing every
f_i by f_i h. The induced transformation of deformation functors lifts h
by invariance of finite etale covers under nilpotent thickenings.

A tangent class xi in H^1(C,T_C)=Ext^1(omega_C,O_C) is represented by
the pointed extension 0→O_C→E_xi→omega_C→0. Equal pullback classes
give an isomorphism preserving BOTH displayed maps; that isomorphism is
unique since Hom(omega_C,O_C)=0. This is a curve-deformation bundle,
not the dormant or nilpotent tangent-defect bundles V_r or E_r.

For a rank-two bundle E with det E=omega_C, normalized strong
semistability means that E tensor theta^(-1) is strongly semistable of
degree zero, where theta²=omega_C. It is independent of theta. Strong
semistability means semistability after every absolute Frobenius pullback.
The first destabilization index is the least n>=0 for which F^(n)*E is
not semistable, when such n exists. Frobenius here acts on bundles;
neither of the two finite etale maps is replaced by Frobenius.
