# A genuine acyclic genus-two Hermitian atlas

Over k=bar(F5), let H: X^6+Y^6+Z^6=0 and choose a primitive cube
root zeta. The projective group generated by

    diag(1,zeta,zeta^2),       (X,Y,Z) -> (Y,Z,X)

is G=C3 x C3, lies in PSU_3(5), and acts freely on H. Its quotient is
the smooth projective genus-two curve

    C: v^2=t^6+3.

In particular H->C is an actual finite etale map of degree9, and
C->[H/PSU_3(5)] is an actual finite etale atlas. The normalized bundle
V=E/O associated with this atlas satisfies H0(C,V)=0.

The entire regular rank-two dormant-oper scheme of C is reduced of
length5. Writing its potentials in the separating coordinate t as

    r=4t^4/(t^6+3)^2+(b0+b1 t+b2 t^2)/(t^6+3),

the points are (0,1,0), (0,-1,0), and (-c^2,0,c) for c^3=2.
The displayed quotient atlas induces (0,1,0). The120 branch-preserving
Mobius transformations act transitively on all five opers, so composition
with actual curve automorphisms realizes every one of them by an atlas.

Thus the acyclic case of the intrinsic atlas criterion is genuinely
nonempty for an actual cover, not merely a formal bundle test. In genus2
the criterion has8 variables and13 equations. Acyclicity alone cannot
be strengthened into an atlas exclusion.
