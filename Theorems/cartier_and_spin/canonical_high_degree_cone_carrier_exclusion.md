# Canonical degree18 and24 spin carriers are excluded at distinguished cones

Version1,3 October2026. Whole-cone argument passed [independent review](../../Research/audits/CANONICAL_HIGH_DEGREE_CONE_CARRIER_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md).

Retain BOTH actual finite étale endpoint maps from the SAME sourceT, its free Galois Y actionG, and an ACTUAL separating G-equivariant spin carrier φ:T→Γ with L=φ*M and L¹⁶≅ωT. Use the inherited genuine G-linearizations and put N=M¹⁶ωΓ^-1, with φ*N≅O_T(qP) identified through the canonical different section. Assume G acts faithfully on Γ, the degree κ is18 or24, the different is exactly qP with index TWO there, and the canonical different coefficient is primitive over Γ. The original infinity sections are pullbacks of sections of M on Γ, so the accepted primitive-even-polynomial obstruction applies. Assume the faithful quotient has signature(2,3,9) when κ=18, or(2,3,8) when κ=24, and N descends as ω_[Γ/G].

The distinguished Γ stabilizer cannot have order greater than ONE on EITHER selected endpoint. Thus ALL distinguished-cone fibers are excluded for these two faithful canonical carrier degrees. The carrier need not be the normalized projective spin image. Both original endpoint maps remain on T throughout.

Together with the accepted ordinary degree18 and degree24 exclusions, this excludes EVERY faithful canonical carrier of either degree on BOTH endpoints. No existence of a faithful canonical carrier is asserted here; it must be extracted inside the actual source field by a separately established reduction. Degree42 and the original common-cover problem remain open.
