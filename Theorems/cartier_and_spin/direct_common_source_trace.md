Version1, 27 September2026. In the actual Cartier-line comparison
normal form for two finite etale maps h1,h2:T->X from the SAME smooth
proper source, let t be nonconstant with3m simple zeros and poles.
Assume its endpoint phase multisets match as integer multisets on
the three cubic sheets over every root of A.

Then the four endpoint trace identities of the sextic calculation
hold with the resulting m-label multisets and the same moments
X=M2,Y=M6 in F_(5^14). Here Mj=sum l_c c^j, where l_c counts ACTUAL
common infinity points of T at t=c and c^29=1.

No cubic quotient, simultaneous Galois closure, tame ramification,
or restriction on m is needed. The traces used are exactly
Tr_(k(T)/k(t))(x_i)/3. At each common infinity x_i has pole three
and ord(t-c)>=2; regularity of (t-c)x_i dt bounds its trace by a
simple pole even in the wild case. The resulting residues and the
universal endpoint jets give all four equations.

Integer phase balance is a hypothesis, not a conclusion in arbitrary
degree. The theorem does not extract a tensor or Cartier line from
an unmarked common cover.
