# Marked generic BT comparisons extend at every level

Version1,20 September2026. Put $k=\overline{\mathbf F}_5$,
$R=k[[t]]$ and $K=k((t))$. Let $H/R$ be an actual height-two,
dimension-one BT1 with ordinary generic fiber, supersingular closed
fiber, simple Hasse zero and an isomorphic continuous Kodaira--Spencer
map. Let $A,B$ be actual BT$_N$ groups, $N\ge2$, with specified
identifications of their five-torsion with $H$.

Every supplied marked generic isomorphism extends uniquely:
\[
\operatorname{Isom}^{\rm marked}_R(A,B)
\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Isom}^{\rm marked}_K(A_K,B_K).
\tag{1}
\]
The integrality assertion needs no determinant hypothesis. A prescribed
determinant condition holding generically also holds for the extension.
This does not assert existence of the generic comparison.

The level-two argument is the returned Pro result. The same argument
on the last five-adic digit proves (1) at every level. It also applies
at an ordinary point. Consequently on a smooth proper curve carrying
an everywhere-versal $H$ with reduced nonordinary divisor, every
marked generic comparison of actual BT$_N$ extensions extends over
the whole curve.

In particular the ordinary invariant of the
[BT2 extension quotient](versal_bt2_extension_quotient.md) is injective:
\[
\Delta_C:Q_C\hookrightarrow\Gamma(C-S,\mathcal O_C).
\tag{2}
\]
There is no remaining subgroup supported only at the supersingular
points. Poles of a nonzero image in (2) are NOT excluded.
