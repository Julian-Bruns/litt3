## Fixed-seed group closure: useful invariant, explicit limitation

[Finite-seed closure theorem and primitive-cover tests](FINITE_SEED_MONODROMY_CLOSURE_AND_UNBOUNDED_PRIMITIVE_COVERS.md)
strengthens the earlier prime-support invariant. All path monodromies lie
in the class closed under subgroups, quotients, and extensions generated
by the two seed monodromies. Equivalently their composition factors lie
in the finite set of simple sections of subgroups of those seed groups.
Base-change component monodromy is a SECTION, not necessarily literally
a subgroup; the older prime-support and solvability consequences survive.

But even two-generated groups of derived length at most two and fixed
simple factors C2,C3 admit unbounded permutation-primitive etale covers
of EVERY characteristic-five base of genus >=2. Explicitly
F_(3^n)^+ semidirect C_(2^r), n=2^(r-2), gives degrees 3^n.
These are one-base examples, not two-leg correspondences. They rule out
using the closure class alone to bound covering degree.
