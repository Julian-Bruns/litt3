# Contact pencils in the positive rank-three double-zero case

Version1,23September2026. Use the actual double-zero case of
[the quadratic trace](positive_rank_three_quadratic_trace.md).
Put C=Y^(1), S=T^(1), pi=q^(1), D=pi^*P1, O_i=h_i^(1)*O,
A_S=pi^*A and deg h_i=d, so deg D=8d and omega_S=O(16O_i).
Write E_i' for the effective degree-d residual contacts. Both
original finite etale maps and their embeddings are retained.

There are canonical identities and exact sequences
\[
A^5=\omega_C(-2P1),\qquad
0\to O_C\to W:=V\otimes(A^2(P1))^{-1}\to A\to0,
\qquad \mathscr H=A\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^2W.
\]
The inverse image Pi of A^2(P1) in E is a degree-one Lagrangian
plane on C, with canonical second line A. Its evaluation is
surjective, with one simple second-fundamental zero at P_*:
\[
0\to\omega_Y^2(-P_*)\to F_Y^*\Pi\to\omega_Y\to0.
\]
The unsaturated quadratic trace gives actual maps
\[
\lambda_i=A_S^3(-2E_i')\to A_S^3,
\qquad O_S(2E_i')=O_S(2O_i)A_S^3.
\]
Also O_S(E_i')=pi^*A^4(P1)O_S(-7O_i) and
O_S(5E_i'+3D)=O_S(29O_i).

After a connected finite etale refinement rho:S'->S of 2-power
degree r, all rho^*E_i' are linearly equivalent and their canonical
sections have no common zero. They give a base-point-free pencil
of degree rd=(g(S')-1)/8. Separability of that pencil is NOT
asserted. The actual etale maps to both twisted endpoints persist.

There is a regular quadratic form
\[
\mathfrak q:\operatorname{Sym}^2E\to A^4(2P1)=\omega_CA^{-1}.
\]
In the double-zero basis it is 2y^2-tau*x*z. If S_i' is the
degree10d contact divisor of A_S and ell_i in P_i, then
\[
\operatorname{div}(\mathfrak q(\ell_i))
=S_i'+(h_i^*\Delta_X)^{(1)}.
\]
There is no additional common-fiber term. Actual norms give
O_X(2h_{i*}E_i')=O_X(2dO) and O_X(2h_{i*}D)=omega_X^d.

These are necessary conditions, not a two-map existence or
nonexistence result. A small normalized gonality alone is not
an exclusion. The new endpoint prerequisite is the existence of
the specified degree-one Lagrangian Pi.
