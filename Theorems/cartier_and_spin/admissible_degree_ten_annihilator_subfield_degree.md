# The annihilator is primitive in every nonzero degree-ten profile

Version2,1 October2026. Retain an ACTUAL nontrivial admissible
degree-ten source in ANY of the nine nonzero profiles of
[the nonzero profile theorem](admissible_degree_ten_nonzero_profiles.md).
Let $K=k(X)$ and let $u$ have the prescribed annihilator divisor
$\operatorname{div}u=2E-10H$.

Then
\[
[K(u):K]=10,\qquad k(S)=K(u).
\]
In particular $u$ cannot descend to any proper intermediate source.
Selected-sheet occupancy forces any residual intermediate degree to
divide five; the remaining quadratic intermediate case contradicts
the next-to-leading source and numerator coefficients. No exact
content pole, critical irreducibility or quadratic-incidence hypothesis
is needed.

This does not prove reciprocal-trace nonvanishing: the degree-ten
minimal polynomial may still have the relevant reciprocal coefficient
zero. No actual-source existence
or unmarked common-cover decision follows.
