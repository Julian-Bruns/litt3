# Proof: all-degree q0-tensor recognition with genus-one elliptic joint field

Version1,3 October2026. Pending independent review.

The [actual common-root bridge](actual_q0_tensor_exact_root_self_span.md) gives the SAME smooth projective S′ and two étale X′-maps pulling η′=w⁸θ back proportionally. Let E:w³=q0(x) and A,B,I be its two w-zero points and infinity. On X′ the zero support of η′ is the pullback of A+B under X′→E, with multiplicity TEN. Consequently the two actual maps S′→E pull A+B back to the SAME reduced divisor.

Let D be the smooth projective curve with field equal to the actual composite of those elliptic fields, and assume g(D)=1. The two maps π1,π2:D→E are separating of equal degree r, since both S′→E degrees are equal. After choosing origins each is a separable isogeny followed by a translation. Let H1,H2 be their translation kernels, of order r. Equality of the pulled A+B divisor descends to D by injectivity of divisor pullback along S′→D. It is a reduced divisor of degree2r, invariant under translations by BOTH kernels.

The finite translation subgroup H generated by H1,H2 acts freely on D. Its orbits inside that divisor have size |H|. Thus |H| divides2r, and |H|/r is ONE or TWO. If it is two, the divisor is one H-orbit, whose image under π1 is a two-point coset differing by nonzero two-torsion. But A−B is nonzero three-torsion: with I as origin, B=−A and div(x−α)=3A−3I. This contradiction forces |H|=r, hence H1=H2. Therefore the two elliptic subfields are equal.

The two elliptic maps then differ by an automorphism preserving A+B. For completeness, E has j=0 and Aut(E,I)=μ6 in characteristic5. Its order-three subgroup fixes A,B, so every u∈μ6 sends A to ±A. Writing a pair-preserving automorphism as t+u gives 2t=0 and t∈{0,2A,−2A}; thus t=0. It acts on x by identity or the affine reflection x↦−b−x for q0=x²+bx+c.

Both original x-functions belong to k(S), so this relation descends there. The reflection would preserve the branch set of S→P1, consisting of the ten P-roots and infinity, because the original two X-maps are étale. It fixes infinity, hence preserves the ten P-roots and lifts to a nontrivial x-acting automorphism of X. This contradicts Aut(X)=C3. Therefore x1=x2; the corresponding y-functions differ by a cube root of unity. The original maps differ by that C3 automorphism, as claimed.

No map to another endpoint was used. If an actual Y-leg exists on S it is retained, while the auxiliary S′→Y remains ramified as stated in the bridge. The proof establishes the genus-one composite case only. In higher genus, D→E need not be an isogeny and there are no global translation kernels to which the divisor argument can be applied.
