# Proof: the exact area, a congruence and the first wild break

Version1. [Statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/wild_small_tame_quotient_profile_reduction.md).
Reuse the actual two-value quotient and pure-inertia ledger. No substitute
source, numerical enumeration or global prime-to-five group hypothesis is used.

The area implies A(Δ−e)=B+1 for A=n/e,B=n/m. Thus gcd(A,B)=1 and
n=lcm(e,m). Write w=5^s=e/h. The ramification-group different formula
gives Δ≥e+w−2. It also gives Δ−e≡−1 mod4, because every positive
ramification-group order is a power of five. On the other hand
\[
\Delta-e=e/m+e/n\le e/m+1.
\]
The case h=1 is the accepted pure-inertia profile(10,5,2,8).

## Tame quotient two

If m=2, then n=e and Δ−e=e/2+1=w+1≡2 mod4, contradicting the
different congruence. Hence m≥3. The lower and upper bounds imply
e/2−2≤e/3+1, so e≤18 and necessarily e=10,w=5.

The wild subgroup is C5. Its unique positive lower break j gives
Δ=(e−1)+j(5−1)=9+4j. Since m≥3, the upper bound gives
Δ≤10+10/3+1<15, so j=1 and Δ=13. Now
3=10/m+10/n. Since m is prime to five, g=gcd(10,m) is1or2 and
n=10m/g. Thus 3m−10=g. Only g=2,m=4 is integral with the correct
gcd; n=20. This yields the second displayed profile.

## Tame quotient three

Here e=3w is odd. Since n=lcm(e,m) is even, m is even. If m=2,
n=2e and Δ−e=(e+1)/2. The different congruence requires e≡5 mod8.
But3·5^s is3or7 mod8, contradiction. Therefore m≥4. The bounds give
e/3−2≤e/4+1, so e≤36 and necessarily e=15,w=5.

Again the wild subgroup is C5, with one break j. The tame quotient has
order three. In a local parameter, conjugation by a tame generator of
tangent character λ multiplies the first wild coefficient by λ^j or
its inverse. The coefficient group of C5 is a one-dimensional F5
space, so λ^j must belong to F5×, of order dividing four. Since λ
has order three, this forces3|j. Hence j≥3 and
Δ=(15−1)+4j≥26. The area upper bound instead gives
Δ≤15+15/4+1<20, contradiction. Thus h=3 is impossible.

This is a profile classification for these small tame quotients only.
No actual common cover or exclusion of its two remaining profiles is claimed.
