# Exact small Cartier image for the fixed-origin pure-odd large-wild forms

Version1,3 October2026. Pending independent review. On the smooth genus-two curve y²=Φ=(w+q)(w⁴−1), q∉F5, put σ=dw/y and let P be infinity. Take F=y(w−a), where a³=4/q. Then the inverse-Frobenius semilinear map u↦C(uF³σ) from L(6P) to H0(ωY(5P)) has rank exactly THREE. Its image is the direct sum of the full TWO-dimensional polynomial-dw sector and ONE polynomial-σ line.

The latter line is generated by C(yF³σ). In the basis σ,wσ,w²σ,w³σ, the fifth powers of its coefficient vector are
\[
\left(
a(2q^2+2qa-a^2),\;
3a(q+a+q^4a),\;
2a(1+q^4+q^3a),\;
q^2(q^2+3qa+3a^2)
\right).
\]
This vector is nonzero. The global small-Cartier obstruction therefore has dimension THREE. For the actual large-wild leading/fourth jet conditions one may choose a hyperelliptically invariant interpolant, so its obstruction Ω lies in the polynomial-σ sector. Vanishing of its class is precisely proportionality of its four coefficients to the displayed line after fifth roots.

This gives an exact bounded image test for each of the THREE remaining fixed-origin pure-odd forms. It does not prove that the obstruction class is nonzero there. The TWO fixed mixed forms and all actual endpoint maps remain outside this image calculation; both original maps stay on the SAME T.
