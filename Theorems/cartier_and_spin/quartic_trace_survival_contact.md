# Surviving quartic traces and stronger branch contact

Version1, 25 September2026. In the actual quartic comparison problem
of [cubic descent](pole_twelve_cubic_descent.md), the two global trace
equations have a nonempty locus. For the same four distinct root labels
at both ends they have exactly30,517,187,490 residue profiles modulo5.
Their canonical integer representatives have14<=n<=128 and meet the
numerical fiber budgets. They do not construct u,v or an etale span.

An explicit profile has n=42, epsilon=2, and weight2 at fifteen of the
29 allowed common-pole fibers, weight0 elsewhere. A connected quartic
parameter cover with the indicated unramified fibers also exists, but
the simultaneous endpoint maps have not been constructed.

For an ACTUAL model, two coefficient-one common-pole branches at the
same parameter value have intersection multiplicity4 modulo5. Hence
\[
g(S)\le3n-21-4\sum_c\binom{s_c}{2},
\]
where s_c counts such branches. A bound retaining singular branches is
\[
g(S)\le3n-21-\sum_c\mu(d_c),\qquad
(\mu(0),\ldots,\mu(6))=(0,0,4,1,3,9,8).
\]
The explicit degree42 profile would have g(S)<=45. These inequalities
do not exclude it. An exhaustive empty-trace strategy is now disproved;
the actual quartic reconstruction problem, n=14..184, remains open.
