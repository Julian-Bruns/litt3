# Unbounded actual acyclic atlas covers with full intrinsic tensor rank

Let C:v^2=t^6+3 be the genus-two Hermitian quotient from
`hermitian_genus_two_test`, and let V be its actual normalized atlas
bundle. For every sufficiently large prime ell!=5 there exists a
connected cyclic finite etale cover

    pi:C_ell -> C,       degree ell,       genus(C_ell)=ell+1,

such that H0(C_ell,pi^*V)=0. The pulled-back Hermitian atlas is actual
and untwisted, its normalized rank-two bundle is stable, and its full
intrinsic extension tensor is surjective, of rank12ell.

In particular acyclicity together with full universal tensor rank
occurs for actual atlases in unbounded genera. Neither condition, nor
their conjunction, is a common-cover exclusion.
