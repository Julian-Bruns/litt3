# Actual comparison norms are invariant through pole2,794,058

Version3,3 October2026. The sharp modular-relaxation boundary is
included directly below; the separate failed-route theorem is removed.
Retain the fixed curve, marked points and
complete principal-divisor lattice in
[marked_divisor_relation_lattice](marked_divisor_relation_lattice.md).
Let two actual finite etale maps from the SAME smooth proper source
satisfy the second-Cartier-line comparison normal form. If their
nonconstant comparison function has degree delta<2,794,059, both
normalized endpoint norms are polynomials in the respective x-coordinate.

The new threshold uses an additional two-map condition from the accepted
marked-norm reply: the three zero multiplicities in each cubic root fibre
are congruent modulo three. It is stronger than the unrestricted
supported-function threshold1,617,894. In particular none of the twelve
old minimizing divisors can be an actual comparison norm.

More precisely, let I be the exact rank-eight principal relation lattice,
with coordinates(a0,...,a3,b0,...,b3) representing the sheet differences.
Every actual noninvariant norm gives a nonzero vector in
I_actual=I intersect 3Z^8. This sublattice has index81 in I. Its minimum
effective-pole gauge is exactly2,794,059, attained by twelve vectors.
This is an exact minimum for a NECESSARY lattice, not a claim that a
minimizer is realized by maps.

Consequently all actual comparison poles<=2,794,058 not divisible by
three are excluded, independently of the covering degree. Whenever
the common sheet mass at EACH marked root is at most19, the
[unbounded modular phase theorem](unbounded_modular_phase_balance.md)
also gives integer phase balance and the four direct-source trace
equations. This extra mass condition holds automatically for delta<=57.
The earlier supported/28 and actual/57/114 threshold nodes are thus
replaced by the two exact lattices; their independent geometric methods
and certificates remain in
[geometric support tests](supported_norm_geometric_methods.md) and
[integer phase costs](actual_comparison_norm_phase_cost.md).

The necessary-lattice minimum2,794,059 is attained by a supported
function whose multiplicities admit nonnegative29-phase arrays with
all nontrivial Fourier moments zero in characteristic five. It also
satisfies the canonical supported-logarithmic equation. Thus these
additional necessary conditions do not enlarge this minimum.
The short construction is in the proof; it realizes a supported
function, not a norm of actual maps.

The invariant norm cases, including comparison pole24, remain open.
No recognition bound in the covering degree and no unmarked common-cover
decision follows.
