# Affine symmetry of prime-field hyperelliptic families

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p≥5. For a∉F_p, let
C_a be the smooth projective model of y²=(x^p−x)(x−a). Its genus
is (p−1)/2 and its geometric automorphism group is C₂.

The curves C_a and C_b are isomorphic exactly when b=ua+v for
u∈F_p^×, v∈F_p. Thus each geometric isomorphism class has exactly
p(p−1) parameters, and a complete invariant for these classes is

    I(a)=(a^p−a)^(p−1).

For algebraic a and q a power of p, put
d=[F_q(a):F_q] and m=[F_q(I(a)):F_q]. The q-Frobenius moduli orbit
has length m, and

    d/m is either p or a divisor of p−1.

In particular m≥d/p, and m=d if gcd(d,p(p−1))=1. This is a
statement about geometric isomorphism classes; it preserves no chosen
branch-point markings.

For the genus-two family Y_t:v²=u(u−1)(u−2)(u−3)(u−t), t∉F₅,
use a=1/(t−4). Its complete invariant is

    I_t=((1/(t−4))^5−1/(t−4))^4.

Its automorphism group is C₂; each class has twenty such parameters.
Its moduli orbit has full length for any prime parameter degree r>5.
All quadratic parameters give the same geometric curve class. Including
the smooth rational parameter t=4, every class in this family has at
most twenty parameters.
