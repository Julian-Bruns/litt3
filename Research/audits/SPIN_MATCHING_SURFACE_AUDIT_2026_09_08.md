## Scope

The checked statement assumes smooth projective connected curves over an
algebraically closed field of characteristic 5. The root-data assertions
used as inputs are that the root covers have degree 7, branch exactly at
the indicated nonempty reduced divisors, have the stated characters, and
that a compatible spin span induces a common equivariant root curve W.
The draft does not define spin-Cartier data, so this audit checks the
surface construction conditional on those explicitly described inputs;
it does not re-audit the earlier root-cover construction.

No substantive obstruction to the global surface or its normal line was
found. Neither an embedding assumption nor a simultaneous Galois closure
is needed. Both original finite etale legs remain part of the inputs.

## Global torsor and its resolution

Regularity of dH implies that every negative Laurent exponent of H is
divisible by 5. Over the stated base, taking fifth roots of its finite
polar part produces a rational local function to subtract. Differences
of the resulting regular primitives lie in the kernel of d on the
function field, hence are fifth powers; their roots are regular on the
overlap by normality. Uniqueness of these roots proves the cocycle
condition for translations of z. Thus the alpha_5 torsor glues globally.
Changing rational primitives changes it by an isomorphism. The nonzero
differential alpha_A-alpha_B proves integrality. The hypersurface
Jacobian criterion and R1/S2 prove the asserted singular locus and
normality. Translation transition maps give the trivial relative
dualizing line; the torsor is projective because it is finite over a
projective surface.

The formal change of parameter to t^9 is legitimate since 9 is invertible
and the base is algebraically closed. After blowing up the base origin,
the normalization at each of the nine roots is indeed the fifth Veronese
ring: its fraction field is generated by a^5, b^5, and a^4b, and its
normalization is generated by all degree-5 monomials. Its standard
resolution has exceptional square -5. Away from the nine roots, writing
u=1-v^9 and r=t/z1 gives t=u*r^5 and z1=u*r^4, exhibiting smoothness.
The central divisor has multiplicity 5 over the base exceptional divisor,
so its rational square before resolving is -1/5. Each attached resolution
subtracts 1/5, giving -2. Adjunction gives the discrepancies -27 and -6.
All components have square at most -2, so the displayed resolution is
minimal over the singularity.

## Actual matching curves and the normal line

The matching equation has a unique fifth root in k(W). Integrality over
the local rings of W extends the section to S, and properness extends
the generically defined lift to R. With s=t*v, integrality of z forces
v(0)^9=1 and ord(z)>=2. Consequently the chart
`w=t*h^5, z=t^2*h` contains the lifted branch with h regular. Its parameter
t proves that the differential is injective and that it meets exactly
one outer component transversely, avoiding the central component.

For any such unramified map, even with self-intersections, the tangent
sequence has a locally free rank-one quotient N. Its determinant gives
`N = omega_W tensor (i*omega_R)^(-1)` without using an image divisor.
Since both legs are etale, the base canonical bundle restricts to
omega_W^2. The exceptional correction is -6D_W, which gives N=O(-2D_W).
This local use of the normal bundle is consistent with the general
unramified/lci discussion in [Stacks, Tag 06B9](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/06B9)
and [Tag 0CBR](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0CBR).

## Equivariance, fixed centers, and descent

Tame averaging supplies eigen-primitives. The unique lift of the group
action to the purely inseparable torsor has z-character 3. Equivariant
formal parameters give the characters (4,2) on (t,h), hence
`h=t^4*lambda(t^7)`. The successive second-coordinate characters are
2,5,1,4,0. At each of the first four required centers the fixed point
away from the preceding boundary intersection is intrinsic: it is the
unique other fixed point on that exceptional P1. Thus these centers
depend on the endpoint surface and its action, not on a particular W.
Formal coordinates suffice to identify the centers and check the local
properties; the blowups themselves are algebraic blowups of these points.

Each strict lifted branch passes through each center with multiplicity
one, so the four blowups twist N by O(-4D_W). On the final chart the
quotient is (t,lambda) -> (t^7,lambda). A branch is the graph
lambda=F(t^7); the differential on its normal quotient is an isomorphism,
including at t=0. Away from D_W, projection to the root endpoint rules
out stabilizers. Hence all relevant images lie in the smooth quotient
locus. Resolving its remaining singularities does not change their normal
lines. Invariance of the two maps to X,Y gives actual descended morphisms
T->X,Y and the required factorizations for every input span.

Riemann-Hurwitz gives pi_W*omega_Z=O(2D_W). The normal calculation yields
pi_W*N_(Z/T)=O(-6D_W). Pullback on Pic is injective for the connected,
ramified prime-degree cover: the norm kills any kernel by 7; a nontrivial
kernel line of order 7 defines a connected etale degree-7 cover of Z
through which W maps. Equality of degrees would identify it with W,
contradicting ramification. This proves N_(Z/T)=omega_Z^(-3).

## Optional numerical check and limits

The numbers also check: K_S^2=640*d_A*d_B, and the discrepancy square
per singularity is 9*(-6)*3=-162. The radicial torsor has the same etale
Euler characteristic as A times B, namely 64*d_A*d_B. Each exceptional
tree has Euler characteristic 20-9=11, replacing one point and adding 10.
Thus K_R^2=478*d_A*d_B and c2(R)=74*d_A*d_B.

This provides one surface carrying all actual matches as unramified
parametrized curves. It proves no bound on their degrees, no exclusion
of such curves, and no solution of the original unmarked problem.
