## Actual hypotheses

\[
k(C₀)=k(t,x₁,x₂),\quad q₀(x₂)=t⁶q₀(x₁),\quad
\operatorname{div}(t)=H₁-H₂,\quad θ₁=κt^{16}θ₂.
\]
Both original endpoint legs stay on the SAME original T when present. Use the ACTUAL integral conic image C normalized by C₀, with u=x₂+1,v=t³(x₁+1), and its SAME exact effective conductor-adjoint R=C−6L with ν*R=Δ. Let ρ define R and let a be the ORIGINAL extension of ρy₂ in3D+10F∞+R, with its GENERAL error ε₂∈H⁰(C−8L).

Suppose R contains NO F∞ component. Let N⊂R be a smooth multiplicity-ONE section disjoint from BOTH boundary sections D± and ordinary on F∞, meaning its finite infinity-conic point has U=t⁻³u≠0. Suppose the COMPLETE divisor R−N, with all its multiplicities, divides a. Boundary and finite vertical components of R−N are included in this premise.

## Conclusion

Then N divides a as well. In particular a shares the ENTIRE conductor factor ρ. Equivalently, an original cubic extension cannot be generically active only on one ordinary section while sharing every other conductor component in the stated degree range.

The individual's own error is forced to vanish in this argument; no zero-error hypothesis or vanishing of the OTHER error is assumed. For r16–19 the complete factor contradicts the accepted [full individual cubic-factor boundary](canonical_split_full_cubic_extension_degree_twenty_boundary.md), so the stated factor premise itself is impossible. At r20 it forces the formal boundary C~10L,R~4L and a full individual extension; that boundary remains OPEN and is not excluded here.

The ACTUAL endpoint swap gives the corresponding first-leg assertion: if R has no F₀ component, N is ordinary on F₀, and R−N divides the original extension a₁ of ρy₁ in3D+10F₀+R, then N also divides a₁. The isomorphic section b=t¹⁰a₁ has the same conductor divisibility. No auxiliary map supplies either original endpoint leg.

The theorem does not cover multiple residual poles, a nonsection or nonsmooth missing component, multiplicity>1 of N, an auxiliary missing section, or the case where R contains the fully extending endpoint's pole fiber. It is not a whole degree16–20 source exclusion or an unmarked common-cover solution.
