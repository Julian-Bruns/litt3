# Proof: a wild fixed-point orbit exceeds the entire row-section degree

Version2,3 October2026. Pending independent whole review; see the [exact statement](../../Theorems/cartier_and_spin/rank_three_scalar_carrier_central_wild_freeness.md). This global row proof supersedes the earlier depth-TEN calculation; no computation is used.

The cyclic FIVE-power subgroup generated by σ has a unique genuine lift on Q compatible with Q⁸=ω. Its scalar obstruction splits and it has no nontrivial scalar character. The paired coefficient lift on V and the adjoint row lift on M are consequently genuine on this subgroup. They have FIVE-power order, so their fiber action at EVERY fixed point of σ is ONE.

For every complete row section s∈V^[5]⊂H0(Z,M), the actual global section difference
\[
\sigma s-s
\]
therefore vanishes at EVERY σ-fixed point. If all such differences vanish identically, σ acts trivially projectively on the complete coefficient module. Its free coefficient kernel then prevents it from fixing z unless it is the identity. For a nonidentity fixed-point element at leastONE section difference is nonzero.

The centralizer C_A(σ) sends z to σ-fixed points. Its orbit has size |C_A(σ)|/|C_A(σ)∩I_z|. A nonzero global section of M can have at most degM zeros, proving the displayed centralizer bound.

If σ is central, this orbit is the ENTIRE A-orbit of z, of size N/i where i=|I_z|. Stabilizers act freely on the actual étale S→Z fiber, so i divides e and i≤e. Thus
\[
N/i\ge N/e=n>3n/4=\deg M.
\]
Every section difference must be zero globally, contrary to the preceding faithful coefficient argument. Hence central wild elements have no fixed points, independently of their local break.

The paired global lift is essential: the differences are sections of the actual line M, not arbitrary local primitives. Centrality or the explicit centralizer orbit is also essential. For a general noncentral element there may be too few fixed points, and no cutoff follows merely from trivial action on one fiber.
