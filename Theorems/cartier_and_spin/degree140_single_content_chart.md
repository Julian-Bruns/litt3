# A sparse chart for one-sheet content, with its exceptional locus excluded

Version1, 27 September2026. Use the normalized constant degree140 family
and the local coefficients B=B0+B1 H, C=C0+C1 H, E=E0+E1 H and D
at a chosen endpoint sheet y=w v. Put Delta=B1 C0-B0 C1 and
M=(Q-L0^5)(r)/t^3, interpreted by exact division before evaluation.

No geometric square residual with positive endpoint content occurs
on Delta=0. At each of the three endpoint roots this exceptional
incidence has15 reduced marked coefficient points, all excluded for
every scale by exact square-coefficient unit identities.

Every remaining positive-content square therefore belongs to B Delta
nonzero. There the rational variable z=C/B gives the equivalent chart
\[
H=\frac{C_0-B_0z}{B_1z-C_1},\qquad
G(v,z)=-D B_1z^6+D C_1z^5+2M\Delta z^2
 +(E_0B_1-E_1B_0)z+(E_1C_0-E_0C_1)=0.
\]
For each endpoint G has34 Laurent monomials, v-exponents -8 through7,
and z-exponents among0,1,2,5,6. Thus v^8G has bidegree at most(15,6).
The inverse denominator B1 z-C1 is nonzero on this chart. This chart
does not decide the remaining square equations or imply a cover.
