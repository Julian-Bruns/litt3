## Nonbreaking suggestions and qualifications

1. An isogeny quotient means a surjective homomorphism J(W) -> A^r,
   equivalently a factor up to isogeny, not that the quotient map has
   finite kernel. The proof uses this wording.
2. Use total degree in norm composed with pullback. Inseparability
   does not prevent surjectivity of norm.
3. Fiber products against an etale map remain smooth over the smooth
   source even if the other map is inseparable; no extra normalization
   should be inserted or assumed necessary there.
4. The y^31=x(x-1) example requires p != 31. Its application in this
   investigation is only at p=5.
5. Do not infer an etale second curve map, bounded multiplicity in the
   full etale tower, or a contradiction to restricted-tower results.
