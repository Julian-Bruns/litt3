# No fully concentrated actual quintic comparison

Version1, 27 September2026. Use the actual pole-fifteen comparison and
simultaneous quotient of [cubic descent](pole_fifteen_cubic_descent.md).
Write N=k(u,t)=k(v,t), [N:k(t)]=5. The five points at each of t=0 and
t=infinity are distinct and unramified. A leading endpoint label is a
root of A together with its allowed29th-root phase.

There is no such actual comparison for which all five labels coincide
at each endpoint. The two endpoint labels need not equal each other.
The exclusion retains arbitrary geometric nonzero scalar, every allowed
covering degree, repeated resonant coefficients and wild ramification
away from the specified fibers. No Galois assumption is made.

The proof uses an additional adjacent-coefficient identity, separation
of F_(5^8) from F_(5^14), and a local five-function trace determinant.
It does not infer zero full traces merely from fivefold cancellation.

This is a conditional tensor-comparison theorem. The original unmarked
common-cover problems and the other pole-fifteen comparisons are not
decided by this statement.
