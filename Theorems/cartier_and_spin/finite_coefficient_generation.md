# Irreducible finite coefficients detect etale generation of K

Version1,23September2026. Let K be the actual stable degree-one bundle
in [the explicit extension theorem](explicit_degree_one_etale_sections.md).
Taking the six proved nonperiodicity exclusions at O,([9],0),([14],0)
as inputs, the following conditions are equivalent:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&K\text{ is globally generated after a connected finite etale cover};\\
&K\text{ has a nonzero section after such a cover};\\
&\operatorname{Hom}(V,K)\ne0
  \text{ for an etale-trivializable finite coefficient bundle }V;\\
&\operatorname{Hom}(R,K)\ne0
  \text{ for an irreducible such bundle }R,\quad\operatorname{rk}R\ge2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Every nonzero finite-coefficient image has rank two. An irreducible
source of rank at least three surjects onto K. A rank-two irreducible
source is a degree-zero elementary modification E in K; one of its
cubic or arithmetic conjugates E' is distinct, and E+E'=K. Thus a
semisimple generating coefficient exists whenever any coefficient
witness exists. This is extraction of a simple subquotient, not
semisimplification of an arbitrary evaluation representation.

For an irreducible finite coefficient R of rank r the exact remaining
test is
\[
\operatorname{Hom}(R,K)=\ker\left[
H^0(R^\vee(6O))\xrightarrow{\ s\mapsto[e\,s]\ }
H^1(R^\vee(-5O))\right],\qquad h^1(R^\vee(-5O))=13r.
\]
No nonzero kernel or all-rank injectivity is proved. The sixteen
strict-periodicity cases also remain open. This is a one-endpoint
equivalence, not a construction of either original common cover.
