# Litt Problem 3 — OPEN

Let k be the algebraic closure of F_5. Must every pair X,Y of smooth
projective connected curves over k, both of genus at least two, have
a smooth projective connected curve Z and finite etale maps Z -> X
and Z -> Y?

The investigation seeks a counterexample, but no counterexample is
proved. Both maps must be everywhere etale and have the SAME source.
Bounds on cored spans, marked-curve counterexamples, and conditional
generic-vanishing claims do not settle this unmarked question.
