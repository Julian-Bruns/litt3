# Uniform exclusion of ordinary common-critical points

Version1,30 September2026. Use the actual trace-zero quadratic-
derivative degree-ten source problem and its constant or linear v
profiles in [the two-profile theorem](admissible_degree_ten_two_profiles.md).
Retain the full coefficient spaces before restricting the residual
degree to140,142 or144. Write t=A/(13(x-alpha)) in the fixed support
convention and B0 for the original regular primitive interpolant.

After Z=yW-B0 put
\[
\phi=W^5+(Q-B_0^5)/y^5,\qquad
S=g_2W^3+g_3W^2+g_4W+g_5.
\]
For any hypothetical actual everywhere-etale source, there is no
affine point of X with tv nonzero at which g2=g3=g4=0. No square-norm,
critical-discriminant or parameter-degree hypothesis is required.
Every geometric coefficient specialization in the original spaces is
included. This also includes the cubic branch points with tv nonzero.

The finite-dimensional input has a stronger exact form. On the entire
eight-dimensional homogeneous source module, with scalar coordinate
kappa, put delta0=3y^2 partial_x+P' partial_y. There are fixed polynomial
functions a0,...,a8 in K[x,y]/(y^3-P) such that
\[
\sum_{j=0}^2\sum_{m=0}^2
 a_{3j+m}\,\delta_0^m g_{j+2}=\kappa.
\]
Thus the three critical coefficients cannot simultaneously vanish to
order three at ANY affine point when kappa is nonzero. The functions
a_i have no affine denominators. Their coefficient data and a small
reconstruction are retained in the proof.

The t-endpoints and the marked point v=0 are outside the actual-source
conclusion: its local splitting argument has a unit hypothesis there.
This theorem excludes a common-critical locus, not the whole remaining
degree-ten coefficient family or the unmarked common-cover problem.
