# Complete exclusion of the actual primitive degree-eight character quotient

ID: `primitive_degree_eight_exclusion`. Version1,2 October2026.

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic5 let Y be smooth genus2. Fix distinct Weierstrass points O,W and a non-Weierstrass point P_s, with hyperelliptic conjugate P_s^-, such that D_s=[P_s-O] satisfies5D_s=[O-W]. Put A=4D_s, of exact order5, and fix ell with divisor5A and dlog ell=-beta for a nonzero regular beta.

There is NO connected finite etale q:S->Y of degree8 with a primitive function k(S)=k(Y)(chi), pole divisor5G for one point G, and dchi=q^*eta+chi q^*beta for a regular eta on Y, satisfying the following ACTUAL first-leg condition: with lambda=q^*A^3(-2G), a further connected finite etale pi:T->S and finite etale h:T->X from this SAME source identify the embedded lines pi^*lambda=h^*lambda_X, where omega_X=lambda_X^-8.

The exclusion covers every residual degree deg(pi) and every monodromy type of q, including genuinely nongalois degree8. It does not assume S=X or that chi is Galois over the projective line.

In the actual opposite-adjunction and same-target nonzero-character sector, both surviving characters on every smooth V^2=(x^4-1)(x-S) have these point data, with W=W_S, A=O(4(P_s-O)), ell=h_s^2 and beta=-2sx dx/V. The primitive quotient S=normalization(k(Y)(phi/u)) retains the ORIGINAL etale factor of the second map and, by the square-root calibration, the ORIGINAL embedded lambda from X. Thus its residual parameter m=n/deg(T/S) cannot be1; necessarily m>=2.

The proof uses the actual canonical norm to exclude all nonzero homogeneous character functions in this small pole space.

This does not supply opposite descent on an arbitrary unmarked span, recognize the original X-field through the quotient, or resolve the unmarked common-cover problem.
