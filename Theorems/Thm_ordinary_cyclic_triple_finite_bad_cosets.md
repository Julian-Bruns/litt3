# Finite bad cosets for ordinary cyclic étale triples of genus-two curves

Over k=F̄_5, let Y be ordinary of genus two and q:U→Y a connected
finite étale cyclic cover of degree three. Assume U is also ordinary.
On scalar Frobenius twists put

    J=J(U^(1)), A=im(q^(1)*), P=(ker Nm_(q^(1)))^0,
    Q=J/A, π:J→Q, ψ=π|P,
    B={z∈Q : π^(-1)(z)⊂Theta_U}.

Then B is a finite closed set of geometric points and 0∉B. Every
other coset has a nonempty open locus where H^0(B_U⊗N)=0. This
includes all geometric points, without a torsion-order restriction.

For α∈P(k), define

    δ_α=generic_L h^0(U^(1),B_U⊗α⊗q^(1)*L), L∈J(Y^(1)).

Only finitely many α have δ_α>0. Let Λ_0 be the finite subgroup
generated by those exceptional α of order prime to five. For any
finite prime-to-five character subgroup Λ⊂P(k), form its connected
abelian étale character cover on U^(1) and untwist to b_Λ:W_Λ→U.
Then

    generic_L h^0(W_Λ^(1),B_(W_Λ)⊗(qb_Λ)^(1)*L)
       =∑_(α∈Λ) δ_α.

These generic defects are uniformly bounded over Λ and constant
whenever Λ contains Λ_0. The constant may be positive; each Λ has
its own generic open set.

This does not eliminate isolated bad cosets, imply ordinarity of
arbitrary covers, bound their a-numbers, or construct a second map.
The ordinary-U hypothesis cannot be dropped.
