# A cyclic order-five wild subgroup leaves nine local profiles

Version1,3 October2026. Conceptual profile classification passed [independent focused review](../../Research/audits/CYCLIC_FIVE_WILD_INERTIA_PROFILE_AUDIT_2026_10_03.md).

Retain both actual finite étale maps and the actual ramified spin quotient of the [two-branch reduction](wild_ramified_spin_two_branch_reduction.md). Suppose the first positive ramification group of its wild inertia is cyclic of order FIVE. Write its inertia order as e=5h, its unique lower break as j, its different as Δ, its other tame inertia as m, and its effective degree as n. Then the complete possible numerical profiles are:

|h|n|e|m|Δ|j|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|10|5|2|8|1|
|2|20|10|4|13|1|
|4|20|20|2|31|3|
|4|40|20|8|23|1|
|4|60|20|3|27|2|
|4|140|20|7|23|1|
|6|30|30|3|41|3|
|8|120|40|6|47|2|
|12|60|60|6|71|3|

This is a necessary numerical list, not an existence claim or an exclusion of these profiles. Larger wild subgroups and the étale branch remain outside this statement. In particular, the original common-cover problem remains unsolved.
