> Let L/K be a finite separable extension generated by b, and let F be

Proposed proof correction:

Choose c in an actual algebraic closure with c^p=−f. The factor W^p+f
is irreducible over K, with minpoly(c)=W^p+f. For a monic separable
source polynomial, actual norm coefficient extension and its actual
split quotient decomposition prove Nm(f+b^p)=((-1)^N F(c))^p.
If this norm is a nonzero p-th power a^p in K, Frobenius injectivity
forces F(c)=((-1)^N)^{-1}a∈K×. Consequently W^p+f divides F−τ,
which gives the presentation. Evaluating any two presentations at c
proves equality of τ, and cancellation of the nonzero factor proves
equality of H. The same norm formula proves the converse. Positive
degree forces H≠0 and then N=p+deg H≥p.

For nonmonic F, normalize by its nonzero leading coefficient. The
actual source quotient equivalence proves its determinant norm is
unchanged; multiplying the normalized presentation by the leading
coefficient gives the exact original-polynomial presentation. Thus
no monic or connectedness input is required in the corrected finite
source-algebra clause. The primitive field is identified with its
actual minimal-polynomial quotient using its genuine generator;
the power basis and quotient equivalence are constructed.

All steps are symbolic and uniform in p. No source-degree division,
prime-specific enumeration, numerical roots or external computation
certificates are used.
