Develop an explicit scalar reconstruction theorem for the following
problem. The goal is to test a prescribed dormant rank-two projective
connection for an additional Hermitian structure, without constructing
unknown rank-three vector bundles. This is one bounded step in work on
Litt's common finite-etale-cover problem; you are not being asked to
resolve that problem.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic 5 and let C be the smooth
projective model of

    y^3=F(x),

where F is any monic squarefree polynomial of degree 10. Let O be the
unique point at infinity, omega=omega_C, and theta=dx/y^2. Then g(C)=9,
div(theta)=16O, and the pole semigroup at O is generated by 3 and 10.
Write D=d/dx. Use a fixed theta characteristic O_C(8O).

The input is a globally regular dormant projective connection, given by
the scalar equation u''=r u, where

    r=R(x)+B(x)/F(x)+C_0(x)y/F(x)+A(x)y^2/F(x)^2,
    R=2F''/F+2(F'/F)^2+2x^8/F,
    deg B<=7,  deg C_0<=4,  deg A<=10,
    r''=3r^2.

All such r, and only such r, give the dormant projective connections
on C. The last equation is scheme-theoretically the zero-p-curvature
condition; we have already derived and independently checked it, and
are solving its 96 quadratic coefficient equations in 24 variables.
Do not spend the response enumerating those connections. Their scheme
has length 29375, possibly with fewer distinct points.

Let H be the Hermitian plane curve X_0^6+X_1^6+X_2^6=0. The target
question is whether there is a representable finite etale atlas

    C -> [H/PSU_3(5)]

whose associated dormant projective connection is the prescribed r.
The untwisted PSU case is intentional; no assertion about the additional
3-torsion PGU cases is requested.

You may take the following proved bundle criterion as input.

Let K be the unique nonsplit extension

    0 -> O_C --e--> K -> omega^-1 -> 0.

Such an atlas is equivalent to an extension and a nonsingular Frobenius
form

    0 -> K -> E --q--> omega^2 -> 0,
    beta:E tensor F_C^*E -> omega^2,
    beta(-,F_C^*e)=q.

Here F_C is absolute Frobenius; nonsingular means that the adjoint is an
isomorphism, not merely injective. No symmetry condition on an O_C-valued
Gram matrix is imposed. The condition really constructs an etale atlas:
projective orthonormal frames form a finite etale PGU_3(5)-torsor, the
distinguished isotropic line maps it to H, and transversality is automatic.
Indeed the second fundamental scalar s satisfies
s[K]=-c_1(det E)/3 in H^1(omega), and deg E=16 is nonzero in k.
The discriminant line is trivial here, giving the PSU reduction.

The associated rank-two object is V=E/O_C, with

    0 -> omega^-1 -> V --pi--> omega^2 -> 0,
    F_C^*V ~= K^vee tensor omega^2.

Equivalently W=V tensor O_C(-8O) has trivial determinant and
F_C^*W is the nonsplit extension of O_C(-8O) by O_C(8O).
Its canonical Cartier connection, up to projective gauge, is the r
specified above. Given r, V is recovered uniquely up to isomorphism by
Cartier descent. Dormant rank-two objects exist on every curve; their
existence alone cannot prove this extra Hermitian lift.

We also already have an abstract extension-class test: for a FIXED pi
and a FIXED isomorphism j:K -> (F_C^*V)^vee tensor omega^2, the remaining
lift reduces to

    i(lambda)-T_j(lambda)=b_j,
    lambda in H^1(omega^-2),
    i:H^1(omega^-2) -> H^1(K tensor omega^-2).

The dimensions are 40 and 96; i is injective and T_j is additive and
5-semilinear. It is obtained by pulling lambda back along pi, applying
Frobenius and dualizing the extension, then identifying its kernel by
j. Projection onto i's image gives an always-solvable finite additive
system, but 56 residual coordinates still need to vanish. The choices
of pi and j are not discrete just because the V classes are finite.
Reproving this abstract test is not the requested advance.

Desired result: replace the remaining unknown bundle maps and abstract
Ext comparison by explicit scalar equations computable from F and r.
Specify the unknowns as rational functions or sections of named line
bundles, give proved pole bounds at O (generous bounds are acceptable),
and express every condition using multiplication, differentiation,
Frobenius, coefficient extraction, or explicitly described linear maps
between finite-dimensional Riemann--Roch spaces. Include reconstruction
of E,beta or of the actual atlas from a solution, proving both necessity
and sufficiency. Preserve nowhere-degeneracy and etaleness globally,
including O; generic function-field compatibility is insufficient.

This should be a usable input to an exact polynomial solver for each r,
not an existence statement about an unspecified moduli space, a restated
rank-three Gram-matrix search, or a generic finiteness argument. No optimal
bound or running time is required. If full elimination is obstructed,
prove the largest explicit reduction you can and isolate the exact
remaining scalar datum; do not replace it by an unjustified implication.

For background, the relevant primary sources are Cheng, q-bic forms,
Props.2.5-2.7 (https://chngr.github.io/assets/qbic-forms.pdf), and
