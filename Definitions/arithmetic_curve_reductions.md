# Arithmetic curve classes and geometric reductions

For h>=2, let S_h be the geometric isomorphism classes of compact
arithmetic complex curves of genus h: quotients Gamma\H for torsion-free
cocompact arithmetic lattices Gamma in PSL_2(R). Include noncongruence
lattices and lattices not themselves contained in a quaternion
maximal-order norm-one group. Their number-field models identify S_h
with the corresponding geometric classes over bar(Q).

For a prime p, E_(h,p) is the set of smooth projective genus-h classes
over bar(F_p) occurring as potential good geometric reductions of S_h.
Allow every number-field model, place over p, finite extension needed
for good reduction, and identification of the geometric residue field
with bar(F_p). Geometric twists with isomorphic special fibers are not
counted separately. Arithmeticity here concerns the characteristic-zero
uniformizing lattice; it is not a property defined by a connection in
characteristic p.
