# Finite-level genus-two BT untwisting and Hodge-degree cutoff

Version4, 23 September 2026. Let \(C\) be a smooth projective
genus-two curve over an algebraically closed field of characteristic
\(p>2\). Let \(G/C\) be a height-two, dimension-one BT\(_N\) group
with both ordinary and supersingular fibers. Put
\(d=\deg\omega_G>0\) and \(a=v_p(d)\).
If \(N\ge a+2\), then \(d=p^a\). Intrinsic successive Frobenius
untwisting produces an everywhere-versal BT\(_{N-a}\) group \(G_0\)
on \(C\), compatible with finite étale pullback, such that
\[
F_C^{a*}G_0\simeq G[p^{N-a}].
\]
Each untwisting loses exactly one BT level. The assertion needs no
global full-group prolongation.

Now let \(X\xleftarrow f Z\xrightarrow gY\) be two ACTUAL finite
étale maps from the same smooth proper connected source, with
\(g(Y)=2\). Suppose compatible mixed BT\(_N\) groups are given on
the endpoints, with their isomorphism on \(Z\) or on a connected
finite étale refinement. If \(a=v_p(\deg\omega_{G_Y})\), then for
every \(m\ge1\),
\[
N\ge a+m+2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{the original span lifts simultaneously over }W_m.
\]
If its joint deformation ring is killed by \(p^e\), these data
necessarily satisfy \(a\ge N-e-2\). Thus arbitrarily high compatible
levels with bounded \(a\) force a simultaneous mixed-characteristic
lift. The groups at different finite levels need not be mutually
compatible.

The corresponding *full-group* untwisting, Hasse profile and
common-lift consequences are the \(f=1\) case of
[unramified rank-two BT untwisting](unramified_bt_genus_two.md).
Its arithmetic companion consequence also contains the earlier
degree-one trace-place case. The finite-level cutoff above is the
distinct assertion retained here.
