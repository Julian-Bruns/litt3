# Stability of a late relative Hodge response

Version2, 2026-09-15. The local calculation is a two-digit consequence
of [integral oper calculus](integral_oper_calculus.md). The global
conclusion concerns genuine compatible marked prefixes.

Let p>=5 and use that theorem's fixed charts, coefficient Frobenius,
twist, current connections and genuine preceding potential P. For
r>=2 use source precision p^(r+3) and flat precision p^(r+2). Change
the source generator X by -p^(r+1)N, where N represents a primary-
kernel direction, and insert its whole signed primary graph repairs
p^r*q_U,p^r*q_O. Retain the preceding object modulo p^r; its actual
change, or a change of its chosen higher lift, therefore lies in p^r.
The induced current-connection change has the additional factor p².

Compare these constructions using common background X modulo p³
and current frames/connections modulo p². Choose the same N and whole
q_U,q_O modulo p² in the original charts. Then, entrywise,

    M_r(N)-M_r(0)=p^r*C_N modulo p^(r+2),

with the same C_N modulo p² for all r. Once the full primary repair
has made C_N zero modulo p, divide the WHOLE repaired normal numerator
by p^(r+1), reduce modulo p and take its normal/primary quotient.
This relative class agrees with the r=2 class. Changing higher
representatives of N or the graph sections changes only terminal
primary or regular-boundary terms. Such changes need not preserve
the unprojected matrix C_N.

There is no hypothesis on AS degree or on agreement of the background
preceding potentials: their direct contribution starts at p². Whole
primary repair is essential. Vanishing of normal H0 is not needed for
the chosen-input identity; in the curve application it makes the
regular primitive unique once its full primary preimage is chosen.

For the original marked rank125 family fix Hdagger=Hstar+[8]nu39
and the default fourth origin and Vstar of the
[actual fifth theorem](elementary_covers/rank125_fixed_line_fifth.md).
For every fourth point Vstar+tau*nu39 and compatible fifth origin,
the complete sixth response to a fifth source direction V in K is

    R6,tau(V)=A4(V)+2Q(Hdagger,V)=R_Hdagger(V),  dim K=43.

This is independent of tau and the fifth origin in the original
transported scalar coordinates. No additional Frobenius appears.
The map is additive, not necessarily linear over the coefficient field.

For every perfect F containing F125, its restriction to Gamma-fixed
points has kernel F*nu39 and image the fixed subspace E100=0.
Consequently a compatible W6(F) tuple above the invariant fourth
point tau exists if and only if its residual scalar psi6(tau) is zero,
with all fifth choices and terminal sixth repairs allowed. Averaging
also shows independence of noninvariant fifth choices. The kernel
of the full43-direction map is larger; no line-kernel claim is made
outside the stated fixed subspace.

This relative theorem computes no absolute value or parameter support.
Those are separate inputs in the
[actual sixth theorem](elementary_covers/rank125_fixed_line_sixth.md).
It supplies neither an arbitrary compatible prefix nor an unmarked
common-cover conclusion.
