# A rank-four reflection group with scalar μ8 has no imprimitive prime-five normalizer

Version1,3 October2026. New elementary linear-group lemma pending independent review. Its application uses the ACTUAL coefficient representation; no abstract representation is substituted for either original map.

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristicFIVE. Let K⊂GL₄(k) be finite and irreducible, generated by rank-ONE involutive reflections, with scalar center μ8. Suppose a finite R⊂PGL₄(k) CONTAINS the projective image of K, normalizes K by linear conjugation, and has no nontrivial quotient of order prime toFIVE. Then K is PRIMITIVE.

In particular this applies to the actual K and ENTIRE coefficient R from [the finite reflection reduction](canonical_ten_finite_reflection_group_reduction.md). It deletes the imprimitive reflection branch but does not classify primitive modular groups or assert the absence of transvections.
