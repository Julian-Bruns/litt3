algebras. Symbolic leading-form elimination specializes it to the actual
polynomial quotient. Distinct solution pairs give distinct quotient algebra
homomorphisms; Dedekind independence bounds their number. Finiteness of the
whole geometric set follows by excluding a finite subset of size m²+1.

canonical numerator and monic-denominator coefficient of both rational

over H(t) survive conjugation after embedding back into Ω(t). The geometric
point bound therefore gives [E:H]≤m². No generation hypothesis, height bound,
coefficient truncation or assumed descent is used by the complete theorem.
