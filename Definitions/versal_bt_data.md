# Compatible versal Barsotti--Tate data

Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic p>2. Write
W_m=W(k)/p^m, so W_1=k. A height-two, dimension-one BT group is a
p-divisible group of height two and dimension one. A BT_N group is a
truncated Barsotti--Tate group of level N, locally the p^N-torsion of
such a group; it is not an arbitrary finite flat group killed by p^N.

On a smooth curve C, call such a group everywhere versal when its
Kodaira--Spencer map

    T_C -> t_G tensor t_(G^dual)

is an isomorphism. Equivalently its two rank-one Hodge pieces have
maximal Higgs field. This is a condition on the actual group and its
Dieudonne data, not on the Jacobian of C. Etale pullback preserves it.

For the specified span X <-f- Z -g-> Y, compatible level-N data means
everywhere-versal BT_N groups G_X,G_Y and a specified group-scheme
isomorphism f^*G_X ~= g^*G_Y. Compatibility after refinement means the
same assertion for fh,gh with some connected finite etale h:W->Z.
The actual endpoint maps are never replaced by unrelated ones.

Data at different N need not extend one another unless explicitly
required. A projective connection, a Fontaine--Laffaille bundle, a
finite flat group, a BT_1 group, and a full BT group are distinct data.
In particular none of their existence or compatibility implications
is part of these definitions. Projective/two-torsion ambiguity cannot
be silently discarded when specifying the isomorphism of groups.
