# Exact normal form for comparisons of the second Cartier line

Version1,23September2026. Use the fixed genus-nine X, infinity O,
theta=dx/y^2, A=(1,21,14,22,13), and f=Q/y^5 with df=A^2 theta.
The saturated primitive line lambda has degree -2 and adjunction
divisor2R, where R=O+x^*div_0 A is reduced of degree13.
For any two actual finite etale maps h_i:T->X from the same smooth
proper connected curve, the following conditions are equivalent:
\[
h_1^{(1)*}\lambda=h_2^{(1)*}\lambda,
\qquad h_2^*\tau\in k^\times h_1^*\tau,
\qquad \tau=A^{16}\theta^{13}=(df)^{13}/A^{10}.
\]
Under these conditions there are z in k(T)^times and kappa in
k^times such that, writing A_i=h_i^*A and similarly for f,theta,
\[
A_2=\kappa^6z^{-13}A_1,\quad
df_2=\kappa^5z^{-10}df_1,\quad
\theta_2=\kappa^{-7}z^{16}\theta_1,\quad
\operatorname{div}z=h_2^*O-h_1^*O.
\]
Also h_1^*R=h_2^*R. If z is nonconstant it is separating, has
simple zeros and poles, and has degree at most deg h_i.

For arbitrary etale h_i, proportional pullbacks of theta force
h_2=gamma h_1 with gamma in Aut(X)=C3. Consequently, under the
equivalent line/tensor premise,
\[
h_1^*k(X)=h_2^*k(X)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad h_1^*O=h_2^*O.
\]
The universal line-recognition implication holds in covering degrees
one and two. It remains OPEN in unrestricted degree.

In the explicit Frobenius-split quotient frames l_i and e_i of the
new-line quotient theorem, a comparison has
\[
l_2=\kappa^{-20}z^{45}l_1,\qquad
e_2=\kappa^{-15}z^{35}e_1.
\]
Both factors are fifth powers even for nonconstant z. The canonical
connection is compatible with them; it does not force z to be constant.
