## The actual quotient and its genuine constant action

Let q:T→Y be that finite étale Galois closure. The trace has rank FOUR: it has rank at least THREE, and if its rank were THREE its B_Y-saturation S would pull back to every h_i*U. Equality of these saturated lattices would give 8d deg S=13d, impossible for the integer deg S. Pullback of the complete first trace is the integral sum of the ORIGINAL conjugate section images h_i*I inside B_T. Thus q*J is globally generated by those actual global sections. Its quotient q*Q is globally generated and has degree ZERO. A globally generated degree-zero vector bundle on a smooth proper curve is trivial: choose a generic basis of global sections; its nonzero determinant has degree zero and hence vanishes nowhere. The chosen sections give an integral isomorphism O_T²≅q*Q.

Global generation also splits q*J→q*Q. The images of all its generating global sections span H⁰(T,q*Q)=k². Choose two whose images are a basis. Their two global lifts define a right inverse O_T²→q*J. This splitting need not be G-equivariant and no such equivariance is asserted.

The native G-linearization on q*Q becomes a GENUINE constant representation ρ:G→GL₂(k), because T is proper and connected. Use the original ordered net basis for every h_i. Their maps to q*Q are therefore constant matrices C_i. The actual conjugate labels are transitive, and C_j=ρ(γ)C_i for a suitable γ∈G. Their ranks and kernels are all the same.

They have rank TWO. Rank zero would prevent the original conjugate images from generating q*Q. Rank one would give a constant two-dimensional kernel in each original net. Its saturation in B_T would be q*E, which has degree E times 8d=8d: E is a rank-two degree-one subbundle saturated in B_Y, as shown next. The accepted complete geometric two-form net bound gives degree at most4d, a contradiction. This is exactly the rank-two quotient argument in the accepted contact proof. Thus their common kernel is one ORIGINAL geometric constant line W.

## Saturation identifies the integral kernel

The kernel E has rank TWO and degree ONE. It is saturated in J. Its saturation in B_Y can have degree at most ONE: stability of B_Y of slope ONE gives degree strictly below TWO for every rank-two subbundle. Its saturation cannot increase the degree, so E is already saturated in B_Y. Étale pullback preserves this saturation.

Let M_W⊂B_X be the saturation of the original constant one-form line W; the accepted one-form bound gives a=deg M_W≤TWO. The generic intersection q*E∩h_i*U is precisely the common matrix kernel W, hence the generic line h_i*M_W. Both the intersection and h_i*M_W are saturated in B_T. Indeed an intersection of saturated subbundles is saturated, since its quotient embeds into the direct sum of their torsion-free quotients. The two integral lattices with this generic line therefore agree:
\[
q^*E\cap h_i^*U=h_i^*M_W.
\]
This use of rank TWO is essential; no automatic B-saturation is asserted for a degree-one rank-three quotient kernel.

## The original intersection surjects onto the quotient

Put L_i=q*J∩h_i*U. The original h_i*I lies in L_i, and its constant rank-two matrix surjects onto ALL of q*Q integrally. Therefore L_i→q*Q is surjective. Its kernel is the preceding intersection, giving the exact sequence in the statement. Degrees now give deg L_i=ad. Since U has degree THIRTEEN and the generic original net spans U,
\[
\operatorname{length}(h_i^*U/L_i)=13d-ad.
\]
Étale pullback from the initial source scales both this contact and the degree of the X leg by the same factor. Hence the initial contact is (13−a)n.
