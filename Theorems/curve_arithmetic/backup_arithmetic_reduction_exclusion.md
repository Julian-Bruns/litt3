# The backup is not a good reduction of an arithmetic genus-two curve

Version1,21September2026. Let
\[
Y/\overline{\mathbf F}_5:\quad
v^2=u(u-1)(u-2)(u-3)(u-\alpha),\qquad \alpha^3+\alpha+1=0.
\]
There is no smooth proper genus-two curve over
$\overline{\mathbf Q}$, uniformized by a compact arithmetic
Fuchsian surface group, with potentially good geometric reduction
isomorphic to $Y$ at a place over five. All arithmetic surface
groups are included, not only derived or congruence subgroups.
In the arithmetic-reduction notation, $[Y]\notin\mathcal E_{2,5}$.

The remaining maximal six-cone family is excluded as follows.
Its unique genus-two double cover has moduli over an elementary
abelian two-extension of the invariant trace field $F$.
The backup forces $3\mid f(v/5)$, and $[F:\mathbf Q]\le10$.
The volume-denominator argument makes the selected local level
split and maximal, even if $F_v/\mathbf Q_5$ is ramified.
Its arithmetic projective connection then has an integral dormant
oper reduction, with pair moduli degree dividing $2f(v/5)$.
Every dormant pair on $Y$, however, has exact moduli degree fifteen.
This is impossible for $f(v/5)\in\{3,6,9\}$.

The integral argument uses actual good Galois atlases and the active
eigensubbundle of the full Dieudonne module. It does not assume
good reduction of an arbitrary quotient map, a prime-to-five
auxiliary deck group, or an unramified trace field at five.

Consequently the backup admits no fully liftable coreless finite
etale correspondence. This still leaves intrinsically
characteristic-five coreless spans. Neither original unmarked
common-cover problem is settled by this theorem.
